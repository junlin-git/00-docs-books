## 第 21 篇：Agriculture and Food 农业与食品

**英文**

Agriculture is a fundamental sector that provides food, fibre, and raw materials for human consumption and industrial use. It involves the cultivation of crops and the rearing of livestock, and it depends on natural resources such as soil, water, and climate. Traditional farming methods have gradually been replaced by mechanised and intensive systems, which increase productivity but also raise concerns about environmental degradation and animal welfare. Furthermore, the global food system faces significant challenges, including population growth, climate change, and unequal distribution. Food security requires not only sufficient production but also equitable access to nutritious food. Researchers in agronomy and nutrition study how to improve crop yields, reduce waste, and promote sustainable practices. Genetically modified organisms offer potential benefits but also provoke ethical and regulatory debates. Moreover, consumers increasingly demand transparency about how food is produced and transported. Ultimately, achieving a sustainable and just food system requires coordinated action among farmers, governments, scientists, and consumers. By integrating ecological principles with economic incentives, we can enhance both food quality and environmental health.

**中文**

农业是一个基础部门，为人类消费和工业用途提供食物、纤维和原材料。它涉及作物种植和牲畜饲养，并依赖土壤、水和气候等自然资源。传统耕作方法已逐渐被机械化和集约化系统取代，这些系统提高了生产力，但也引发了关于环境退化和动物福利的担忧。此外，全球粮食系统面临重大挑战，包括人口增长、气候变化和不平等分配。粮食安全不仅需要充足的生产，还需要公平获取营养食品。农学和营养学的研究者研究如何提高作物产量、减少浪费和促进可持续实践。转基因生物提供了潜在好处，但也引发了伦理和监管辩论。此外，消费者越来越要求食品生产和运输方式的透明度。最终，实现可持续和公正的粮食系统需要农民、政府、科学家和消费者之间的协调行动。通过将生态原则与经济激励相结合，我们可以提高食品质量和环境健康。

## 第 22 篇：Energy and Resources 能源与资源

**英文**

Energy is essential for economic development and modern life, powering homes, industries, and transportation. However, the reliance on fossil fuels has led to environmental problems, including air pollution and greenhouse gas emissions. Renewable energy sources, such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power, offer cleaner alternatives but require significant investment and technological innovation. Furthermore, the transition to a low-carbon economy involves complex economic, social, and political considerations. Resource depletion is another critical issue; minerals, water, and arable land are finite, and their extraction and use can cause ecological damage. Efficient use and recycling can help conserve resources and reduce waste. Moreover, energy security is a major concern for many countries, as dependence on imported fuels can create geopolitical risks. Researchers are exploring new materials and storage technologies to improve efficiency and reduce costs. Ultimately, sustainable energy management requires a comprehensive approach that balances affordability, reliability, and environmental protection. By adopting evidence-based policies and encouraging behavioural change, societies can move toward a more resilient and equitable energy future.

**中文**

能源对经济发展和现代生活至关重要，为家庭、工业和交通提供动力。然而，对化石燃料的依赖导致了环境问题，包括空气污染和温室气体排放。可再生能源，如太阳能、风能和水力发电，提供了更清洁的替代方案，但需要大量投资和技术创新。此外，向低碳经济转型涉及复杂的经济、社会和政治考量。资源枯竭是另一个关键问题；矿物、水和耕地是有限的，其开采和使用可能造成生态破坏。高效利用和回收有助于节约资源 and 减少浪费。此外，能源安全是许多国家的重大关切，因为对进口燃料的依赖可能带来地缘政治风险。研究者正在探索新材料和储存技术以提高效率并降低成本。最终，可持续能源管理需要一种综合方法，平衡可负担性、可靠性和环境保护。通过采用基于证据的政策和鼓励行为改变，社会可以走向更具韧性和公平的能源未来。

## 第 23 篇：Data and Statistics 数据与统计

**英文**

Data and statistics are indispensable tools for understanding the world, making informed decisions, and evaluating the effectiveness of policies and interventions. In academic research, quantitative methods involve the collection, analysis, and interpretation of numerical data. Descriptive statistics summarise basic features of a dataset, such as mean, median, and standard deviation, while inferential statistics allow researchers to draw conclusions about populations based on samples. However, the validity of statistical findings depends on the quality of the data, the appropriateness of the methods, and the avoidance of bias. Misuse of statistics, whether intentional or accidental, can mislead the public and undermine trust in science. Furthermore, the growing availability of big data presents both opportunities and challenges, including concerns about privacy and data security. Researchers must therefore adhere to ethical guidelines and ensure transparency in their reporting. Moreover, statistical literacy is essential for citizens in a democratic society, enabling them to critically evaluate claims and participate in evidence-based debates. Ultimately, the responsible use of data and statistics enhances knowledge, improves decision-making, and contributes to social progress. By integrating rigorous methodology with ethical awareness, we can harness the power of data for the common good.

**中文**

数据和统计是理解世界、做出知情决策以及评估政策和干预效果不可或缺的工具。在学术研究中，定量方法涉及数值数据的收集、分析和解释。描述性统计概括数据集的基本特征，如均值、中位数和标准差，而推断性统计使研究者能够基于样本对总体得出结论。然而，统计发现的有效性取决于数据质量、方法的适当性以及偏差的避免。统计数据的误用，无论是有意还是无意，都可能误导公众并破坏对科学的信任。此外，大数据日益增长的可用性既带来了机遇也带来了挑战，包括对隐私和数据安全的担忧。因此，研究者必须遵守伦理准则并确保报告透明度。此外，统计素养对民主社会中的公民至关重要，使他们能够批判性地评估主张并参与基于证据的辩论。最终，负责任地使用数据和统计能增进知识、改善决策并促进社会进步。通过将严谨方法论与伦理意识相结合，我们可以利用数据的力量服务共同利益。

## 第 24 篇：Globalisation and Diversity 全球化与多样性

**英文**

Globalisation refers to the increasing interconnectedness of economies, societies, and cultures across the world. It is driven by advances in transportation, communication, and information technology, as well as by trade liberalisation and foreign investment. Globalisation has generated significant economic benefits, including access to new markets, lower prices, and the spread of innovation. However, it has also been associated with negative consequences, such as job displacement, cultural homogenisation, and rising inequality. Furthermore, the movement of people across borders has increased cultural diversity within many countries, enriching societies but also creating challenges related to integration and social cohesion. Multiculturalism, as a policy approach, seeks to accommodate diversity while promoting shared values and equal rights. Critics argue that globalisation often favours powerful corporations over local communities and vulnerable groups. Moreover, environmental degradation and labour exploitation have been linked to global supply chains. Ultimately, managing globalisation requires international cooperation, fair trade rules, and inclusive institutions. By recognising both the opportunities and risks, societies can develop strategies that maximise benefits while protecting cultural heritage and social justice.

**中文**

全球化指的是世界经济、社会和文化日益相互关联。它由交通、通信和信息技术进步，以及贸易自由化和外国投资推动。全球化产生了显著的经济效益，包括进入新市场、降低价格和创新的传播。然而，它也带来了负面后果，如工作岗位流失、文化同质化和不平等加剧。此外，人口跨境流动增加了许多国家的文化多样性，丰富了社会，但也带来了与融合和社会凝聚力相关的挑战。多元文化主义作为一种政策方法，旨在容纳多样性，同时促进共同价值观和平等权利。批评者认为，全球化往往偏袒强大的企业，而非当地社区和弱势群体。此外，环境退化和劳动剥削与全球供应链有关。最终，管理全球化需要国际合作、公平贸易规则和包容性制度。通过认识到机遇和风险，社会可以制定战略，在最大化利益的同时保护文化遗产和社会正义。

## 第 25 篇：Media and Democracy 媒体与民主

**英文**

A free and independent media is widely regarded as a cornerstone of democracy. It provides citizens with information, facilitates public debate, and holds those in power accountable. However, the media landscape has undergone profound changes in recent decades, with the rise of digital platforms and the decline of traditional newspapers. While the internet has democratised access to information, it has also enabled the rapid spread of misinformation, propaganda, and hate speech. Furthermore, media concentration and commercial pressures can limit diversity of opinion and undermine journalistic integrity. In some countries, governments restrict press freedom and harass journalists who criticise authority. Researchers in communication studies examine how media framing, agenda-setting, and ownership patterns influence public perception and political behaviour. Moreover, social media algorithms can create echo chambers, reinforcing existing beliefs and polarising society. Ultimately, a healthy democracy requires media literacy, transparent regulation, and support for independent journalism. By protecting freedom of expression while addressing harmful content, societies can maintain an informed and engaged citizenry.

**中文**

自由和独立的媒体被广泛视为民主的基石。它向公民提供信息，促进公共辩论，并让掌权者承担责任。然而，近几十年来，媒体格局发生了深刻变化，数字平台兴起，传统报纸衰落。虽然互联网使信息获取民主化，但也使错误信息、宣传和仇恨言论迅速传播。此外，媒体集中和商业压力可能限制意见多样性并破坏新闻诚信。在一些国家，政府限制新闻自由并骚扰批评当局的记者。传播学研究者考察媒体框架、议程设置和所有权模式如何影响公众认知和政治行为。此外，社交媒体算法可能制造回音室，强化现有信念并使社会两极分化。最终，健康的民主需要媒体素养、透明监管和对独立新闻的支持。通过在保护表达自由的同时处理有害内容，社会可以保持知情和参与的公民。
