## 第 26 篇：Urbanisation and Migration 城市化与移民

**英文**

Urbanisation is a global trend that involves the increasing concentration of populations in cities and towns. It is driven by economic opportunities, improved access to services, and changing patterns of employment. Migration, both internal and international, plays a significant role in this process, as individuals and families move in search of better living conditions and prospects. However, rapid urban growth can strain infrastructure, housing, and public services, leading to problems such as congestion, pollution, and informal settlements. Furthermore, migrants often face challenges related to integration, discrimination, and legal status. Governments must therefore adopt comprehensive policies that address both the causes and consequences of migration. This includes investing in affordable housing, expanding public transportation, and promoting social inclusion. Researchers in urban studies and demography examine how migration shapes cities and how cities, in turn, influence migrant experiences. Moreover, effective governance requires coordination among multiple levels of authority and meaningful participation from local communities. Ultimately, sustainable urbanisation depends on balancing economic dynamism with social equity and environmental quality. By recognising the contributions of migrants and planning inclusively, cities can become more resilient and harmonious.

**中文**

城市化是一种全球趋势，涉及人口日益集中在城市和城镇。它由经济机会、服务获取改善和就业模式变化推动。移民，包括国内和国际移民，在这一过程中发挥着重要作用，因为个人和家庭为寻求更好的生活条件和前景而迁移。然而，快速的城市增长可能使基础设施、住房和公共服务不堪重负，导致拥堵、污染和非正规住区等问题。此外，移民往往面临与融合、歧视和法律身份相关的挑战。因此，政府必须采取综合政策，解决移民的原因和后果。这包括投资经济适用房、扩大公共交通和促进社会包容。城市研究和人口学的研究者考察移民如何塑造城市，以及城市如何反过来影响移民经历。此外，有效的治理需要多个权威层级之间的协调以及当地社区的有意义参与。最终，可持续城市化取决于平衡经济活力与社会公平和环境质量。通过承认移民的贡献并进行包容性规划，城市可以变得更具韧性和和谐。

## 第 27 篇：Education Policy and Reform 教育政策与改革

**英文**

Education policy encompasses the laws, regulations, and guidelines that govern educational systems. Reform efforts aim to improve quality, equity, and relevance in response to changing social and economic demands. Key issues include curriculum design, teacher training, assessment methods, and funding allocation. Furthermore, policymakers must consider how to address disparities in access and outcomes among different groups, including those defined by socioeconomic status, ethnicity, and geographic location. International comparisons, such as those provided by PISA, influence national debates and policy decisions. However, simply borrowing practices from other countries may not work without adapting them to local contexts. Researchers emphasise the importance of evidence-based approaches and continuous evaluation. Moreover, education systems must prepare students for lifelong learning and the challenges of a rapidly evolving labour market. Technology can support personalised learning but also raises concerns about the digital divide. Ultimately, successful reform requires collaboration among governments, schools, teachers, parents, and communities. By fostering a culture of innovation and accountability, societies can enhance educational achievement and promote social mobility.

**中文**

教育政策涵盖治理教育系统的法律、法规和指导方针。改革努力旨在提高质量、公平性和相关性，以应对不断变化的社会和经济需求。关键问题包括课程设计、教师培训、评估方法和资金分配。此外，政策制定者必须考虑如何解决不同群体在获取和结果方面的差距，包括由社会经济地位、民族和地理位置定义的群体。国际比较，如PISA提供的比较，影响国家辩论和政策决策。然而，简单借用其他国家的做法可能行不通，除非将其适应本地情境。研究者强调基于证据的方法和持续评估的重要性。此外，教育系统必须为学生终身学习和快速变化的劳动力市场挑战做好准备。技术可以支持个性化学习，但也引发了关于数字鸿沟的担忧。最终，成功的改革需要政府、学校、教师、家长和社区之间的合作。通过培养创新和问责的文化，社会可以提高教育成就并促进社会流动。

## 第 28 篇：Health Communication and Public Health 健康传播与公共卫生

**英文**

Health communication is a multidisciplinary field that studies how information about health is created, disseminated, and interpreted. It plays a critical role in public health by promoting healthy behaviours, preventing disease, and responding to emergencies. Effective health campaigns rely on accurate data, clear messaging, and an understanding of the target audience. However, misinformation and distrust can undermine public health efforts, as seen during recent pandemics. Furthermore, health disparities persist among different populations, influenced by factors such as income, education, and access to care. Communication strategies must therefore be culturally sensitive and tailored to specific communities. Researchers examine how social media, traditional media, and interpersonal networks shape health beliefs and actions. Moreover, public health officials must communicate uncertainty transparently and build trust with the public. Ultimately, improving health literacy and ensuring equitable access to information are essential for reducing inequalities and enhancing well-being. By integrating insights from psychology, sociology, and communication studies, practitioners can design more effective interventions. The goal is not only to inform but also to empower individuals and communities to make informed decisions about their health.

**中文**

健康传播是一个多学科领域，研究关于健康的信息如何被创造、传播和解释。它通过促进健康行为、预防疾病和应对紧急情况，在公共卫生中发挥着关键作用。有效的健康运动依赖于准确的数据、清晰的信息以及对目标受众的理解。然而，错误信息和不信任可能破坏公共卫生努力，正如近期流行病期间所见。此外，不同人群之间持续存在健康差距，受收入、教育和护理获取等因素影响。因此，沟通策略必须具有文化敏感性，并针对特定社区量身定制。研究者考察社交媒体、传统媒体和人际网络如何塑造健康信念和行为。此外，公共卫生官员必须透明地传达不确定性，并与公众建立信任。最终，提高健康素养和确保公平获取信息对于减少不平等和增进福祉至关重要。通过整合心理学、社会学和传播学的见解，从业者可以设计更有效的干预措施。目标不仅是告知，而且是赋予个人和社区权力，使其能够就自身健康做出知情决策。

## 第 29 篇：Environmental Policy and Governance 环境政策与治理

**英文**

Environmental policy refers to the set of regulations, incentives, and international agreements designed to protect the natural environment and manage natural resources sustainably. It addresses issues such as climate change, biodiversity loss, pollution, and waste management. Effective governance in this domain requires coordination across local, national, and international levels, as environmental problems often transcend political boundaries. Furthermore, policies must balance environmental protection with economic development and social equity. Instruments include carbon taxes, emissions trading, subsidies for renewable energy, and protected areas. However, implementation can be hindered by limited enforcement capacity, corruption, and political opposition. Researchers in environmental governance study how institutions, actors, and networks interact to produce policy outcomes. Moreover, public participation and access to information are essential for accountability and legitimacy. International cooperation, as seen in the Paris Agreement, is crucial for addressing global challenges. Ultimately, sustainable environmental governance depends on strong institutions, evidence-based decision-making, and a long-term perspective. By integrating ecological, economic, and social objectives, societies can transition toward a more sustainable and resilient future. The involvement of civil society and the private sector is also vital for achieving meaningful progress.

**中文**

环境政策是指旨在保护自然环境和可持续管理自然资源的一套法规、激励措施和国际协议。它涉及气候变化、生物多样性丧失、污染和废物管理等问题。该领域的有效治理需要地方、国家和国际层面的协调，因为环境问题往往超越政治边界。此外，政策必须平衡环境保护与经济发展和社会公平。工具包括碳税、排放交易、可再生能源补贴和保护区。然而，实施可能受到执法能力有限、腐败和政治反对的阻碍。环境治理的研究者研究制度、行动者和网络如何互动以产生政策结果。此外，公众参与和信息获取对于问责制和合法性至关重要。国际合作，如《巴黎协定》中所见，对于应对全球挑战至关重要。最终，可持续环境治理取决于强有力的制度、基于证据的决策和长期视角。通过整合生态、经济和社会目标，社会可以迈向更可持续和更具韧性的未来。民间社会和私营部门的参与对于取得实质性进展也至关重要。

## 第 30 篇：Technology and Social Change 科技与社会变迁

**英文**

Technology has always been a powerful driver of social change, but the pace and scope of recent developments are unprecedented. Digital technologies, including the internet, mobile devices, and artificial intelligence, have transformed how people work, communicate, learn, and participate in society. These innovations offer significant benefits, such as increased productivity, access to information, and new forms of collaboration. However, they also generate challenges, including job displacement, privacy concerns, and the digital divide. Furthermore, the concentration of technological power in a few large corporations raises questions about economic inequality and democratic accountability. Social scientists study how technology shapes social structures, cultural norms, and individual behaviour. Moreover, the relationship between technology and society is reciprocal: societal values and policies also influence which technologies are developed and how they are used. Ultimately, managing technological change requires inclusive dialogue, ethical reflection, and proactive regulation. By fostering digital literacy and ensuring equitable access, societies can harness innovation for the common good while mitigating potential harms. The goal is not to resist change but to guide it in directions that enhance human welfare and social justice.

**中文**

技术一直是社会变革的强大驱动力，但近期发展的速度和范围是前所未有的。数字技术，包括互联网、移动设备和人工智能，改变了人们工作、沟通、学习和参与社会的方式。这些创新带来了显著的好处，如生产力提高、信息获取和新形式的协作。然而，它们也带来了挑战，包括工作岗位流失、隐私担忧和数字鸿沟。此外，技术权力集中在少数大公司手中，引发了关于经济不平等和民主问责的问题。社会科学家研究技术如何塑造社会结构、文化规范和个体行为。此外，技术与社会之间的关系是互惠的：社会价值观和政策也影响哪些技术被开发以及如何使用。最终，管理技术变革需要包容性对话、伦理反思和主动监管。通过培养数字素养和确保公平获取，社会可以利用创新服务共同利益，同时减轻潜在危害。目标不是抵制变革，而是引导其朝着增进人类福祉和社会正义的方向发展。
