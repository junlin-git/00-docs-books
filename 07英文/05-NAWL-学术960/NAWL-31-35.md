## 第 31 篇：Media and Political Communication 媒体与政治传播

**英文**

Political communication is a dynamic field that examines how information, ideas, and images are exchanged among political actors, the media, and the public. It encompasses not only news reporting but also advertising, social media campaigns, and public relations. The way political messages are framed can significantly influence public opinion, voting behaviour, and policy preferences. Furthermore, the rise of digital platforms has altered the traditional relationship between politicians and citizens, allowing for more direct interaction but also enabling the rapid spread of misinformation and polarising content. Researchers analyse how agenda-setting, priming, and framing shape perceptions of candidates and issues. Moreover, the concentration of media ownership and the influence of commercial interests raise concerns about the independence and diversity of political discourse. In authoritarian contexts, media may be used as a tool of propaganda, while in democratic societies, a pluralistic media environment is considered essential for accountability and informed citizenship. Ultimately, the quality of political communication affects the legitimacy of governance and the health of democracy. By promoting media literacy and ensuring transparent regulation, societies can foster a more informed and engaged electorate.

**中文**

政治传播是一个动态领域，研究信息、思想和形象如何在政治行动者、媒体和公众之间交换。它不仅包括新闻报道，还包括广告、社交媒体运动和公共关系。政治信息的框架方式可以显著影响公众舆论、投票行为和政策偏好。此外，数字平台的兴起改变了政治家与公民之间的传统关系，允许更直接的互动，但也使错误信息和两极分化内容迅速传播。研究者分析议程设置、启动效应和框架如何塑造对候选人和问题的认知。此外，媒体所有权的集中和商业利益的影响引发了关于政治话语独立性和多样性的担忧。在威权环境中，媒体可能被用作宣传工具，而在民主社会中，多元媒体环境被视为问责制和知情公民的必要条件。最终，政治传播的质量影响治理的合法性和民主的健康。通过促进媒体素养和确保透明监管，社会可以培养更知情和参与的选民。

## 第 32 篇：Art and Cultural Policy 艺术与文化政策

**英文**

Cultural policy refers to the strategies and actions adopted by governments and institutions to support, regulate, and promote artistic and cultural activities. It encompasses funding for museums, theatres, and festivals, as well as legal frameworks for copyright and heritage protection. Cultural policy can serve multiple objectives: preserving national identity, fostering creativity, stimulating economic development through cultural tourism, and enhancing social cohesion. However, policymakers must balance competing priorities, such as artistic freedom versus public funding accountability, and elite versus popular culture. Furthermore, globalisation has intensified debates about cultural homogenisation and the need to protect local traditions. Researchers in cultural studies examine how power relations, identity, and representation are embedded in cultural products and institutions. Moreover, access to cultural participation remains unequal, with marginalised groups often facing barriers related to cost, location, or discrimination. Ultimately, inclusive cultural policy should ensure that diverse voices are heard and that artistic expression is valued as a public good. By integrating evidence-based evaluation and community engagement, governments can design policies that enrich cultural life and promote social justice.

**中文**

文化政策是指政府和机构为支持、规范和促进艺术与文化活动而采取的战略和行动。它包括对博物馆、剧院和节日的资助，以及版权和遗产保护的法律框架。文化政策可以服务于多重目标：保护国家身份、促进创造力、通过文化旅游刺激经济发展，以及增强社会凝聚力。然而，政策制定者必须平衡相互竞争的优先事项，如艺术自由与公共资金问责制，以及精英文化与大众文化。此外，全球化加剧了关于文化同质化和保护地方传统的辩论。文化研究的研究者考察权力关系、身份和表征如何嵌入文化产品和制度中。此外，文化参与的获取仍然不平等，边缘化群体往往面临与成本、地点或歧视相关的障碍。最终，包容性文化政策应确保多元声音被听到，并将艺术表达视为公共物品。通过整合基于证据的评估和社区参与，政府可以设计丰富文化生活并促进社会正义的政策。

## 第 33 篇：Sport and Society 体育与社会

**英文**

Sport is a significant social phenomenon that extends beyond physical competition. It plays a role in shaping identities, fostering community cohesion, and promoting health and well-being. Participation in sport can contribute to personal development, discipline, and teamwork, while spectatorship generates shared experiences and collective emotions. However, sport also reflects and reproduces social inequalities, including those based on gender, race, class, and disability. Access to sporting opportunities remains uneven, and discrimination persists in many contexts. Furthermore, commercialisation and media coverage have transformed sport into a global industry, raising ethical questions about doping, corruption, and the exploitation of athletes. Researchers in sociology and sport studies examine how sport interacts with politics, economics, and culture. Moreover, mega-events such as the Olympics can bring economic benefits but also impose significant costs on host cities and displace local communities. Ultimately, sport has the potential to promote inclusion and social change, but realising this potential requires deliberate policies and ethical governance. By ensuring fair access and challenging discriminatory practices, societies can harness the power of sport for the common good.

**中文**

体育是一种重要的社会现象，超越了身体竞争。它在塑造身份、促进社区凝聚力和促进健康与福祉方面发挥作用。参与体育可以促进个人发展、纪律和团队合作，而观赛则产生共同体验和集体情感。然而，体育也反映并再生产社会不平等，包括基于性别、种族、阶级和残疾的不平等。体育机会的获取仍然不均衡，歧视在许多情境中持续存在。此外，商业化和媒体报道已将体育转变为全球产业，引发了关于兴奋剂、腐败和运动员剥削的伦理问题。社会学和体育研究的研究者考察体育如何与政治、经济和文化互动。此外，奥运会等大型赛事可以带来经济效益，但也给主办城市带来巨大成本，并使当地社区流离失所。最终，体育有潜力促进包容和社会变革，但实现这一潜力需要深思熟虑的政策和伦理治理。通过确保公平获取和挑战歧视性做法，社会可以利用体育的力量服务共同利益。

## 第 34 篇：Tourism and Culture 旅游与文化

**英文**

Tourism is a major global industry that involves the movement of people to destinations outside their usual environment for leisure, business, or other purposes. It generates significant economic benefits, including employment, foreign exchange earnings, and infrastructure development. However, tourism can also have negative impacts, such as environmental degradation, cultural commodification, and the displacement of local residents. Furthermore, the relationship between tourism and culture is complex: while tourism can help preserve heritage and promote intercultural understanding, it can also lead to the erosion of traditions and the staging of inauthentic experiences. Researchers in tourism studies examine the motivations, behaviours, and impacts of tourists, as well as the strategies used by destinations to manage visitation sustainably. Moreover, the rise of digital platforms and social media has changed how travellers choose destinations and share experiences. Ultimately, sustainable tourism requires a balanced approach that maximises benefits while minimising harm. By involving local communities in decision-making and ensuring that economic gains are equitably distributed, destinations can protect their cultural and natural assets for future generations.

**中文**

旅游业是一个主要的全球产业，涉及人们为休闲、商务或其他目的前往其通常环境之外的目的地。它产生显著的经济效益，包括就业、外汇收入和基础设施建设。然而，旅游业也可能产生负面影响，如环境退化、文化商品化和当地居民流离失所。此外，旅游与文化之间的关系是复杂的：虽然旅游可以帮助保护遗产和促进跨文化理解，但它也可能导致传统的侵蚀和不真实体验的表演化。旅游研究的研究者考察游客的动机、行为和影响，以及目的地用于可持续管理游客的策略。此外，数字平台和社交媒体的兴起改变了旅行者选择目的地和分享体验的方式。最终，可持续旅游需要一种平衡的方法，在最大化利益的同时最小化伤害。通过让当地社区参与决策并确保经济收益公平分配，目的地可以为其后代保护文化和自然资产。

## 第 35 篇：Future Studies and Trend Prediction 未来学与趋势预测

**英文**

Future studies is an interdisciplinary field that explores possible, probable, and preferable futures. It draws on methods such as scenario planning, trend analysis, and forecasting to help organisations and societies anticipate change and make better decisions. Unlike prediction, which seeks to determine what will happen, future studies acknowledges uncertainty and emphasises the importance of preparing for multiple possibilities. Key drivers of change include technological innovation, demographic shifts, environmental pressures, and geopolitical developments. Furthermore, the field examines how values, policies, and institutions shape which futures are realised. Researchers use quantitative and qualitative approaches, including modelling, Delphi surveys, and backcasting. However, forecasts are often limited by incomplete data, cognitive biases, and unforeseen events. Moreover, the complexity of global systems means that small changes can have large, unpredictable consequences. Ultimately, future studies aims not to predict the future with certainty but to expand awareness, foster strategic thinking, and inform decision-making. By integrating diverse perspectives and evidence-based methods, it can help societies navigate uncertainty and pursue sustainable and equitable pathways. The goal is to empower individuals and communities to shape their own futures rather than merely react to change.

**中文**

未来学是一个跨学科领域，探索可能的、或然的 and 更可取的未来。它借鉴情景规划、趋势分析和预测等方法，帮助组织和社会预见变化并做出更好的决策。与寻求确定将发生什么的预测不同，未来学承认不确定性，并强调为多种可能性做好准备的重要性。变化的关键驱动因素包括技术创新、人口结构变化、环境压力和地缘政治发展。此外，该领域考察价值观、政策和制度如何塑造哪些未来得以实现。研究者使用定量和定性方法，包括建模、德尔菲调查和回溯法。然而，预测往往受到不完整数据、认知偏差和不可预见事件的限制。此外，全球系统的复杂性意味着小变化可能产生巨大、不可预测的后果。最终，未来学旨在不是确定性地预测未来，而是扩展意识、培养战略思维并为决策提供信息。通过整合多元视角和基于证据的方法，它可以帮助社会应对不确定性并追求可持续和公平的路径。目标是赋予个人和社区权力，使其塑造自己的未来，而不仅仅是对变化做出反应。
