# 句子结构与成分详解

理解英语句子结构是学好语法的基石。英语句子遵循**严格的语序规则（SVO）**，与中文相比有明显差异。

---

## 第一章：句子的基本成分

英语句子由以下核心成分构成：

| 成分 | 英文 | 说明 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 主语 | Subject (S) | 动作的执行者 | **Tom** loves music. |
| 谓语 | Predicate (V) | 主语做的动作或状态 | Tom **loves** music. |
| 宾语 | Object (O) | 动作的对象 | Tom loves **music**. |
| 补语 | Complement (C) | 补充说明主语或宾语 | She is **a doctor**. |
| 定语 | Attribute | 修饰名词 | I have a **red** car. |
| 状语 | Adverbial | 修饰动词/形容词/句子 | She sings **beautifully**. |
| 同位语 | Appositive | 对名词的补充说明 | My friend, **a doctor**, helped me. |

---

### 1.1 主语（Subject）

主语是**谁/什么**在做这件事，通常是名词、代词、短语或从句。

| 主语类型 | 例句 | 说明 |
|---------|------|------|
| 名词 | **Dogs** are loyal. | 普通名词 |
| 代词 | **She** works hard. | 人称代词 |
| 动名词短语 | **Running every day** is healthy. | Ving作主语 |
| 不定式短语 | **To learn English** takes time. | to do作主语 |
| 名词从句 | **What he said** surprised me. | 从句作主语 |
| 形式主语 it | **It** is important to be honest. | it作形式主语 |

> **形式主语 it 用法**：
> `It is + adj + to do sth / that从句`
> - It is **difficult** to master English.
> - It is **clear** that he is lying.

---

### 1.2 谓语（Predicate）

谓语是句子的**核心动词**，包含时态、语态、语气信息。

```
谓语 = 助动词（可选）+ 主动词
例：She  has been studying  all night.
        助动词         主动词
```

**谓语类型**：

| 类型 | 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| 简单谓语 | He **runs**. | 单一动词 |
| 复合谓语 | She **is reading**. | 助动词+主动词 |
| 双宾语谓语 | He **gave her a book**. | 给予类动词 |
| 复杂宾语谓语 | I **found him sleeping**. | 宾语+宾语补语 |

---

### 1.3 宾语（Object）

宾语是动作作用的**对象**，分直接宾语和间接宾语。

| 宾语类型 | 结构 | 例句 |
|---------|------|------|
| 直接宾语 | V + O | She reads **books**. |
| 间接宾语 | V + 人 + 物 | He gave **me** a letter. |
| 宾语从句 | V + that/what/how... | I know **that he is right**. |
| 不定式宾语 | V + to do | She wants **to travel**. |
| 动名词宾语 | V + Ving | I enjoy **reading**. |

**接不定式 vs 接动名词的动词区别**：

| 只接 to do | 只接 Ving | 两者均可（意义不同） |
|------------|-----------|---------------------|
| want, wish, decide, plan, hope, refuse, agree, manage | enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, suggest, consider, practice, admit | remember, forget, stop, try, regret |

> **记忆对比**：
> - `I **stopped to smoke**.` （停下来去抽烟）→ 停止原来的事，开始抽烟
> - `I **stopped smoking**.` （戒烟了）→ 停止抽烟这件事

---

### 1.4 补语（Complement）

补语分**主语补语**和**宾语补语**。

**主语补语**（表语）：接在系动词后面，说明主语的状态。

| 系动词类型 | 动词 | 例句 |
|-----------|------|------|
| be动词 | am, is, are, was, were | She **is** a nurse. |
| 感官动词 | look, sound, smell, taste, feel | The soup **tastes** delicious. |
| 变化动词 | become, get, turn, grow, go | She **became** a doctor. |
| 保持动词 | stay, remain, keep | The door **remained** open. |
| 表象动词 | seem, appear, prove | He **seems** tired. |

**宾语补语**：说明宾语的状态或动作结果。

| 例句 | 宾补类型 |
|------|---------|
| She found him **asleep**. | 形容词作宾补 |
| They elected him **president**. | 名词作宾补 |
| I saw her **dancing**. | 现在分词作宾补（正在进行） |
| I had my phone **repaired**. | 过去分词作宾补（被动完成） |
| Let me **help** you. | 不定式（省略to）作宾补 |
| I want you **to succeed**. | 不定式作宾补 |

---

### 1.5 定语（Attribute）

定语修饰名词，分**前置定语**和**后置定语**。

| 类型 | 位置 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 形容词 | 前置 | a **beautiful** flower |
| 数词 | 前置 | **three** students |
| 限定词 | 前置 | **my** book, **the** cat |
| 介词短语 | 后置 | the girl **in red** |
| 分词短语 | 后置 | the man **standing there** |
| 不定式短语 | 后置 | a chance **to succeed** |
| 定语从句 | 后置 | the book **that I bought** |
| 同位语从句 | 后置 | the news **that he resigned** |

---

## 第二章：五大基本句型

所有英语简单句都可以归入以下五种基本句型：

### 句型1：S + V（主谓）

主语 + 不及物动词

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Birds **fly**. | 鸟儿飞翔。 |
| He **smiled** warmly. | 他温暖地微笑。 |
| The sun **rises**. | 太阳升起。 |

---

### 句型2：S + V + C（主系表）

主语 + 系动词 + 主语补语

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| She **is** a teacher. | 她是一名老师。 |
| The sky **turned** dark. | 天空变暗了。 |
| He **looks** tired. | 他看起来很累。 |

---

### 句型3：S + V + O（主谓宾）

主语 + 及物动词 + 宾语

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| I **love** coffee. | 我爱咖啡。 |
| She **reads** books every day. | 她每天读书。 |
| They **built** a house. | 他们建了一栋房子。 |

---

### 句型4：S + V + O + O（主谓双宾）

主语 + 及物动词 + 间接宾语(人) + 直接宾语(物)

| 例句 | 中文 | 改写（for/to） |
|------|------|---------------|
| He **gave her** a gift. | 他给她一份礼物。 | He gave a gift **to her**. |
| She **taught us** English. | 她教我们英语。 | She taught English **to us**. |
| Can you **buy me** a coffee? | 你能给我买杯咖啡吗？ | Can you buy a coffee **for me**? |

> 常见双宾动词：give, send, show, tell, teach, buy, make, get, bring, lend, write

---

### 句型5：S + V + O + C（主谓宾补）

主语 + 动词 + 宾语 + 宾语补语

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| She **made** him **happy**. | 她让他很开心。 |
| They **named** the baby **Emma**. | 他们给孩子取名Emma。 |
| I **found** the test **difficult**. | 我发现这次考试很难。 |

---

## 第三章：从句详解

从句是包含**主语+谓语**的句子结构，充当另一个句子的某一成分。

### 3.1 定语从句（Relative Clauses）

定语从句修饰名词，相当于中文"...的"。

**关系词选用规则**：

| 先行词 | 引导词 | 说明 |
|--------|--------|------|
| 人（主语/宾语） | who | `The man **who** helped me is kind.` |
| 物（主语/宾语） | which | `The car **which** I bought is red.` |
| 人或物（宾语，可省略） | that | `The book **(that)** I read was great.` |
| 所有格 | whose | `A girl **whose** hair is long.` |
| 时间 | when | `The day **when** I met her.` |
| 地点 | where | `The city **where** I was born.` |
| 原因 | why | `The reason **why** I left.` |

**限制性 vs. 非限制性定语从句**：

| 类型 | 例句 | 特点 |
|------|------|------|
| 限制性 | The man **who stole the bag** was caught. | 不加逗号；缩小范围 |
| 非限制性 | My brother, **who lives in Beijing**, is a doctor. | 加逗号；补充说明；不能用that |

---

### 3.2 名词从句（Noun Clauses）

名词从句充当主语、宾语、表语或同位语。

**常见引导词**：
- `that`（无实际意义）：`I know **that** he is right.`
- `what/which/who`（疑问词引导）：`Tell me **what you want**.`
- `whether/if`（是否）：`I wonder **whether** it will rain.`
- `how/when/where/why`（特殊疑问词）：`I don't know **how** to do it.`

**四种位置**：

| 位置 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 主语从句 | **That he lied** surprised everyone. / **It** surprised everyone **that he lied**. |
| 宾语从句 | I know **that she is honest**. |
| 表语从句 | The fact is **that we failed**. |
| 同位语从句 | The news **that he resigned** was shocking. |

> **宾语从句注意点**：
> - 语序一律用**陈述句语序**（不倒装）
> - ❌ `I don't know where **is** he.` → ✅ `I don't know where **he is**.`

---

### 3.3 状语从句（Adverbial Clauses）

状语从句修饰主句动词，表示时间、原因、条件等关系。

| 类型 | 常用引导词 | 例句 |
|------|-----------|------|
| 时间 | when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as | I'll call you **when I arrive**. |
| 原因 | because, since, as, for | She stayed home **because she was sick**. |
| 条件 | if, unless, provided, as long as | **If it rains**, I'll stay home. |
| 让步 | although, though, even though, even if | **Although it was late**, he kept working. |
| 目的 | so that, in order that | He studied hard **so that he could pass**. |
| 结果 | so...that, such...that | It was **so cold that** the river froze. |
| 比较 | as...as, than | She runs faster **than I do**. |
| 方式 | as if, as though | He talks **as if he knows** everything. |
| 地点 | where, wherever | Sit **where you like**. |

## 第四章：句子类型

### 4.1 按结构分类

| 类型 | 定义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 简单句 | 一个主谓结构 | She **sings**. |
| 并列句 | 两个简单句+并列连词 | She **sings** and he **plays** guitar. |
| 复合句 | 主句+从句 | She sings **because she loves music**. |
| 并列复合句 | 两个及以上的主句+从句 | **When she sings**, everyone listens and the room fills with joy. |

**并列连词（FANBOYS）**：

| 缩写 | 连词 | 意义 |
|------|------|------|
| F | for | 因为 |
| A | and | 并且 |
| N | nor | 也不 |
| B | but | 但是 |
| O | or | 或者 |
| Y | yet | 然而 |
| S | so | 因此 |

### 4.2 按功能分类

| 类型 | 功能 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 陈述句 | 陈述事实 | She is a student. |
| 疑问句 | 提问 | Is she a student? |
| 祈使句 | 命令/请求 | Please sit down. |
| 感叹句 | 表达强烈感情 | What a wonderful day! / How beautiful! |

**感叹句结构**：
- `What + a/an + 形容词 + 可数名词单数 + 主语 + 谓语！`
  - What a beautiful sunset it is!
- `What + 形容词 + 复数名词/不可数名词 + 主语 + 谓语！`
  - What lovely flowers they are!
- `How + 形容词/副词 + 主语 + 谓语！`
  - How clever she is! / How fast he runs!

## 第五章：特殊句式

### 5.1 强调句（Cleft Sentences）

**结构**：`It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分`

| 原句 | 强调主语 | 强调宾语 | 强调状语 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Tom met Lisa in Paris last year. | **It was Tom** who met Lisa in Paris. | **It was Lisa** that Tom met in Paris. | **It was in Paris** that Tom met Lisa. |

> 判断方法：去掉 `It is/was...that`，剩余部分能组成完整句子即为强调句。

---

### 5.2 倒装句（Inversion）

**完全倒装**（助动词+主语）：

| 触发条件 | 例句 |
|---------|------|
| Here/There + be | **Here comes** the bus. |
| 地点/方向状语前置 | **In the classroom sat** a teacher. |

**部分倒装**（助动词提前）：

| 触发词 | 例句 |
|--------|------|
| Never, seldom, rarely | **Never have I** seen such a mess. |
| Not only...but also | **Not only did he lie**, but he also stole. |
| Hardly...when | **Hardly had I** sat down **when** the phone rang. |
| Only + 状语前置 | **Only then did I** understand. |
| So/Such...that | **So tired was I** that I fell asleep immediately. |
| If省略，had/were/should前置 | **Were I** you, I would leave. = If I were you... |

---

### 5.3 省略句（Ellipsis）

为避免重复而省略已知成分：

| 例句 | 省略内容 |
|------|---------|
| I can swim, and she can, too. | `swim` 省略 |
| He arrived before I did. | `arrived` 省略 |
| A: Are you coming? B: Yes, I am. | `coming` 省略 |
| If possible, call me. | `it is` 省略 |
