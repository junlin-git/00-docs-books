# 英语长难句分析模板（TPM + 传统语法 ASCII树）
## 一、原句与翻译
**原句：**
Modern pedagogy champions innovative thinking, interdisciplinary exploration, and self-directed inquiry, urging students to engage deeply with subject matter rather than passively memorize facts.

**中文翻译：**
现代教学法倡导创新思维、跨学科探索与自主探究，敦促学生深入钻研学科内容，而非被动记忆知识点。

## 二、TPM 结构分析（Topic / Property / Modifier）
> 逻辑：T话题 → P核心断言 → M附加修饰信息
```
Sentence
├─ T: Modern pedagogy        # Topic 话题：句子讨论的核心对象
├─ P: champions innovative thinking, interdisciplinary exploration, and self-directed inquiry

      # Property 属性：描述话题的核心陈述

└─ M: urging students to engage deeply with subject matter rather than passively memorize facts

# Modifier 修饰语：现在分词伴随状语，修饰整句

```

## 三、传统语法分析
```

Sentence
├─ S: Modern pedagogy                  # Subject 主语，动作发出者
├─ V: champions                        # Verb 谓语，句子核心动词
├─ O: innovative thinking, interdisciplinary exploration, and self-directed inquiry

    # Object 宾语，三项并列名词短语

└─ 附加成分: urging students to engage deeply with subject matter rather than passively memorize facts

# 备注：现在分词伴随状语；逻辑主语 = Modern pedagogy；urge sb to do A rather than (to) do B，第二个 to 省略

```
