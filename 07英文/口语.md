# 雅思口语 Part B · 地道短语与习语深度工具书（完整版）

> 适用人群：已掌握 AWL 学术骨架，急需补足“日常口语灵活度”的考生。
> 核心理念：口语高分 ≠ 用大词，而是用 **“母语者习惯的小词搭配 + 恰到好处的习语点缀”**。
> 内容结构：10 个口语功能模块 × 3-4 个黄金表达 × 固定结构/介词 × 完整高分口语例句。


## 模块 1：表达个人观点（告别 I think / In my opinion）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **As far as I'm concerned, ...**
   - **结构**：后接完整句子
   - **使用场景**：引出个人核心立场，比 I think 更显冷静客观

2. **The way I see it, ...**
   - **结构**：后接完整句子
   - **使用场景**：强调这是“从我的视角看”，适合 Part 3 辩证讨论

3. **I'd argue that ...**
   - **结构**：`I'd argue that + 完整句子`
   - **使用场景**：用于提出有挑战性的观点，比 I think 更有力度

4. **I'm inclined to think that ...**
   - **结构**：`I'm inclined to think + that 从句`
   - **使用场景**：表示“我倾向于认为”，语气委婉且学术，口语 7+ 必备

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 3 实战）

> *Well, **as far as I'm concerned**, city life is far more stimulating than rural life, although I do see the appeal of a quieter pace.*

> *If you ask me, **the way I see it**, technology has actually brought people closer together, rather than isolating them.*


## 模块 2：表达喜好与兴趣（告别 I like / I love）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **I'm really into + 名词 / -ing**
   - **结构**：`I'm really into + N / V-ing`
   - **使用场景**：表示“我超迷…”，极高频口语表达

2. **I'm keen on + 名词 / -ing**
   - **结构**：`I'm keen on + N / V-ing`
   - **使用场景**：英式口语高频，表示“很喜欢”，比 like 地道 10 倍

3. **I'm quite fond of + 名词 / -ing**
   - **结构**：`I'm fond of + N / V-ing`
   - **使用场景**：语气温和，表示“我很喜欢”，适合 Part 1 聊爱好

4. **... really appeals to me.**
   - **结构**：`某物 + really appeals to me`
   - **使用场景**：主语是物，表示“某物很吸引我”，完美替换 I like it

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 1 实战）

> *I'm really into photography, especially street photography, because I love capturing candid moments.*

> *What really appeals to me about my hometown is the sense of community; everyone knows each other.*


## 模块 3：描述人物性格（告别 He is a good person）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **... is a people person.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + is a people person`
   - **使用场景**：形容人“善于社交、喜欢与人打交道”

2. **... is down-to-earth.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + is down-to-earth`
   - **使用场景**：形容人“务实的、接地气的、不浮夸的”，比 practical 口语化

3. **... is incredibly easy-going.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + is easy-going`
   - **使用场景**：形容人“随和、好相处”，极高频口语形容词

4. **... has a great sense of humour.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + has a great sense of humour`
   - **使用场景**：形容人“很有幽默感”，口语必备

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 2 描述人物实战）

> *My grandfather is incredibly down-to-earth; he never complains and always sees the bright side of things, which makes him a real people person in our community.*

> *What I admire most about my best friend is that she has a great sense of humour and is easy-going, so it's never boring when she's around.*


## 模块 4：描述地点与城市（告别 It's a nice place）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **... is a stone's throw away from ...**
   - **结构**：`A is a stone's throw away from B`
   - **使用场景**：形容两个地方“距离非常近”，比 near 生动

2. **... is bustling with energy / activity.**
   - **结构**：`地点 + is bustling with + 名词`
   - **使用场景**：形容“充满活力、熙熙攘攘”，比 busy 高级

3. **... is a melting pot of ...**
   - **结构**：`地点 + is a melting pot of + 文化/人群`
   - **使用场景**：形容“文化大熔炉”，描述多元城市必背

4. **... is surrounded by ...**
   - **结构**：`地点 + is surrounded by + 自然景观`
   - **使用场景**：描述地理位置，被动语态在口语中极常用

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 1 家乡实战）

> *My hometown is a stone's throw away from the coast, so we often go to the beach at weekends. It's also a melting pot of different cultures, which makes it incredibly vibrant.*

> *The city centre is always bustling with energy, especially during festivals, but the suburbs are much quieter and surrounded by greenery.*


## 模块 5：描述情绪与感受（告别 I am happy / sad）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **... is over the moon.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + is over the moon`
   - **使用场景**：形容“欣喜若狂”，比 very happy 地道 10 倍

2. **... is down in the dumps.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + is down in the dumps`
   - **使用场景**：形容“心情低落、沮丧”，口语化极强

3. **... is stressed out.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + is stressed out`
   - **使用场景**：形容“压力巨大”，比 stressed 更夸张、更口语

4. **... feels on top of the world.**
   - **结构**：`主语 + feels on top of the world`
   - **使用场景**：形容“感觉棒极了、处于世界之巅”

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 1 情绪实战）

> *When I got my exam results, I was over the moon because I'd worked so hard for months.*

> *These days, many young people feel stressed out due to the intense competition in the job market, which is a real concern.*


## 模块 6：让步与对比（口语辩证神器）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **That being said, ...**
   - **结构**：`That being said, + 完整句子`
   - **使用场景**：先肯定一方，再抛出相反观点，比 However 自然

2. **Having said that, ...**
   - **结构**：`Having said that, + 完整句子`
   - **使用场景**：与上面同义，极高频口语转折词

3. **It's a double-edged sword.**
   - **结构**：`某事物 + is a double-edged sword`
   - **使用场景**：形容“有利有弊、双刃剑”，Part 3 辩证时必背

4. **I'm sitting on the fence.**
   - **结构**：`I'm sitting on the fence + about...`
   - **使用场景**：形容“我还在观望、没有明确立场”

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 3 辩证实战）

> *Online education offers great flexibility; **that being said**, it lacks the face-to-face interaction that many students need.*

> *Social media is a double-edged sword; it connects people globally, but it also spreads misinformation and creates echo chambers.*


## 模块 7：给出建议与推荐（告别 You should）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **It's worth giving it a shot.**
   - **结构**：`It's worth + V-ing`
   - **使用场景**：表示“值得一试”，极口语化建议

2. **You'd better + 动词原形 ...**
   - **结构**：`You'd better + do`
   - **使用场景**：强烈建议（注意：否定形式为 You'd better not）

3. **I'd highly recommend + V-ing / 名词**
   - **结构**：`I'd highly recommend + V-ing / N`
   - **使用场景**：强烈推荐，比 suggest 更主动

4. **Why not + 动词原形 ...?**
   - **结构**：`Why not + do?`
   - **使用场景**：反问式建议，非常自然的口语语气

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 2 推荐实战）

> *If you're visiting my city, I'd highly recommend trying the local street food, especially the grilled seafood. It's worth giving it a shot!*

> *You'd better book tickets in advance, because the place gets packed during peak season.*


## 模块 8：解释原因与因果关系（告别 Because）


### 🔥 黄金表达（4 个）

1. **The main reason is that ...**
   - **结构**：`The main reason is that + 完整句子`
   - **使用场景**：正式引出主要原因，比 Because 更清楚

2. **... which is why ...**
   - **结构**：`前面事实, which is why + 结果`
   - **使用场景**：连接因果，极地道口语结构

3. **This is largely due to ...**
   - **结构**：`This is largely due to + 名词`
   - **使用场景**：归因于某事，比 because of 更高级

4. **That's precisely why ...**
   - **结构**：`That's precisely why + 完整句子`
   - **使用场景**：表示“这正是为什么…”，表强调因果

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 3 解释原因实战）

> *Many young people prefer city life. **The main reason is that** cities offer better job opportunities and entertainment.*

> *Traffic congestion has worsened in recent years, **which is why** the government is investing heavily in public transport.*


## 模块 9：万能口语动词短语（替换 AWL 书面词）


### 🔥 黄金表达（5 个）

| AWL 书面词 | 口语动词短语 | 固定结构 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| propose / suggest | **come up with** | `come up with + 想法/方案` |
| reduce / decrease | **cut down on** | `cut down on + 消耗品` |
| tolerate / endure | **put up with** | `put up with + 难事/人` |
| finally reach | **end up + V-ing** | `end up + 结果状态` |
| find / discover | **figure out** | `figure out + 问题/答案` |

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（实战替换）

> *Instead of using 'suggest', I can say: The government should **come up with** a better plan to tackle pollution.*

> *If I don't manage my time well, I'll **end up** feeling exhausted and stressed out before the exam.*

> *I simply can't **put up with** the noise in the city centre; that's why I prefer suburban areas.*


## 模块 10：高分固定习语（直接背诵，口语 7+ 点睛）


### 🔥 黄金表达（5 个）

1. **It's not rocket science.**
   - **含义**：这又不是造火箭（指“这事没那么复杂”）
   - **使用场景**：解释简单事情时

2. **It takes two to tango.**
   - **含义**：一个巴掌拍不响（指双方都有责任）
   - **使用场景**：讨论冲突或合作时

3. **Actions speak louder than words.**
   - **含义**：行动胜于空谈
   - **使用场景**：强调实际行动的重要性

4. **Kill two birds with one stone.**
   - **含义**：一举两得
   - **使用场景**：描述高效做事的优点时

5. **It's a piece of cake.**
   - **含义**：小菜一碟
   - **使用场景**：描述某事非常容易做时（Part 1 极常用）

### 🗣️ 完整口语例句（Part 3 点睛）

> *Some people think reducing pollution is complicated, but actually, **it's not rocket science**; simply using public transport can make a huge difference.*

> *When it comes to environmental protection, **actions speak louder than words**; governments need to implement real policies, not just make promises.*

> *By studying online, I can **kill two birds with one stone** – I save commuting time and also learn at my own pace.*
