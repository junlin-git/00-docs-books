# 🐾 Lecture 4–5 Summary (P004 + P005) — Full English Terms

## P004 — Type of Animals 🦴🔖
> How scientists group animals

### 🧬 Core Split: Vertebrate vs Invertebrate
- 🦴 **Vertebrates** (have a backbone)
  - 🐟 **Fish** — *salmon, shark, goldfish*
  - 🐸 **Amphibians** — *frog, toad, salamander*
  - 🐍 **Reptiles** — *snake, lizard, turtle, crocodile*
  - 🐦 **Birds** — *eagle, sparrow, penguin, owl*
  - 🐭 **Mammals** — *dog, cat, whale, human, kangaroo*
- 🪱 **Invertebrates** (no backbone)
  - 🐜 **Insects** — *ant, bee, butterfly* (6 legs, 3 body parts)
  - 🕷️ **Arachnids** — *spider, scorpion* (8 legs, 2 body parts)
  - 🪱 **Worms** — *earthworm, tapeworm*
  - 🦀 **Crustaceans** — *crab, lobster, shrimp*
  - 🪼 **Cnidarians** — *jellyfish, coral*

### ⚠️ Common Traps (from the video)
- Spider ≠ Insect → spider is an **arachnid**
- Whale ≠ Fish → whale is a **mammal** (breathes air, gives live birth, feeds milk)
- Frog is an **amphibian**, not a fish or reptile

### ✅ Quick Quiz
- Two groups by backbone? → **Vertebrates / Invertebrates**
- Which animal has feathers? → **Bird**
- Is a frog a fish? → **No, it's an amphibian.**

## P005 — Body Parts of Animals 🦵👁️
> Structure explains function — body parts match the animal's class

### 🫁 Respiratory Organs (Breathe)
- 🐟 **Gills** → fish, tadpoles (breathe underwater)
- 🐶 **Lungs** → mammals, birds, reptiles, adult amphibians (breathe air)

### 🏃 Movement Organs (Move)
- 🐟 **Fins & tail** → swim
- 🐦 **Wings** → fly (some birds like penguin use wings to swim)
- 🐎 **Legs / hooves** → walk, run, jump
- 🐍 **No legs** → slither (snake), crawl (worm)

### 🍽️ Feeding & Sensing Organs (Eat & Sense)
- 🐦 **Beak** → birds (no teeth, peck food)
- 👃 **Eyes / Ears / Nose** → sense danger, find food
- 🐱 **Whiskers** → cats (test narrow spaces)
- 🪲 **Antennae** → insects, crustaceans (detect environment)

### 🧥 Body Coverings
- 🐶 **Fur / Hair** → mammals (warmth)
- 🐦 **Feathers** → birds (light, warm, fly)
- 🐟 **Scales** → fish, reptiles (protection)
- 🐛 **Exoskeleton / Shell** → insects, crabs, snails (hard outer cover)

### 🧠 Structure–Function Logic (key takeaway)
> Body parts tell you the animal's class and lifestyle.
- Gills → lives in water
- Wings → can fly (or swim)
- Fur → warm-blooded, land mammal
- Antennae → invertebrate, senses environment

### ✅ Quick Quiz
- What helps a fish breathe underwater? → **Gills**
- What covers a bird's body? → **Feathers**
- Name a part that helps animals sense danger → **Eyes / Ears / Nose**
