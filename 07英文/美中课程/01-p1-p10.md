## P001 — Parts of Plants 🌱

> Every plant has basic parts — each part does a special job

### 🌿 Main Parts of a Plant
- 🌾 **Roots** → hold the plant in soil, take in water
- 🌱 **Stem** → carries water and food to leaves
- 🍃 **Leaves** → make food for the plant (with sunlight)
- 🌸 **Flower** → makes seeds for new plants
- 🍎 **Fruit** → protects seeds
- 🫘 **Seeds** → grow into new plants

### 🐾 Animals (mentioned / implied)
| Animal | Connection to Plant Parts |
|---|---|
| 🐝 **Bee** | visits flowers → pollination |
| 🦋 **Butterfly** | drinks nectar from flowers |
| 🐦 **Bird** | eats fruit and spreads seeds |
| 🐿️ **Squirrel** | buries seeds, helps new plants grow |
| 🐛 **Caterpillar** | eats leaves |
| 🐄 **Cow** | eats grass (leaves) |
| 🐝 **Honeybee** | lives in hives near gardens |
| 🦇 **Bat** | pollinates night-blooming flowers |

### 👤 People
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| 👧 **Student** | observes plant parts in science class |
| 👨‍🌾 **Farmer** | grows crops, knows each part's job |
| 👩‍🔬 **Scientist** | studies how leaves make food |
| 👩‍🏫 **Teacher** | explains roots, stem, leaves |
| 👨‍🌾 **Gardener** | plants flowers and vegetables |

### 🏞️ Places
| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| 🏫 **Classroom** | plant diagram on the wall |
| 🌻 **Garden** | flowers, fruits, vegetables growing |

| 🌳 **Forest** | tall trees with all plant parts |
| 🌿 **Greenhouse** | plants grown under glass |
| 🏡 **Backyard** | small plants, flowers |
| 🌲 **Orchard** | fruit trees |

### 🏔️ Scenery
| Scenery | Description |
|---|---|
| 🌅 **Sunny field** | sunlight hits leaves |
| 🌧️ **Rainy garden** | water soaks into roots |
| 🍂 **Autumn tree** | leaves change color, fruit falls |
| 🌄 **Mountain slope** | trees with deep roots |
| 🌾 **Wheat field** | stems wave in wind |
| 🌸 **Flower bed** | colorful flowers |

### 🔄 How Parts Work Together
> Roots → Stem → Leaves → Flower → Fruit → Seeds
- Roots soak up water → Stem moves it up → Leaves use sunlight to make food → Flower makes seeds → Fruit wraps seeds → Seeds grow into new plants

### ✅ Quick Quiz

- What protects the seeds? → **Fruit**

## P002 — Life Cycle of a Plant 🔄

> Plants grow in stages — then start the cycle again

### 📋 Stages (in order)
1. 🫘 **Seed** — small, waits for water and warmth
2. 🌱 **Sprout** — root and tiny stem come out
3. 🪴 **Seedling** — small plant with a few leaves
4. 🌿 **Adult plant** — big, strong, makes flowers
5. 🌸 **Flower** — makes seeds
6. 🔁 **New seeds** — fall, grow into new plants

### ⏱️ Different Speeds
- 🫘 **Bean plant** → fast (a few weeks)
- 🌳 **Oak tree** → slow (many years)

### 🐾 Animals
| Animal | Connection |
|---|---|
| 🐦 **Bird** | eats seeds, drops them elsewhere |
| 🐿️ **Squirrel** | buries acorns (oak seeds) |

| 🐦 **Crow** | eats seeds from the ground |

### 🏞️ Places

### 🏔️ Scenery

### ✅ Quick Quiz
- What comes after seedling? → **Adult plant**

- Is the plant life cycle a circle? → **Yes**

## P003 — What Plants Need to Grow ☀️💧

> Plants need 4 things to stay alive and grow

### 🧩 The 4 Needs
- ☀️ **Sunlight** → gives energy to make food
- 💧 **Water** → keeps plant healthy, moves nutrients
- 🌬️ **Air** → carbon dioxide for making food
- 🟤 **Soil** → holds roots, gives nutrients

### ❌ What Happens Without Them
- No sunlight → plant turns yellow, can't make food
- No water → plant wilts and dries
- Bad soil → plant grows weak

### 🐾 Animals
| Animal | Connection |
|---|---|
| 🐝 **Bee** | needs flowers that grow with sunlight |
| 🐛 **Caterpillar** | eats leaves from healthy plants |
| 🐄 **Cow** | eats grass that needs all 4 things |
| 🐞 **Ladybug** | lives on plants that need sun/water |

| 👨‍🌾 **Gardener** | adds fertilizer to soil |
| 👩‍🏫 **Teacher** | explains photosynthesis simply |

### 🏞️ Places
| Place | Description |
|---|---|

| 🌳 **Forest** | natural sunlight, rich soil |

### 🏔️ Scenery

|---|---|
| ☀️ **Sunny hill** | plants grow tall |
| 🌧️ **Rainy day** | plants drink water |

### ✅ Quick Quiz

## P005 — Body Parts of Animals 🦵👁️

> Structure explains function — body parts match the animal's class

### 🫁 Respiratory Organs (Breathe)
- 🐟 **Gills** → fish, tadpoles (breathe underwater)
- 🐶 **Lungs** → mammals, birds, reptiles, adult amphibians (breathe air)

### 🏃 Movement Organs (Move)
- 🐟 **Fins & tail** → swim
- 🐦 **Wings** → fly (some birds like penguin use wings to swim)
- 🐎 **Legs / hooves** → walk, run, jump
- 🐍 **No legs** → slither (snake), crawl (worm)

### 🍽️ Feeding & Sensing Organs (Eat & Sense)
- 🐦 **Beak** → birds (no teeth, peck food)
- 👃 **Eyes / Ears / Nose** → sense danger, find food
- 🐱 **Whiskers** → cats (test narrow spaces)
- 🪲 **Antennae** → insects, crustaceans (detect environment)

### 🧥 Body Coverings
- 🐶 **Fur / Hair** → mammals (warmth)
- 🐦 **Feathers** → birds (light, warm, fly)
- 🐟 **Scales** → fish, reptiles (protection)
- 🐛 **Exoskeleton / Shell** → insects, crabs, snails (hard outer cover)

## P010 — Rocks and Soil 🪨

> Rocks break down into soil — soil helps plants grow

### 🪨 Types of Rock (simple)
| Type | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🌋 **Igneous** | from cooled lava/magma | granite |
| 🟤 **Sedimentary** | layers pressed together | sandstone |
| 💎 **Metamorphic** | changed by heat/pressure | marble |

### 🟤 Soil Layers
| Layer | Description |
|---|---|
| 🟤 **Topsoil** | dark, rich, has rotting plants/animals (plants grow best here) |
| 🟫 **Subsoil** | lighter, fewer nutrients |
| ⛰️ **Bedrock** | solid rock underneath |

### 🔄 Soil Formation
> Rock breaks → mixes with dead plants/animals → becomes soil

### 🐾 Animals
