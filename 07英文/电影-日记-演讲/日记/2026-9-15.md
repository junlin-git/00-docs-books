I’d like to share a particularly serene morning that took place recently at my home. It was one of those rare occasions where I woke up feeling completely rejuvenated—both mentally and physically—which is quite unusual given my hectic schedule.

The night before had been rather tempestuous, with incessant rain lashing against the windows. However, inside our bedroom, the ambience was perfectly calibrated. The gentle hum of the air conditioner acted as a sort of sonic blanket, allowing my wife and me to succumb to a profoundly deep slumber. We literally slept like logs until the next morning.
When the alarm clock finally blared, it took us a moment to stir from our lethargic repose. Before getting up, we shared a tender moment of intimacy—a lingering kiss and a tight-knit embrace. It was a small ritual that helped us ward off the stress of the impending workday.

As is often the case, my wife roused herself earlier than I did. I remained reclined, half-asleep, observing her through the dim morning light as she orchestrated her ablutions. There’s something incredibly soothing about watching a familiar routine unfold: the sound of the toothbrush, the rustle of fabric as she adorned herself in professional attire, and finally, the soft whir of her electric bicycle as she mounted it and pedaled toward the corporate district.

What made this morning so relaxing was the contrast. Outside, the weather was grim, but inside, there was a palpable sense of warmth and security. That brief period of stillness, coupled with the affection we shared, set a positive tone for the rest of the day. It reminded me that relaxation isn’t just about sleeping in; it’s about feeling safe and connected.
