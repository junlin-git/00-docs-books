# 雅思写作 Part A · 十大话题高级搭配深度工具书（完整版）

> 适用人群：已掌握 AWL 570 核心词族，急需将“学术骨架”填充为“高分血肉”的考生。
> 内容结构：每个话题包含 **6 个黄金动词短语（含介词结构）** + **话题专用名词** + **学术逻辑连词** + **2 个完整高分例句**。


## Topic 1：教育（Education）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **acquire** (v.) + practical skills / critical thinking / academic knowledge
   - **介词结构**：直接加宾语，如 `acquire practical skills`

2. **bridge** (v.) + the gap between A and B
   - **固定结构**：`bridge the gap between theory and practice`

3. **impart** (v.) + knowledge / values / wisdom
   - **介词结构**：`impart A to B`（把 A 传授给 B）

4. **cater to** (v.) + individual needs / diverse learning styles
   - **注意**：学术写作用 `cater to`，而不用 `cater for`

5. **instil** (v.) + a sense of + discipline / responsibility / curiosity
   - **介词结构**：`instil A in B`（把 A 灌输给 B）

6. **equip** (v.) + students with + skills / tools / competences
   - **固定结构**：`equip sb with sth`

### 📚 话题专用名词

- curriculum（课程体系） / pedagogy（教学法）
- vocational training（职业培训）
- cognitive development（认知发展）
- rote memorisation（死记硬背）
- compulsory education（义务教育）
- tertiary education（高等教育）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- Thereby fostering...（从而培养...）
- This is conducive to...（这有利于...）
- Rather than merely...（而不仅仅是...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *Instead of relying solely on rote memorisation, modern pedagogy should **equip** students with practical problem-solving skills, **thereby bridging** the gap between academic qualifications and real-world employability.*

> *While compulsory education lays a foundation, tertiary institutions must **cater to** diverse learning styles and **instil** a sense of intellectual curiosity, which is conducive to lifelong learning.*


## Topic 2：环境（Environment）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **tackle / combat** (v.) + climate change / global warming / pollution
   - **介词结构**：直接加宾语

2. **mitigate** (v.) + the adverse effects / environmental degradation / carbon emissions
   - **注意**：常接“负面影响”，不要直接接“问题本身”

3. **deplete** (v.) + natural resources / fossil fuels / the ozone layer
   - **介词结构**：及物动词，被动常用 `be depleted`

4. **adopt** (v.) + sustainable practices / renewable energy sources
   - **注意**：`adopt` 比 `use` 更显主动选择

5. **curb** (v.) + emissions / waste production / deforestation
   - **含义**：意为“遏制”，比 `reduce` 更强烈

6. **rely on** (v.) + non-renewable resources / fossil fuels
   - **介词结构**：固定搭配，接 `on`（不可用 `in`）

### 📚 话题专用名词

- biodiversity（生物多样性）
- ecological footprint（生态足迹）
- renewable energy（可再生能源）
- exhaustible resources（不可再生资源）
- legislative measures（立法措施）
- public awareness（公众意识）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- Unless immediate action is taken...（除非立即采取行动...）
- This, in turn, leads to...（这反过来又导致...）
- Given the finite nature of...（鉴于...的有限性）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *Given the finite nature of fossil fuels, governments must **adopt** renewable energy sources and **curb** industrial emissions, **otherwise** future generations will face severe ecological crises.*

> *To **mitigate** the adverse effects of urbanisation, city planners should **tackle** air pollution by promoting public transport, **thereby** reducing the overall ecological footprint of metropolitan areas.*


## Topic 3：科技（Technology）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **revolutionise** (v.) + the way we live / communication / industries
   - **含义**：极强动词，表示“彻底变革”

2. **pose** (v.) + a threat / a risk / a challenge + to...
   - **介词结构**：`pose A to B`（对 B 构成 A）

3. **facilitate** (v.) + remote communication / data sharing / daily tasks
   - **含义**：促进、使便利，学术极高频

4. **outpace** (v.) + regulations / our ability to cope / traditional systems
   - **含义**：发展速度超过，科技类必背

5. **become** + ubiquitous / indispensable / prevalent
   - **注意**：`ubiquitous`（无处不在的）是高分形容词

6. **render** (v.) + traditional methods / jobs + obsolete / redundant
   - **固定结构**：`render + 宾语 + 形容词补语`

### 📚 话题专用名词

- automation（自动化）
- surveillance（监控）
- artificial intelligence（人工智能）
- digital divide（数字鸿沟）
- data privacy（数据隐私）
- the virtual realm（虚拟领域）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- With the advent of...（随着...的到来）
- While...brings convenience, it simultaneously...（虽然...带来便利，同时也...）
- Notwithstanding its benefits...（尽管有其好处...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *With the advent of artificial intelligence, automation has **revolutionised** manufacturing, yet it simultaneously **poses** a significant threat to low-skilled employment, **rendering** certain traditional roles obsolete.*

> *Although digital devices **facilitate** instant communication, they also **outpace** existing privacy regulations, thereby exposing users to unprecedented levels of surveillance.*


## Topic 4：政府与城市（Government & Urbanisation）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **allocate** (v.) + public funds / resources / budgets + to...
   - **介词结构**：`allocate A to B`（把 A 分配给 B）

2. **alleviate / ease** (v.) + traffic congestion / poverty / housing shortages
   - **注意**：`alleviate` 比 `reduce` 更正式

3. **stimulate** (v.) + economic growth / investment / job creation
   - **含义**：刺激、推动

4. **invest in** (v.) + infrastructure / public transport / healthcare
   - **介词结构**：固定搭配 `invest in`（不可用 `on`）

5. **impose** (v.) + restrictions / taxes + on...
   - **介词结构**：`impose A on B`（把 A 强加于 B）

6. **tackle** (v.) + urban sprawl / inequality / social issues
   - **介词结构**：直接加宾语

### 📚 话题专用名词

- infrastructure（基础设施）
- urbanisation（城市化）
- congestion（拥堵）
- fiscal policy（财政政策）
- public welfare（公共福利）
- social mobility（社会流动性）
- affordable housing（经济适用房）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- To this end...（为此...）
- This would necessitate...（这将需要...）
- As a consequence...（其结果是...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *To this end, city councils should **allocate** substantial public funds **to** affordable housing projects, **alleviating** both homelessness and social inequality.*

> *If governments **invest in** modern infrastructure rather than **imposing** punitive taxes on commuters, they can **stimulate** economic growth while simultaneously **easing** traffic congestion.*


## Topic 5：健康（Health）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **lead to** (v.) + a sedentary lifestyle / obesity / chronic illness
   - **注意**：`lead to` 后接名词（结果）

2. **pose** (v.) + a risk to / a threat to + physical / mental health
   - **介词结构**：`pose a risk to health`

3. **alleviate** (v.) + stress / symptoms / pain / anxiety
   - **注意**：比 `relieve` 更学术

4. **deteriorate** (v.) + mental wellbeing / physical condition / health outcomes
   - **注意**：可作及物或不及物，意为“恶化”

5. **incorporate** (v.) + physical activity / a balanced diet + into daily routine
   - **介词结构**：`incorporate A into B`

6. **suffer from** (v.) + chronic diseases / mental disorders / burnout
   - **介词结构**：固定搭配，接 `from`

### 📚 话题专用名词

- wellbeing（身心健康）
- nutrition（营养）
- sedentary behaviour（久坐行为）
- life expectancy（预期寿命）
- healthcare provision（医疗供给）
- obesity epidemic（肥胖流行病）
- stress-related illness（压力相关疾病）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- With the rise in...（随着...的上升）
- Given the prevalence of...（鉴于...的普遍性）
- This is partly attributable to...（这部分可归因于...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *Given the prevalence of fast food and desk-bound jobs, many urban dwellers **suffer from** a sedentary lifestyle, which in turn **deteriorates** their mental wellbeing and **poses** a significant risk to life expectancy.*

> *To **alleviate** stress-related illnesses, individuals should **incorporate** moderate physical activity **into** their daily routines, as this is conducive to both cardiovascular health and psychological resilience.*


## Topic 6：犯罪（Crime & Law）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **deter** (v.) + potential offenders / criminal behaviour / crime rates
   - **介词结构**：`deter sb from doing`（威慑某人不去做）

2. **rehabilitate** (v.) + prisoners / offenders / convicts
   - **含义**：比 `reform` 更正式，强调重新融入社会

3. **impose** (v.) + stricter penalties / harsher sentences + on...
   - **介词结构**：`impose A on B`

4. **commit** (v.) + a crime / an offence / fraud / theft
   - **固定搭配**：不可用 `do`

5. **address** (v.) + the root causes of crime / juvenile delinquency / social inequality
   - **含义**：比 `solve` 更深层，指“处理根源”

6. **reoffend** (v.) / reoffending rates
   - **注意**：动词或名词，表“再次犯罪”

### 📚 话题专用名词

- recidivism（再犯、累犯）
- incarceration（监禁）
- delinquency（违法）
- law enforcement（执法）
- punitive measure（惩罚性措施）
- community service（社区服务）
- justice system（司法系统）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- Rather than merely...（而不仅仅是...）
- ...with a view to...（旨在...）
- It is counterproductive to...（...会适得其反）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *Rather than merely imposing harsher sentences, the justice system should focus on rehabilitating offenders, with a view to reducing recidivism rates and addressing the root causes of criminal behaviour.*

> *If governments fail to **deter** potential offenders by addressing social inequality, punitive measures alone may prove counterproductive, as they do little to prevent young people from **committing** minor offences.*


## Topic 7：全球化（Globalisation）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **eradicate** (v.) + poverty / hunger / illiteracy / inequality
   - **含义**：意为“根除”，程度极强，不轻易用

2. **promote** (v.) + cultural integration / economic ties / mutual understanding
   - **含义**：促进、推动

3. **widen** (v.) + the gap / disparity / inequality + between A and B
   - **介词结构**：`widen the gap between A and B`

4. **undermine** (v.) + local traditions / cultural identity / sovereignty
   - **含义**：意为“逐渐削弱、侵蚀”

5. **facilitate** (v.) + cross-border trade / international cooperation / the exchange of ideas
   - **含义**：促进（使容易进行）

6. **expose to** (v.) + diverse cultures / global norms / foreign influences
   - **介词结构**：常用被动 `be exposed to`

### 📚 话题专用名词

- cultural homogenisation（文化同质化）
- global village（地球村）
- multinational corporation（跨国公司）
- free trade（自由贸易）
- brain drain（人才流失）
- cultural heritage（文化遗产）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- While...brings prosperity, it also...（虽然...带来繁荣，但也...）
- This phenomenon is two-fold...（这一现象是双面的...）
- As a result of...（由于...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *While globalisation facilitates cross-border trade and the exchange of ideas, it may simultaneously **undermine** local traditions and **widen** the economic disparity between developed and developing nations.*

> *If multinational corporations continue to dominate domestic markets, they may inadvertently **expose** local populations to cultural homogenisation, thereby **eroding** the unique cultural identity that defines their heritage.*


## Topic 8：媒体（Media）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **distort** (v.) + the truth / reality / facts / information
   - **含义**：意为“歪曲”，媒体类核心动词

2. **manipulate** (v.) + public opinion / emotions / perceptions
   - **含义**：意为“操纵”，与媒体搭配极佳

3. **give rise to** (v.) + misleading information / public concern / controversy
   - **含义**：意为“引发、引起”，比 `cause` 高级

4. **sensationalise** (v.) + news stories / events / headlines
   - **含义**：意为“耸人听闻地渲染”

5. **expose to** (v.) + graphic content / biased reporting / advertising
   - **介词结构**：常用被动 `be exposed to`

6. **hold accountable** (v.) + media outlets / journalists / platforms
   - **介词结构**：`hold + 宾语 + accountable`（对...问责）

### 📚 话题专用名词

- mass media（大众传媒）
- impartiality（公正性）
- propaganda（宣传）
- clickbait（标题党）
- echo chamber（信息茧房）
- freedom of press（新闻自由）
- fake news（假新闻）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- With the proliferation of...（随着...的激增）
- Not only...but also...（不仅...而且...）
- ...which in turn...（...这反过来又...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *With the proliferation of social media, certain outlets **sensationalise** headlines to attract clicks, thereby **distorting** the truth and **giving rise to** widespread public misinformation.*

> *Not only does biased reporting **manipulate** public opinion, but it also diminishes trust in the media; hence, regulators must **hold** platforms **accountable** for the content they disseminate.*


## Topic 9：文化与传统（Culture & Tradition）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **preserve** (v.) + cultural heritage / traditions / ancient customs
   - **含义**：保护、保存，比 `protect` 更强调“留存”

2. **rekindle** (v.) + interest in / enthusiasm for + traditional arts / history
   - **含义**：意为“重新唤起”，很有文采

3. **lose** (v.) + (one's) identity / cultural roots / connection to the past
   - **含义**：失去（抽象事物）

4. **pass down** (v.) + traditions / stories / knowledge + from generation to generation
   - **介词结构**：`pass down A from... to...`

5. **embody** (v.) + cultural values / national spirit / historical memory
   - **含义**：意为“体现、象征”，抽象类绝佳动词

6. **dilute** (v.) + cultural authenticity / traditional practices / beliefs
   - **含义**：意为“稀释、削弱”，指文化纯度下降

### 📚 话题专用名词

- intangible heritage（非物质文化遗产）
- ancestry（祖先）
- folklore（民间传说）
- globalisation（全球化）
- cultural appropriation（文化挪用）
- sense of belonging（归属感）
- intergenerational gap（代沟）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- In an era of...（在...的时代）
- Lest...（以免...）
- This serves as a reminder that...（这提醒我们...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *In an era of rapid globalisation, governments should actively **preserve** intangible heritage and **rekindle** the younger generation's interest in traditional arts, **lest** they gradually **lose** their cultural identity.*

> *While modernisation is inevitable, it should not **dilute** the authenticity of local customs; instead, communities should **pass down** ancestral knowledge, as this **embodies** the unique values that define their heritage.*


## Topic 10：工作与就业（Work & Employment）


### 🔥 黄金动词短语（6 个）

1. **secure** (v.) + a stable job / long-term employment / a position
   - **含义**：意为“获得”，比 `get` 正式

2. **cope with** (v.) + heavy workloads / pressure / stressful deadlines
   - **介词结构**：固定搭配，意为“应对困难”

3. **offer** (v.) + job satisfaction / flexibility / career progression
   - **含义**：提供（抽象福利/回报）

4. **adapt to** (v.) + new working conditions / technological changes / the evolving market
   - **含义**：适应，比 `adjust to` 更常用

5. **burn out** (v.) / burnout (n.)
   - **注意**：动词短语或名词，表“职业倦怠”

6. **strike a balance** (v.) + between work and life / professional and personal commitments
   - **固定结构**：`strike a balance between A and B`

### 📚 话题专用名词

- workforce（劳动力）
- automation（自动化）
- remote working（远程工作）
- job mobility（职业流动性）
- wage gap（工资差距）
- employee wellbeing（员工福祉）
- career prospects（职业前景）

### 🔗 学术逻辑连词

- In light of...（鉴于...）
- This not only...but also...（这不仅...而且...）
- ...otherwise...（...否则...）

### ✍️ 完整高分例句

> *In light of increasing automation, employees must continuously **adapt to** technological changes; otherwise, they risk being left behind in a highly competitive job market.*

> *While a well-paying job can **offer** financial security, it is equally crucial to **strike a balance** between professional demands and personal wellbeing, **lest** employees **burn out** and lose their long-term productivity.*
