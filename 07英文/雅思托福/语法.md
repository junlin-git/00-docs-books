# 📚 雅思与托福 · 完整语法详解手册（超详细版）

本手册涵盖雅思（IELTS）和托福（TOEFL）考试中所有核心语法考点，按模块划分，从基础到高阶，逐一详解。每个模块均包含：**核心规则 + 细分用法 + 大量例句 + 常见错误 + 考试应用场景 + 高频考点提示**。

## 一、动词时态系统（Tenses）—— 12 种时态全解

时态是雅思写作和口语中**最基础也最容易失分**的板块。托福同样重视时态在学术语境中的准确使用。掌握时态的核心是：**动作发生的时间** + **动作的状态（完成/进行/一般）**。

---

### 1. 一般现在时（Simple Present）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **客观事实/普遍真理** | 永远不会改变的科学事实、自然规律、社会常识 | Water boils at 100°C. / The Earth revolves around the Sun. / Pollution is a global issue. |
| **习惯性/反复发生的动作** | 常与频率副词（always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day/week/year）连用 | I drink coffee every morning. / She visits her grandmother twice a month. |
| **固定时间表/日程安排** | 用于谈论预定的未来事件（交通工具、课程、会议等） | The train leaves at 8 PM. / My class starts at 9 AM tomorrow. |
| **即时评论/现场解说** | 用于体育解说、演示说明、故事叙述中的“现场感” | He passes the ball to Johnson. Johnson shoots — and scores! |
| **标题/摘要/书评** | 新闻标题、书籍摘要、电影简介中常用现在时 | The report concludes that emissions are rising. / In the film, the hero dies at the end. |
| **时间状语从句和条件状语从句中表将来** | 在 `when`, `if`, `before`, `after`, `as soon as`, `until`, `unless`, `provided that` 等引导的从句中，用一般现在时替代将来时 | I will call you when I **arrive**. / If it **rains** tomorrow, we will cancel the trip. |

#### 动词第三人称单数变化规则

| 情况 | 规则 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般情况 | 加 `-s` | work → works, run → runs |
| 以 `s, x, sh, ch, o` 结尾 | 加 `-es` | kiss → kisses, fix → fixes, wash → washes, watch → watches, go → goes |
| 以辅音 + `y` 结尾 | 变 `y` 为 `i` 加 `-es` | study → studies, fly → flies |
| 以元音 + `y` 结尾 | 加 `-s` | play → plays, say → says |
| 不规则 | 特殊变化 | have → has, do → does, be → is |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| She **go** to school. | 第三人称单数忘记加 `-s` | She **goes** to school. |
| He **doesn't knows** the answer. | 助动词后接了带 `-s` 的动词 | He **doesn't know** the answer. |
| If he **will come**, I'll tell you. | 条件状语从句中用了将来时 | If he **comes**, I'll tell you. |

#### 雅思/托福考试应用场景

- **写作 Task 2 / 托福独立写作**：论述普遍现象、社会规律时。
  - `It is widely believed that education is the key to success.`
- **口语 Part 1 / 托福独立口语**：描述日常生活、习惯时。
  - `I usually wake up at 7 AM.`

### 2. 现在进行时（Present Continuous）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **此刻正在发生的动作** | 说话此时此刻正在进行中 | She is writing an essay now. / Listen! Someone is singing. |
| **近期计划/安排（表将来）** | 与时间状语连用，表示近期确定的安排（比 `be going to` 更具体） | I am meeting my supervisor tomorrow. / They are flying to Paris next week. |
| **反复发生的习惯（带有情绪色彩）** | 与 `always`, `constantly`, `forever` 等副词连用，表示说话人的情绪（抱怨、赞赏、惊讶等） | He is **always** losing his keys. （抱怨） / She is **always** helping others.（赞赏） |
| **变化的趋势/发展** | 表示正在逐渐变化的过程 | The climate is getting warmer. / The population is growing rapidly. |
| **与 `nowadays`, `currently`, `these days` 连用** | 表示当前一段时间内的状态（不一定在说话此刻发生） | I am currently reading a book about psychology. |

#### 静态动词（Stative Verbs）通常不用进行时

静态动词描述**状态**而非**动作**，通常不用于进行时。常见静态动词分类：

| 类别 | 例词 |
|------|------|
| 感官类 | see, hear, smell, taste, feel（表示感官时） |
| 认知/情感类 | know, believe, think（认为）, understand, remember, forget, want, wish, like, love, hate, prefer |
| 拥有/存在类 | have（拥有）, own, belong, possess, exist, contain |
| 度量/关系类 | cost, weigh, measure, seem, appear, resemble, depend, matter |

**例外情况**：当这些词转义为**动态动作**时，可用进行时。

| 静态用法（不用进行时） | 动态用法（可用进行时） |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| I **think** he is right.（认为） | I **am thinking** about the problem.（在思考） |
| The soup **tastes** good.（尝起来） | She **is tasting** the soup.（在尝） |
| I **see** the house.（看到） | I **am seeing** my doctor tomorrow.（会见） |
| He **has** a car.（拥有） | He **is having** dinner.（吃/进行） |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I **am knowing** the answer. | `know` 是静态动词 | I **know** the answer. |
| She **is having** a car. | 表示拥有时不能用进行时 | She **has** a car. |

#### 雅思/托福考试应用场景

- **口语 Part 1 / 托福独立口语**：描述当前正在做的事、近期计划。
  - `Currently, I am preparing for the IELTS exam.`
- **写作 Task 2**：描述当前社会趋势。
  - `Many countries are investing heavily in renewable energy.`

### 3. 一般过去时（Simple Past）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **过去发生且已完成的动作** | 有明确的过去时间状语（yesterday, last week, in 2019, three days ago） | I visited London in 2019. / She graduated from university last year. |
| **过去的一连串动作** | 按时间顺序叙述过去的多个动作 | He **entered** the room, **turned on** the light, and **sat down**. |
| **过去的习惯/状态** | 现在已不再成立的状态或习惯（也可用 `used to`） | When I was a child, I **played** the piano. / He **lived** in Paris for 5 years.（现在不住了） |
| **在间接引语中** | 主句为过去时时，从句动词需后移一个时态 | She said she **was** tired. |

#### 动词过去式变化规则

**规则变化（加 `-ed`）**：

| 情况 | 规则 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般情况 | 加 `-ed` | work → worked, play → played |
| 以 `-e` 结尾 | 加 `-d` | live → lived, like → liked |
| 以辅音 + `y` 结尾 | 变 `y` 为 `i` 加 `-ed` | study → studied, try → tried |
| 重读闭音节（辅元辅） | 双写尾字母加 `-ed` | stop → stopped, plan → planned |
| 以 `-l` 结尾（英式） | 双写 `l` 加 `-ed` | travel → travelled（英式）/ traveled（美式） |

**不规则动词（常见高频）**：

| 原形 | 过去式 | 过去分词 | 原形 | 过去式 | 过去分词 |
|------|--------|----------|------|--------|----------|
| go | went | gone | see | saw | seen |
| come | came | come | take | took | taken |
| do | did | done | give | gave | given |
| have | had | had | make | made | made |
| say | said | said | get | got | got/gotten |
| think | thought | thought | know | knew | known |
| find | found | found | leave | left | left |
| meet | met | met | speak | spoke | spoken |
| write | wrote | written | read | read | read |
| eat | ate | eaten | drink | drank | drunk |
| sing | sang | sung | swim | swam | swum |
| drive | drove | driven | fly | flew | flown |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I **go** to the park yesterday. | 时间状语为过去时，动词必须用过去式 | I **went** to the park yesterday. |
| She **didn't went** to school. | 助动词后接动词原形 | She **didn't go** to school. |
| I have **went** there. | 现在完成时需用过去分词 | I have **gone** there. |

#### 雅思/托福考试应用场景

- **口语 Part 2 / 托福独立口语**：描述个人经历、过去事件。
  - `When I was in high school, I participated in a volunteer program.`
- **写作 Task 1（图表题）**：描述过去的数据（如果图表展示过去年份）。
  - `In 2000, the population stood at 5 million.`

### 4. 过去进行时（Past Continuous）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **过去某一时刻正在进行的动作** | 强调在过去的某个时间点，动作正在进行（而非"发生了"） | I **was reading** when she called. / At 8 PM yesterday, I **was having** dinner. |
| **两个过去同时进行的动作** | 用 `while` 连接两个同时进行的过去动作 | While I **was cooking**, he **was watching** TV. |
| **过去背景描述（烘托氛围）** | 在叙事中用于设置背景 | The sun **was shining**, birds **were singing**, and we **were walking** along the beach. |
| **礼貌询问（比一般过去时更委婉）** | 在请求或询问时，过去进行时听起来更客气 | I **was wondering** if you could help me. / What **were you** doing last night? |
| **未实现的计划（与 `want`, `hope`, `plan` 等连用）** | 表示本想做但没做的事 | I **was going to** call you, but I forgot. / She **was planning to** travel abroad. |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I **was receiving** your email yesterday. | 短暂动作（receiving 本身不持续）用一般过去时 | I **received** your email yesterday. |
| While I **was having** dinner, the phone **was ringing**. | 用一般过去时表示"插入了"另一短暂动作 | While I was having dinner, the phone **rang**. |

#### 考试应用场景

- **口语 Part 2**：描述过去经历中的场景、氛围。
  - `It was a rainy evening, and I was watching TV when suddenly...`
- **听力**：在对话中捕捉背景信息。

### 5. 现在完成时（Present Perfect）⭐⭐ 雅思托福核心重点

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **过去动作对现在有影响/结果** | 过去的动作或状态对现在产生了可见的结果 | I **have lost** my key.（我现在进不去门） / She **has broken** her leg.（她现在走路不便） |
| **从过去持续到现在的动作** | 表示从过去开始一直持续到现在的动作（常与 `for + 时间段` 或 `since + 时间点` 连用） | I **have lived** here for 5 years. / She **has worked** here since 2010. |
| **过去经历/经验（不强调具体时间）** | 表示"有……的经历"，不关心具体什么时候发生 | I **have seen** that movie before. / Have you **ever been** to Japan? |
| **刚完成的动作（常与 `just` 连用）** | 表示刚刚结束的动作 | She **has just left**. / They **have just arrived**. |
| **到目前为止尚未发生的动作** | 与 `yet`, `never`, `not...yet` 连用 | I haven't finished my homework yet. / He has never been abroad. |
| **多次重复的动作** | 表示到目前为止发生了多次（与 `twice`, `several times`, `many times` 连用） | I **have visited** Paris three times. / She **has called** me several times. |
| **在最高级句型中** | "这是我见过最好的……" | This is the best movie I **have ever seen**. / She is the most talented person I **have ever met**. |

#### `for` 与 `since` 的区别

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `for` | 后接**时间段**（duration） | for 5 years, for 3 hours, for a long time |
| `since` | 后接**时间点**（starting point） | since 2010, since Monday, since I was a child |

- `I have studied English **for** 10 years.`（持续了10年）
- `I have studied English **since** 2014.`（从2014年开始）

#### 现在完成时 vs. 一般过去时（核心区别）

| 现在完成时（Present Perfect） | 一般过去时（Simple Past） |
|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| 关注**对现在的影响**，不关心具体时间 | 关注**过去发生的动作**，关心具体时间 |
| 不能与明确的过去时间状语连用（yesterday, last year, in 2019） | 必须与过去时间状语连用 |
| I **have seen** that movie.（我知道剧情了） | I **saw** that movie last week. |
| He **has lost** his wallet.（现在没钱） | He **lost** his wallet yesterday.（陈述过去的事实） |
| She **has lived** here since 2010.（现在还在住） | She **lived** here for 10 years.（现在不住了） |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I **have seen** him yesterday. | 不能与过去时间状语连用 | I **saw** him yesterday. |
| She **has** already **went** home. | 助动词后需用过去分词 | She **has** already **gone** home. |
| I **have** here **for** 3 years. | 漏掉了动词 | I **have been** here **for** 3 years. |

#### 雅思/托福考试应用场景

- **写作 Task 2 / 托福独立写作**：表达与现在相关的影响、趋势。
  - `In recent years, technology has transformed the way we communicate.`
- **口语 Part 1 / 托福独立口语**：谈论经历、兴趣爱好。
  - `I have been learning English for over 10 years.`

### 6. 现在完成进行时（Present Perfect Continuous）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **从过去持续到现在，强调"一直在做"** | 强调动作的持续性、未中断性，常与 `for` / `since` 连用 | I **have been studying** for 3 hours. / She **has been waiting** since 9 AM. |
| **强调动作本身而非结果** | 关心的是"在做"，而不是"做完了" | I **have been reading** this book.（还没读完） / I **have read** this book.（读完了） |
| **表达暂时性/最近的习惯** | 最近一段时间反复发生的动作 | I **have been feeling** tired lately. / He **has been exercising** a lot recently. |
| **表达情感或暗示** | 通过动作的持续性暗示某种状态（如疲惫、忙碌） | You **have been working** too hard. You need a break. |

#### 现在完成时 vs. 现在完成进行时（核心区别）

| 现在完成时（Present Perfect） | 现在完成进行时（Present Perfect Continuous） |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| 强调**结果** | 强调**过程/持续性** |
| I **have read** the book.（读完了，结果） | I **have been reading** the book.（一直在读，可能没读完） |
| I **have painted** the room.（漆完了） | I **have been painting** the room.（一直在漆，可能还没结束） |
| 可与静态动词连用 | 不能与静态动词连用 |
| I **have known** him for years. | ❌ I have been knowing him for years. |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I **have been knowing** her since childhood. | `know` 是静态动词 | I **have known** her since childhood. |
| She **has been finishing** her homework.（搭配不当） | 短暂动作不需强调持续 | She **has finished** her homework. |

#### 考试应用场景

- **口语 Part 1**：描述近期正在做的事。
  - `Lately, I have been reading a lot of science fiction novels.`
- **写作 Task 2**：描述持续的社会变化。
  - `Over the past decade, people have been moving from rural areas to cities.`

### 7. 过去完成时（Past Perfect）⭐⭐ 雅思阅读、写作 Task 1 高频

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **"过去的过去"** | 表示在某个过去的动作或时间点之前，另一个更早的动作已经完成 | When I **arrived**, the train **had left**. / Before he moved to London, he **had never traveled** abroad. |
| **过去因果关系** | 表示过去两个动作之间的因果关系（先发生的原因导致后发生的结果） | He **was** tired because he **had worked** all night. / She **failed** the exam because she **hadn't studied** enough. |
| **过去未曾实现的希望/愿望** | 与 `hope`, `expect`, `want`, `think`, `intend`, `plan` 等连用，表示原想做但没做 | I **had hoped** to see you, but I was too busy. / She **had planned** to travel, but she got sick. |
| **在间接引语中** | 当主句为过去时时，从句用过去完成时（比一般过去时更"过去"） | He said that he **had already eaten**. / She told me she **had seen** the movie. |
| **`hardly...when...` / `no sooner...than...`** | 表示"一……就……"（与倒装结构连用） | Hardly **had I arrived** when the meeting started. / No sooner **had he entered** the room than the phone rang. |
| **It was the first time that...** | "那是第一次……" | It was the first time that I **had seen** such a beautiful sunset. |

#### 过去完成时 vs. 一般过去时（核心区别）

| 过去完成时（Past Perfect） | 一般过去时（Simple Past） |
|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| 强调"过去的过去"，有先后顺序 | 只陈述过去的动作，不强调先后 |
| When I arrived, she **had left**.（她已走了，我没见到她） | When I arrived, she **left**.（她在我到的时候走了） |
| By 2000, he **had worked** there for 10 years.（到2000年为止工作了10年） | He **worked** there for 10 years.（过去工作过10年，与现在无关） |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I **had seen** the movie yesterday. | 没有"过去的过去"参照点时，用一般过去时 | I **saw** the movie yesterday. |
| Before I **had arrived**, she left. | 时间状语已表明先后，不需要过去完成时 | Before I **arrived**, she **had left**. |
| He said that he **is** tired.（时态呼应错误） | 主句为过去时，从句需后移 | He said that he **was** tired. |

#### 雅思/托福考试应用场景

- **写作 Task 1**：描述过去时间点之前的数值变化。
  - `By 2015, the number of users had reached 10 million.`
- **阅读**：理解长难句中的时间逻辑关系。
  - `The researchers, who had been studying the phenomenon for decades, finally published their findings.`

### 8. 过去完成进行时（Past Perfect Continuous）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **过去某一时刻之前一直在进行的动作** | 强调在过去某个时间点之前，某个动作一直在进行 | He **had been waiting** for two hours when she finally arrived. / The children **had been playing** in the garden before it started to rain. |
| **过去因果（强调持续过程导致结果）** | 强调因为长时间持续的某个动作，导致了后面的结果 | He was out of breath because he **had been running**. / She was very tired because she **had been studying** all night. |

#### 考试应用场景

- **阅读**：识别时间顺序，理解叙事逻辑。
  - `The project team, which had been working on the problem for months, finally found a solution.`
- **口语 Part 2**：描述过去经历中的背景。
  - `When I arrived, my friends had been waiting for me for over an hour.`

### 9. 一般将来时（Simple Future）—— 多种表达方式

#### `will` vs. `be going to` vs. 其他形式

| 形式 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `will + do` | **即兴决定**（说话时才做出的决定） | A: "The phone is ringing." B: "I **will answer** it." |
| `will + do` | **预测**（基于主观判断、信念，无客观依据） | I think it **will rain** tomorrow. / She **will probably** come. |
| `will + do` | **承诺/威胁/意愿** | I **will always love** you. / I **will pay** you back tomorrow. |
| `be going to + do` | **已有计划的打算**（事先想好的计划） | We **are going to buy** a new car next month. / I **am going to start** a business. |
| `be going to + do` | **有迹象的预测**（有客观证据的推测） | Look at those clouds – it **is going to rain**. / The car is so dirty – she **is going to wash** it. |
| `be to + do` | **正式安排/命令/规定**（公文、新闻、官方计划） | The president **is to visit** the UK next week. / The meeting **is to be held** at 10 AM. |
| `be about to + do` | **即将马上做某事**（非常临近将来） | The train **is about to leave**. Hurry up! / She **is about to** give a speech. |
| `be doing`（现在进行时表将来） | **已确定的近期安排**（强调计划性和确定性） | I **am meeting** my friend at 6 PM. / They **are flying** to Paris tomorrow. |
| 一般现在时表将来 | **固定时间表**（交通工具、课程、日程） | The plane **takes off** at 9 PM. / The class **starts** next Monday. |

#### `will` vs. `be going to` 对比

| 场景 | `will` | `be going to` |
|------|--------|---------------|
| 决定时间 | 说话时当场决定 | 事前已决定 |
| 预测依据 | 主观判断、个人信念 | 客观证据、迹象 |
| 例句 | A: "I need help." B: "I **will help** you." | I **am going to** study medicine because I've always wanted to be a doctor. |
| 例句 | I think he **will** pass the exam.（我个人认为） | Look at those dark clouds – it **is going to** rain.（有证据） |

#### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I **will to go** home. | `will` 后接动词原形 | I **will go** home. |
| She **will** in London tomorrow. | 缺少动词 | She **will be** in London tomorrow. |
| If it **will rain**, we'll stay inside. | 条件状语从句中不用将来时 | If it **rains**, we'll stay inside. |

---

### 10. 将来进行时（Future Continuous）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **将来某一时刻正在进行的动作** | 强调在将来某个时间点，动作正在进行 | This time tomorrow, I **will be flying** to New York. / At 8 PM tonight, she **will be having** dinner. |
| **委婉询问他人的计划** | 比一般将来时更礼貌、更自然 | **Will you be using** the computer later? / **Will you be attending** the meeting tomorrow? |
| **预先安排好的未来事件** | 表示"按计划会发生"的事，不带个人意图 | The band **will be performing** at the stadium next month. |

#### 考试应用场景

- **口语 Part 1/Part 2**：描述未来计划。
  - `This time next year, I will be studying at a university in Canada.`
- **听力**：在对话中捕捉未来的时间安排。

---

### 11. 将来完成时（Future Perfect）⭐⭐ 写作高分句型

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **将来某一时刻前将完成的动作** | 强调在将来某个时间点之前，动作已经完成 | By 2030, renewable energy **will have replaced** fossil fuels in many countries. / I **will have finished** the project by next Friday. |
| **推测过去的动作（对现在之前的推测）** | "到……时候应该已经……" | By now, he **will have arrived** at the airport. / By this time next year, we **will have graduated**. |

#### 考试应用场景

- **写作 Task 2 / 托福独立写作**：预测未来发展趋势（高分句式）。
  - `By 2050, scientists will have developed more effective treatments for many diseases.`
  - `If governments do not take action, the planet's average temperature will have risen by 2°C by the end of the century.`

---

### 12. 将来完成进行时（Future Perfect Continuous）

#### 核心用法

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **将来某一时刻前一直在进行的动作** | 强调从过去一直持续到未来某个时间点的动作（强调持续性） | By next year, I **will have been teaching** English for 20 years. / By the time she retires, she **will have been working** at the company for 40 years. |

#### 考试应用场景

- **写作**：描述长期趋势、累积过程。
  - `By 2030, China will have been building high-speed rail networks for over two decades.`

## 二、语态（Voice）—— 主动与被动

被动语态在学术英语中极为重要，尤其在雅思写作 Task 1（流程图、图表）和托福综合写作（科学实验描述）中高频出现。

### 被动语态结构

**核心结构**：`be (时态) + 过去分词 (done)`

### 各时态被动语态一览表

| 时态 | 主动语态 | 被动语态 |
|------|----------|----------|
| 一般现在时 | The company **produces** cars. | Cars **are produced** by the company. |
| 一般过去时 | The team **finished** the project. | The project **was finished** by the team. |
| 现在进行时 | The workers **are building** the bridge. | The bridge **is being built** by the workers. |
| 过去进行时 | They **were repairing** the road. | The road **was being repaired**. |
| 现在完成时 | They **have completed** the experiment. | The experiment **has been completed**. |
| 过去完成时 | They **had finished** the report. | The report **had been finished**. |
| 将来时 | They **will build** a new hospital. | A new hospital **will be built**. |
| 将来完成时 | They **will have completed** the project by then. | The project **will have been completed** by then. |
| 情态动词 | You **should check** the data. | The data **should be checked**. |
| 不定式 | I **need to finish** this task. | This task **needs to be finished**. |
| 动名词 | I **enjoy being** praised.（带有被动含义） | — |

### 被动语态在雅思/托福中的核心应用场景

| 场景 | 说明 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **学术/科技/实验描述（托福综合写作）** | 强调实验操作而非操作者 | The samples **were analyzed** under controlled conditions. / The data **were collected** over a period of six months. |
| **雅思写作 Task 1（流程图/地图/图表）** | 描述客观变化、流程步骤 | The coal **is transported** to the power plant. / The old market **was demolished** in 2015. |
| **避免主观性（写作 Task 2 / 托福独立写作）** | 用被动结构使表达更正式、客观 | It **is believed** that education is the key to development.（比 People believe... 更正式） / New policies **should be implemented** to reduce pollution. |
| **不强调动作执行者** | 动作的执行者不重要或不明确 | My wallet **was stolen**.（不知谁偷的） / English **is spoken** all over the world. |
| **雅思听力（学术讲座/对话）** | 识别被动结构，理解流程和信息 | The report **will be published** next month. / The test **is being conducted** at the moment. |

### 被动语态的常见结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `It + 被动 + that + 从句`（主语从句） | **It is said that** he is a genius. / **It is believed that** the government will take action. |
| `主语 + 被动 + 不定式`（省略 `that` 从句） | He **is said to be** a genius. / The company **is reported to have lost** millions. |
| `get + 过去分词`（非正式口语） | I **got lost** in the city. / He **got injured** in the game. |
| `have/get + 宾语 + 过去分词`（使役被动） | I **had my hair cut**. / She **got her car repaired**. / We **need to have the documents checked**. |

### 主动形式表被动含义

有些词在英语中虽为主动形式，但表达被动含义，这些在考试中极为常见：

| 动词/结构 | 例句 | 说明 |
|-----------|------|------|
| `need, require, want + doing`（主动表被动） | The car **needs washing**.（= needs to be washed） / The plants **need watering**. | `need doing` = `need to be done` |
| `be worth doing` | This movie is **worth watching**.（值被看） | — |
| `sell / read / wash / write + well / easily`（表示性质） | This book **sells well**.（书被卖得好） / The material **washes easily**.（易被洗） | 以主动形式表被动意义 |
| `感官动词 + 宾语 + 动词原形/doing` | I **saw him cross** the street.（看到全过程） / I **heard her singing**.（听到正在唱） | — |
| `prefer + doing` | I **prefer reading** to watching TV. | — |

### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| The letter **was wrote** by her. | 过去分词写错 | The letter **was written** by her. |
| This book **is belonged** to me. | `belong` 无被动形式 | This book **belongs** to me. |
| The event **was happened** yesterday. | `happen` 为不及物动词，无被动 | The event **happened** yesterday. |
| The proposal **has been** already **approve**. | 过去分词形式错误 | The proposal **has been** already **approved**. |

## 三、虚拟语气（Subjunctive Mood）⭐⭐ 写作高分必备

虚拟语气用来表达**假设、愿望、建议、与事实相反**的情况。在雅思/托福写作中恰当使用虚拟语气，是语法复杂度的显著标志。

### 1. 与现在事实相反（对现在的虚拟）

**结构**：`If + 主语 + 动词过去式 (be → were), 主语 + would/could/might/should + 动词原形`

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| If I **were** you, I **would accept** the offer. | 对现在的虚拟，`be` 一律用 `were`（不论主语人称） |
| If he **studied** harder, he **would get** better grades. | 事实是"他学习不努力" |
| If I **had** more money, I **could buy** a new car. | 事实是"我没那么多钱" |
| If she **knew** the truth, she **would be** angry. | 事实是"她不知道真相" |

**主句情态动词选择**：
- `would`：表示结果
- `could`：表示能力或可能性
- `might`：表示较小可能性
- `should`：表示应该（较少用于虚拟主句）

**倒装形式**（正式文体）：
- `Were I you, I would accept the offer.`（= If I were you...）

### 2. 与过去事实相反（对过去的虚拟）

**结构**：`If + 主语 + had done, 主语 + would/could/might + have done`

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| If I **had known** about the traffic, I **would have left** earlier. | 事实是"我当时不知道" |
| She **could have won** the competition if she **had trained** more. | 事实是"她训练不够" |
| If he **had studied** harder, he **would have passed** the exam. | 事实是"他没通过" |
| I **might have missed** the train if I **hadn't run**. | 事实是"我跑了" |

**倒装形式**（正式文体）：
- `Had I known about the traffic, I would have left earlier.`（= If I had known...）

### 3. 与将来事实相反或可能性极小（对将来的虚拟）

**结构**：`If + 主语 + should do / were to do, 主语 + would/could/might + 动词原形`

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| If he **should come** tomorrow, please tell him. | 可能性很小（但并非不可能） |
| If I **were to win** the lottery, I **would travel** around the world. | 几乎不可能实现的假想 |
| If it **were to rain** tomorrow, the match **would be** cancelled. | 发生概率极小 |

**倒装形式**（正式文体）：
- `Should he come tomorrow, please tell him.`（= If he should come...）
- `Were I to win the lottery, I would travel around the world.`（= If I were to win...）

### 4. 表示"建议、命令、要求、坚持"的虚拟（写作高频）

在 `suggest`, `advise`, `recommend`, `demand`, `insist`, `require`, `request`, `propose`, `order`, `urge`, `prefer` 等动词后的**宾语从句**中，谓语用 `(should) + 动词原形`。

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| I suggest that he **(should) study** harder. | `should` 可以省略，直接接动词原形（美式常省略） |
| The teacher demanded that all students **hand in** their homework on time. | 美式英语常省略 `should` |
| The committee recommends that the plan **be approved**. | 被动形式也直接用动词原形 |
| I insist that he **leave** immediately. | 虚拟语气，不用 `leaves` |

**在形容词后的主语从句中**（同样用 `(should) + 动词原形`）：

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `It is + 形容词 + that...` | It is **essential** that the data **be** reviewed carefully. |
| `It is vital / crucial / necessary / important / advisable / urgent + that...` | It is **vital** that every student **submit** the application before the deadline. |

### 5. 表示"愿望"的虚拟（wish / if only）

| 时间 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 对现在（不能实现） | `wish + 主语 + 动词过去式 (be → were)` | I wish I **were** taller. / She wishes she **lived** in Paris. |
| 对过去（后悔） | `wish + 主语 + had done` | I wish I **had taken** that course. / He wishes he **had listened** to his mother. |
| 对将来（不太可能实现） | `wish + 主语 + would do` | I wish you **would stop** smoking. / She wishes the baby **would stop** crying. |

**`would rather` / `would sooner`**（宁愿）：
- `I would rather you **stayed** at home tonight.`（对现在/将来的虚拟，用一般过去式）
- `I would rather you **had stayed** at home yesterday.`（对过去的虚拟，用过去完成时）

### 6. `as if / as though`（"好像"）引导的虚拟

| 时间 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 与现在事实相反 | `as if + 主语 + 动词过去式 (be → were)` | He spoke **as if** he **knew** everything.（事实是他不知道） |
| 与过去事实相反 | `as if + 主语 + had done` | She looks **as if** she **had seen** a ghost.（事实是她没见鬼） |

**注意**：如果 `as if` 表示"看起来很可能"（不一定是虚假的），则可以用陈述语气。
- `It looks **as if** it **is** going to rain.`（看起来真的要下雨了，可能是真的）

### 7. 虚拟语气的其他固定表达

| 表达 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `If only...`（要是……就好了） | **If only** I **had known** the truth! / **If only** he **were** here! |
| `It's (high/about) time + 主语 + 动词过去式`（早该做某事了） | It's **high time** we **left**. / It's **time** you **found** a better job. |
| `otherwise / or`（否则，暗含虚拟） | I was very busy; **otherwise** I **would have come**. / She ran fast; **or** she **would have missed** the bus. |

### 常见错误汇总

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| If I **was** you, I would go. | 虚拟语气中 `be` 一律用 `were` | If I **were** you, I would go. |
| I suggest that he **studies** harder. | 建议类动词后应接虚拟 | I suggest that he **(should) study** harder. |
| If I had known, I **would come**. | 与过去事实相反的虚拟，主句用 `would have done` | If I had known, I **would have come**. |
| I wish I **can** fly. | 表示愿望时用过去式 | I wish I **could** fly. |

## 四、情态动词（Modal Verbs）—— 详解与进阶用法

### 核心情态动词列表及含义

| 情态动词 | 核心含义 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| `can` | 能力、可能性（现时）、允许 | I **can** speak French. / You **can** see the stars from here. / **Can** I go now? |
| `could` | 过去能力、礼貌请求、可能性（较弱推测） | I **could** swim when I was 5. / **Could** you help me? / It **could** rain tonight. |
| `may` | 允许、可能性（正式） | **May** I come in? / It **may** be true. / He **may** arrive late. |
| `might` | 可能性（比 `may` 更弱） | It **might** snow tomorrow. / She **might** not come. |
| `must` | 必须、强烈推测（肯定） | You **must** wear a seatbelt. / He **must** be home by now.（强烈推测） |
| `shall` | 将来（第一人称，旧式）、征询建议 | I **shall** return.（正式） / **Shall** we go? |
| `should` | 应该、推测（应该会） | You **should** see a doctor. / It **should** arrive soon. |
| `will` | 将来、意愿、决心 | I **will** help you. / She **will** succeed. |
| `would` | 过去将来、委婉请求、过去习惯 | He said he **would** come. / **Would** you pass the salt? / When I was young, I **would** often walk to school. |
| `ought to` | 应该（同 `should`，多用于英式） | You **ought to** apologize. / He **ought to** be here by now. |
| `need` | 需要（否定和疑问中作情态动词） | You **needn't** come if you're busy. / **Need** I finish it today? |
| `dare` | 敢（常用于否定和疑问） | She **daren't** ask for help. / **Dare** he say that? |

### 情态动词的推测用法（可能性由强到弱）

| 强度 | 情态动词 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| 最强（几乎100%） | `must` | He **must** be at home. The light is on. |
| 很强（很可能） | `should / ought to` | She **should** arrive soon. The train left on time. |
| 中等（可能） | `may` | It **may** be true. I'm not sure. |
| 较弱（可能但不肯定） | `could / might` | He **might** be lost. / It **could** rain later. |
| 几乎不可能 | `can't / couldn't` | She **can't** be at home. I just saw her leave. |

### 情态动词 + 完成时（对过去的推测/虚拟）

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `must have done` | 对过去**肯定的推测**（有依据） | The ground is wet. It **must have rained**. / She isn't here – she **must have left** early. |
| `may / might have done` | 对过去**不太肯定的推测** | She **might have missed** the bus. / He **may have forgotten** his promise. |
| `could have done` | ①对过去**可能的推测** ②**本来可以做但没做**（遗憾） | He **could have taken** the earlier train.（推测） / You **could have told** me earlier!（遗憾） |
| `should / ought to have done` | ①**本应该做但没做**（批评/遗憾） ②**推测过去应该已经** | You **should have studied** harder.（批评） / He **should have arrived** by now.（推测） |
| `needn't have done` | **本不需要做但做了**（多余） | You **needn't have brought** an umbrella.（但带了） / She **needn't have worried**; everything was fine. |
| `would have done` | ①**本来会做但没做**（虚拟） ②**推测过去** | I **would have helped** if I had known.（虚拟） / The party **would have been** a success.（推测） |
| `can't / couldn't have done` | 对过去**否定的推测**（不可能） | He **can't have stolen** the money; he wasn't there. / She **couldn't have taken** the book because she wasn't in the room. |

### 情态动词的其他常见考点

| 考点 | 说明 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `need` 的两种用法 | 情态动词（否定/疑问） vs. 实义动词（肯定句） | You **needn't** go. / He **needs** to go. |
| `used to` vs. `would` | `used to` 表示过去习惯或状态（现在不再有）；`would` 只表示过去习惯（不表示过去状态） | I **used to** smoke. / I **used to** be fat.（不能用 would） / When I was young, I **would** often play outside. |
| `be able to` vs. `could` | `be able to` 强调"成功做到"（相当于 `manage to do`） | He **could** climb the mountain.（有能力） / He **was able to** climb the mountain.（成功做到了） |

### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| He **must** to go. | 情态动词后接动词原形 | He **must go**. |
| She **can** swims. | 情态动词后接动词原形 | She **can swim**. |
| You **mustn't** be late.（语境错误） | `mustn't` 表示"禁止"，如果只是"不必"用 `needn't` | You **needn't** be late.（不必） / You **mustn't** smoke here.（禁止） |
| He **should** have **went** home. | 助动词后接过去分词 | He **should** have **gone** home. |

## 五、句子结构（Sentence Structure）

句子按结构分为三类：**简单句、并列句、复合句**。在雅思/托福写作中，必须能做到三种句型交替使用，展示句式多样性。评分标准中明确要求"使用多种复杂结构"。

### 1. 简单句（Simple Sentence）

**结构**：`主语 + 谓语 + 宾语/补语`（只有一个主谓结构）

| 句型类型 | 结构 | 例句 |
|----------|------|------|
| 主谓（SV） | 主语 + 不及物动词 | The sun **rose**. / The population **increased**. |
| 主谓宾（SVO） | 主语 + 及物动词 + 宾语 | She **likes** coffee. / The government **passed** a new law. |
| 主系表（SVC） | 主语 + 系动词 + 表语 | The book **is** interesting. / He **became** a doctor. |
| 主谓双宾（SVOO） | 主语 + 及物动词 + 间接宾语 + 直接宾语 | She **gave** him a gift. / The teacher **taught** us grammar. |
| 主谓宾补（SVOC） | 主语 + 及物动词 + 宾语 + 宾语补足语 | They **made** him the leader. / I **found** the book interesting. |

**注意**：简单句可以包含多个并列的主语或谓语。

- **并列主语**：`Tom and Jerry are best friends.`
- **并列谓语**：`She sang and danced all night.`
- **并列宾语**：`I like apples, oranges, and bananas.`

### 2. 并列句（Compound Sentence）

**结构**：`简单句 + 并列连词 + 简单句`

**并列连词（FANBOYS）**：

| 连词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **F**or | 因为（表原因，比 because 正式） | He is happy, **for** he has won the prize. |
| **A**nd | 和、而且 | She studied hard, **and** she passed the exam. |
| **N**or | 也不（倒装） | He doesn't like coffee, **nor** does he like tea. |
| **B**ut | 但是 | I wanted to go, **but** I was too tired. |
| **O**r | 或者、否则（表选择） | You can stay here, **or** you can come with us. / Hurry up, **or** you will miss the train. |
| **Y**et | 然而（= but，语气更强） | It is simple, **yet** effective. |
| **S**o | 所以 | She was tired, **so** she went to bed early. |

**标点注意**：
- 并列句用 **逗号 + 连词** 连接。
- 不能用逗号连接两个独立分句（逗号拼接错误 `comma splice`）。

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| I like apples, she likes oranges. | I like apples, **but** she likes oranges. |
| It was raining, we stayed inside. | It was raining, **so** we stayed inside. |
|  — | I like apples; she likes oranges.（分号也可） |

### 3. 复合句（Complex Sentence）

**结构**：`主句 + 从句`（由一个主句和一个或多个从句构成，从句由从属连词引导）

复合句的核心是**三大从句**：名词性从句、定语从句、状语从句（详见第六章）。

| 例句 | 从句类型 |
|------|----------|
| I know **that he is honest**. | 名词性从句（宾语从句） |
| The man **who is wearing a hat** is my uncle. | 定语从句（形容词性从句） |
| **When I arrived**, the meeting had already started. | 状语从句（副词性从句） |
| **What she said** made me angry. | 名词性从句（主语从句） |

### 4. 复杂复合句（Compound-Complex Sentence）

**结构**：`并列句 + 从句`（至少两个主句 + 至少一个从句）

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Although I was tired**, I finished my homework, **and** I went to bed early. | 状语从句（Although）+ 并列句（and） |
| The teacher **who is from the US** gave us a lecture, **and** we all enjoyed it. | 定语从句 + 并列句 |
| **Because she studied hard**, she passed the exam, **so** her parents were proud of her. | 原因状语从句 + 并列句 |

**雅思/托福写作高分要求**：每篇文章至少出现 2-3 个复杂复合句。

## 六、三大从句（Clauses）⭐⭐ 核心重点

### 1. 名词性从句（Noun Clauses）

在句中充当名词功能的从句，可作**主语、宾语、表语、同位语**。

#### (1) 主语从句（Subject Clause）

**结构**：`引导词 + 从句 + 谓语`

**引导词分类**：

| 引导词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `that` | 陈述事实（在主语从句中**不可省略**） | **That he succeeded** surprised everyone. / **That she didn't come** was disappointing. |
| `whether` | 是否（表疑问，不用 `if` 引导主语从句） | **Whether she will come** is still unknown. / **Whether it rains** doesn't matter. |
| 疑问词（what, why, how, where, when, who） | 特殊疑问词 | **Why he left** remains a mystery. / **What she said** is true. / **How they did it** impressed me. |

**常见高分句型**（托福/雅思写作常用）：

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `It is + 形容词 + that...` | **It is clear that** climate change is a serious issue. / **It is essential that** measures be taken immediately.（虚拟语气） |
| `It is + 名词 + that...` | **It is a fact that** pollution is worsening. / **It is a common belief that** education is the key to success. |
| `It is + 过去分词 + that...` | **It is believed that** the government will take action. / **It is reported that** the economy is recovering. |
| `It + 不及物动词 + that...` | **It seems that** he is right. / **It happens that** I know his brother. |

#### (2) 宾语从句（Object Clause）

**结构**：`及物动词/介词 + 引导词 + 从句`

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| I think **that he is right**. | 动词的宾语从句（`that` 可省略） |
| She is worried **about whether she can pass the exam**. | 介词的宾语从句 |
| Could you tell me **where the station is**? | 间接疑问句（**不用倒装**） |
| He didn't know **what had happened**. | 疑问词引导 |
| I don't care **whether he comes or not**. | `whether...or not` |

**间接疑问句语序**（重点）：

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| Could you tell me where **is the station**? | Could you tell me where **the station is**? |
| I don't know what **is his name**. | I don't know what **his name is**. |
| She asked me why **did I leave**. | She asked me why **I left**. |

**`that` 宾语从句的省略**：
- 在 `think`, `believe`, `hope`, `know`, `say`, `tell` 等动词后，`that` 在口语和非正式写作中常可省略。
  - `I think (that) he is right.`
- 但在正式学术写作中，建议保留 `that` 以增加正式性。

#### (3) 表语从句（Predicative Clause）

**结构**：`主语 + 系动词 + 引导词 + 从句`

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| The problem is **that we don't have enough time**. | `that` 引导 |
| The question is **whether we can afford it**. | `whether` 引导（不用 `if`） |
| This is **what I want**. | 疑问词引导 |
| That is **why I came here**. | `why` 引导，表原因 |
| The result was **that we won**. | `that` 引导表语从句 |

#### (4) 同位语从句（Appositive Clause）

对前面的**抽象名词**（如 `fact`, `idea`, `news`, `belief`, `suggestion`, `doubt`, `thought`, `hope`, `feeling`, `conclusion`, `truth`）进行解释说明。

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| The news **that she won the prize** surprised us. | 同位语从句说明 `news` 的具体内容 |
| I have no idea **where he went**. | 对 `idea` 的具体解释 |
| The fact **that he lied** disappointed everyone. | `that` 说明 `fact` 的具体内容 |
| The suggestion **that we should leave** was rejected. | 同位语从句中可用虚拟语气 |

**同位语从句 vs. 定语从句（核心区别）**：

| 同位语从句（Appositive Clause） | 定语从句（Relative Clause） |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------|
| 解释名词的**内容** | 修饰名词的**特征** |
| `that` 在从句中**不作任何成分** | `that` 在从句中**作主语或宾语** |
| The news **that he won the game** is true.（他赢了，这个新闻的内容） | The news **that he told me** is true.（他告诉我的那个新闻） |

---

### 2. 定语从句（Attributive Clause / Relative Clause）⭐⭐ 核心重点

修饰名词或代词的从句，相当于"形容词"。定语从句是雅思/托福写作中最常见的复杂句型之一。

#### (1) 关系代词（Relative Pronouns）

| 关系代词 | 先行词（人/物） | 在从句中的成分 | 例句 |
|----------|----------------|--------------|------|
| `who` | 人 | 主语 | The man **who called you** is my brother. |
| `whom` | 人 | 宾语（书面语） | The teacher **whom I respect** is kind. / The girl **to whom** I spoke is from London.（介词提前） |
| `which` | 物 | 主语/宾语 | The book **which I read** is great. / This is a problem **which** needs to be solved. |
| `that` | 人或物 | 主语/宾语（限制性定语从句中） | The car **that I bought** is red. / She is the person **that** helped me. |
| `whose` | 人或物 | 定语（所有格） | The student **whose father is a doctor** is here. / The house **whose roof is red** is mine. |

**注意**：
- `who` 在口语中也可作宾语（非正式），但正式写作中宾格用 `whom`。
- `that` 不能用于非限制性定语从句。

#### (2) 关系副词（Relative Adverbs）

| 关系副词 | 先行词 | 在从句中的成分 | 例句 |
|----------|--------|--------------|------|
| `when` | 时间名词（day, year, moment, time） | 时间状语 | I'll never forget the day **when we met**. |
| `where` | 地点名词（place, house, city, room） | 地点状语 | This is the house **where I grew up**. |
| `why` | 原因（`the reason`） | 原因状语 | Tell me the reason **why you left**. |

**关系副词 = 介词 + which/whom**：

| 关系副词 | 等价结构 | 例句（等价替换） |
|----------|----------|------------------|
| `when` | `in/on/at + which` | the day **when** we met = the day **on which** we met |
| `where` | `in/at + which` | the house **where** I grew up = the house **in which** I grew up |
| `why` | `for + which` | the reason **why** I left = the reason **for which** I left |

#### (3) 限制性定语从句 vs. 非限制性定语从句

| 类型 | 特征 | 标点 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **限制性定语从句** | 对先行词起限定作用，去掉会影响句意（无法确定是谁/什么） | 无逗号 | The students **who are lazy** will fail.（只指懒惰的学生，而非所有学生） |
| **非限制性定语从句** | 起补充说明作用，去掉不影响主干句意（先行词已经明确） | **逗号**隔开 | My brother, **who lives in London**, is a lawyer.（只有一个哥哥，补充信息） |

**非限制性定语从句不能用 `that` 引导。**

❌ `My car, **that** is very old, still runs well.`  
✅ `My car, **which** is very old, still runs well.`

**非限制性定语从句可以修饰整个主句**（`which` 指代前面整个句子）：

- `He passed the exam, **which** surprised everyone.`（他通过了考试，这让所有人惊讶）
- `The company went bankrupt, **which** we had not expected.`

#### (4) 限制性定语从句中 `that` 与 `which` 的选择

**只用 `that` 的情况**：

| 情况 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 先行词是 `all`, `everything`, `nothing`, `something`, `anything`, `the only`, `the very`, `the same`, `the last` 等 | All **that** glitters is not gold. / Everything **that** he said was true. / This is the only thing **that** matters. |
| 先行词被**最高级**或**序数词**修饰 | This is the best movie **that** I have ever seen. / She was the first person **that** arrived. |
| 先行词**既有人又有物** | The people and things **that** I miss most are in my hometown. |

**只用 `which` 的情况**：

| 情况 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| **非限制性定语从句** | The museum, **which** was built in 1800, is very famous. |
| **介词提前**（介词 + 关系代词） | The house in **which** I live is old. / The company for **which** he works is big. |

#### (5) 定语从句的省略（简化）—— 高分写作技巧

**当关系代词在从句中作宾语时，可以省略**：
- `The book (that) I read is interesting.`
- `The man (whom) you met is my uncle.`

**当关系代词作主语且从句谓语为 `be` 时，可以省略"关系代词 + be"**：
- `The man **who is** sitting there is my teacher.` → `The man **sitting** there is my teacher.`（现在分词短语作定语）
- `The book **which is** written by him is great.` → `The book **written** by him is great.`（过去分词短语作定语）
- `The students **who are** interested in music can join.` → `The students **interested** in music can join.`

### 3. 状语从句（Adverbial Clause）

在句中作状语，修饰主句的动词、形容词或整个句子，表示时间、原因、条件、目的、结果、让步、比较、方式、地点等。

#### (1) 时间状语从句（Time）

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `when` | 当……时（可表示瞬时或延续） | I was reading **when** she called. |
| `while` | 在……期间（强调持续性，常接进行时） | **While** I was sleeping, the phone rang. |
| `as` | 一边……一边……；随着（强调动作同时发生） | She sang **as** she walked. / **As** time passes, things change. |
| `before` | 在……之前 | **Before** he arrived, we had already left. |
| `after` | 在……之后 | **After** he finished his homework, he went out to play. |
| `since` | 自从（主句用现在完成时） | I have lived here **since** I was born. |
| `until / till` | 直到……才（主句动词为延续性） / 直到……为止（主句动词为短暂性） | I waited **until** he came back. / He didn't leave **until** I arrived. |
| `as soon as` | 一……就 | I'll call you **as soon as** I arrive. |
| `every time / each time` | 每次 | **Every time** I see her, she smiles. |
| `once` | 一旦 | **Once** you start, you can't stop. |
| `by the time` | 到……时候为止（主句常用过去/将来完成时） | **By the time** he arrived, we had left. |

**时态呼应**：时间状语从句中，用**一般现在时**表将来（主将从现）。

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| I will tell him when he **will come**. | I will tell him when he **comes**. |
| She will wait until you **will arrive**. | She will wait until you **arrive**. |

#### (2) 原因状语从句（Reason）

| 引导词 | 语气强度 | 使用场景 | 例句 |
|--------|----------|----------|------|
| `because` | **最强** | 直接、核心的原因（回答 `why` 提问） | I didn't go **because** I was sick.（原因是唯一且直接的） |
| `since` | 较强 | 已知的、明显的原因（"既然"） | **Since** you are here, let's start.（双方都知道你在，没必要强调） |
| `as` | 较弱 | 附带的、次要的原因，常放句首 | **As** I was tired, I went to bed early. |
| `now that` | 较新 | "既然"（强调出现了新情况） | **Now that** I have finished my homework, I can go out. |
| `in that` | 正式 | "因为"（书面语，比 `because` 正式） | This theory is important **in that** it explains many phenomena.（托福写作常用） |

**`because` vs. `since` vs. `as`**：

| 连词 | 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| `because` | I succeeded **because** I worked hard. | 最直接的原因，语气最强 |
| `since` | **Since** we have no money, we can't buy it. | 已知事实，逻辑推断 |
| `as` | **As** it was late, we went home. | 附带说明，非核心原因 |

#### (3) 条件状语从句（Condition）⭐⭐ 写作重点

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `if` | 如果 | **If** it rains, we'll stay at home. |
| `unless` | 除非（= if not） | You will fail **unless** you study hard.（= if you don't study） |
| `provided / providing that` | 只要、假如（正式） | You can go out, **provided that** you finish your homework. |
| `as long as` | 只要 | I will support you **as long as** you are honest. |
| `on condition that` | 在……条件下（正式） | I will lend you the money **on condition that** you pay it back next week. |
| `in case` | 万一、以防 | Take an umbrella **in case** it rains. |
| `only if` | 只有……才（强调） | I will come **only if** you invite me. |
| `if only` | 要是……就好了（虚拟，见虚拟语气章节） | **If only** I had known! |

**条件状语从句中的时态**：用**一般现在时**表将来（主将从现）。

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| If he **will come**, I'll be happy. | If he **comes**, I'll be happy. |
| Unless you **will study**, you will fail. | Unless you **study**, you will fail. |

#### (4) 让步状语从句（Concession）⭐⭐ 写作加分

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `although / though` | 虽然（不能与 `but` 连用） | **Although** it was raining, we went out. / **Though** he is rich, he is unhappy. |
| `even though / even if` | 即使 | **Even if** I fail, I'll try again. / **Even though** he tried hard, he didn't succeed. |
| `while` | 虽然（放句首，正式） | **While** I admit his talent, I don't like him. |
| `no matter + 疑问词` | 无论…… | **No matter what** you do, I'll support you. / **No matter how** hard it is, I'll try. |
| `疑问词 + -ever` | 无论……（比 no matter 更正式） | **Whatever** you do, I'll support you. / **However** difficult it is, I'll try. / **Wherever** you go, I'll follow. |
| `whether...or...` | 无论……还是…… | **Whether** you agree **or** not, I'm going. |
| `for all + that` | 尽管（正式） | He is a great leader, **for all that** he is not popular. |

**常见错误**：`although` 不能与 `but` 连用（中文思维干扰）。

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| **Although** he is rich, **but** he is unhappy. | **Although** he is rich, he is unhappy. |
|  — | He is rich, **but** he is unhappy. |

#### (5) 目的状语从句（Purpose）

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `so that` | 以便、为了（+ 情态动词 `can/could/will/would/may/might`） | I study hard **so that** I **can** pass the exam. / He spoke loudly **so that** everyone **could** hear him. |
| `in order that` | 为了（更正式，+ 情态动词） | We need to save money **in order that** we **may** buy a house. |
| `for fear that` | 以防、生怕（+ `should` 或虚拟） | She locked the door **for fear that** someone **should** enter. |

**`so that` vs. `in order to`**（目的状语从句 vs. 不定式）：
- `so that` 后接**从句**（有主语+谓语）
- `in order to` 后接**不定式**（无主语，主语需与主句一致）
  - `I study hard **in order to** pass the exam.`（主语 I 与主句一致）

#### (6) 结果状语从句（Result）

| 引导词 | 结构 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `so ... that` | `so + 形容词/副词 + that` | It was **so hot that** we couldn't sleep. / He ran **so fast that** no one could catch him. |
| `such ... that` | `such + 名词词组 + that` | It was **such a hot day that** everyone stayed inside. / She is **such a kind person that** everyone likes her. |

**`so` vs. `such` 的区别**：

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `so + 形容词 + (a/an) + 单数可数名词` | **so** hot **a** day（正式，少见） |
| `such + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词` | **such** a hot day（常用） |
| `so + 形容词/副词`（无名词） | **so** hot / **so** quickly |
| `so + many/few + 复数名词` | **so** many people |
| `so + much/little + 不可数名词` | **so** much water |

#### (7) 方式状语从句（Manner）

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `as` | 像……一样 | Do **as** I say, not as I do. / She runs **as** fast as a deer. |
| `as if / as though` | 好像（常用虚拟语气，见虚拟语气章节） | He spoke **as if** he **knew** everything.（与现在事实相反） / She looked **as if** she **had seen** a ghost.（与过去事实相反） |

#### (8) 比较状语从句（Comparison）

| 引导词 | 结构 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| `than` | 比较级 + than | She is taller **than** I (am). / He earns more money **than** his brother. |
| `as ... as` | 同级比较 | He is **as tall as** his brother. / She runs **as fast as** me. |
| `not so/as ... as` | 否定同级比较 | I am **not as busy as** you. / This book is **not so interesting as** that one. |

**比较状语从句的省略**：
- `She is taller than I **am**.`（`am` 可省略）
- `He earns more than **I do**.`（`do` 替代 `earn`）

## 七、非谓语动词（Non-finite Verbs）⭐⭐ 雅思托福高分核心

非谓语动词指**不定式（to do）**、**动名词（doing）** 和**分词（doing / done）**，在句中不作谓语，但可作主语、宾语、定语、状语、补语。在雅思/托福写作中，合理使用非谓语动词能**有效提升句式多样性**和**语言简洁性**。

### 1. 不定式（Infinitive）：`to + 动词原形`

#### 核心用法一览表

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **作主语** | 单个不定式作主语，常用 `It + be + adj. + to do` 结构 | **To learn English** is important. / **It is important to learn** English. |
| **作宾语** | 放在特定动词后面作宾语 | I **want to go** home. / She **decided to study** abroad. |
| **作定语** | 修饰名词，放在名词后面 | I have a lot of work **to do**. / She needs a partner **to work with**. |
| **作状语** | 表目的、原因、结果 | I came here **to see** you.（目的） / I'm glad **to meet** you.（原因） / He woke up **to find** himself alone.（结果） |
| **作补语** | 宾语补足语 | He told me **to wait**. / I expect you **to be** on time. |
| **作表语** | 放在系动词后 | His dream is **to become** a doctor. / Our goal is **to finish** the project by June. |

#### 接不定式作宾语的常见动词（列表）

| 动词 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `agree` | I **agreed to help** him. |
| `decide` | She **decided to leave** early. |
| `hope` | I **hope to see** you soon. |
| `plan` | We **plan to travel** abroad next year. |
| `refuse` | He **refused to answer** the question. |
| `want` | I **want to go** home. |
| `wish` | She **wishes to become** a doctor. |
| `expect` | I **expect to finish** it today. |
| `offer` | He **offered to drive** me home. |
| `promise` | She **promised to call** me back. |
| `manage` | We **managed to catch** the last train. |
| `fail` | He **failed to pass** the exam. |
| `pretend` | She **pretended to be** asleep. |
| `tend` | He **tends to forget** things. |
| `choose` | I **chose to stay** at home. |
| `attempt` | They **attempted to climb** the mountain. |
| `afford` | I **can't afford to buy** a new car. |

#### 不定式作状语（表目的、原因、结果）

| 功能 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| **目的状语** | I came here **to see** you. / She went to the supermarket **to buy** some food. / He woke up early **in order to catch** the first train.（更正式） |
| **原因状语**（与情感形容词连用） | I'm glad **to meet** you. / She was surprised **to hear** the news. / He is lucky **to be alive**. |
| **结果状语**（常与 `only` 连用，表意外的结果） | He hurried to the station, only **to find** the train had left. / She arrived early, only **to discover** the meeting had been cancelled. |

**不定式表目的 vs. `so that` 从句**：
- `I study hard **to pass** the exam.`（简洁，常用）
- `I study hard **in order to pass** the exam.`（更正式）
- `I study hard **so that** I **can pass** the exam.`（有主语，强调能力）

#### 不定式的省略 `to`（接动词原形）

| 情况 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| **情态动词后** | He **can speak** French. / You **must go** now. |
| **感官动词后**（see, hear, watch, feel, notice）表"看到全过程" | I **saw him cross** the street.（看到全过程） / She **heard him sing** a song. |
| **使役动词后**（make, let, have） | She **made me cry**. / He **let me go**. / I **had him carry** the bag. |
| **`help` 后**（`to` 可省略也可保留） | She **helped me (to) clean** the room. |

#### 不定式的时态和语态

| 形式 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般式（主动） | `to do` | I want **to go**. |
| 一般式（被动） | `to be done` | The work needs **to be done**. |
| 进行式 | `to be doing` | He seems **to be sleeping**. |
| 完成式（主动） | `to have done` | She seems **to have forgotten** it.（在谓语动作之前已经忘记） |
| 完成式（被动） | `to have been done` | The report appears **to have been written** carelessly. |

### 2. 动名词（Gerund）：`动词-ing`

#### 核心用法一览表

| 用法 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **作主语** | 动词作主语，视为**单数** | **Smoking** is harmful. / **Reading** is my hobby. |
| **作宾语** | 放在特定动词后面作宾语 | I enjoy **reading**. / She finished **writing** the report. |
| **作介词的宾语** | 介词后一律用动名词 | He is good at **swimming**. / I'm interested in **learning** English. / She left without **saying** goodbye. |
| **作定语** | 表示**用途**（而非动作） | a **swimming** pool（游泳池，用来游泳的池子） / a **walking** stick（拐杖） |
| **作表语** | 放在系动词后，表示"是做什么的" | My favorite hobby is **reading**. / The hardest part is **starting**. |

#### 只能接动名词作宾语的常见动词（列表）

| 动词 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `admit` | He **admitted stealing** the money. |
| `avoid` | You should **avoid making** the same mistake. |
| `consider` | I **am considering changing** my job. |
| `delay` | They **delayed** **sending** the package. |
| `deny` | She **denied having** done it. |
| `enjoy` | I **enjoy playing** football. |
| `finish` | He **finished reading** the book. |
| `keep` | She **keeps interrupting** me. |
| `mind` | Do you **mind opening** the window? |
| `miss` | I **miss living** in the countryside. |
| `practice` | She **practices playing** the piano every day. |
| `quit` | He **quit smoking** two years ago. |
| `risk` | You shouldn't **risk losing** your job. |
| `suggest` | I **suggest going** there by train. |
| `imagine` | Can you **imagine living** on Mars? |
| `appreciate` | I **appreciate your helping** me.（+ 所有格） |
| `avoid` | He **avoided answering** the question. |
| `resume` | They **resumed working** after lunch. |

#### 介词后接动名词（重点）

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `be + 形容词 + 介词 + doing` | I'm tired of **waiting**. / She is good at **cooking**. / He is interested in **learning** Chinese. |
| `be + 名词 + 介词 + doing` | I have no interest in **watching** TV. / There is no point in **arguing**. |
| `动词 + 介词 + doing` | I look forward to **hearing** from you. / He succeeded in **passing** the exam. / She insisted on **paying** the bill. |
| `介词 + doing`（无特定搭配） | I went home without **saying** goodbye. / He studied hard instead of **playing** games. |

#### 动词 + 不定式 vs. 动词 + 动名词（核心区别）

| 动词 | 接不定式 | 接动名词 |
|------|----------|----------|
| `remember` | 记得要去做（**未做**）<br>Remember to lock the door. | 记得做过（**已做**）<br>I remember locking it. |
| `forget` | 忘记要去做（**未做**）<br>Don't forget to call me. | 忘记做过（**已做**）<br>I forgot seeing him. |
| `stop` | 停下来**去做另一件事**<br>He stopped to smoke.（停止当前动作，去抽烟） | 停止**正在做的事**<br>He stopped smoking.（不再抽烟了） |
| `try` | 尝试去做（**尽力**做）<br>I tried to open the door.（尽力开） | 试着做（**方法/实验**）<br>Try adding some salt.（试试这个方法） |
| `regret` | 遗憾要去做（**将来**）<br>I regret to inform you...（遗憾地通知） | 后悔做过（**过去**）<br>I regret telling her. |
| `go on` | 接着做**另一件不同的事**<br>He went on to talk about physics.（转而谈物理） | 接着做**同一件事**<br>He went on talking.（继续说话） |
| `mean` | 打算做<br>I mean to help you.（我打算帮你） | 意味着<br>This means losing weight.（这意味会减重） |
| `like / love / hate / prefer` | 特定一次的行为 | 一般性的喜好 |
| `I like to swim today.`（今天想游） | I like swimming.（喜欢游泳这项活动） |

#### 动名词的时态和语态

| 形式 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般式（主动） | `doing` | I enjoy **reading**. |
| 一般式（被动） | `being done` | He hates **being laughed at**. / I don't like **being told** what to do. |
| 完成式（主动） | `having done` | He admitted **having stolen** the money. |
| 完成式（被动） | `having been done` | She denied **having been involved** in the incident. |

### 3. 分词（Participle）：`现在分词（doing）` 和 `过去分词（done）`

#### 核心用法一览表

| 用法 | 现在分词（doing） | 过去分词（done） | 例句 |
|------|-------------------|------------------|------|
| **作定语** | 表**主动/进行** | 表**被动/完成** | a **developing** country（发展中国家） / a **developed** country（发达国家） / **boiling** water（沸腾的水） / **boiled** water（烧开的水） |
| **作状语** | 表**主动**，逻辑主语与主句主语一致 | 表**被动**，逻辑主语与主句主语一致 | **Seeing** the police, he ran away. / **Given** more time, I could do better. |
| **作补语** | 表"正在进行" | 表"被动/状态" | I saw him **crossing** the street.（正在过马路） / I want it **done** immediately. |
| **作表语** | "令人……"（修饰物） | "感到……"（修饰人） | The story is **interesting**. / I am **interested** in it. |

#### 现在分词 vs. 过去分词作定语的详细对比

| 现在分词（doing） | 过去分词（done） |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| **主动**关系（动作的发出者） | **被动**关系（动作的承受者） |
| **进行**意义（正在做） | **完成**意义（已经被做） |
| a **developing** country（在发展中的国家） | a **developed** country（已发展国家） |
| **boiling** water（沸腾的水） | **boiled** water（烧开过的水） |
| a **falling** leaf（正在飘落的叶子） | a **fallen** leaf（已经落在地上的叶子） |
| the **changing** world（变化中的世界） | the **changed** world（变化后的世界） |
| **exciting** news（令人兴奋的消息） | **excited** people（感到兴奋的人们） |

#### 分词短语作定语（相当于一个定语从句的简化）

| 定语从句 | 分词短语简化 | 说明 |
|----------|--------------|------|
| The man **who is sitting** there is my uncle. | The man **sitting** there is my uncle. | 去掉 who is |
| The book **which is written** by him is great. | The book **written** by him is great. | 去掉 which is |
| The students **who are interested** in music can join. | The students **interested** in music can join. | 去掉 who are |
| The girl **who is wearing** a red dress is my sister. | The girl **wearing** a red dress is my sister. | 去掉 who is |

#### 分词短语作状语（相当于一个状语从句的简化）

| 状语从句 | 分词短语简化 | 说明 |
|----------|--------------|------|
| **Because I felt** tired, I went to bed early. | **Feeling** tired, I went to bed early. | 原因状语（主动） |
| **After he had finished** his homework, he went out. | **Having finished** his homework, he went out. | 时间状语（完成式） |
| **Because it was** written in simple English, the book is easy to read. | **Written** in simple English, the book is easy to read. | 原因状语（被动） |
| **When he was** asked about the incident, he remained silent. | **Asked** about the incident, he remained silent. | 时间状语（被动） |
| **Although he was** exhausted, he continued to work. | **Exhausted**, he continued to work. | 让步状语（被动） |

#### 独立主格结构（Nominative Absolute Construction）

当分词的逻辑主语与主句主语**不一致**时，需要在分词前保留自己的主语，形成"名词/代词 + 分词"结构。

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Weather permitting**, we'll go out. | "天气允许的话"——weather 是 permitting 的逻辑主语 |
| **All things considered**, his plan is better. | "综合考虑所有因素"——things 是 considered 的逻辑主语 |
| **The work finished**, we went home. | "工作完成后"——the work 是 finished 的逻辑主语 |
| **She sat there, her eyes closed**. | 她坐在那里，眼睛闭着——her eyes 是 closed 的逻辑主语 |

**独立主格结构的常用固定搭配**：
- `generally speaking`（一般来说）—— 固定用法，不加独立主语
- `strictly speaking`（严格来说）
- `judging from / by`（根据……判断）

#### 垂悬分词错误（Dangling Modifier）—— 写作中必须避免

**规则**：分词作状语时，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致。

| 错误（垂悬） | 原因 | 正确 |
|-------------|------|------|
| **Walking** along the street, **a dog bit me**. | 狗在走路？逻辑主语应为"我" | **Walking** along the street, **I was bitten by a dog**. |
| **Being** a student, **the exam was difficult**. | 考试是学生？ | **Being** a student, **I found** the exam difficult. |
| **Having finished** the homework, **the TV was turned on**. | 电视完成了作业？ | **Having finished** the homework, **I turned on** the TV. |

## 八、主谓一致（Subject-Verb Agreement）

主谓一致是雅思/托福写作中的**基础准确性考点**，错误会直接影响语法评分。

### 核心规则

| 规则 | 详细说明 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **主语为单数，谓语用单数；主语为复数，谓语用复数** | 最基本规则 | He **is** a teacher. / They **are** teachers. / She **works** hard. / They **work** hard. |
| **并列主语（`and`）通常接复数谓语** | 两个或以上并列主语 | Tom and Jerry **are** friends. / The teacher and the student **have** arrived. |
| **`with`, `together with`, `along with`, `as well as`, `including`, `in addition to` 等连接的附加成分不影响谓语** | 谓语与**前面的主语**一致，而不是与附加成分 | The teacher, together with the students, **is** going.（主语是 teacher） / The manager, as well as his assistants, **was** present. |
| **`either...or` / `neither...nor` / `not only...but also`** | 遵循**就近原则**（谓语与最近的主语一致） | Neither the teacher nor the students **are** here. / Not only the students but also the teacher **is** here. |
| **不定代词（everyone, nobody, each, either, neither, someone, anybody, nothing）** | 接**单数谓语** | Everyone **is** here. / Nobody **knows** the truth. / Each of them **has** a car. |
| **集体名词（family, team, audience, committee, staff, government, class, group）** | 强调**整体**用单数；强调**个体成员**用复数 | My family **is** large.（整体） / My family **are** watching TV.（个体） / The team **is** the best.（整体） / The team **are** arguing.（成员在争吵） |
| **以 `-s` 结尾的学科名词（physics, mathematics, economics, statistics）** | 视为**单数** | Physics **is** my favorite subject. / Economics **deals** with the allocation of resources. |
| **以 `-s` 结尾的疾病名词（measles, mumps）** | 视为**单数** | Measles **is** a contagious disease. |
| **时间、距离、金额、数量作整体** | 视为**单数** | Two thousand dollars **is** a lot of money. / Ten years **is** a long time. / 100 kilometers **is** a long distance. |
| **`the + 形容词` 表示一类人** | 视为**复数** | The rich **are** getting richer. / The elderly **need** more care. |
| **`there be` 句型** | 遵循**就近原则** | There **is** a book and two pens on the desk. / There **are** two pens and a book on the desk. |
| **`the number of` + 复数名词** | "……的数量"，接**单数谓语** | The number of students **is** increasing. |
| **`a number of` + 复数名词** | "许多"，接**复数谓语** | A number of students **are** waiting outside. |

## 九、冠词（Articles）—— `a/an`, `the`, 零冠词

冠词是雅思/托福写作中的高频失分点，尤其是在学术写作中。

### 核心用法

| 冠词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **`a`**（辅音音素前） / **`an`**（元音音素前） | **泛指**，首次提及，表示"某一个/类" | I saw **a** cat. / He is **an** engineer. / **A** dog is a loyal animal.（泛指狗这类动物） |
| **`the`** | **特指**，再次提及，双方都知道的唯一事物，最高级/序数词前，独一无二的事物 | The cat is black.（前面提到过的那只猫） / **The** sun rises in the east.（唯一） / She is **the** best.（最高级） / **The** first time（序数词） |
| **零冠词（不填）** | 复数泛指，抽象名词（不可数），专有名词，三餐，学科，球类，语言，月份/星期 | **Cats** are cute.（泛指猫） / I like **music**.（泛指音乐） / I have **lunch** at 12. / I play **basketball**. / She speaks **English**. / I was born in **June**. / See you on **Monday**. |

### `a/an` 的选择（根据发音而非字母）

| 字母 | 发音 | 冠词 |
|------|------|------|
| `a` | /eɪ/（元音音素开头） | **an** apple |
| `h` 不发音（hour, honest, honor） | /aʊ/ /ɒ/（元音音素开头） | **an** hour / **an** honest man |
| `u` 读作 /juː/（辅音音素开头） | /juː/（以辅音 /j/ 开头） | **a** university / **a** useful tool |
| `h` 发音（house, hotel） | /h/（辅音音素开头） | **a** house / **a** hotel（注意：美式 `a hotel`，英式可读 `an hotel` 但少见） |
| `F` 等字母 | /ɛf/（元音音素开头） | **an** F / **an** MA degree |

### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| I went to **the** China. | 专有名词（国名）通常不加冠词 | I went to **China**. |
| She is **the** nurse.（泛指） | 泛指职业用 `a/an` | She is **a** nurse. |
| I like **the** music.（泛指音乐） | 泛指抽象名词不加冠词 | I like **music**. |
| She is **an** university student. | `university` 以辅音音素开头 | She is **a** university student. |
| He is **a** honest man. | `honest` 的 `h` 不发音，元音音素开头 | He is **an** honest man. |
| The water in this glass is dirty.（特指） | `the` 用在特指语境中是正确的 | ✅ The water in this glass is dirty.（玻璃杯里的水，特指） |
| Water is essential for life.（泛指） | 零冠词 | ✅ Water is essential for life. |

## 十、介词（Prepositions）—— 时间、地点、固定搭配

### 时间介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `at` | 具体时刻、节日、用餐时间 | at 5 PM / at midnight / at Christmas / at lunch |
| `on` | 具体某一天、日期、星期几、节日（带 day） | on Monday / on July 1st / on New Year's Day / on my birthday |
| `in` | 月份、年份、季节、一天中的时段（morning/afternoon/evening）、世纪 | in 2024 / in summer / in the morning / in the 21st century |
| `since` | 从某个时间点开始（现在完成时） | since 2010 / since Monday / since I was a child |
| `for` | 持续一段时间 | for 5 years / for 3 hours / for a long time |
| `by` | 到……之前（不晚于） | by 5 PM / by tomorrow / by the end of this month |
| `until / till` | 直到……（某事持续到某时） | I waited until 6 PM. / I slept till 9 AM. |
| `during` | 在……期间 | during the summer / during the meeting |
| `over` | 在……期间（强调跨越整个时间段） | over the past decade / over the last three years |
| `within` | 在……之内（不超过） | within 2 hours / within a week |

### 地点介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `at` | 具体小地点、点、位置 | at the station / at the door / at the bus stop / at the corner |
| `in` | 大区域、容器、三维空间内 | in London / in the box / in the room / in the ocean |
| `on` | 表面、街道、楼层 | on the table / on Main Street / on the second floor / on the wall |
| `above` | 在上方（不接触，不一定垂直） | The painting is above the sofa. |
| `over` | 正上方（垂直，不接触） | The plane flew over the city. |
| `below` | 在下方（不接触） | The temperature is below zero. |
| `under` | 在正下方（不接触） | The cat is under the table. |
| `between` | 两者之间 | between you and me / between the two buildings |
| `among` | 三者或以上之间 | among the students / among the trees |
| `into` | 进入（从外部到内部） | He walked into the room. |
| `onto` | 到……上面（到表面） | She jumped onto the stage. |
| `through` | 穿过（内部） | The train went through the tunnel. |
| `across` | 穿过（表面/横跨） | He swam across the river. |
| `beside` / `next to` | 在旁边 | beside the bed / next to the window |
| `near` | 在附近（距离较近） | near the park |
| `behind` | 在……后面 | behind the door |
| `in front of` | 在……前面（外部） | in front of the house |
| `in the front of` | 在……前部（内部） | in the front of the car（汽车的前排） |

### 常见介词固定搭配（高频考点）

#### 动词 + 介词

| 动词短语 | 例句 |
|----------|------|
| depend **on** | Success depends **on** hard work. / I depend **on** my parents. |
| rely **on** | You can rely **on** him. |
| apply **for** | She applied **for** a job. |
| apply **to**（适用于） | This rule applies **to** everyone. |
| wait **for** | I'm waiting **for** the bus. |
| look **at** | Look **at** this picture. |
| look **for**（寻找） | I'm looking **for** my keys. |
| look **after**（照顾） | She looks **after** her grandmother. |
| belong **to** | This book belongs **to** me. |
| listen **to** | Listen **to** the music. |
| talk **to** / talk **with** | I talked **to** her yesterday. |
| speak **to** / speak **with** | Can I speak **to** the manager? |
| pay **for** | I paid **for** the meal. |
| spend **on** | I spent $100 **on** this book. |
| worry **about** | Don't worry **about** me. |
| care **about**（在乎） | I don't care **about** money. |
| care **for**（照顾/喜欢） | She cares **for** her sick mother. / I don't care **for** him. |
| agree **with**（同意某人/观点） | I agree **with** you. |
| agree **to**（同意某事/计划） | She agreed **to** the proposal. |
| agree **on**（在某事上达成一致） | We agreed **on** the date. |
| result **from**（由……引起） | His illness resulted **from** stress. |
| result **in**（导致） | The accident resulted **in** his death. |
| suffer **from** | She suffers **from** headaches. |

## 十一、比较级与最高级（Comparatives & Superlatives）

### 变化规则一览表

| 类型 | 规则 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| 单音节词 | 加 `-er / -est` | tall → taller | tall → tallest |
| 以 `-e` 结尾 | 加 `-r / -st` | nice → nicer | nice → nicest |
| 以辅音 + `y` 结尾 | 变 `y` 为 `i` 加 `-er / -est` | happy → happier | happy → happiest |
| 重读闭音节（辅元辅） | 双写尾字母 + `-er / -est` | big → bigger | big → biggest |
| 多音节词（3 个及以上音节） | `more / most + 原级` | beautiful → more beautiful | beautiful → most beautiful |
| 部分双音节词（以 `-ly`, `-ful`, `-less` 结尾等） | `more / most + 原级` | careful → more careful | careful → most careful |
| 双音节词（以 `-y` 结尾） | 变 `y` 为 `i` 加 `-er / -est` | happy → happier | happy → happiest |
| 不规则 | 特殊记忆 | good → better | good → best |
| 不规则 | 特殊记忆 | bad → worse | bad → worst |
| 不规则 | 特殊记忆 | far → farther/further | far → farthest/furthest |
| 不规则 | 特殊记忆 | little → less | little → least |
| 不规则 | 特殊记忆 | many/much → more | many/much → most |

### 比较级常见句型

| 句型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `A + be + 比较级 + than + B` | She is **taller than** me. / This problem is **more difficult than** the previous one. |
| `A + 动词 + 比较级 + than + B` | He runs **faster than** me. / She works **harder than** her brother. |
| `A + be + as + 原级 + as + B`（同级比较） | He is **as tall as** his brother. / This book is **as interesting as** that one. |
| `A + be + not + so/as + 原级 + as + B`（否定同级比较） | I am **not as busy as** you. / This movie is **not so good as** that one. |
| `the + 比较级, the + 比较级`（"越……越……"） | **The more** you practice, **the better** you become. / **The harder** you work, **the more** you will achieve. |
| `比较级 + and + 比较级`（"越来越……"） | It is **getting hotter and hotter**. / The problem is **becoming more and more serious**. |
| `the + 比较级 + of the two`（"两个中较……的那个"） | She is **the taller of the two**. / This is **the more expensive of the two**. |

### 最高级用法

**结构**：`the + 最高级 + 范围（in / of / among）`

| 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| She is the **tallest** in her class. | 同类范围内（in + 单数/群体） |
| This is the most expensive car **of all**. | 所有同类中（of + 复数/群体） |
| He is the best student **among the applicants**. | 三者及以上之间（among + 复数） |
| This is **the most** interesting book I have **ever read**. | 与"ever"连用，表示"有史以来最……" |

### 常见错误

| 错误 | 原因 | 正确 |
|------|------|------|
| She is **more taller** than me. | 双重比较，不能同时用两种比较形式 | She is **taller** than me. |
| This is **the most easiest** question. | 双重比较 | This is **the easiest** question. |
| He is **more happy** than me. | 单音节词应用 `-er` | He is **happier** than me. |
| She is **beautifuller** than her sister. | 多音节词应用 `more` | She is **more beautiful** than her sister. |

## 十二、平行结构（Parallel Structure）⭐⭐ 托福写作高度重视

在列举、比较或对比时，并列的成分必须在**语法形式上保持一致**（名词对名词，动词对动词，不定式对不定式，形容词对形容词等）。

### 核心规则与对比

| 错误（非平行） | 原因 | 正确（平行） |
|---------------|------|-------------|
| I like **swimming**, **to run**, and **dancing**. | 动词形式混用 | I like **swimming**, **running**, and **dancing**. |
| I like **to swim**, **to run**, and **dancing**. | 动词形式混用 | I like **to swim**, **to run**, and **to dance**. |
| She is smart, beautiful, and **has kindness**. | 形容词 + 动词短语混用 | She is smart, beautiful, and **kind**. |
| He wanted **to study** and **getting** a degree. | 不定式 + 动名词混用 | He wanted **to study** and **to get** a degree. |
| The government should reduce taxes, improve education, and **to create** jobs. | 动词语态混用 | The government should reduce taxes, improve education, and **create** jobs. |
| I prefer **reading** to **watch** TV. | 介词后应用动名词 | I prefer **reading** to **watching** TV. |

### 平行结构标志词

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| `and` / `or` / `but` | She is **kind, generous, and hardworking**. / I want **to go, to see, and to learn**. |
| `both...and...` | She is **both** smart **and** beautiful. |
| `either...or...` | You can **either** stay **or** leave. |
| `neither...nor...` | He is **neither** rich **nor** famous. |
| `not only...but also...` | She is **not only** intelligent **but also** hardworking. / He **not only** passed the exam **but also** got the highest score. |
| `more than` / `as much as` | She is **more than** a teacher; she is a mentor. |

## 十三、强调与倒装（Emphasis & Inversion）⭐⭐ 雅思阅读高频

### 1. 强调句（Emphatic Structure）

**结构**：`It is / was + 被强调部分 + that / who + 其他部分`

| 原句 | 强调句 |
|------|--------|
| I met him in the park yesterday. | **It was I** that met him in the park yesterday.（强调主语） |
| I met him in the park yesterday. | **It was him** that I met in the park yesterday.（强调宾语） |
| I met him in the park yesterday. | **It was in the park** that I met him yesterday.（强调地点状语） |
| I met him in the park yesterday. | **It was yesterday** that I met him in the park.（强调时间状语） |
| He broke the window. | **It was he who** broke the window.（强调人可用 who） |

**注意**：强调人时可用 `who`，强调其他（物、时间、地点、原因）一律用 `that`。

### 2. 倒装句（Inversion）

#### 部分倒装（Partial Inversion）—— 助动词/情态动词提前到主语之前

**结构**：`否定词/特殊结构 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词原形/其他`

**触发部分倒装的结构**：

| 结构 | 例句 | 正常语序 |
|------|------|----------|
| **否定词放句首**（never, hardly, seldom, rarely, little, nowhere, not, no） | **Never** have I seen such a beautiful sunset. | I have never seen such a beautiful sunset. |
| **否定副词短语放句首** | **Hardly** had I arrived when the meeting started. | I had hardly arrived when... |
| **`not only...but also...`** | **Not only** did he pass the exam, **but also** he got the highest score. | He not only passed... |
| **`not until...`**（直到……才） | **Not until** I got home did I realize I had lost my key. | I didn't realize... until I got home. |
| **`no sooner...than...`**（一……就……） | **No sooner** had I entered the room **than** the phone rang. | I had no sooner entered the room than... |
| **`only + 状语`**（时间/方式/条件/介词短语）放句首 | **Only then** did I realize the truth. / **Only by studying** can you succeed. / **Only when** he arrived did I understand. | I only realized the truth then. / You can succeed only by studying. |
| **`so / such ... that...`** 中的 `so / such` 放句首 | **So** difficult **was** the exam that few students passed. | The exam was so difficult that... |
| **`neither / nor`** 放句首（表示"也不"） | I don't like it. **Neither** does he. / She can't come. **Nor** can I. | — |
| **虚拟条件句省略 `if`**（`had`, `were`, `should` 提前） | **Had** I known, I would have come.（= If I had known） / **Were** I you, I would go.（= If I were you） / **Should** he come, please tell him.（= If he should come） | — |

#### 全部倒装（Full Inversion）—— 谓语动词直接提前到主语之前

**结构**：`地点状语/方位词/表语 + 谓语动词 + 主语`

| 触发条件 | 例句 | 正常语序 |
|----------|------|----------|
| **地点状语/方位词放句首**（here, there, in, out, up, down, away, off, on the wall, in the corner），谓语为 `come`, `go`, `stand`, `lie`, `live`, `exist`, `sit` 等不及物动词 | **Here comes** the bus. / **In the corner stood** a tall tree. / **There goes** the bell. / **On the hill sits** a beautiful castle. | The bus comes here. / A tall tree stood in the corner. |
| **表语（形容词、分词、介词短语）放句首**，强调状态 | **Seated** in the front row **was** the famous professor. / **Happy** are those who are content. / **In the center of the room stood** a large table. | The famous professor was seated in the front row. |

**注意**：如果主语是代词，则不用倒装。
- `Here **he comes**.`（主语是代词 he，不倒装）
- `Here **comes the bus**.`（主语是名词 the bus，倒装）

## 十四、引语（Reported Speech / Indirect Speech）

### 时态变化规则

| 直接引语（Direct Speech） | 间接引语（Reported Speech） | 变化规则 |
|--------------------------|----------------------------|----------|
| He said, "I **am** tired." | He said that he **was** tired. | 一般现在时 → 一般过去时 |
| She said, "I **have finished**." | She said that she **had finished**. | 现在完成时 → 过去完成时 |
| He said, "I **will** come." | He said that he **would** come. | will → would |
| She said, "I **can** swim." | She said that she **could** swim. | can → could |
| He said, "I **may** come." | He said that he **might** come. | may → might |
| She said, "I **am studying**." | She said that she **was studying**. | 现在进行时 → 过去进行时 |
| He said, "I **went** home." | He said that he **had gone** home. | 一般过去时 → 过去完成时 |
| She said, "I **had finished**." | She said that she **had finished**. | 过去完成时不变 |
| He said, "I would like to go." | He said that he would like to go. | 不变 |

### 代词和状语的变化

| 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|----------|----------|
| I / me / my | he / him / his（根据说话人） |
| you / your | I / me / my / we / our（根据听话人） |
| here | there |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| ago | before |
| this | that |
| these | those |

### 特殊规则

**真理和客观事实**在间接引语中时态**不变**：
- 直接：`He said, "The Earth revolves around the sun."`
- 间接：`He said that the Earth **revolves** around the sun.`

**祈使句**变为间接引语时，用 `tell / ask / order + 宾语 + (not) + to do`：
- 直接：`She said, "Please help me."`
- 间接：`She asked me **to help** her.`
- 直接：`He said, "Don't be late."`
- 间接：`He told me **not to be late**.`

## 十五、标点符号（Punctuation）—— 写作中常忽略的重点

| 符号 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `.` 句号 | 结束陈述句 | I am a student. |
| `,` 逗号 | ①列举三项及以上 ②分隔状语从句（从句在前时） ③非限制性定语从句 ④插入语 ⑤连接副词后（however, therefore, moreover） | I like apples, oranges, and bananas. / When I arrived, he had already left. / My brother, who lives in London, is a doctor. / However, I don't agree. |
| `;` 分号 | 连接两个关系密切的独立分句（不用连词） | I love reading; my brother prefers watching TV. / She is very hardworking; she never gives up. |
| `:` 冒号 | 引出解释、列举、总结 | I have three hobbies: reading, writing, and drawing. / The reason is simple: we don't have enough time. |
| `'` 撇号 | ①所有格 ②缩写 | This is John's book. / It's raining. / The students' dormitory.（复数所有格） |
| `?` 问号 | 疑问句 | Do you like it? |
| `!` 感叹号 | 感叹句（学术写作中尽量避免） | How beautiful! |

### 常见标点错误

| 错误（逗号拼接 Comma Splice） | 正确 |
|------------------------------|------|
| I am tired, I want to sleep. | I am tired, **so** I want to sleep.（加连词） |
|  — | I am tired; I want to sleep.（分号） |
|  — | I am tired. I want to sleep.（句号） |

| 错误（滥用逗号） | 正确 |
|------------------|------|
| She is, a doctor. | She is a doctor.（主语和谓语之间不加逗号） |
| The teacher, who is from the US gave us a lecture.（缺少逗号） | The teacher, who is from the US, gave us a lecture.（非限制性定语从句需成对逗号） |

## 十六、雅思与托福语法侧重点总结

| 语法点 | 雅思（IELTS）侧重 | 托福（TOEFL）侧重 |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **时态** | 口语/写作中准确切换，尤其现在完成时和过去完成时 | 阅读中理解时间逻辑，写作中学术语境使用 |
| **虚拟语气** | 口语表达愿望/建议，写作表达假设场景 | 写作中表达建议、必要条件（+ 正式写作） |
| **三大从句** | 写作中增加句式复杂性，口语中自然使用 | 阅读/听力中处理长难句，写作中构建学术句型 |
| **非谓语动词** | 写作中简化句子，口语中流利表达 | 阅读中识别，写作中精炼学术表达 |
| **平行结构** | 写作中保持句式优美（较重视） | **托福写作重点评分项** |
| **倒装/强调** | 阅读中识别，写作中偶尔使用（加分项） | 阅读中高频出现，写作中可适当使用 |
| **主谓一致/冠词/介词** | **基础准确性**（扣分重灾区） | **基础准确性**（扣分重灾区） |
| **被动语态** | Task 1 流程图/地图题高频 | 综合写作（实验/学术报告）高频 |
| **比较结构** | 写作 Task 1 图表描述高频 | 写作中对比分析 |

## 十七、备考核心建议

1. **先打好简单句的地基**：确保能写出 `S + V + O` 的正确结构，再尝试扩展为复合句。

2. **以读促写**：每天精读一篇雅思/托福范文或阅读文章，划出所有复杂句，分析句子结构（主谓宾、从句类型、非谓语动词）。

3. **限时写作+自查清单**：完成作文后，专门留出 5 分钟检查以下项目：
   - [ ] 谓语动词时态是否一致？
   - [ ] 主谓是否一致？
   - [ ] 冠词（a/an/the）是否用对？
   - [ ] 介词搭配是否正确？
   - [ ] 并列结构是否平行？
   - [ ] 标点（尤其是逗号和句号）是否正确？

4. **口语练习录音回听**：口语中语法的准确性容易被忽视，录音回听能发现时态跳跃（一会过去一会现在）、主谓不一致、`he/she` 混淆等问题。

5. **建立语法错题集**：记录每次练习中发现的语法错误，按类型（时态/非谓语/从句/介词/冠词）分类，每周集中攻克一种类型。

6. **利用范文进行改写练习**：
   - 找到一篇高分范文，把其中的复合句改写为简单句（练简化）。
   - 再把简单句合并为复合句（练复杂性）。
   - 这样能快速提升对语法结构的掌控能力。

7. **托福考生特别关注**：托福写作的 E-rater（机器评分系统）对**语法准确性**和**句式复杂性**非常敏感，确保每段至少有一个状语从句+一个定语从句+一个非谓语动词结构。

8. **雅思考生特别关注**：雅思写作 Task 1 图表题中，**时态**（过去/现在/将来/完成时）的准确切换是评分关键；Task 2 中，**虚拟语气**和**情态动词 + 完成时**的表达能有效提升语法分数。

---

> 💡 **最后记住**：语法不是语言的天花板，而是你表达思想的脚手架。掌握得越扎实，你搭建的思想之楼就越高、越稳。每天精进一个知识点，三个月后你会在考场上发现——那些曾经让你头疼的长难句，已经变成了你笔下自然的表达。
