## 📄 文章 11：学生租房经历

### 🔖 英文原文

> When I arrived in London for my postgraduate studies, **securing accommodation** was my first **ordeal**. After **browsing** numerous online listings, I arranged **viewings** for several flats.
>
> The first had a spacious living room but the kitchen appliances were **archaic**, and the landlord insisted on a six-month **lease** with a **hefty deposit** equivalent to two months' rent. The second, located in a quieter suburb, offered **furnished** rooms with **utilities** included in the rent—but the **commute** to campus was over an hour by tube.
>
> Eventually, I **settled on** a studio apartment near the university, despite the rent being slightly above my budget. The letting **agent** was **amicable** and explained the **tenancy** agreement thoroughly, highlighting **clauses** about maintenance responsibilities and **renewal** options. I submitted my **references** and signed the contract, paying the first month's rent and the agency fee.
>
> Moving in was chaotic, but now I enjoy the convenience of walking to lectures and having a cozy study corner.

---

### 🌐 中文翻译

当我到达伦敦攻读研究生时，找到住处是我的第一场考验。在浏览了众多在线房源后，我安排了几次看房。第一间有一个宽敞的客厅，但厨房电器老旧，房东坚持要六个月的租约和相当于两个月租金的高额押金。第二间位于较安静的郊区，提供带家具的房间且租金包含水电费——但坐地铁到校园要一个多小时。最后，我选择了一间靠近大学的工作室公寓，尽管租金略高于我的预算。租房中介很友善，并详细解释了租赁协议，强调了有关维护责任和续约选项的条款。我提交了推荐信并签署了合同，支付了第一个月的租金和中介费。搬进去时很混乱，但现在我享受步行去上课的便利，并有一个舒适的学习角落。

---

### 📖 内容解说

本文记叙学生找房全过程，结构按时间顺序：寻找（browsing）→ 看房（viewings）→ 比较（first... second... eventually）→ 签约（submitted, signed）→ 入住。学习用过去时叙述经历，掌握租房场景词。

## 📄 文章 12：大学图书馆指南

### 🔖 英文原文

> Welcome to the university central library. This **facility** **operates** 24/7 during exam periods, but during term time, opening hours are from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. Your student ID card serves as your library card; you can borrow up to 20 **items** simultaneously, including books, **journals**, and DVDs.
>
> The **loan** period is four weeks for general books, but high-demand textbooks are **restricted** to a two-day short loan, with a hefty **overdue** fine of £1 per day. You can **renew** items online up to three times unless someone has **reserved** them.
>
> For research, the **repository** of electronic **databases** provides access to thousands of **peer-reviewed** articles. If you need assistance, the information desk is staffed from 9 to 5. Additionally, the library offers group study rooms that can be booked via the online **portal**, and a silent reading area on the fourth floor.
>
> Remember to switch off mobile phones in quiet zones. For any lost items, **report** them to the **circulation** desk immediately.

---

### 🌐 中文翻译

欢迎来到大学中心图书馆。该馆在考试期间24/7开放，但学期内开放时间为上午8点至晚上10点。你的学生证就是借书证；你可以同时借阅最多20件物品，包括书籍、期刊和DVD。普通书籍的借阅期为四周，但高需求教科书限制为两天的短借，逾期罚款为每天1英镑。你可以在线续借最多三次，除非有人预订了它们。对于研究，电子数据库资料库提供成千上万篇同行评审文章的访问。如需帮助，咨询台在9点到5点有人值守。此外，图书馆提供小组学习室，可通过在线门户预订，四楼有安静阅读区。请记住在安静区域关闭手机。如有物品遗失，立即向借阅台报告。

---

### 📖 内容解说

本文为新生图书馆介绍，属于说明文。结构按功能分区：开放时间→借阅权限→借阅期限→续借与预订→电子资源→服务台→学习室→注意事项。熟悉图书馆场景高频词。

## 📄 文章 13：机场与航班旅行

### 🔖 英文原文

> My flight to New York was **scheduled** to **depart** at 10:30 a.m., so I arrived at the airport three hours early to allow ample time for **check-in** and security. After **queuing** at the check-in counter, the agent informed me that my economy class seat had been **upgraded** to **premium** economy—a pleasant surprise!
>
> I then proceeded to the departure **lounge**, where I passed through passport control and the security **screening**, removing my laptop and liquids from my **carry-on**. The flight was announced with a twenty-minute delay due to air traffic **congestion**, but eventually we **boarded** via the **jet bridge**.
>
> During the seven-hour flight, the **crew** served meals and **beverages**, and the in-flight entertainment system offered a wide selection of movies. Upon arrival at JFK, I collected my checked luggage from the **carousel** and cleared **customs**, where the officer asked a few routine questions about the purpose of my visit.
>
> I then took a **shuttle** to the hotel, feeling both exhausted and excited.

---

### 🌐 中文翻译

我飞往纽约的航班计划于上午10:30起飞，所以我提前三小时到达机场，为值机和安检留出充足时间。在值机柜台排队后，工作人员告知我的经济舱座位已升级为豪华经济舱——一个惊喜！然后我前往候机区，通过护照检查和安检，从手提行李中取出笔记本电脑和液体。由于空中交通拥堵，航班通知延误二十分钟，但最终我们通过登机桥登机。在七小时的飞行中，机组提供餐食和饮料，机上娱乐系统提供了丰富的电影选择。到达肯尼迪机场后，我从行李转盘取回托运的行李并过海关，官员就我此行的目的问了几个常规问题。然后我乘接驳车去了酒店，感到既疲惫又兴奋。

---

## 📄 文章 14：社区诊所就诊

### 🔖 英文原文

> I visited the local community **clinic** this morning because I had been suffering from a **persistent** cough and mild fever for three days. After **registering** at the reception, I waited for about fifteen minutes before a nurse called me into the **consultation** room.
>
> She took my temperature and blood pressure, and inquired about my **symptoms** and medication history. Then the general practitioner examined my throat and chest, and **diagnosed** me with a viral respiratory infection. He **prescribed** a course of **antibiotics**, although he mentioned that it might be **viral**, so antibiotics would only help if there was a secondary **bacterial** infection.
>
> He also recommended **over-the-counter** cough **syrup** for symptomatic relief. I took the prescription to the **pharmacy** next door, where the **pharmacist** dispensed the medication and explained the **dosage** and potential side effects—mainly **drowsiness**.
>
> I was advised to drink plenty of fluids, rest, and return if my condition worsened. The entire visit cost only a **nominal** fee due to the public health coverage.

---

### 🌐 中文翻译

我今天早上去了本地社区诊所，因为我持续咳嗽和轻微发烧已经三天了。在接待处登记后，我等了大约十五分钟，然后护士叫我进诊室。她量了我的体温和血压，询问了我的症状和用药史。然后全科医生检查了我的喉咙和胸部，诊断我为病毒性呼吸道感染。他开了一个疗程的抗生素，不过他提到可能是病毒性的，所以抗生素只有在继发细菌感染时才有效。他还推荐了非处方止咳糖浆以缓解症状。我把处方带到隔壁的药房，药剂师配了药并解释了剂量和可能的副作用——主要是嗜睡。我被建议多喝水、休息，如果病情恶化就回来。由于公共医疗保障，整个就诊只花了象征性的费用。

---

### 📖 内容解说

本文记录一次门诊经历，属医疗场景。结构：症状→登记→护士检查→医生诊断→开药→药房取药→医嘱→费用。掌握就医高频词。

## 📄 文章 15：银行开户与理财

### 🔖 英文原文

> As an international student, opening a bank account was essential for managing my **finances**. I chose a high-street bank that offered a student **current account** with no monthly **maintenance fees** and an interest-free **overdraft** of up to £1,000.
>
> I booked an **appointment** and visited the **branch** with my passport, student ID, and proof of address. The personal banker guided me through the application, explaining the terms and conditions, including daily **withdrawal** limits, foreign **transaction** charges, and the online banking facility.
>
> I also set up a savings account to **deposit** my **surplus** funds, earning a modest **interest rate**. The banker recommended a credit card to build my **credit history**, but warned about the high APR if I failed to repay on time. I opted for a **debit card** instead, which **deducts** money directly from my current account.
>
> After **activating** the mobile app, I could instantly check my **balance**, **transfer** money to friends, and pay bills. The whole process took about forty minutes, and I left the bank feeling financially organised.

---

### 🌐 中文翻译

作为一名国际学生，开立银行账户对管理我的财务至关重要。我选择了一家提供学生活期账户的高街银行，无月管理费，且有一千英镑的免息透支额。我预约了时间，带着护照、学生证和地址证明去了分行。个人理财顾问引导我完成申请，解释了条款和条件，包括每日取款限额、境外交易费和网上银行服务。我还开了一个储蓄账户，存入多余资金，赚取适中的利率。理财顾问推荐了信用卡来建立我的信用记录，但警告如果未能按时还款，年利率会很高。我选择了借记卡，它直接从我的活期账户扣款。激活手机应用程序后，我可以立即查看余额、转账给朋友和支付账单。整个过程大约用了四十分钟，我离开银行时感觉财务井然有序。

---

### 📖 内容解说

本文叙述银行开户流程，属金融场景。结构：选择银行→预约→带证件→开户过程→设置储蓄账户→信用卡建议→选择借记卡→激活移动端→总结。掌握银行高频词。

## 📄 文章 20：校园服务与设施

### 🔖 英文原文

> My university **campus** is equipped with a comprehensive range of services to support students' academic and personal lives. The student **union** building houses a **cafeteria**, a bookshop, and a health centre. The cafeteria offers **subsidised** meals, with a daily special for under £5.
>
> The bookshop sells both new and second-hand textbooks, and also stocks **stationery** and university **merchandise**. **Adjacent** to the union is the sports **complex**, which includes a **gymnasium**, a swimming pool, and courts for basketball and tennis. Students can purchase **memberships** at a reduced rate.
>
> For those needing academic support, the writing centre provides one-to-one **tutoring** on essay structuring and **referencing**, while the maths clinic helps with **quantitative** modules. Moreover, the careers service organises **workshops** on CV writing, interview skills, and **networking**.
>
> The international student office regularly arranges social events and **orientation** sessions. All these services are funded by student fees, and their availability is **publicised** through the university app and noticeboards. I find these resources **invaluable** for adjusting to university life.

---

### 🌐 中文翻译

我的大学校园配备了全面的服务设施，以支持学生的学术和个人生活。学生会大楼设有自助餐厅、书店和健康中心。食堂提供补贴餐食，每日特价低于5英镑。书店出售新旧教科书，也出售文具和大学纪念品。学生会大楼旁边是体育中心，包括体育馆、游泳池和篮球场、网球场。学生可以以优惠价格购买会员资格。对于需要学术支持的学生，写作中心提供关于论文结构和参考文献的一对一辅导，而数学诊所帮助定量课程。此外，职业服务部组织关于简历撰写、面试技巧和社交网络的研讨会。国际学生办公室定期安排社交活动和新生指南。所有这些服务由学生费用资助，其可用性通过大学应用程序和公告板公布。我发现这些资源对于适应大学生活非常宝贵。

---

### 📖 内容解说

本文介绍校园设施和服务，属校园生活场景。结构：总体→学生会大楼（食堂、书店、健康中心）→体育中心→学术支持（写作中心、数学诊所）→职业服务→国际学生办公室→资金来源→宣传方式→个人评价。掌握校园服务高频词。
