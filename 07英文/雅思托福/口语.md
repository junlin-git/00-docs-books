## 三、口语常用连接词与填充词

> 流畅度（Fluency）和连贯性（Coherence）的关键在于自然使用连接词和填充词。注意不要过度使用。

### 1. 表示观点

- I think / In my opinion / From my perspective
- It seems to me that...
- I believe that...
- Personally, I would say...
- As far as I'm concerned...
- If you ask me...

### 2. 表示对比/转折

- but, however, although, though
- On the other hand...
- While it's true that..., I think...
- Even so, ...

### 3. 表示举例

- for example / for instance
- such as / like
- ...and things like that
- ...or something like that

### 4. 表示因果

- because / since / as
- so / therefore
- that's why...
- as a result

### 5. 表示递进

- also / as well / too
- besides / in addition
- what's more / moreover
- not only... but also...

### 6. 表示总结

- in conclusion / to sum up
- overall / all in all
- at the end of the day...
- in a nutshell...

### 7. 填充词（用于思考时）

- well...
- you know...
- actually...
- basically...
- I mean...
- let me see...
- that's a good question...
- to be honest...
- frankly speaking...

## 六、口语考试实用模板

### Part 1（日常问题，20-30秒回答）

> 结构：直接回答 + 1-2个解释/例子

**Q: Do you like reading?**  
**A:** Yes, I'm really into reading. I usually read before bedtime because it helps me relax. For instance, I'm currently reading a fantasy novel.

---

### Part 2（个人陈述，1-2分钟）

> 结构：引入 → 描述 → 细节/例子 → 感受/影响

**Topic: Describe a memorable trip.**  
**A:** I'd like to talk about a trip I took to Paris a few years ago. I went there with two of my best friends during the summer break. We stayed in a small hotel near the Eiffel Tower. The highlight was visiting the Louvre Museum, where we saw the Mona Lisa. I was amazed by the art and architecture. It was a wonderful experience because it broadened my horizons and I bonded with my friends even more.

---

### Part 3（抽象讨论，30-60秒）

> 结构：观点 → 理由 → 对比/举例

**Q: Do you think people are more stressed now than in the past?**  
**A:** In my opinion, yes, people today experience more stress.
