## 文章 71：Small Language Models on Personal Devices

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约226词 | **主题**：端侧小模型

---

The recent excitement around AI has focused on enormous cloud-based models, but another movement is gaining momentum: smaller language models that run partly or entirely on personal devices. These systems are less powerful than the largest online models, yet they offer advantages that matter in daily use. A phone or laptop assistant that works locally can respond faster, keep sensitive data on the device, and continue functioning when the network is weak.

This local approach is especially useful for private or repetitive tasks. Users may feel more comfortable summarizing personal notes, transcribing meetings, or searching documents if raw data never leaves their hardware. Developers also see opportunities in specialized assistants trained for narrow domains, such as medical coding, field maintenance, or offline language learning. In these cases, accuracy on a focused task may matter more than broad general knowledge.

The trade-off is clear: smaller models have tighter memory and power limits, so they may hallucinate more, reason less deeply, or fail on complex instructions. Good product design must decide which tasks stay on device and when to hand work to a remote service. The future of AI may therefore be hybrid. Instead of one giant model doing everything in the cloud, we may rely on layers of intelligence distributed across chips, apps, and servers.

### 全文翻译

最近关于 AI 的热潮主要聚焦于巨大的云端模型，但另一股趋势正在升温：在个人设备上部分甚至完全运行的小型语言模型。这些系统不如最大的在线模型强大，但它们在日常使用中拥有重要优势。一个能在本地运行的手机或笔记本助手，响应更快，敏感数据可以留在设备中，而且在网络较弱时仍能继续工作。

这种本地方式尤其适合私密或重复性的任务。如果原始数据从不离开硬件，用户可能会更放心地总结个人笔记、转写会议内容或搜索本地文档。开发者也看到了面向狭窄领域的专业助手机会，比如医疗编码、现场维护或离线语言学习。在这些场景里，聚焦任务上的准确性可能比广泛的常识更重要。

权衡也很明确：小模型受限于内存和功耗，因而更容易出现幻觉、推理不够深入，或者在复杂指令前失效。好的产品设计必须决定哪些任务留在设备上，哪些任务再交给远程服务。未来的 AI 因此很可能是混合式的。与其让一个巨大的云端模型包办一切，我们更可能依赖分布在芯片、应用和服务器上的多层智能。

## 文章 72：Carbon-Aware Computing and Smarter Energy Use

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约224词 | **主题**：碳感知计算

---

Most digital services run whenever users request them, regardless of where electricity is cleanest at that moment. Carbon-aware computing tries to change that. The idea is simple: when a task is flexible in time or location, software can schedule it to run when renewable energy is more available or when the grid has a lower carbon intensity. Instead of treating electricity as identical everywhere, computing systems begin to notice when and where energy is cleaner.

This approach is especially useful for background jobs such as data processing, software testing, video rendering, or some forms of AI training. A company might delay a non-urgent batch task by two hours to use midday solar power, or move work between data centers based on grid conditions. Even modest scheduling changes can reduce emissions if they are applied consistently at large scale.

The challenge is that not all workloads are flexible. A live video call cannot wait for the wind to pick up. Carbon data can also be incomplete, and shifting tasks between regions may affect cost, latency, or regulation. Still, carbon-aware computing shows an important shift in design thinking. Digital efficiency is no longer just about using fewer servers; it is about using computation at the right moment.

### 全文翻译

大多数数字服务只要用户发起请求就会运行，不论此刻哪里的电力更清洁。碳感知计算试图改变这一点。它的思路很简单：当某项任务在时间或地点上具有灵活性时，软件可以把它安排在可再生能源更充足、或电网碳强度更低的时段和地点执行。计算系统不再把所有电力都视为完全相同，而是开始分辨“何时、何地的电更干净”。

这种方法尤其适合后台任务，比如数据处理、软件测试、视频渲染，或者某些 AI 训练。企业可能会把一项不紧急的批处理延后两小时，以利用中午的太阳能；也可能根据电网状况，在不同数据中心之间移动计算任务。如果能够在大规模上持续应用，即使只是适度的调度变化，也能减少排放。

挑战在于，并不是所有工作负载都具有这种灵活性。一次实时视频通话不可能等风大起来再开始。碳数据本身也可能不完整，而把任务迁移到其他地区又会影响成本、延迟或合规要求。尽管如此，碳感知计算体现了设计思路上的重要变化。数字效率不再只是“少用几台服务器”，而是“在合适的时刻使用计算”。

## 文章 73：Autonomous Ships and the Future of Logistics

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约231词 | **主题**：自主航运

---

Shipping carries most of world trade, yet much of the industry still relies on processes that would look familiar decades ago. Autonomous and semi-autonomous ships aim to modernize that system. Using radar, cameras, satellite navigation, onboard sensors, and AI-assisted route planning, these vessels could reduce fuel use, support safer navigation, and allow some operations to be monitored from shore-based control centers.

The strongest case for autonomy may first appear in limited environments. Short coastal routes, harbor operations, and survey vessels are more predictable than crowded international shipping lanes. In such settings, automation can help crews monitor hazards, optimize speed for weather conditions, and reduce repetitive workload. Some systems may never remove crews entirely but can still improve efficiency and safety through better decision support.

Yet the ocean is a difficult place to automate. Weather changes quickly, communication links are not always stable, and international maritime law was written for human captains, not software stacks. Cybersecurity is also critical when navigation and engine systems become more connected. Autonomous shipping will probably expand gradually, with human oversight remaining central for years. The real transformation may come not from fully crewless ships, but from smarter vessels operating within a more digital logistics chain.

### 全文翻译

航运承担着世界上大部分贸易运输，但这个行业中的许多流程，至今看起来仍和几十年前没有本质区别。自主或半自主船舶试图让这一系统现代化。借助雷达、摄像头、卫星导航、船上传感器以及 AI 辅助航线规划，这些船只可能减少燃油消耗、提升航行安全，并让部分操作由岸基控制中心进行监控。

自治技术最强的应用理由，可能首先出现在受限环境里。短途沿海航线、港口作业和测量船，比繁忙的国际航运航道更可预测。在这类场景中，自动化可以帮助船员监测危险、根据天气优化速度，并减少重复性负担。有些系统也许永远不会完全取消船员，但仍可以通过更好的决策支持提升效率和安全。

然而，海洋是一个很难自动化的环境。天气变化快，通信链路并不总是稳定，而国际海事法原本是为人类船长写的，不是为软件栈写的。当导航和动力系统越来越互联时，网络安全也变得至关重要。自主航运很可能会渐进式扩张，人类监督在很多年里仍将居于核心。真正的变化，也许并不是“完全无人船”，而是更聪明的船舶嵌入更数字化的物流链条。

## 文章 74：3D Printing for Personalized Medicine

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约233词 | **主题**：3D 打印与个性化医疗

---

Three-dimensional printing is often associated with hobby workshops or industrial prototypes, but medicine has become one of its most promising fields. Because 3D printers can produce objects layer by layer from digital designs, they are well suited to cases where each patient needs something slightly different. Hospitals and labs now use additive manufacturing to create surgical guides, dental devices, prosthetic sockets, and anatomical models for planning complex procedures.

Personalization is the key advantage. A surgeon can practice on a printed model of a patient's specific anatomy before entering the operating room. A prosthetic device can be shaped more precisely to improve comfort and fit. In the future, researchers hope to print more advanced biological structures, including scaffolds that help tissue grow in the right form. Even when full bioprinting remains experimental, the current practical benefits are already significant.

The limitations are important too. Medical products must meet strict safety and sterility standards, and printing materials are not interchangeable. A device that looks correct is not necessarily strong enough or safe enough for clinical use. Regulation, quality control, and staff training matter as much as printer hardware. 3D printing will not replace mass manufacturing in medicine, but it is changing the boundary between standardized production and individualized care.

### 全文翻译

三维打印往往让人想到爱好者工作室或工业原型，但医疗已经成为它最有前景的领域之一。由于 3D 打印机能够根据数字设计逐层制造物体，所以当每位患者都需要略有不同的东西时，它就特别合适。医院和实验室如今会用增材制造来制作手术导板、牙科装置、假肢接受腔，以及用于规划复杂手术的解剖模型。

个性化是其核心优势。外科医生可以在进入手术室前，先在病人特定解剖结构的打印模型上进行练习。假肢装置也可以更精确地贴合身体，从而提升舒适度和适配度。未来，研究人员希望打印更复杂的生物结构，包括帮助组织按正确形态生长的支架。即使完整生物打印仍然处于实验阶段，当下已经有许多现实收益。

当然，它的限制同样重要。医疗产品必须满足严格的安全与无菌标准，而打印材料也不能随意互换。一个外形看起来正确的装置，并不一定有足够强度，也不一定适合临床使用。因此，监管、质量控制和人员培训与打印机硬件本身一样重要。3D 打印不会取代医疗中的大规模制造，但它正在改变标准化生产与个体化治疗之间的边界。

## 文章 75：Open RAN and the Reinvention of Telecom Networks

**难度**：C1 | **字数**：约215词 | **主题**：开放无线接入网

---

Traditional mobile networks have usually been built from tightly integrated equipment supplied by a small number of major vendors. Open RAN, short for Open Radio Access Network, proposes a different model. It separates hardware and software functions, defines open interfaces between them, and aims to let operators combine components from multiple suppliers. In theory, this can increase competition, lower long-term costs, and reduce dependence on any single vendor.

Supporters also argue that openness can speed innovation. If network functions run on more standardized computing platforms, operators may be able to update software more flexibly or introduce new features without replacing entire systems. That could be especially valuable as 5G networks expand and telecom infrastructure becomes more software-defined.

But telecom networks are not easy places for experimentation. Performance, security, and interoperability must be proven under heavy real-world loads. Operators need confidence that mixed-vendor systems will remain stable and supportable over many years. Open RAN therefore represents both a technical and industrial transition. Its success will depend not only on open standards, but on whether the ecosystem can deliver carrier-grade reliability at scale.

### 全文翻译

传统移动网络通常由少数大型供应商提供高度一体化的设备。Open RAN，也就是开放无线接入网，提出了另一种模式。它把硬件和软件功能拆分开，定义它们之间的开放接口，并希望让运营商能够混合来自多个供应商的组件。理论上，这能提升竞争、降低长期成本，并减少对单一厂商的依赖。

支持者还认为，开放性可以加快创新。如果网络功能运行在更标准化的计算平台上，运营商可能就能更灵活地更新软件，或在不更换整套系统的前提下引入新功能。随着 5G 网络扩张、通信基础设施越来越软件化，这一点尤其有价值。

但通信网络并不是适合轻率试验的地方。性能、安全和互操作性都必须在高负载真实环境中得到证明。运营商需要确信，混合供应商系统在多年运行中依然稳定、可支持。因此，Open RAN 既是一场技术转型，也是一场产业转型。它能否成功，不只取决于开放标准，还取决于整个生态能否在大规模上交付运营商级可靠性。
