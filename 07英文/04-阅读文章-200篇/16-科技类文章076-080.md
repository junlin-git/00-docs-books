## 文章 76：Semiconductor Manufacturing and Water Pressure

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约220词 | **主题**：芯片制造与水资源

---

When people discuss semiconductor manufacturing, they usually focus on extreme precision, expensive tools, and geopolitical competition. Less visible is the industry's dependence on water. Chip fabrication plants use enormous volumes of ultra-pure water to clean wafers between processing steps. In regions facing drought or population growth, that demand creates tension between industrial expansion and local resource limits.

Manufacturers are responding in several ways. New plants increasingly include advanced water recycling systems that recover and purify wastewater for reuse. Some companies report reuse rates above 80 percent for parts of their process. Engineers are also redesigning certain steps to reduce consumption and monitoring water quality more closely because contamination can destroy entire batches of chips.

Even so, water resilience is becoming a strategic issue for the semiconductor supply chain. A fab cannot simply pause for weeks without major economic consequences, yet building in water-stressed regions increases long-term vulnerability. Policymakers and industry planners therefore need to think beyond subsidies and output targets. The future of chip manufacturing will depend not only on lithography and talent, but also on whether factories can operate within the environmental limits of the places that host them.

### 全文翻译

当人们讨论半导体制造时，通常关注的是极高精度、昂贵设备和地缘政治竞争。更不显眼的是，这个行业对水的依赖。芯片制造厂要用巨量超纯水，在工艺步骤之间清洗晶圆。在面临干旱或人口增长压力的地区，这种需求会让产业扩张与本地资源上限之间产生紧张关系。

制造商正在从多个方向作出回应。新建工厂越来越多地配备先进水回收系统，把废水回收、净化后重新使用。有些公司报告称，其部分流程的回用率已超过 80%。工程师也在重新设计某些步骤以降低耗水量，并更严密地监测水质，因为一点污染就可能毁掉整批芯片。

即便如此，水资源韧性正成为半导体供应链的战略议题。一座晶圆厂不可能轻易停摆数周，否则会带来巨大的经济后果；但把工厂建在缺水地区，又会增加长期脆弱性。因此，政策制定者和产业规划者需要考虑的不只是补贴和产量目标。芯片制造的未来，不仅取决于光刻技术和人才，也取决于工厂能否在所在地的环境边界内持续运行。

## 文章 77：Brain-Computer Interfaces Beyond the Hype

**难度**：C1 | **字数**：约228词 | **主题**：脑机接口

---

Brain-computer interfaces, or BCIs, are often described as science-fiction technology that will let people control machines with thought alone. The reality is more modest but still meaningful. A BCI records signals related to brain activity and translates some patterns into commands that a computer can use. For people with severe paralysis, that can offer a new way to communicate, move a cursor, or control an assistive device.

This medical use is where the field has its clearest value today. Researchers are improving electrodes, signal processing, and machine-learning methods so that systems become faster and more accurate. Some non-invasive approaches use sensors placed on the scalp, while invasive systems aim for richer signals through implants. Each method involves trade-offs between precision, safety, convenience, and long-term reliability.

Public imagination, however, often runs ahead of evidence. Consumer neurotechnology products may promise concentration tracking or effortless control, but many applications remain limited. Ethical questions also matter: who owns neural data, how is consent managed, and what happens if brain signals are used in workplaces or schools? BCIs deserve serious research, yet progress should be measured by clinical benefit and proven reliability, not by futuristic slogans alone.

### 全文翻译

脑机接口，简称 BCI，常常被描述成科幻技术，仿佛人类只凭思想就能操控机器。现实更克制，但依然意义重大。BCI 会记录与脑活动相关的信号，并把其中一些模式转化成计算机可以使用的指令。对于严重瘫痪患者来说，这可能意味着一种新的交流方式，也可能意味着可以移动光标或控制辅助设备。

医疗用途正是这个领域目前最清晰的价值所在。研究人员正在改进电极、信号处理和机器学习方法，使系统更快、更准确。一些非侵入式方案使用放置在头皮上的传感器，而侵入式系统则试图通过植入物获取更丰富的信号。两种方法都在精度、安全、便利性和长期可靠性之间做权衡。

然而，公众想象常常跑在证据前面。消费级神经技术产品可能承诺专注力追踪或轻松控制，但许多应用仍然非常有限。伦理问题也同样重要：神经数据归谁所有，如何管理同意，以及如果脑信号被用于职场或学校会怎样？脑机接口值得认真研究，但衡量进步的尺度应当是临床价值和经证实的可靠性，而不是未来感十足的口号。

## 文章 78：Passwordless Security and the End of Memorized Secrets

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约218词 | **主题**：无密码认证

---

For years, security experts have told users to create long, unique passwords for every service, then add two-factor authentication on top. The advice is correct, but it asks humans to perform a task they are consistently bad at. Passwordless security tries to replace memorized secrets with stronger methods such as device-based cryptographic keys, biometrics, or approval through trusted hardware. Recent standards like passkeys are designed to make this approach practical at large scale.

The main advantage is that many common attacks become harder. Users cannot be tricked into reusing the same password on many sites if no password exists to reuse. Properly implemented passkeys also reduce phishing because authentication is tied to a legitimate website domain. For ordinary users, the experience can actually become simpler: unlock a phone or laptop, and the secure credential is handled in the background.

Adoption, however, brings transition problems. People own multiple devices, switch ecosystems, and sometimes lose access to their primary hardware. Recovery methods must be secure without becoming so complicated that users are locked out of essential services. Passwordless security is best understood not as magic, but as better alignment between human behaviour and technical protection. If designed carefully, it could end one of the internet's most persistent weaknesses.

### 全文翻译

多年来，安全专家一直要求用户为每项服务创建又长又唯一的密码，并额外开启双因素认证。这些建议没有错，但它要求人类长期完成一项自己一直不擅长的任务。无密码安全试图用更强的方法替代“记住秘密”这件事，比如基于设备的加密密钥、生物识别，或者通过受信任硬件确认身份。像 passkeys 这样的新标准，就是为了让这条路线能够大规模落地。

它最大的好处在于，许多常见攻击会变得更难。若根本不存在密码，用户就不可能在很多网站之间重复使用同一个密码。若 passkey 实现得当，它还会降低网络钓鱼成功率，因为认证过程绑定在合法网站域名上。对普通用户来说，体验反而可能更简单：解锁手机或笔记本，安全凭证就在后台自动完成工作。

当然，推广会带来过渡问题。人们拥有多台设备，会更换生态系统，有时还会失去最主要的硬件。恢复机制既要足够安全，又不能复杂到把用户锁在关键服务之外。理解无密码安全的最好方式，不是把它看成魔法，而是看成一种让人类行为与技术保护更一致的设计。如果做得好，它可能终结互联网最顽固的弱点之一。

## 文章 79：Drone Delivery After the Marketing Hype

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约227词 | **主题**：无人机配送

---

A few years ago, drone delivery was promoted as an imminent revolution that would fill the sky with flying packages. Reality has been slower and more selective. Small aircraft do offer clear advantages in some situations: urgent medical deliveries, remote communities, industrial sites, and areas with difficult road access. In such cases, drones can save time by traveling directly rather than following congested ground routes.

The limitations are equally real. Batteries restrict range and payload, weather affects reliability, and airspace rules are complex. A drone system also requires landing zones, maintenance, traffic management, and public acceptance. Delivering one lightweight item quickly may be impressive, but it does not automatically produce an efficient business model when thousands of routine deliveries must be coordinated every day.

That is why the industry is shifting from broad promises to narrower use cases. Healthcare networks use drones to move blood samples or medicines between facilities. Utility companies inspect remote assets without sending teams long distances. These applications matter, even if they look less dramatic than the early marketing. The future of drone delivery will probably be practical rather than spectacular: valuable in the right niches, but not a universal replacement for trucks and couriers.

### 全文翻译

几年前，无人机配送被宣传成一场迫在眉睫的革命，仿佛天空很快就会布满送货飞行器。现实的发展更慢，也更有选择性。小型飞行器在某些场景确实具有明显优势，例如紧急医疗运输、偏远社区、工业站点以及道路条件困难的地区。在这些情况下，无人机能够直接飞行，从而比地面路线更节省时间。

但限制同样真实。电池限制了航程和载重，天气影响可靠性，空域规则也十分复杂。一个无人机系统还需要起降点、维护体系、交通管理以及公众接受度。快速送达一件轻小物品固然令人印象深刻，但当需要每天协调成千上万笔常规配送时，它未必自动形成高效商业模式。

这正是为什么行业正在从宏大承诺转向更窄的应用场景。医疗网络利用无人机在机构之间运输血样或药品；公用事业公司则用它们检查远端资产，而不必派出长途现场团队。这些应用非常重要，即便它们看起来没有早期宣传那样戏剧化。无人机配送的未来很可能是务实的，而不是炫目的：它会在合适的细分场景中很有价值，但不会普遍取代卡车和快递员。

## 文章 80：Reusable Rockets and the Economics of Space

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约222词 | **主题**：可重复使用火箭

---

For most of the space age, rockets were treated like disposable machines: launch once, fall away, and build another. That model made space missions extraordinarily expensive. Reusable rockets challenge the logic by recovering major components, refurbishing them, and flying them again. The concept sounds obvious in hindsight, yet achieving it has required advances in guidance software, materials, landing control, and operational discipline.

The economic effect could be profound. If launch providers can spread manufacturing costs across many flights, the price of sending satellites, cargo, or research payloads into orbit can fall dramatically. Lower costs encourage more experimentation. Universities, startups, and smaller countries gain access to missions that would once have been unrealistic. Reusability also supports the rapid deployment of large satellite constellations, which is transforming communications and Earth observation.

But reuse is not automatic savings. Hardware inspection, refurbishment time, insurance, and launch cadence all matter. A rocket that lands successfully but requires expensive rebuilding may not be truly economical. There are also broader questions about congestion in orbit and environmental impact as launch frequency rises. Even so, reusability has already changed expectations. Space is still hard, but it no longer looks quite as financially unreachable as it once did.

### 全文翻译

在太空时代的大部分时间里，火箭都被当作一次性机器：发射一次、抛弃掉，再造新的。这种模式让太空任务异常昂贵。可重复使用火箭通过回收主要部件、翻修并再次飞行，挑战了这种逻辑。事后看来这个概念似乎很直观，但真正实现它，却需要制导软件、材料、着陆控制和运营纪律的多方面进步。

它带来的经济影响可能非常深远。如果发射服务商能够把制造成本分摊到多次飞行上，那么把卫星、货物或科研载荷送入轨道的价格就可能显著下降。更低的成本会鼓励更多试验。大学、初创公司以及较小国家，都可能获得过去难以想象的任务机会。重复使用还支持大型卫星星座的快速部署，而这正在改变通信和地球观测。

不过，“回收”并不自动等于“省钱”。硬件检查、翻修时间、保险以及发射频率都很关键。一枚成功回收却需要昂贵大修的火箭，未必真正具有经济性。随着发射频率上升，轨道拥堵和环境影响也成为更广泛的问题。即便如此，可重复使用已经改变了人们的预期。太空依然困难，但它看起来已经不像过去那样在财务上遥不可及了。
