## 文章 61：Undersea Cables and the Hidden Internet

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约228词 | **主题**：海底光缆与全球互联网

---

Most people imagine the internet as something wireless, floating through mobile towers, satellites, and Wi-Fi routers. In reality, most international data still travels through fiber-optic cables laid on the ocean floor. These undersea cables connect continents and carry financial transactions, video calls, cloud traffic, and scientific data. Without them, the global internet would be slower, more expensive, and far less reliable.

Building a cable is an engineering and geopolitical exercise. Operators must map the seabed, avoid volcanic zones and busy shipping routes, and negotiate landing rights with multiple governments. Once installed, the cable must survive fishing equipment, anchors, earthquakes, and deliberate sabotage. Because a single break can disrupt connectivity for entire regions, redundancy matters as much as raw bandwidth.

The politics are becoming more visible as digital infrastructure becomes a strategic asset. Governments worry about who owns cable stations, who can access traffic, and how quickly damaged links can be repaired during conflict or natural disaster. New satellite systems attract attention, but undersea cables remain the hidden backbone of global communication. Understanding them helps explain why digital sovereignty is now discussed alongside ports, pipelines, and power grids.

### 全文翻译

多数人把互联网想象成某种无线的东西，似乎漂浮在移动基站、卫星和 Wi-Fi 路由器之间。实际上，绝大部分国际数据仍然通过铺设在海底的光纤电缆传输。这些海底电缆连接各大洲，承载金融交易、视频通话、云流量和科研数据。没有它们，全球互联网会更慢、更贵，也远没有现在可靠。

铺设一条电缆既是工程问题，也是地缘政治问题。运营商必须绘制海床地形、避开火山带和繁忙航道，并与多个政府协商登陆权。电缆铺好之后，还要抵御渔具、船锚、地震以及蓄意破坏。由于一次断裂就可能影响整个地区的连通性，冗余和原始带宽同样重要。

随着数字基础设施成为战略资产，其中的政治性也越来越明显。各国政府关心谁拥有电缆站、谁能够接触流量，以及在冲突或自然灾害期间受损链路能多快修复。新卫星系统虽然很吸引眼球，但海底电缆仍是全球通信隐形的骨干。理解它们，有助于解释为什么如今人们会把数字主权与港口、管道和电网放在一起讨论。

## 文章 62：Low-Code Platforms and the New Software Workforce

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约213词 | **主题**：低代码平台

---

Low-code and no-code platforms promise that people can build applications with visual interfaces, reusable components, and minimal hand-written code. For businesses facing software shortages, that promise is attractive. A logistics team might create an internal dashboard without waiting six months for central IT, while a hospital department could automate approvals or reporting using predefined modules.

These platforms can genuinely speed up routine work. When a problem is mostly about forms, workflows, permissions, and data connections, visual development may be enough. Professional engineers also benefit because they can spend less time on repetitive internal tools and more time on systems that require deeper architecture, performance tuning, or security review.

But low-code does not erase complexity; it often moves complexity into configuration, vendor dependence, and integration limits. Poorly governed platforms can generate a patchwork of fragile internal apps that no one fully documents or owns. The best results appear when low-code is treated as one part of a broader software strategy, with clear governance, security rules, and a realistic understanding of which problems still require traditional engineering.

### 全文翻译

低代码和无代码平台承诺，人们可以利用可视化界面、可复用组件和极少的手写代码来构建应用。对于面临软件人手短缺的企业来说，这个承诺很有吸引力。物流团队也许无需等待中心 IT 半年排期，就能做出内部仪表板；医院部门也能用预定义模块自动化审批或报表流程。

这些平台确实能够加快常规工作的完成。当问题主要涉及表单、流程、权限和数据连接时，可视化开发往往已经足够。专业工程师也会因此受益，因为他们可以减少在重复性内部工具上的时间，把精力放在真正需要架构设计、性能优化或安全审查的系统上。

但低代码并不会消灭复杂性；它通常只是把复杂性转移到配置、厂商依赖和集成边界里。若缺乏治理，平台很容易生成一堆脆弱的内部小应用，而这些应用没有人真正完整记录或负责。最好的结果出现在低代码被视为更广泛软件策略的一部分时，也就是配合清晰治理、安全规则，以及对哪些问题仍然需要传统工程的现实判断。

## 文章 63：Precision Agriculture and Data-Driven Farming

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约214词 | **主题**：精准农业

---

Agriculture has always depended on observation, but digital tools are changing the scale and speed of that observation. With drones, soil sensors, satellite imagery, and connected machinery, farmers can now measure field conditions in extraordinary detail. Instead of treating an entire field as if every square meter were identical, precision agriculture allows decisions to vary by location, crop stage, and weather pattern.

This matters because seeds, water, fertilizer, and fuel are expensive, while climate conditions are increasingly unpredictable. Data-driven systems can show where irrigation is really needed, which areas are under nutrient stress, or where weeds are spreading before they become obvious from the ground. That can improve yields while also reducing wasted chemicals and water.

Yet technology is not automatically equal to better farming. Sensors break, software subscriptions cost money, and small farms may struggle to justify expensive equipment. Data ownership is another concern when tractors and farm platforms constantly upload information. Precision agriculture is most useful when it supports farmer judgment rather than pretending to replace it. The goal is not farming by algorithm alone, but farming with better evidence.

### 全文翻译

农业一直依赖观察，但数字工具正在改变这种观察的尺度与速度。借助无人机、土壤传感器、卫星图像和联网农机，农民如今可以以极高精度测量田间条件。不再把整块土地视为完全一致，精准农业允许根据位置、作物生长阶段和天气模式做出差异化决策。

这很重要，因为种子、水、化肥和燃料都很昂贵，而气候条件也越来越难以预测。数据驱动系统可以显示哪些区域真正需要灌溉，哪些地块正承受养分压力，或者杂草在什么时候开始扩散而地面还不易察觉。这既可能提升产量，也可能减少化学品和水的浪费。

但技术并不自动等于更好的农业。传感器会损坏，软件订阅要花钱，小农场也未必能证明昂贵设备值得投入。当拖拉机和农业平台不断上传信息时，数据所有权同样是一个问题。精准农业最有价值的时候，是它支持农民判断，而不是假装取代农民判断。目标不是“完全由算法种田”，而是“带着更好证据去种田”。

## 文章 64：Facial Recognition in Public Spaces

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约223词 | **主题**：公共场所人脸识别

---

Facial recognition systems have improved rapidly, making it technically possible to identify people in airports, stadiums, schools, and city streets within seconds. Supporters argue that this can help find missing persons, verify identity more quickly, and improve security in crowded spaces. For governments and private operators, the appeal is obvious: automated recognition promises scale that human staff cannot match.

The controversy is equally obvious. A face is not like a password that can be changed after a leak. If biometric data is collected, shared, or misidentified, the consequences can be lasting. Researchers have also documented uneven accuracy across age groups, genders, and skin tones, raising concerns that errors may fall disproportionately on certain communities. In public settings, people may also be tracked without meaningful consent.

These issues turn facial recognition into a governance problem, not just a technical one. Rules must address where the technology is allowed, what evidence thresholds are required, how long data is stored, and who can audit outcomes. In some cases, a slower human process may be more legitimate than a faster automated one. Societies must decide not only what systems can do, but what they should do.

### 全文翻译

人脸识别系统进步极快，从技术上说，如今已经能够在机场、体育场、学校和城市街道等公共空间内，在几秒钟之内识别个人。支持者认为，这可以帮助寻找失踪人口、更快验证身份，并提升拥挤场所的安全性。对政府和私营运营者来说，它的吸引力很明显：自动识别承诺了人力难以匹配的规模。

争议同样明显。人脸不像密码，泄露之后还可以更换。如果生物特征数据被收集、共享或误判，其后果可能持续很久。研究人员还记录到，它在不同年龄、性别和肤色群体上的准确率并不一致，这使人担心错误会不成比例地落在某些群体身上。在公共场景中，人们也可能在没有真正同意的情况下被跟踪。

这些问题让人脸识别成为治理问题，而不仅仅是技术问题。规则必须明确技术可以在哪些地方使用、需要什么证据门槛、数据保存多久，以及谁可以审计结果。在某些场景里，一个更慢的人类流程，可能比更快的自动化流程更具正当性。社会必须决定的不只是系统“能做什么”，还包括系统“应该做什么”。

## 文章 65：APIs: The Invisible Glue of Digital Services

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约215词 | **主题**：API 与数字服务

---

When one app displays a map, processes a payment, checks the weather, or signs in with another account, an API is often working in the background. Application Programming Interfaces define how different software systems request data or trigger actions from one another. They are not glamorous, but they are among the most important building blocks of modern digital services.

APIs let companies combine specialized tools rather than build everything themselves. A startup can plug into existing payment, messaging, language translation, or identity services and focus on its core product. Inside large organizations, APIs also help separate teams work independently while still connecting billing, inventory, analytics, and customer support systems. In effect, APIs turn software into a network of reusable capabilities.

Their importance becomes obvious when they fail. A badly designed API can expose private data, slow down an entire service, or break dozens of downstream applications with one small change. Good API design therefore involves documentation, version control, authentication, and clear limits on usage. As software ecosystems grow more interconnected, APIs are becoming less like technical details and more like public interfaces between digital institutions.

### 全文翻译

当一个应用展示地图、处理支付、查询天气，或者使用另一个账户登录时，背后往往有 API 在工作。应用程序编程接口定义了不同软件系统如何向彼此请求数据或触发动作。它们并不耀眼，却是现代数字服务最重要的基础构件之一。

API 让公司可以组合专业化工具，而不必什么都自己重做。一个初创公司可以接入现成的支付、消息、语言翻译或身份服务，把精力放在自己的核心产品上。在大型组织内部，API 也帮助不同团队相对独立地工作，同时又把计费、库存、分析和客服系统连接起来。可以说，API 把软件变成了一个由可复用能力组成的网络。

它们一旦失效，其重要性就会立刻显现。一个设计糟糕的 API 可能暴露隐私数据、拖慢整个服务，或者仅仅因为一次小改动就让下游几十个应用一起出问题。因此，好的 API 设计离不开文档、版本管理、身份认证以及清晰的使用限制。随着软件生态越来越互联，API 正在变得不像“技术细节”，而更像数字机构之间的公共接口。
