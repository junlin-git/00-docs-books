## 文章 16：Urban Loneliness in the Modern City

**难度**：B2 | **字数**：约330词 | **主题**：城市孤独感

---

There is a paradox at the heart of modern city life. Cities are places of dense human contact — millions of people jostling in crowded streets, packed commuter trains, and busy workplaces. Yet surveys consistently reveal that urban dwellers are among the loneliest people on earth.

Loneliness is not the same as solitude. Solitude — chosen, temporary aloneness — can be restorative and pleasurable. Loneliness is the painful perception of an unwanted gap between the social connections one has and the social connections one needs. By this measure, loneliness has reached epidemic proportions in many countries. The United Kingdom became the first country to appoint a Minister for Loneliness, after a 2017 report found that nine million Britons considered themselves "always or often lonely."

The structural conditions of modern urban life make connection difficult. People live increasingly mobile lives, moving cities or countries for work, breaking the geographic ties that once anchored communities. High-rise apartment living reduces the casual encounters — in corridors, gardens, and local shops — that sustain neighbourly acquaintance. Long working hours leave little time for maintaining friendships. Digital communication, while providing constant contact, often substitutes for rather than supplements in-person connection.

The health consequences of loneliness are severe. Research by Julianne Holt-Lunstad, a leading expert on the subject, found that social isolation is as harmful to physical health as smoking 15 cigarettes a day. Loneliness increases the risk of heart disease, stroke, dementia, and premature death.

Cities are beginning to respond. Urban planners are incorporating "social infrastructure" into city design — communal spaces, benches, libraries, and community gardens that facilitate chance encounters. Some municipalities have established "friendship benches" — places where people can sit to signal openness to conversation.

The challenge is to rebuild social fabric in an era of mobility and digital distraction without romanticising past community structures that excluded many people.

---

### 全文翻译

现代城市生活的核心存在一种悖论。城市是人口密集接触的地方，数百万人在拥挤的街道、满员的通勤列车和繁忙的工作场所里摩肩接踵。然而，调查却不断显示，城市居民恰恰是地球上最孤独的人群之一。

孤独感并不等同于独处。独处是被选择的、暂时的单独状态，它可以带来恢复和愉悦。孤独感则是人们痛苦地感知到，自己拥有的社会联系与自己需要的社会联系之间存在不想要的缺口。按照这个标准，许多国家的孤独问题已经达到流行病规模。英国在 2017 年一份报告发现有 900 万英国人认为自己“总是或经常感到孤独”之后，成为第一个设立“孤独事务大臣”的国家。

现代城市生活的结构性条件让建立联结变得困难。人们的生活越来越流动化，为了工作而搬到新的城市或国家，从而切断了曾经把社区固定在一起的地理纽带。高层公寓生活减少了那些维系邻里熟悉感的偶遇，比如在走廊、花园和社区小店里。漫长的工作时间让人几乎没有时间维持友谊。数字通信虽然提供了持续联系，但它往往取代而非补充面对面的联结。

孤独带来的健康后果很严重。该领域的重要专家 Julianne Holt-Lunstad 的研究发现，社会隔离对身体健康的危害，相当于每天抽 15 支烟。孤独会增加心脏病、中风、痴呆和早逝的风险。

城市开始作出回应。城市规划者正在把“社会基础设施”纳入城市设计，例如公共空间、长椅、图书馆和社区花园，这些都能促进偶然相遇。一些地方政府还设置了“友谊长椅”，让人们可以通过坐在那里来表明自己愿意交谈。

挑战在于，在一个高度流动和数字分心的时代重建社会纽带，同时又不去浪漫化那些曾排斥许多人的旧式社区结构。

## 文章 17：The Changing Face of the Family

**难度**：B1-B2 | **字数**：约300词 | **主题**：家庭结构变迁

---

For most of the 20th century, the "standard" family was easily defined: a married man and woman, living together with their biological children. While this model was always more mythical than universal, it was the dominant social norm and the primary lens through which governments, religions, and communities understood family life.

In recent decades, that image has fragmented. Divorce rates have risen significantly in most developed countries, though they have stabilised or even declined among more educated populations in some nations. Cohabitation — couples living together without marrying — has become widely accepted. Single-parent families, blended families, same-sex families, multigenerational households, and chosen families (non-biological communities of care and support) have all become more common and more visible.

These changes reflect broader shifts in values and economics. Increased financial independence for women has made it easier to leave unhappy marriages. Greater legal and social acceptance of LGBTQ+ relationships has enabled more diverse family structures to exist openly. Economic pressures — housing costs, childcare costs — have made the traditional single-income nuclear family financially unviable for many.

Research on child development consistently finds that what matters most for children's wellbeing is not the structure of the family but the quality of relationships within it — warmth, stability, responsiveness, and emotional availability. Children raised in loving, stable same-sex families, for example, show the same developmental outcomes as those in opposite-sex families.

As family forms diversify, so must the policies and institutions that support them. Parental leave policies, housing design, tax systems, and social care must evolve to reflect the reality of contemporary family life rather than an increasingly outdated ideal.

---

### 全文翻译

在 20 世纪的大部分时间里，“标准”家庭很容易定义：一对已婚男女与他们的亲生子女共同生活。虽然这种模式从来都更像是一种神话而非普遍现实，但它曾是占主导地位的社会规范，也是政府、宗教和社区理解家庭生活的主要视角。

近几十年来，这种形象已经碎片化。大多数发达国家的离婚率显著上升，尽管在一些国家受教育程度较高的人群中，它已趋于稳定甚至有所下降。未婚同居已被广泛接受。单亲家庭、重组家庭、同性家庭、多代同堂家庭，以及选择性家庭，也就是由非血缘关系构成的照料与支持共同体，都变得更常见，也更可见。

这些变化反映了价值观和经济环境的更广泛转变。女性经济独立程度提高，使她们更容易离开不幸福的婚姻。对 LGBTQ+ 关系更大的法律和社会接纳，使更多样的家庭结构能够公开存在。经济压力，例如住房成本和育儿成本，则让传统的单收入核心家庭对许多人来说在经济上不再可行。

关于儿童发展的研究一贯发现，对儿童福祉最重要的并不是家庭结构本身，而是家庭内部关系的质量，包括温暖、稳定、回应性和情感可获得性。比如，在充满爱和稳定的同性家庭中长大的孩子，其发展结果与异性家庭中的孩子并无差异。

随着家庭形式日益多样化，支持它们的政策和制度也必须随之调整。带薪育儿假政策、住房设计、税收制度和社会照护体系，都需要反映当代家庭生活的真实样貌，而不是一种越来越过时的理想模式。

## 文章 18：The Global Ageing Crisis

**难度**：B2 | **字数**：约330词 | **主题**：人口老龄化

---

For the first time in human history, we are on the verge of a world where elderly people outnumber children. By 2050, the number of people aged 65 and over is projected to reach 1.5 billion — more than double the current figure. In some countries, the demographic shift is already dramatic: Japan's median age is approaching 50, and Europe is not far behind.

This demographic transition is the product of two forces working in tandem: falling birth rates and increasing life expectancy. Medical advances have dramatically extended human lifespan. Average life expectancy at birth globally has risen from around 50 years in 1960 to over 73 today. Simultaneously, birth rates have fallen in almost all developed countries — in many cases well below the replacement rate of approximately 2.1 births per woman.

The consequences for societies are profound. Pension systems designed when average life expectancy was much lower now face long periods of retirement stretching decades beyond the working life. Healthcare systems face escalating demand from an older population that consumes a disproportionate share of medical resources. Fewer working-age adults must support a growing population of retirees, creating increasing fiscal pressure.

Labour force shortages are already evident in some sectors and countries. Many governments are responding by encouraging higher immigration — a politically sensitive approach — raising retirement ages, and reforming pension systems.

Technology offers some relief. Robotics and automation can substitute for labour in some industries. Assistive technologies and remote healthcare can help older people remain independent longer, reducing care needs. Artificial intelligence may help manage complex medical conditions more efficiently.

But the most fundamental challenges are social and cultural. Societies must find new ways to value and integrate older people rather than treating them as a burden — harnessing their experience, knowledge, and continued desire to contribute.

---

### 全文翻译

在人类历史上第一次，我们正接近一个老年人口数量超过儿童人口的世界。到 2050 年，65 岁及以上人口预计将达到 15 亿，超过当前数量的两倍。在一些国家，这种人口结构转变已经非常显著：日本的年龄中位数已接近 50 岁，欧洲也相去不远。

这一人口转型是两股力量共同作用的结果：出生率下降和预期寿命上升。医学进步极大延长了人类寿命。全球出生时平均预期寿命已从 1960 年的约 50 岁上升到今天的 73 岁以上。与此同时，几乎所有发达国家的出生率都下降了，在许多情况下远低于每名女性约 2.1 个孩子的人口置换水平。

对社会而言，其后果十分深远。养老金制度原本设计于平均寿命更低的时代，如今却要面对长达数十年的退休期，时间甚至远超工作年限。医疗系统则要应对老龄人口带来的不断增长的需求，而这部分人消耗着不成比例的医疗资源。更少的劳动年龄成年人必须供养越来越多的退休者，从而形成越来越大的财政压力。

一些行业和国家已经显现劳动力短缺。许多政府正通过鼓励更多移民这一政治上敏感的方式、提高退休年龄以及改革养老金体系来应对。

技术提供了一定缓解。机器人和自动化可以在某些行业替代部分劳动力。辅助技术和远程医疗可以帮助老年人更长时间保持独立，从而减少照护需求。人工智能也可能帮助更高效地管理复杂医疗状况。

但最根本的挑战是社会和文化层面的。社会必须找到新的方式去重视并融入老年人，而不是把他们视为负担，要善用他们的经验、知识以及继续贡献的意愿。

## 文章 19：Rethinking Success: Beyond Money and Status

**难度**：B2 | **字数**：约310词 | **主题**：重新定义成功

---

For much of recent history, success has been defined in remarkably narrow terms: salary, job title, material possessions, and social status. The implicit cultural message — in education, media, and the workplace — has been that the purpose of a human life is to climb as high as possible on these metrics.

But a growing body of research — and a growing number of ordinary people — is challenging this definition. Studies of subjective wellbeing consistently find that beyond a certain income threshold (often cited around $75,000–$100,000 in the US), additional income produces diminishing returns in happiness. Above this threshold, what predicts life satisfaction is less about money and more about the quality of relationships, sense of purpose, autonomy, and engagement.

The Harvard Study of Adult Development — one of the longest longitudinal studies of human happiness ever conducted, following over 700 men for more than 80 years — found that the single most important predictor of health and happiness in old age was the warmth and depth of close relationships. Loneliness, not financial failure, was the greatest threat to long-term wellbeing.

The COVID-19 pandemic prompted many people to reassess their priorities. Surveys conducted during and after lockdowns found widespread questioning of pre-pandemic lifestyles — long commutes, excessive work hours, and the sacrifice of personal relationships and health in pursuit of career advancement.

There is no single definition of a good life, and it would be patronising to prescribe one. But the evidence suggests that the cultural emphasis on wealth and status as primary measures of worth is not just philosophically questionable, but empirically wrong. A richer, more pluralistic understanding of what it means to live well — one that makes room for rest, relationships, creativity, and contribution — may serve individuals and societies better.

---

### 全文翻译

在近代很长一段历史中，成功一直被定义得异常狭窄：薪资、职位头衔、物质财富和社会地位。在教育、媒体和职场之中，一直存在一种隐含的文化信息，即人生的目的就是尽可能在这些指标上爬得更高。

但是，越来越多的研究以及越来越多普通人，正在挑战这种定义。关于主观幸福感的研究持续发现，当收入达到某个门槛之后，在美国常被引用为 7.5 万到 10 万美元，额外收入带来的幸福感会逐渐递减。超过这一门槛后，真正预测生活满意度的因素就不再主要是金钱，而是关系质量、意义感、自主性以及投入感。

哈佛成人发展研究是关于人类幸福最长时间的纵向研究之一，它跟踪了 700 多名男性超过 80 年，结果发现，预测老年健康与幸福的最重要单一因素，是亲密关系的温暖和深度。对长期福祉的最大威胁，不是经济失败，而是孤独。

新冠疫情促使许多人重新审视自己的优先事项。封锁期间及之后开展的调查发现，人们普遍开始质疑疫情前的生活方式，例如漫长通勤、过度工作时长，以及为了职业晋升而牺牲个人关系与健康。

所谓美好生活并没有唯一标准，强行为人规定一种定义也会显得居高临下。但证据表明，把财富和地位作为衡量价值的主要标准，这种文化强调不仅在哲学上值得怀疑，在实证上也是错误的。对“活得好”形成一种更丰富、更多元的理解，让休息、关系、创造力和贡献都有位置，也许会更有利于个人和社会。

## 文章 20：The Power of Habits

**难度**：B1-B2 | **字数**：约300词 | **主题**：习惯的力量

---

It is estimated that around 40 to 50 percent of our daily actions are not conscious decisions but habits — automatic behaviours triggered by familiar cues in our environment. Understanding how habits work is one of the most practical things a person can learn, because it provides a roadmap for changing behaviour that goes far beyond simple willpower.

Neuroscientist and author Charles Duhigg describes the basic structure of every habit as a "habit loop": a cue that triggers a routine, which delivers a reward. The cue could be a time of day, a location, an emotion, or the presence of other people. The routine is the habitual behaviour itself. The reward is the positive feeling — however subtle — that the brain associates with completing the routine.

To change an unwanted habit, research suggests the most effective approach is not to eliminate it entirely, but to replace the routine while keeping the same cue and reward. A smoker who always lights a cigarette when stressed (cue: stress; routine: smoking; reward: relaxation) might have more success replacing the smoking with a five-minute walk or breathing exercise than trying simply to resist the urge.

"Keystone habits" — habits that seem to have a positive knock-on effect on other areas of life — are particularly powerful targets for change. Regular exercise, for example, has been shown to improve sleep, diet, and mood, even without any deliberate intention to change those things.

The environment plays a crucial role. Research consistently shows that behaviour is shaped by the environment around us. Making healthy food visible and accessible, leaving exercise equipment in plain sight, and reducing friction in desired behaviours while adding friction to unwanted ones are all effective strategies for building better habits.

---

### 全文翻译

据估计，我们每天大约 40% 到 50% 的行为并非有意识的决定，而是由环境中熟悉线索触发的习惯性自动行为。理解习惯如何运作，是一个人能学到的最实用的事情之一，因为它为改变行为提供了一张路线图，而这种路线图远远超出了单纯依赖意志力。

神经科学家兼作者 Charles Duhigg 将每个习惯的基本结构描述为“习惯回路”：一个触发常规行为的提示，接着是例行行为本身，然后是所带来的奖励。提示可能是一天中的某个时间、某个地点、一种情绪，或其他人的存在。例行行为就是那个习惯动作本身。奖励则是大脑与完成这一动作联系在一起的积极感受，无论这种感受多么微妙。

要改变一个不想要的习惯，研究表明最有效的方法并不是彻底消除它，而是在保留相同提示和奖励的前提下替换行为本身。一个总是在压力来临时点烟的人，提示是压力，行为是吸烟，奖励是放松，如果把吸烟换成五分钟散步或呼吸练习，往往比单纯硬抗冲动更容易成功。

“基石习惯”指的是那类会对生活其他方面产生积极连锁效应的习惯，它们尤其值得优先改变。例如，规律运动已被证明会改善睡眠、饮食和情绪，即使当事人并没有刻意想去改变这些方面。

环境起着关键作用。研究一再表明，人的行为会受到周围环境塑造。让健康食物看得见、拿得到，把运动器材放在显眼处，并在想要建立的行为上减少阻力、在不想要的行为上增加阻力，都是培养更好习惯的有效策略。
