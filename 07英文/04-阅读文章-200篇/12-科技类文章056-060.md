## 文章 56：Quantum Sensors Beyond Physics Labs

**难度**：C1 | **字数**：约225词 | **主题**：量子传感器

---

Quantum technology is often associated with futuristic computers, but quantum sensors may influence daily life sooner. These devices use extremely sensitive quantum states to measure time, gravity, magnetic fields, or motion with remarkable precision. That makes them useful in places where conventional sensors struggle, including underground mapping, medical imaging, and navigation in environments where GPS is unavailable or unreliable.

For example, quantum gravimeters can detect tiny changes in underground density, which helps engineers locate tunnels, pipes, water reserves, or geological faults without drilling. Quantum magnetometers may improve brain imaging or mineral exploration. Highly accurate atomic clocks already support communication networks and financial systems, and next-generation versions could make navigation systems more resilient and scientific measurements more exact.

The challenge is moving from the laboratory to large-scale deployment. Quantum devices can be expensive, delicate, and hard to calibrate outside controlled conditions. Engineers must reduce size, cost, and power consumption before these tools become common in hospitals, ships, or construction sites. Even so, quantum sensing deserves more attention than it usually receives. It may not sound as dramatic as quantum computing, but it could deliver earlier and more practical benefits.

### 全文翻译

量子技术常常让人联想到未来感十足的量子计算机，但量子传感器或许会更早影响日常生活。这些设备利用极其敏感的量子态，以惊人的精度测量时间、重力、磁场或运动。因此，在传统传感器表现不佳的场景里，它们尤其有用，例如地下测绘、医学成像，以及 GPS 不可用或不可靠环境中的导航。

例如，量子重力仪可以探测地下密度的细微变化，帮助工程师在不钻探的情况下定位隧道、管道、水储层或地质断层。量子磁力计可能改进脑成像或矿产勘探。高精度原子钟已经在支撑通信网络和金融系统，而下一代版本可能使导航系统更具韧性，也让科学测量更加精确。

真正的挑战，是把这些设备从实验室带向大规模部署。量子设备可能价格高、结构脆弱，而且在受控环境之外难以校准。在它们真正进入医院、船舶或工地之前，工程师必须先降低其体积、成本和功耗。即便如此，量子传感值得比现在更多的关注。它听起来也许不如量子计算那样戏剧化，但它可能更早带来务实的收益。

## 文章 57：AI Tutors in the Everyday Classroom

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约240词 | **主题**：AI 教育助手

---

Schools have used software for years, but generative AI has introduced a different possibility: systems that can explain ideas, ask follow-up questions, and adapt examples to an individual learner. In theory, an AI tutor can give every student extra practice without forcing teachers to divide their attention into ever smaller pieces. A learner struggling with algebra might receive step-by-step hints, while another student gets extension tasks instead of waiting for the rest of the class.

Teachers are interested because real classrooms are uneven places. Students arrive with different background knowledge, reading speeds, and confidence levels. Personalized support is valuable, but human time is limited. AI tools can help with revision quizzes, draft feedback, language practice, and lesson preparation. They may also reduce some administrative burden, giving teachers more time for discussion and pastoral care.

But educational value depends on design and supervision. An AI system can sound confident while giving a wrong answer, reinforce shallow learning if it always gives the quickest response, or collect sensitive student data without clear safeguards. The best role for AI may be as an assistant inside a teacher-led environment, not a replacement for human judgment. Good education is more than information delivery; it also involves trust, motivation, and the ability to notice what a student does not say aloud.

### 全文翻译

学校多年来一直在使用软件，但生成式 AI 带来了另一种可能：系统不仅能解释概念，还能追问、并根据个体学习者调整示例。理论上，一个 AI 辅导器可以为每个学生提供额外练习，而不必让教师把注意力切得越来越碎。一个在代数上吃力的学生可以得到分步骤提示，另一个学生则能获得拓展任务，而不必等待全班进度。

教师之所以感兴趣，是因为真实课堂从来都不整齐。学生带着不同的背景知识、阅读速度和自信程度进入教室。个性化支持很有价值，但人类时间有限。AI 工具可以帮助完成复习测验、草稿反馈、语言练习和备课工作。它们也可能减轻部分行政负担，让教师有更多时间用于讨论和学生关怀。

但教育价值取决于设计和监督。AI 系统可能在给出错误答案时依然语气笃定；如果它总是提供最快的答案，还可能强化浅层学习；它也可能在缺乏明确保护措施的情况下收集敏感学生数据。AI 最合适的角色，也许是处在教师主导环境中的助手，而不是替代人类判断。优质教育不只是信息传递，它还包含信任、动机，以及察觉学生没有说出口内容的能力。

## 文章 58：The Right to Repair and the Future of Electronics

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约228词 | **主题**：维修权与电子产品设计

---

For many consumers, a broken phone, printer, or laptop leads quickly to replacement rather than repair. Devices are often glued shut, parts are hard to buy, and official manuals may be unavailable. The right-to-repair movement argues that this is not just inconvenient but economically and environmentally wasteful. If users and independent shops had access to spare parts, repair information, and diagnostic tools, products could stay useful for much longer.

Supporters say repairability improves competition and lowers costs. Schools could keep tablets running, farmers could fix equipment faster, and families would not need to replace electronics after minor failures. Longer device lifetimes also reduce demand for new raw materials and help slow the growth of electronic waste. In this sense, repair policy is linked to both consumer rights and climate policy.

Manufacturers respond that modern devices are compact, waterproof, and security-sensitive, so unrestricted repair can be difficult. Some concerns are valid: batteries can be dangerous, and poor repairs may damage performance. Yet these arguments do not justify making repair impossible. A balanced approach would require safe design, honest parts pricing, and documentation that allows qualified people to fix products without undermining security. A technological society should not treat disposability as innovation.

### 全文翻译

对许多消费者来说，手机、打印机或笔记本一旦损坏，很快就会被替换，而不是维修。设备常常被胶封得很死，零部件难买，官方维修手册也未必公开。维修权运动认为，这不仅不方便，也在经济和环境上造成浪费。如果用户和独立维修店能够获得备件、维修信息和诊断工具，产品的使用寿命就能显著延长。

支持者认为，可维修性能够改善竞争并降低成本。学校可以让平板电脑继续使用，农民能更快修好设备，家庭也不必因为小故障就更换整件电子产品。更长的设备寿命还会减少对新原材料的需求，并有助于减缓电子垃圾增长。从这个意义上说，维修政策同时关联消费者权益和气候政策。

制造商则回应说，现代设备追求紧凑、防水和安全性，因此完全开放维修并不容易。有些担忧确实合理：电池可能危险，糟糕的维修会损害性能。但这些理由并不足以让维修变得不可能。一个平衡方案应当要求安全设计、诚实的零件定价，以及能够让合格人员在不破坏安全性的前提下维修产品的文档。一个技术社会不应把“一次性用完就扔”当作创新。

## 文章 59：Cybersecurity Lessons from Connected Hospitals

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约225词 | **主题**：医疗网络安全

---

Hospitals are among the most technologically complex workplaces in modern society. A single building may contain networked imaging machines, connected drug pumps, electronic health records, smart building controls, and remote consultation platforms. That complexity improves care, but it also creates a broad attack surface. When hospital systems fail, the consequences are not limited to lost data or delayed emails; patient treatment itself can be disrupted.

Recent ransomware incidents have shown how serious the problem is. If staff cannot access records, laboratory results, or scheduling systems, emergency departments slow down and surgeries may be postponed. Many hospitals also rely on older devices that were designed for clinical accuracy, not modern cybersecurity expectations. Patching them can be difficult because downtime affects care and some vendors no longer support legacy equipment.

Improving security therefore requires more than buying another software product. Hospitals need asset inventories, network segmentation, backup procedures, staff training, and procurement rules that consider security throughout a device's life cycle. Regulators and vendors also have a role, especially when medical technology remains in service for many years. In healthcare, cybersecurity is no longer an IT side issue. It is part of patient safety.

### 全文翻译

医院是现代社会技术系统最复杂的工作场所之一。一栋建筑里可能同时存在联网影像设备、智能输液泵、电子病历、智能楼宇控制系统以及远程会诊平台。这种复杂性提升了医疗质量，但也形成了广阔的攻击面。当医院系统失效时，后果并不只是数据丢失或邮件延迟，病人的实际治疗过程本身都可能被打断。

近年的勒索软件事件已经表明，这个问题非常严重。如果医护人员无法访问病历、化验结果或排班系统，急诊科运转就会变慢，手术也可能被迫推迟。许多医院还依赖较老的设备，这些设备在设计时优先考虑的是临床准确性，而不是现代网络安全要求。给它们打补丁往往很困难，因为停机会影响医疗，而有些供应商又早已停止支持旧设备。

因此，提高安全性远不只是再买一套软件产品。医院需要完整的资产清单、网络分区、备份流程、员工培训，以及在设备全生命周期内都考虑安全的采购规则。监管机构和供应商也都负有责任，尤其是在医疗技术通常会服役很多年的情况下。在医疗场景里，网络安全不再是 IT 部门的边缘事务，它已经成为患者安全的一部分。

## 文章 60：Beyond Lithium-Ion: The Search for Better Batteries

**难度**：B2-C1 | **字数**：约236词 | **主题**：新型电池技术

---

Lithium-ion batteries power phones, laptops, tools, and most electric vehicles, and their success has shaped the modern energy transition. Yet no battery chemistry is perfect. Engineers still want lower costs, longer lifetimes, faster charging, reduced fire risk, and less dependence on scarce materials. That is why researchers are exploring alternatives such as sodium-ion, solid-state, lithium-sulfur, and iron-air batteries.

Different chemistries solve different problems. Sodium-ion batteries use more abundant materials and may be attractive for stationary storage, even if they currently store less energy per kilogram than lithium-ion cells. Solid-state designs promise higher energy density and improved safety by replacing flammable liquid electrolytes. Iron-air systems are being studied for long-duration storage that could support renewable power grids over many hours or even days.

The important lesson is that the battery future will probably be diverse rather than dominated by one miracle technology. Cars, household storage, aviation, and grid balancing do not all need the same performance profile. Commercialization will take time because new batteries must be tested for cost, manufacturing scale, durability, and safety. Still, the search matters. Better energy storage could reshape transport, electricity systems, and industrial planning as profoundly as the smartphone reshaped communication.

### 全文翻译

锂离子电池为手机、笔记本电脑、工具以及大多数电动汽车供电，它的成功塑造了现代能源转型。然而，没有哪种电池化学体系是完美的。工程师仍然希望进一步降低成本、延长寿命、加快充电、减少起火风险，并降低对稀缺材料的依赖。这就是为什么研究人员正在探索钠离子、固态、锂硫和铁空气等替代方案。

不同化学体系解决的是不同问题。钠离子电池使用更丰富的材料，即使目前单位质量储能不如锂离子，也可能很适合固定式储能。固态设计通过用固体电解质替代易燃液体电解质，承诺更高能量密度和更好的安全性。铁空气系统则被研究用于长时储能，有望为可再生电网提供数小时甚至数天的支撑。

真正重要的结论是，未来的电池格局很可能是多样化的，而不是被某一种“奇迹技术”完全统治。汽车、家庭储能、航空和电网调峰，并不需要完全相同的性能曲线。商业化需要时间，因为新型电池必须经受成本、制造规模、耐久性和安全性的检验。尽管如此，这场探索意义重大。更好的储能技术，可能会像智能手机重塑通信那样，深刻改变交通、电力系统和工业规划。
