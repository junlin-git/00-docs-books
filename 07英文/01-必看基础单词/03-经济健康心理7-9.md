# 07-01 经济趋势

**Economic Trends**

**Economic trends** describe how an **economy** changes over time. Key **indicators** include **GDP**, **inflation**, **deflation**, **recession**, **boom**, and **depression**. A **recovery** follows a **downturn**. **Growth** may be **rapid** or **slow**. **Unemployment**, **interest rates**, **exchange rates**, and **trade** affect **markets**. **Supply** and **demand** drive **prices**. **Consumers**, **investors**, and **businesses** react to **confidence** and **expectations**. **Governments** use **fiscal** and **monetary** **policy** to **stabilise** the economy. **Forecasts** predict **trends**. **Recession** brings **layoffs** and **bankruptcy**. A **boom** brings **profit** and **investment**.

**经济趋势**

**经济趋势**描述**经济**随时间的变化。关键**指标**包括**GDP**、**通货膨胀**、**通货紧缩**、**衰退**、**繁荣**和**萧条**。**复苏**紧随**低迷**之后。**增长**可能**快速**或**缓慢**。**失业**、**利率**、**汇率**和**贸易**影响**市场**。**供给**和**需求**驱动**价格**。**消费者**、**投资者**和**企业**对**信心**和**预期**做出反应。**政府**使用**财政**和**货币****政策**来**稳定**经济。**预测**预测**趋势**。**衰退**带来**裁员**和**破产**。**繁荣**带来**利润**和**投资**。

# 07-02 就业与失业

**Employment and Unemployment**

**Employment** provides **income**, **security**, and **satisfaction**. **Jobs** may be **full-time**, **part-time**, **temporary**, or **permanent**. **Unemployment** occurs when people cannot find **work**. **Types** include **frictional**, **structural**, **cyclical**, and **seasonal** unemployment. **Causes** include **recession**, **automation**, **globalisation**, and **skills mismatch**. **Job seekers** need **skills**, **qualifications**, and **experience**. **Recruitment**, **interview**, **CV**, and **application** are part of the **process**. **Workers** may face **redundancy**, **layoffs**, or **downsizing**. **Governments** provide **training**, **welfare**, and **job centres**. **Self-employment**, **freelance**, and **gig economy** are growing.

**就业与失业**

**就业**提供**收入**、**保障**和**满足感**。**工作**可能是**全职**、**兼职**、**临时**或**永久**的。**失业**发生在人们找不到**工作**时。**类型**包括**摩擦性**、**结构性**、**周期性**和**季节性**失业。**原因**包括**衰退**、**自动化**、**全球化**和**技能不匹配**。**求职者**需要**技能**、**资格**和**经验**。**招聘**、**面试**、**简历**和**申请**是**过程**的一部分。**工人**可能面临**裁员**、**解雇**或**精简**。**政府**提供**培训**、**福利**和**就业中心**。**自雇**、**自由职业**和**零工经济**正在增长。

# 07-03 企业管理

**Business Management**

**Business management** involves **planning**, **organising**, **leading**, and **controlling** **resources**. A **company** or **firm** has **managers**, **employees**, and **departments**. **Leadership**, **strategy**, **vision**, and **mission** guide the **organisation**. **Structure** may be **hierarchical** or **flat**. **Human resources** handle **recruitment**, **training**, and **performance**. **Marketing**, **finance**, **production**, and **sales** are key **functions**. **Efficiency**, **productivity**, and **quality** matter. **Innovation** and **competitiveness** drive **success**. **Risk** and **decision-making** are part of **management**. **Corporate** **social** **responsibility** and **ethics** are increasingly important.

**企业管理**

**企业管理**涉及**计划**、**组织**、**领导**和**控制****资源**。**公司**或**企业**有**管理者**、**员工**和**部门**。**领导力**、**战略**、**愿景**和**使命**指导**组织**。**结构**可以是**层级式**或**扁平式**。**人力资源**处理**招聘**、**培训**和**绩效**。**营销**、**财务**、**生产**和**销售**是关键**职能**。**效率**、**生产力**和**质量**很重要。**创新**和**竞争力**驱动**成功**。**风险**和**决策**是**管理**的一部分。**企业****社会****责任**和**伦理**越来越重要。

# 07-04 消费与市场

**Consumption and Markets**

**Consumption** is the **use** of **goods** and **services**. **Consumers** make **choices** based on **price**, **quality**, **brand**, and **advertising**. **Demand** and **supply** determine **market** **price**. **Competition** affects **producers** and **sellers**. **Marketing** and **promotion** influence **buying** **behaviour**. **Income**, **taste**, **trend**, and **fashion** shape **spending**. **Online shopping**, **e-commerce**, and **retail** are growing. **Monopoly**, **oligopoly**, and **competition** describe **market** **structures**. **Consumer** **rights** and **protection** matter. **Advertising** can be **persuasive** or **misleading**. **Budget** and **saving** balance **spending**.

**消费与市场**

**消费**是**商品**和**服务**的**使用**。**消费者**根据**价格**、**质量**、**品牌**和**广告**做出**选择**。**需求**和**供给**决定**市场****价格**。**竞争**影响**生产者**和**卖家**。**营销**和**促销**影响**购买****行为**。**收入**、**品味**、**趋势**和**时尚**塑造**支出**。**网上购物**、**电子商务**和**零售**正在增长。**垄断**、**寡头**和**竞争**描述**市场****结构**。**消费者****权利**和**保护**很重要。**广告**可能是**有说服力的**或**误导性的**。**预算**和**储蓄**平衡**支出**。

# 07-05 全球化

**Globalisation**

**Globalisation** is the **integration** of **economies**, **societies**, and **cultures** worldwide. It involves **trade**, **investment**, **migration**, and **communication**. **Multinational** **corporations** operate across **borders**. **Free trade**, **tariffs**, and **trade agreements** shape **commerce**. **Outsourcing** and **offshoring** move **jobs** and **production**. **Interdependence** grows. **Global** **supply chains** connect **producers** and **consumers**. **Competition** and **efficiency** increase, but **inequality** and **exploitation** may worsen. **Cultural** **exchange** and **homogenisation** occur. **Protectionism** and **nationalism** push back. **Sustainable** and **fair trade** aim to balance **growth** and **equity**.

**全球化**

**全球化**是**经济**、**社会**和**文化**在全球范围内的**一体化**。它涉及**贸易**、**投资**、**迁移**和**交流**。**跨国公司**跨越**边界**经营。**自由贸易**、**关税**和**贸易协定**塑造**商业**。**外包**和**离岸外包**转移**工作**和**生产**。**相互依赖**加深。**全球****供应链**连接**生产者**和**消费者**。**竞争**和**效率**提高，但**不平等**和**剥削**可能加剧。**文化****交流**和**同质化**发生。**保护主义**和**民族主义**反弹。**可持续**和**公平贸易**旨在平衡**增长**和**公平**。

# 07-06 贫富差距

**The Wealth Gap**

The **wealth gap** or **income inequality** is the **disparity** between **rich** and **poor**. **Factors** include **education**, **opportunity**, **inheritance**, **taxation**, **globalisation**, and **technology**. **Poverty** and **affluence** coexist. **Social mobility** may be limited. **Class**, **privilege**, and **disadvantage** persist. **Wages**, **salary**, **wealth**, and **assets** vary widely. **Welfare**, **minimum wage**, and **progressive tax** aim to **reduce** **inequality**. **Education** and **training** improve **opportunity**. **Crime**, **health**, and **social** **unrest** may rise. **Fairness**, **justice**, and **equity** are key. **Redistribution** and **inclusion** are debated.

**贫富差距**

**贫富差距**或**收入不平等**是**富**与**穷**之间的**差距**。**因素**包括**教育**、**机会**、**继承**、**税收**、**全球化**和**技术**。**贫困**与**富裕**并存。**社会流动**可能有限。**阶级**、**特权**和**劣势**持续存在。**工资**、**薪水**、**财富**和**资产**差异很大。**福利**、**最低工资**和**累进税**旨在**减少****不平等**。**教育**和**培训**改善**机会**。**犯罪**、**健康**和**社会****动荡**可能加剧。**公平**、**正义**和**平等**是关键。**再分配**和**包容**引发争论。

# 07-07 财政与税收

**Fiscal Policy and Taxation**

**Fiscal policy** uses **government** **spending** and **taxation** to influence the **economy**. **Tax** may be **direct** or **indirect**, **progressive**, **regressive**, or **proportional**. **Income tax**, **corporate tax**, **sales tax**, and **property tax** are common. **Revenue** funds **public** **services** such as **healthcare**, **education**, and **infrastructure**. **Budget** **deficit** and **surplus** affect **debt**. **Subsidy** and **incentive** encourage **investment**. **Monetary policy** works with **fiscal policy**. **Tax avoidance**, **evasion**, and **haven** are issues. **Fairness**, **efficiency**, and **transparency** matter.

**财政政策与税收**

**财政政策**利用**政府****支出**和**税收**影响**经济**。**税收**可以是**直接**或**间接**、**累进**、**累退**或**比例**的。**所得税**、**公司税**、**销售税**和**财产税**很常见。**收入**为**公共****服务**提供资金，如**医疗**、**教育**和**基础设施**。**预算****赤字**和**盈余**影响**债务**。**补贴**和**激励**鼓励**投资**。**货币政策**与**财政政策**协同。**避税**、**逃税**和**避税天堂**是问题。**公平**、**效率**和**透明**很重要。

# 08-01 身体健康

**Physical Health**

**Physical health** is the **well-being** of the **body**. It depends on **exercise**, **fitness**, **nutrition**, and **sleep**. Regular **physical activity** includes **workout**, **training**, **walking**, **running**, **cycling**, and **swimming**. A **balanced diet** provides **vitamins**, **minerals**, **protein**, **carbohydrate**, **fat**, and **fibre**. **Hydration** and **water** are essential. **Sleep** and **rest** help **recovery**. Good **hygiene** prevents **infection** and supports **immunity**. **Check-up**, **screening**, and **vaccination** detect or prevent **disease**. **Symptoms** such as **pain**, **fatigue**, and **weakness** should not be ignored. **Weight**, **BMI**, **obesity**, and **sedentary** **lifestyle** affect health. **Stamina**, **strength**, **flexibility**, and **endurance** improve with regular **habit**.

**身体健康**

**身体健康**是**身体**的**福祉**。它取决于**锻炼**、**体能**、**营养**和**睡眠**。规律的**体育活动**包括**锻炼**、**训练**、**步行**、**跑步**、**骑行**和**游泳**。**均衡饮食**提供**维生素**、**矿物质**、**蛋白质**、**碳水化合物**、**脂肪**和**纤维**。**补水**和**水**至关重要。**睡眠**和**休息**有助于**恢复**。良好的**卫生**预防**感染**并支持**免疫力**。**体检**、**筛查**和**接种疫苗**检测或预防**疾病**。**症状**如**疼痛**、**疲劳**和**虚弱**不应被忽视。**体重**、**BMI**、**肥胖**和**久坐****生活方式**影响健康。**耐力**、**力量**、**柔韧性**和**持久力**通过规律的**习惯**改善。

# 08-02 心理健康

**Mental Health**

**Mental health** is as important as **physical health**. It includes **psychological**, **emotional**, and **social** **well-being**. **Stress**, **anxiety**, and **depression** are common **mental health** issues. **Mood**, **emotion**, and **feeling** affect daily life. **Self-esteem**, **confidence**, and **resilience** help people **cope** with problems. **Therapy**, **counselling**, and **support** can **treat** **mental illness**. **Stigma** and **awareness** matter. **Mindfulness**, **meditation**, and **relaxation** reduce **tension**. **Burnout** results from **pressure** and poor **work-life balance**. **Isolation** and **loneliness** harm **well-being**. **Social connection**, **happiness**, and **satisfaction** improve **mental health**. **Disorder**, **diagnosis**, and **treatment** require professional care.

**心理健康**

**心理健康**与**身体健康**同样重要。它包括**心理**、**情感**和**社会****福祉**。**压力**、**焦虑**和**抑郁**是常见的**心理健康**问题。**情绪**、**情感**和**感受**影响日常生活。**自尊**、**自信**和**韧性**帮助人们**应对**问题。**治疗**、**咨询**和**支持**可以**治疗****心理疾病**。**污名**和**意识**很重要。**正念**、**冥想**和**放松**减少**紧张**。**倦怠**源于**压力**和糟糕的**工作与生活平衡**。**孤立**和**孤独**损害**福祉**。**社会联系**、**幸福**和**满足感**改善**心理健康**。**障碍**、**诊断**和**治疗**需要专业护理。

# 08-03 医疗体系

**Healthcare Systems**

A **healthcare system** provides **medical** **services** to a **population**. It includes **hospital**, **clinic**, **doctor**, **physician**, **nurse**, **surgeon**, and **specialist**. **Patients** receive **treatment**, **diagnosis**, **prescription**, **medicine**, and **surgery**. **Emergency** care and **ambulance** services save lives. **Insurance**, **public**, and **private** **healthcare** affect **access**, **affordability**, and **quality**. **Universal** **healthcare** aims to cover everyone. **Wait time**, **referral**, **primary care**, **secondary care**, and **tertiary care** are part of the system. **Pharmacy**, **vaccination**, and **prevention** reduce costs. **Funding**, **resource**, **staff shortage**, and **infrastructure** are key challenges.

**医疗体系**

**医疗体系**为**人口**提供**医疗****服务**。它包括**医院**、**诊所**、**医生**、**内科医生**、**护士**、**外科医生**和**专科医生**。**患者**接受**治疗**、**诊断**、**处方**、**药物**和**手术**。**急诊**护理和**救护车**服务拯救生命。**保险**、**公共**和**私人****医疗**影响**机会**、**可负担性**和**质量**。**全民** **医疗**旨在覆盖所有人。**等待时间**、**转诊**、**初级护理**、**二级护理**和**三级护理**是体系的一部分。**药房**、**接种疫苗**和**预防**降低成本。**资金**、**资源**、**人员短缺**和**基础设施**是关键挑战。

# 08-04 疾病与预防

**Disease and Prevention**

A **disease** is an **illness** or **disorder** of the body or mind. **Infection** is caused by **virus**, **bacteria**, **pathogen**, or **parasite**. Some diseases are **contagious**, **infectious**, or **epidemic**. A **pandemic** spreads worldwide. **Chronic** diseases last long; **acute** diseases are short. **Symptom**, **sign**, **diagnosis**, **treatment**, and **cure** are key terms. **Prevention** includes **vaccination**, **hygiene**, **sanitation**, **clean water**, and **screening**. **Immune** **system** and **immunity** protect the body. **Risk factor** includes **smoking**, **alcohol**, **obesity**, and **sedentary lifestyle**. **Early detection**, **outbreak**, **transmission**, **quarantine**, and **medication** are important. **Antibiotic** **resistance** is a growing **public health** threat.

**疾病与预防**

**疾病**是身体或精神的**疾病**或**障碍**。**感染**由**病毒**、**细菌**、**病原体**或**寄生虫**引起。有些疾病是**传染性的**、**感染性的**或**流行性的**。**大流行**在全球传播。**慢性**疾病持续很久；**急性**疾病较短。**症状**、**体征**、**诊断**、**治疗**和**治愈**是关键术语。**预防**包括**接种疫苗**、**卫生**、**环境卫生**、**清洁水**和**筛查**。**免疫****系统**和**免疫力**保护身体。**风险因素**包括**吸烟**、**酒精**、**肥胖**和**久坐生活方式**。**早期发现**、**爆发**、**传播**、**隔离**和**药物**很重要。**抗生素****耐药性**是一个日益严重的**公共卫生**威胁。

# 08-05 营养与运动

**Nutrition and Exercise**

**Nutrition** and **exercise** are essential for **health**. A **balanced diet** provides **nutrient**, **vitamin**, **mineral**, **protein**, **carbohydrate**, **fat**, **fibre**, and **calorie**. Too much **sugar**, **salt**, and **processed food** can cause **obesity** and **malnutrition**. **Physical activity** includes **aerobic** exercise, **strength training**, **flexibility**, and **endurance**. **Workout**, **fitness**, **stamina**, and **metabolism** improve with regular **habit**. **Hydration** and **sleep** support **recovery**. A **sedentary** **lifestyle** increases **risk**. **Weight**, **BMI**, and **diet** should be managed carefully. **Health** is a **balance** of **nutrition** and **movement**.

**营养与运动**

**营养**和**运动**对**健康**至关重要。**均衡饮食**提供**营养素**、**维生素**、**矿物质**、**蛋白质**、**碳水化合物**、**脂肪**、**纤维**和**卡路里**。过多的**糖**、**盐**和**加工食品**会导致**肥胖**和**营养不良**。**体育活动**包括**有氧**运动、**力量训练**、**柔韧性**和**耐力**。**锻炼**、**体能**、**耐力**和**新陈代谢**通过规律的**习惯**改善。**补水**和**睡眠**支持**恢复**。**久坐**的**生活方式**增加**风险**。**体重**、**BMI**和**饮食**应谨慎管理。**健康**是**营养**和**运动**的**平衡**。

# 08-06 老龄化健康

**Health in Ageing**

**Ageing** affects **physical** and **mental health**. The **elderly** and **senior** population faces **chronic disease**, **disability**, and reduced **mobility**. **Life expectancy** has risen, but **old age** brings **risk** of **fall**, **fracture**, **dementia**, and **Alzheimer’s**. **Care**, **caregiver**, **nursing home**, and **assisted living** support **elderly** people. **Retirement** and **pension** affect **quality of life**. **Isolation** and **loneliness** harm **well-being**. **Medication**, **polypharmacy**, **healthcare**, and **long-term care** are important. **Palliative** and **hospice** care provide **dignity**. **Independence**, **support**, and **well-being** are key goals.

**老龄化健康**

**老龄化**影响**身体**和**心理健康**。**老年人**和**年长者**人口面临**慢性疾病**、**残疾**和**行动能力**下降。**预期寿命**上升，但**老年**带来**跌倒**、**骨折**、**痴呆**和**阿尔茨海默病**的**风险**。**护理**、**照护者**、**养老院**和**辅助生活**支持**老年人**。**退休**和**养老金**影响**生活质量**。**孤立**和**孤独**损害**福祉**。**药物**、**多重用药**、**医疗**和**长期护理**很重要。**姑息**和**临终关怀**提供**尊严**。**独立**、**支持**和**福祉**是关键目标。

# 08-07 公共卫生

**Public Health**

**Public health** protects the **health** of the **population**. It uses **epidemiology**, **statistics**, and **surveillance** to study **disease**. **Prevention** and **promotion** are central. **Vaccination**, **screening**, **sanitation**, **hygiene**, and **clean water** improve **health**. **Outbreak**, **epidemic**, **pandemic**, **quarantine**, **isolation**, and **contact tracing** control **infection**. **Health education** and **awareness** change **behaviour**. **Policy**, **regulation**, and **funding** support **healthcare** and **access**. **Inequality** affects **vulnerable** groups. **Mortality**, **morbidity**, and **life expectancy** are key **statistics**. **Emergency** **response** and **public health** **surveillance** protect **communities**.

**公共卫生**

**公共卫生**保护**人口**的**健康**。它使用**流行病学**、**统计**和**监测**来研究**疾病**。**预防**和**促进**是核心。**接种疫苗**、**筛查**、**环境卫生**、**卫生**和**清洁水**改善**健康**。**爆发**、**流行病**、**大流行**、**隔离**、**检疫**和**接触者追踪**控制**感染**。**健康教育**和**意识**改变**行为**。**政策**、**法规**和**资金**支持**医疗**和**机会**。**不平等**影响**弱势**群体。**死亡率**、**发病率**和**预期寿命**是关键**统计**。**紧急****响应**和**公共卫生****监测**保护**社区**。

# 09-01 情绪

**Emotions**

**Emotions** are complex **feelings** that affect **mood**, **thought**, and **behaviour**. Basic emotions include **anger**, **fear**, **joy**, **sadness**, **disgust**, and **surprise**. Other emotions include **love**, **hate**, **anxiety**, **stress**, **excitement**, **depression**, **frustration**, **jealousy**, **guilt**, **shame**, and **pride**. **Emotional** **expression** varies between people and cultures. Some people **suppress** their feelings; others **vent** or **express** them openly. **Empathy** and **sympathy** help us understand others. **Triggers** can cause an emotional **response**. Learning to **control** and **cope** with emotions is important for **mental health** and **well-being**.

**情绪**

**情绪**是影响**心情**、**思想**和**行为**的复杂**感受**。基本情绪包括**愤怒**、**恐惧**、**快乐**、**悲伤**、**厌恶**和**惊讶**。其他情绪包括**爱**、**恨**、**焦虑**、**压力**、**兴奋**、**抑郁**、**挫败**、**嫉妒**、**内疚**、**羞耻**和**骄傲**。**情绪**的**表达**因人和文化而异。有些人**压抑**自己的感受；另一些人则公开**发泄**或**表达**。**同理心**和**同情**帮助我们理解他人。**触发因素**可以引起情绪**反应**。学会**控制**和**应对**情绪对**心理健康**和**福祉**很重要。

# 09-02 压力

**Stress**

**Stress** is a feeling of **pressure** or **tension**. It can be **acute** or **chronic**. Common **causes** include **workload**, **deadline**, **exam**, **money** problems, and **relationship** issues. **Symptoms** include **anxiety**, **fatigue**, **headache**, **insomnia**, and **irritability**. **Burnout** results from long-term **stress**. People **cope** with stress in different ways: **exercise**, **relaxation**, **meditation**, **mindfulness**, **hobby**, and **social support**. **Time management** and **balance** help reduce **pressure**. **Chronic stress** harms **health** and **well-being**. **Relaxation** and **rest** are essential.

**压力**

**压力**是一种**压迫**或**紧张**感。它可以是**急性的**或**慢性的**。常见**原因**包括**工作量**、**截止日期**、**考试**、**金钱**问题和**人际关系**问题。**症状**包括**焦虑**、**疲劳**、**头痛**、**失眠**和**易怒**。**倦怠**源于长期**压力**。人们以不同方式**应对**压力：**锻炼**、**放松**、**冥想**、**正念**、**爱好**和**社会支持**。**时间管理**和**平衡**有助于减少**压力**。**慢性压力**损害**健康**和**福祉**。**放松**和**休息**至关重要。

---

# 09-03 幸福

**Happiness**

**Happiness** is a state of **well-being** and **contentment**. It includes **joy**, **pleasure**, **satisfaction**, and **fulfillment**. **Positive psychology** studies what makes people **happy**. **Factors** include **relationship**, **health**, **meaning**, **purpose**, **gratitude**, and **optimism**. **Money** can help, but only to a point. **Happiness** is not the same as **pleasure**. **Contentment** and **satisfaction** come from within. **Gratitude** and **kindness** increase **well-being**. **Social** **connection** and **support** are important. **Happiness** improves **health**, **creativity**, and **productivity**.

**幸福**

**幸福**是一种**福祉**和**满足**的状态。它包括**快乐**、**愉悦**、**满足**和**成就**。**积极心理学**研究什么让人**幸福**。**因素**包括**人际关系**、**健康**、**意义**、**目标**、**感恩**和**乐观**。**金钱**有帮助，但有限度。**幸福**不等同于**愉悦**。**满足**和**满意**来自内心。**感恩**和**善良**增加**福祉**。**社会****联系**和**支持**很重要。**幸福**改善**健康**、**创造力**和**生产力**。

---

# 09-04 动机

**Motivation**

**Motivation** is the **drive** or **desire** to do something. It can be **intrinsic** or **extrinsic**. **Goals**, **ambition**, **reward**, and **incentive** influence **motivation**. **Inspiration** and **encouragement** help people **persist**. **Effort** and **persistence** lead to **achievement** and **success**. **Self-discipline** and **willpower** are important. **Demotivation** can come from **failure**, **boredom**, or **lack** of **support**. **Motivation** affects **learning**, **work**, and **performance**.

**动机**

**动机**是做某事的**驱动力**或**欲望**。它可以是**内在的**或**外在的**。**目标**、**雄心**、**奖励**和**激励**影响**动机**。**灵感**和**鼓励**帮助人们**坚持**。**努力**和**坚持**带来**成就**和**成功**。**自律**和**意志力**很重要。**动力不足**可能来自**失败**、**无聊**或缺乏**支持**。**动机**影响**学习**、**工作**和**表现**。

# 09-05 人格

**Personality**

**Personality** is a set of **traits** and **characteristics** that shape **behaviour**. Common **traits** include **introvert**, **extrovert**, **conscientiousness**, **agreeableness**, **neuroticism**, and **openness**. **Personality** is relatively **stable** but can **change** over time. It affects **relationship**, **work**, and **health**. **Nature** and **nurture** both play a role. **Character** and **temperament** are related concepts. **Self-awareness** helps people understand their **personality**.

**人格**

**人格**是塑造**行为**的一系列**特质**和**特征**。常见**特质**包括**内向**、**外向**、**尽责性**、**宜人性**、**神经质**和**开放性**。**人格**相对**稳定**，但会随时间**变化**。它影响**人际关系**、**工作**和**健康**。**天性**和**教养**都起作用。**性格**和**气质**是相关概念。**自我意识**帮助人们理解自己的**人格**。

---

# 09-06 人际关系

**Interpersonal Relationships**

**Interpersonal relationships** include **friendship**, **family**, **colleague**, and **peer** relationships. They involve **trust**, **respect**, **communication**, and **support**. **Conflict** can occur, but **resolution** and **compromise** help. **Bond** and **intimacy** strengthen **connection**. **Social** **skills** and **empathy** improve **relationships**. **Loneliness** and **isolation** harm **well-being**. Healthy **relationships** improve **happiness** and **mental health**.

**人际关系**

**人际关系**包括**友谊**、**家庭**、**同事**和**同伴**关系。它们涉及**信任**、**尊重**、**沟通**和**支持**。**冲突**可能发生，但**解决**和**妥协**有帮助。**纽带**和**亲密**加强**联系**。**社交****技能**和**同理心**改善**关系**。**孤独**和**孤立**损害**福祉**。健康的**关系**改善**幸福**和**心理健康**。

---

# 09-07 行为习惯

**Habits and Behaviour**

A **habit** is a repeated **behaviour** or **routine**. **Habits** can be good or bad. **Addiction**, **procrastination**, and **laziness** are negative **habits**. **Discipline**, **self-control**, and **willpower** help build positive **habits**. **Cue**, **routine**, and **reward** form a **habit** loop. **Trigger** and **environment** influence **behaviour**. **Consistency** and **repetition** strengthen **habits**. Changing **habits** requires **effort** and **patience**.

**行为习惯**

**习惯**是重复的**行为**或**常规**。**习惯**可以是好或坏。**成瘾**、**拖延**和**懒惰**是负面**习惯**。**纪律**、**自我控制**和**意志力**帮助建立积极**习惯**。**提示**、**常规**和**奖励**构成**习惯**回路。**触发因素**和**环境**影响**行为**。**一致性**和**重复**强化**习惯**。改变**习惯**需要**努力**和**耐心**。
