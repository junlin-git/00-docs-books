# 04-07 创新与研发

## 一、精读文章

**Innovation and Research & Development**

**Innovation** creates new **ideas**, **products**, **services**, and **processes**. **Research and development** (R&D) drives **technological** **progress**. **Companies** invest in **labs**, **scientists**, **engineers**, and **equipment**. **Patents** protect **inventions** and **intellectual property**. **Startups** and **entrepreneurs** bring **new** **solutions** to **markets**. **Governments** support **innovation** through **funding**, **grants**, and **tax** **incentives**. **Collaboration** between **universities** and **industry** speeds up **commercialisation**. **Creativity**, **experimentation**, and **risk-taking** are key. **Failure** is part of the **process**. **Disruptive** **technologies** change **industries**. **Innovation** improves **competitiveness**, **productivity**, and **quality of life**.

## 二、中文翻译

**创新与研发**

**创新**创造新的**想法**、**产品**、**服务**和**流程**。**研究与开发**（R&D）推动**技术****进步**。**公司**投资于**实验室**、**科学家**、**工程师**和**设备**。**专利**保护**发明**和**知识产权**。**初创企业**和**企业家**将**新****解决方案**推向**市场**。**政府**通过**资金**、**补助**和**税收****激励**支持**创新**。**大学**与**产业**之间的**合作**加速**商业化**。**创造力**、**实验**和**冒险**是关键。**失败**是**过程**的一部分。**颠覆性****技术**改变**行业**。**创新**提高**竞争力**、**生产力**和**生活质量**。
