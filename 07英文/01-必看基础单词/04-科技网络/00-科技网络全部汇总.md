# 04-01 信息技术

## 一、精读文章

**Information Technology**

**Information technology** (IT) includes **computers**, **hardware**, **software**, **networks**, and **data**. A **system** processes **information** and **digital** **data**. **Users** interact with **devices** such as **laptops**, **smartphones**, and **tablets**. **Operating systems**, **applications**, and **programs** run on these devices. **Databases** store **information**, while **servers** provide **services**. **Cloud computing** allows **storage** and **processing** online. **Cybersecurity** protects **networks** from **hackers**, **viruses**, and **malware**. **IT support** helps **users** solve **technical** problems. **Digital transformation** changes how **businesses** and **governments** operate.

# 04-02 人工智能

## 一、精读文章

**Artificial Intelligence**

**Artificial intelligence** (AI) enables machines to **learn**, **reason**, and **solve problems**. **Machine learning** uses **algorithms** and **data** to **train** **models**. **Deep learning** uses **neural networks**. AI can **recognise** **images**, **speech**, and **patterns**. **Natural language processing** helps computers **understand** and **generate** **language**. **Robots** and **self-driving cars** use AI. **Automation** increases **efficiency** but may replace **jobs**. **Ethical** concerns include **bias**, **privacy**, and **accountability**. **AI ethics** guides **responsible** **development**. **Regulation** and **transparency** are important. AI can **assist** **doctors**, **teachers**, and **researchers**. **Future** **innovation** depends on **human** **control** and **trust**.

# 04-05 数据隐私

## 一、精读文章

**Data Privacy**

**Data privacy** protects **personal information** from **misuse**. **Personal data** includes **names**, **addresses**, **phone numbers**, **emails**, **passwords**, and **financial** details. **Companies** collect **data** through **cookies**, **tracking**, and **surveys**. **Users** often give **consent** without reading **terms** and **conditions**. **Data breaches** and **leaks** expose **private** information. **Hackers** steal **data** for **fraud** and **identity theft**. **Governments** pass **laws** such as **GDPR** to **protect** **privacy**. **Encryption**, **anonymity**, and **security** measures reduce **risk**. **Transparency** and **accountability** are important. **Individuals** should **protect** their **data** and **stay alert** online.

## 二、中文翻译

**数据隐私**保护**个人信息**免遭**滥用**。**个人数据**包括**姓名**、**地址**、**电话号码**、**电子邮件**、**密码**和**财务**细节。**公司**通过 **Cookie**、**追踪**和**调查**收集**数据**。**用户**常常在未阅读**条款**和**条件**的情况下给出**同意**。**数据泄露**和**泄漏**暴露**私人**信息。**黑客**窃取**数据**用于**欺诈**和**身份盗窃**。**政府**通过**法律**，如 **GDPR**，来**保护****隐私**。**加密**、**匿名**和**安全**措施降低**风险**。**透明度**和**问责**很重要。**个人**应**保护**自己的**数据**并在网上**保持警惕**。
