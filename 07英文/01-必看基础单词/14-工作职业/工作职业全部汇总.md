# 14-01 职业选择

## 一、精读文章

**Career Choice**

**Career choice** is one of the most important **decisions** in life. It is influenced by **interest**, **ability**, **personality**, **value**, **education**, and **opportunity**. Some people choose a **profession** based on **passion**; others prioritise **salary**, **security**, and **stability**. **Career** **counselling** and **guidance** help young people **plan** their **future**. **Job** **market** **demand**, **skill** **shortage**, and **economic** **trend** shape **opportunity**. **Work** **experience**, **internship**, and **volunteering** help people **explore** **options**. **Career** **change** is increasingly common. **Satisfaction**, **fulfilment**, and **work-life balance** matter as much as **income**.

## 二、中文翻译

# 14-02 工作环境

## 一、精读文章

**Work Environment**

The **work environment** includes **physical**, **social**, and **psychological** **conditions** at **work**. A **positive** **environment** improves **productivity**, **morale**, and **well-being**. **Factors** include **lighting**, **noise**, **temperature**, **space**, **ergonomics**, and **safety**. **Workplace** **culture**, **atmosphere**, and **relationship** with **colleagues** and **manager** matter. **Teamwork**, **communication**, and **respect** create **harmony**. **Stress**, **harassment**, and **discrimination** harm **employees**. **Flexible** **hours**, **break**, and **facility** improve **satisfaction**. **Occupational** **health** and **safety** **regulation** protect **workers**.

## 二、中文翻译

**工作环境**包括**工作**中的**物理**、**社会**和**心理****条件**。**积极的****环境**改善**生产力**、**士气**和**福祉**。**因素**包括**照明**、**噪音**、**温度**、**空间**、**人体工程学**和**安全**。**工作场所****文化**、**氛围**以及与**同事**和**经理**的**关系**很重要。**团队合作**、**沟通**和**尊重**创造**和谐**。**压力**、**骚扰**和**歧视**伤害**员工**。**灵活****工时**、**休息**和**设施**改善**满意度**。**职业****健康**和**安全****法规**保护**工人**。

# 14-03 工作与生活平衡

## 一、精读文章

**Work-Life Balance**

**Work-life balance** is the **ability** to **manage** **work** and **personal** **life**. It involves **time**, **energy**, and **priority**. **Overwork**, **long** **hours**, and **burnout** harm **health** and **relationship**. **Flexible** **working**, **remote** **work**, and **part-time** **arrangement** help **balance**. **Family**, **leisure**, **exercise**, and **rest** are important. **Employers** can support **balance** through **policy**, **leave**, and **childcare**. **Boundary** between **work** and **home** is essential. **Well-being**, **happiness**, and **satisfaction** improve when **balance** is achieved.

## 二、中文翻译

**工作与生活平衡**是**管理****工作**和**个人****生活**的**能力**。它涉及**时间**、**精力**和**优先事项**。**过度工作**、**长时间**和**倦怠**损害**健康**和**关系**。**灵活****工作**、**远程****工作**和**兼职****安排**有助于**平衡**。**家庭**、**休闲**、**锻炼**和**休息**很重要。**雇主**可以通过**政策**、**休假**和**托儿**支持**平衡**。**工作**和**家庭**之间的**界限**至关重要。实现**平衡**时，**福祉**、**幸福**和**满足感**会改善。

# 14-04 远程办公

## 一、精读文章

**Remote Work**

**Remote work** allows **employees** to work from **home** or another **location** outside the **office**. It uses **technology** such as **video** **conference**, **email**, **cloud** **platform**, and **messaging** **app**. **Advantages** include **flexibility**, **time** **saving**, **autonomy**, and better **work-life balance**. **Disadvantages** include **isolation**, **distraction**, **communication** **barrier**, and **blurred** **boundary** between **work** and **home**. **Employers** need **trust**, **clear** **expectation**, and **performance** **measurement**. **Hybrid** **work** combines **office** and **remote** **arrangement**. **Digital** **literacy** and **self-discipline** are essential.

## 二、中文翻译

**远程办公**

**远程办公**允许**员工**在**办公室**以外的**家**或其他**地点**工作。它使用**技术**，如**视频****会议**、**电子邮件**、**云****平台**和**消息****应用**。**优势**包括**灵活性**、**节省时间**、**自主性**和更好的**工作与生活平衡**。**劣势**包括**孤立**、**分心**、**沟通****障碍**以及**工作**和**家庭**之间**模糊**的**界限**。**雇主**需要**信任**、**明确****期望**和**绩效****衡量**。**混合****办公**结合了**办公室**和**远程****安排**。**数字****素养**和**自律**至关重要。

# 14-05 职业技能

## 一、精读文章

**Professional Skills**

**Professional skills** are **abilities** needed for **work**. They include **technical** **skill**, **soft** **skill**, **communication**, **teamwork**, **leadership**, **problem-solving**, and **time** **management**. **Hard** **skill** is **job-specific**, such as **coding**, **accounting**, or **engineering**. **Soft** **skill** is **interpersonal**, such as **empathy**, **adaptability**, and **creativity**. **Employers** value both. **Training**, **workshop**, **course**, and **certification** develop **skill**. **Experience** and **practice** build **competence**. **Continuous** **learning** and **upskilling** are essential in a changing **job market**. **Professional** **development** improves **career** **prospect** and **employability**.

**Recruitment and Interviews**

**Recruitment** is the **process** of **attracting**, **selecting**, and **hiring** **candidates**. **Workplace Relationships**

**Workplace relationships** are the **connections** between **colleagues**, **managers**, **subordinates**, and **teams**.
