# 06-01 政府职能

## 一、精读文章

**Government Functions**

Government functions include making laws, providing public services, and maintaining order. The **government** has **authority** over the **state**. It creates **policy** and **law**, and carries out **administration** through **officials** and the **bureaucracy**. A **parliament** or **congress** makes **legislation**. The **president** or **prime minister** leads the **executive**. **Local**, **central**, **federal**, and **municipal** governments share power. **Elections** allow citizens to **vote**. **Democracy**, **republic**, and **monarchy** are different systems. A **constitution** defines **sovereignty** and **legitimacy**. **Accountability** and **transparency** are important. Governments **regulate** the economy, collect **taxation**, and manage the **budget**. They provide **welfare**, **infrastructure**, and **defense**. The **judiciary**, **executive**, and **legislative** branches balance each other.

## 二、中文翻译

政府职能包括制定法律、提供公共服务和维护秩序。**政府**对**国家**拥有**权威**。它制定**政策**和**法律**，并通过**官员**和**官僚机构**进行**行政**管理。**议会**或**国会**制定**立法**。**总统**或**总理**领导**行政**部门。**地方**、**中央**、**联邦**和**市政**政府分享权力。**选举**让公民**投票**。**民主**、**共和**和**君主制**是不同的制度。**宪法**界定**主权**和**合法性**。**问责**和**透明**很重要。政府**监管**经济，征收**税收**，管理**预算**。它们提供**福利**、**基础设施**和**国防**。**司法**、**行政**和**立法**部门相互制衡。

# 06-02 公共政策

## 一、精读文章

**Public Policy**

**Public policy** is a **decision** or **plan** by **government**. It includes **regulation**, **legislation**, and **reform**. The **process** has **agenda** setting, **formulation**, **implementation**, and **evaluation**. **Objectives** and **targets** guide **policy**. **Tools** include **subsidy**, **incentive**, **tax**, and **funding**. **Welfare**, **healthcare**, **education**, **housing**, **transport**, and **environment** are key **areas**. A **budget** determines **allocation**. **Stakeholders** and **consultation** shape **debate**. **Opposition** and **support** affect **outcome**. **Impact**, **effectiveness**, **efficiency**, **equity**, and **feasibility** are **criteria**.

## 二、中文翻译

**公共政策**

**公共政策**是**政府**的**决定**或**计划**。它包括**监管**、**立法**和**改革**。**过程**包括**议程**设定、**制定**、**实施**和**评估**。**目标**和**指标**指导**政策**。**工具**包括**补贴**、**激励**、**税收**和**资金**。**福利**、**医疗**、**教育**、**住房**、**交通**和**环境**是关键**领域**。**预算**决定**分配**。**利益相关者**和**咨询**塑造**辩论**。**反对**和**支持**影响**结果**。**影响**、**有效性**、**效率**、**公平**和**可行性**是**标准**。

# 06-03 法律体系

## 一、精读文章

**Legal System**

A **legal system** includes **courts**, **judges**, **lawyers**, and **laws**. A **case** may go to **trial**. The **prosecutor** and **defendant** present **evidence** and **witnesses**. **Testimony** and **verdict** lead to a **sentence**. **Appeals** are possible. **Constitutions**, **statutes**, and **regulations** form the **law**. **Civil** law deals with **contracts**, **property**, and **torts**. **Criminal** law deals with **offences**. **Rights**, **obligations**, and **liability** matter. **Justice**, **judiciary**, **enforcement**, and **compliance** are essential.

## 二、中文翻译

**法律体系**

**法律体系**包括**法院**、**法官**、**律师**和**法律**。**案件**可能进入**审判**。**检察官**和**被告**出示**证据**和**证人**。**证词**和**裁决**导致**判决**。**上诉**是可能的。**宪法**、**法规**和**规章**构成**法律**。**民事**法律处理**合同**、**财产**和**侵权**。**刑事**法律处理**犯罪**。**权利**、**义务**和**责任**很重要。**正义**、**司法**、**执法**和**合规**至关重要。

# 06-04 犯罪与惩罚

## 一、精读文章

**Crime and Punishment**

**Crime** includes **offences** such as **theft**, **robbery**, **burglary**, **fraud**, **murder**, and **assault**. **Drug trafficking**, **corruption**, and **bribery** are serious. **Punishment** may be a **fine**, **probation**, **community service**, **prison**, or **capital punishment**. **Rehabilitation**, **deterrence**, and **retribution** are goals. **Victims** and **offenders** are central. **Law enforcement** and **police** investigate. **Recidivism** is a concern. **Justice** requires fair **trials** and **sentences**.

## 二、中文翻译

**犯罪**包括**罪行**，如**盗窃**、**抢劫**、**入室盗窃**、**欺诈**、**谋杀**和**袭击**。**贩毒**、**腐败**和**贿赂**很严重。**惩罚**可以是**罚款**、**缓刑**、**社区服务**、**监狱**或**死刑**。**康复**、**威慑**和**报应**是目标。**受害者**和**罪犯**是核心。**执法**和**警察**进行调查。**累犯**是一个问题。**正义**需要公正的**审判**和**判决**。

# 06-05 人权

## 一、精读文章

**Human Rights**

**Human rights** are basic **freedoms** and **entitlements** for all people. They include **civil**, **political**, **economic**, **social**, and **cultural** rights. **Freedom** of **speech**, **religion**, **assembly**, and **association** are fundamental. **Equality** and **non-discrimination** are key. **Dignity**, **liberty**, and **justice** must be protected. **Torture**, **slavery**, and **genocide** are violations. **Refugees** and **asylum seekers** flee **persecution**. **International law**, the **UN**, and **treaties** promote **human rights**. **Protection**, **promotion**, and **obligation** are important.

## 二、中文翻译

**人权**是所有人的基本**自由**和**权利**。它们包括**公民**、**政治**、**经济**、**社会**和**文化**权利。**言论**、**宗教**、**集会**和**结社**自由是基本的。**平等**和**不歧视**是关键。**尊严**、**自由**和**正义**必须受到保护。**酷刑**、**奴隶制**和**种族灭绝**是侵犯行为。**难民**和**寻求庇护者**逃离**迫害**。**国际法**、**联合国**和**条约**促进**人权**。**保护**、**促进**和**义务**很重要。
