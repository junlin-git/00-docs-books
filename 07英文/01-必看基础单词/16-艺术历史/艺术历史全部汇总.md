# 16-01 艺术形式

## 一、精读文章

**Art Forms**

**Art** takes many **forms**, including **painting**, **sculpture**, **drawing**, **printmaking**, **photography**, **architecture**, **music**, **dance**, **theatre**, **film**, and **literature**. **Visual art** appeals to the **eye**; **performing art** involves **body**, **voice**, and **stage**. **Artist** uses **colour**, **shape**, **line**, **texture**, **form**, and **space** to express **idea** and **emotion**. **Style**, **genre**, and **movement** describe **artistic** **expression**. **Creativity**, **imagination**, and **skill** are essential. **Art** can **reflect** **culture**, **society**, and **history**. It can also **challenge**, **inspire**, and **provoke**. **Gallery**, **museum**, and **exhibition** display **artwork** to the **public**.

## 二、中文翻译

**艺术形式**

**艺术**有多种**形式**，包括**绘画**、**雕塑**、**素描**、**版画**、**摄影**、**建筑**、**音乐**、**舞蹈**、**戏剧**、**电影**和**文学**。**视觉艺术**吸引**眼睛**；**表演艺术**涉及**身体**、**声音**和**舞台**。**艺术家**使用**色彩**、**形状**、**线条**、**质感**、**形式**和**空间**来表达**思想**和**情感**。**风格**、**体裁**和**运动**描述**艺术的****表达**。**创造力**、**想象力**和**技巧**至关重要。**艺术**可以**反映****文化**、**社会**和**历史**。它也可以**挑战**、**启发**和**激起**。**画廊**、**博物馆**和**展览**向**公众**展示**艺术品**。

# 16-02 音乐电影

## 一、精读文章

**Music and Film**

**Music** and **film** are powerful **art** forms. **Music** includes **classical**, **pop**, **rock**, **jazz**, **folk**, **hip-hop**, and **electronic**. It uses **melody**, **harmony**, **rhythm**, **beat**, **tempo**, and **instrument**. **Film** includes **genre** such as **comedy**, **drama**, **action**, **horror**, **documentary**, and **animation**. **Director**, **actor**, **actress**, **script**, **scene**, **soundtrack**, and **editing** are key **elements**. **Cinema**, **screening**, **festival**, and **award** celebrate **film**. **Music** and **film** **influence** **emotion**, **culture**, and **society**. They **entertain**, **inspire**, and **educate** **audience**.

# 16-03 博物馆

## 一、精读文章

**Museums**

A **museum** is an **institution** that **collects**, **preserves**, **studies**, and **displays** **objects** of **art**, **history**, **science**, and **culture**. **Types** include **art museum**, **history museum**, **science museum**, **natural history museum**, and **war museum**. **Collection**, **exhibit**, **exhibition**, **gallery**, and **display** are key **terms**. **Curator**, **archivist**, and **guide** manage and **interpret** **collection**. **Visitor** can **observe**, **learn**, and **engage** with **artifact**, **specimen**, and **document**. **Education**, **research**, and **conservation** are core **functions**. **Museum** **preserves** **heritage** for future **generation**. **Ticket**, **membership**, and **donation** support **operation**.

## 二、中文翻译

**博物馆**

**博物馆**是**收集**、**保存**、**研究**和**展示****艺术**、**历史**、**科学**和**文化****物品**的**机构**。**类型**包括**艺术博物馆**、**历史博物馆**、**科学博物馆**、**自然历史博物馆**和**战争博物馆**。**收藏**、**展品**、**展览**、**画廊**和**展示**是关键**术语**。**策展人**、**档案管理员**和**导游**管理和**解读****收藏**。**参观者**可以**观察**、**学习**并与**文物**、**标本**和**文献****互动**。**教育**、**研究**和**保护**是核心**功能**。**博物馆**为未来**世代****保存****遗产**。**门票**、**会员**和**捐赠**支持**运营**。

# 16-04 历史遗产

## 一、精读文章

**Historical Heritage**

**Historical heritage** includes **building**, **monument**, **site**, **ruin**, **artifact**, and **tradition** from the **past**. It represents **identity**, **memory**, and **culture**. **Heritage** can be **tangible** or **intangible**. **UNESCO** **World Heritage Site** protects **outstanding** **cultural** and **natural** **heritage**. **Preservation**, **restoration**, and **conservation** maintain **heritage** for future **generation**. **Threat** includes **war**, **neglect**, **pollution**, **urbanisation**, and **climate change**. **Tourism** can **fund** **conservation** but may also **damage** **site**. **Education** and **awareness** are essential to **protect** **heritage**.

## 二、中文翻译

**历史遗产**

**历史遗产**包括来自**过去**的**建筑**、**纪念碑**、**遗址**、**废墟**、**文物**和**传统**。它代表**身份**、**记忆**和**文化**。**遗产**可以是**有形的**或**无形的**。**联合国教科文组织****世界遗产地**保护**杰出**的**文化**和**自然****遗产**。**保存**、**修复**和**保护**为未来**世代**维护**遗产**。**威胁**包括**战争**、**忽视**、**污染**、**城市化**和**气候变化**。**旅游业**可以**资助****保护**，但也可能**破坏****遗址**。**教育**和**意识**对**保护****遗产**至关重要。

# 16-05 文化保护

## 一、精读文章

**Cultural Preservation**

**Cultural preservation** means **protecting** **culture**, **tradition**, **language**, **art**, **music**, **craft**, and **heritage** from **loss** and **decline**. It includes **documentation**, **conservation**, **restoration**, and **revitalisation**. **Globalisation**, **urbanisation**, and **mass tourism** can **threaten** **local culture**. **Government**, **organisation**, and **community** work together to **preserve** **intangible** and **tangible** **heritage**. **Museum**, **archive**, **library**, and **festival** play a key **role**. **Language** **revitalisation** and **craft** **transmission** are important. **Education** and **awareness** pass **culture** to the next **generation**. **Respect** and **authenticity** guide **preservation**.
