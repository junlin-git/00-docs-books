# 09-01 情绪

## 一、精读文章

**Emotions**

**Emotions** are complex **feelings** that affect **mood**, **thought**, and **behaviour**. Basic emotions include **anger**, **fear**, **joy**, **sadness**, **disgust**, and **surprise**. Other emotions include **love**, **hate**, **anxiety**, **stress**, **excitement**, **depression**, **frustration**, **jealousy**, **guilt**, **shame**, and **pride**. **Emotional** **expression** varies between people and cultures. Some people **suppress** their feelings; others **vent** or **express** them openly. **Empathy** and **sympathy** help us understand others. **Triggers** can cause an emotional **response**. Learning to **control** and **cope** with emotions is important for **mental health** and **well-being**.

## 二、中文翻译

**情绪**是影响**心情**、**思想**和**行为**的复杂**感受**。基本情绪包括**愤怒**、**恐惧**、**快乐**、**悲伤**、**厌恶**和**惊讶**。其他情绪包括**爱**、**恨**、**焦虑**、**压力**、**兴奋**、**抑郁**、**挫败**、**嫉妒**、**内疚**、**羞耻**和**骄傲**。**情绪**的**表达**因人和文化而异。有些人**压抑**自己的感受；另一些人则公开**发泄**或**表达**。**同理心**和**同情**帮助我们理解他人。**触发因素**可以引起情绪**反应**。学会**控制**和**应对**情绪对**心理健康**和**福祉**很重要。

# 09-02 压力

## 一、精读文章

**Stress**

**Stress** is a feeling of **pressure** or **tension**. It can be **acute** or **chronic**. Common **causes** include **workload**, **deadline**, **exam**, **money** problems, and **relationship** issues. **Symptoms** include **anxiety**, **fatigue**, **headache**, **insomnia**, and **irritability**. **Burnout** results from long-term **stress**. People **cope** with stress in different ways: **exercise**, **relaxation**, **meditation**, **mindfulness**, **hobby**, and **social support**. **Time management** and **balance** help reduce **pressure**. **Chronic stress** harms **health** and **well-being**. **Relaxation** and **rest** are essential.

## 二、中文翻译

**压力**

**压力**是一种**压迫**或**紧张**感。它可以是**急性的**或**慢性的**。常见**原因**包括**工作量**、**截止日期**、**考试**、**金钱**问题和**人际关系**问题。**症状**包括**焦虑**、**疲劳**、**头痛**、**失眠**和**易怒**。**倦怠**源于长期**压力**。人们以不同方式**应对**压力：**锻炼**、**放松**、**冥想**、**正念**、**爱好**和**社会支持**。**时间管理**和**平衡**有助于减少**压力**。**慢性压力**损害**健康**和**福祉**。**放松**和**休息**至关重要。

# 09-03 幸福

## 一、精读文章

**Happiness**

**Happiness** is a state of **well-being** and **contentment**. It includes **joy**, **pleasure**, **satisfaction**, and **fulfillment**. **Positive psychology** studies what makes people **happy**. **Factors** include **relationship**, **health**, **meaning**, **purpose**, **gratitude**, and **optimism**. **Money** can help, but only to a point. **Happiness** is not the same as **pleasure**. **Contentment** and **satisfaction** come from within. **Gratitude** and **kindness** increase **well-being**. **Social** **connection** and **support** are important. **Happiness** improves **health**, **creativity**, and **productivity**.

## 二、中文翻译

# 09-04 动机

## 一、精读文章

**Motivation**

**Motivation** is the **drive** or **desire** to do something. It can be **intrinsic** or **extrinsic**. **Goals**, **ambition**, **reward**, and **incentive** influence **motivation**. **Inspiration** and **encouragement** help people **persist**. **Effort** and **persistence** lead to **achievement** and **success**. **Self-discipline** and **willpower** are important. **Demotivation** can come from **failure**, **boredom**, or **lack** of **support**. **Motivation** affects **learning**, **work**, and **performance**.

**Personality**

**Personality** is a set of **traits** and **characteristics** that shape **behaviour**. Common **traits** include **introvert**, **extrovert**, **conscientiousness**, **agreeableness**, **neuroticism**, and **openness**. **Personality** is relatively **stable** but can **change** over time. It affects **relationship**, **work**, and **health**. **Nature** and **nurture** both play a role. **Character** and **temperament** are related concepts. **Self-awareness** helps people understand their **personality**.

**Interpersonal Relationships**

**Interpersonal relationships** include **friendship**, **family**, **colleague**, and **peer** relationships. # 09-07 行为习惯

## 一、精读文章

**Habits and Behaviour**

A **habit** is a repeated **behaviour** or **routine**. **Habits** can be good or bad. **Addiction**, **procrastination**, and **laziness** are negative **habits**. **Discipline**, **self-control**, and **willpower** help build positive **habits**. **Cue**, **routine**, and **reward** form a **habit** loop. **Trigger** and **environment** influence **behaviour**. **Consistency** and **repetition** strengthen **habits**. Changing **habits** requires **effort** and **patience**.

## 二、中文翻译

**行为习惯**

**习惯**是重复的**行为**或**常规**。**习惯**可以是好或坏。**成瘾**、**拖延**和**懒惰**是负面**习惯**。**纪律**、**自我控制**和**意志力**帮助建立积极**习惯**。**提示**、**常规**和**奖励**构成**习惯**回路。**触发因素**和**环境**影响**行为**。**一致性**和**重复**强化**习惯**。改变**习惯**需要**努力**和**耐心**。
