# 01 教育学习

# 01-01 教育体系与阶段

**Education Systems and Stages**

Every student’s **academic** journey follows a series of stages. It often begins with **kindergarten**, where children develop basic social skills. Then comes **primary** education, followed by **secondary** school. After secondary school, many students enter **tertiary** education at a **college**, an **institute**, or a **university**.

At a university, the **academic year** is usually divided into two **semesters** or three **terms**. Each **course**, or **module**, belongs to a **programme**. Every programme has a **curriculum** and a **syllabus**. Students often choose a **major** and sometimes a **minor**. The **faculty** in each **department**, such as the Department of Education, teaches these courses. The institute or college may offer **certificates**, **diplomas**, and **degrees**. An **undergraduate** studies for a bachelor’s **degree**, while a **postgraduate** continues to a master’s or doctorate. After graduation, a **graduate** becomes one of the **alumni**.

**Admission** and **enrolment** can be competitive. Students must meet the **qualification** requirements and pay **tuition** and other **fees**. Many apply for a **scholarship** or a **grant**. Each course is worth a number of **credits**. At the end of each semester or term, students take exams. The academic year usually starts in September and ends in June.

On **campus**, students also learn outside their main **discipline**. Some take a gap year, but the structure remains: from kindergarten to primary, secondary, and tertiary education. Finally, they earn a degree, a diploma, or a certificate, and begin the next stage of life.

**教育体系与阶段**

每个学生的**学术**旅程都遵循一系列阶段。它通常从**幼儿园**开始，孩子们在那里发展基本的社交技能。然后是**小学**教育，接着是**中学**。中学之后，许多学生进入**高等教育**，在**学院**、**研究所**或**大学**学习。

在大学里，**学年**通常分为两个**学期**或三个**学期**。每门**课程**，或**模块**，都属于一个**项目**。每个项目都有**课程体系**和**教学大纲**。学生通常选择**主修**，有时也选择**辅修**。每个**系**（如教育系）的**全体教员**教授这些课程。学院或大学可以提供**证书**、**文凭**和**学位**。**本科生**攻读学士**学位**，而**研究生**继续攻读硕士或博士学位。毕业后，**毕业生**成为**校友**中的一员。

**录取**和**注册**可能竞争激烈。学生必须满足**资格**要求，并支付**学费**和其他**费用**。许多人申请**奖学金**或**助学金**。每门课程都有一定的**学分**。在每个学期或学期末，学生参加考试。学年通常从九月开始，到六月结束。

在**校园**里，学生也在主**学科**之外学习。有些人会休学一年，但结构仍然不变：从幼儿园到小学、中学和高等教育。最后，他们获得学位、文凭或证书，并开始人生的下一阶段。

# 01-02 课程与教学

**Courses and Teaching**

At university, teaching takes many forms. A **lecture** is a large class where a **lecturer** or **professor** speaks. A **tutorial** is smaller, and a **seminar** or **workshop** involves discussion. Students give a **presentation** and complete an **assignment**, **essay**, **paper**, **project**, or **coursework**. A **thesis** or **dissertation** is longer. **Reading**, **revision**, and **feedback** are important. **Assessment** and **evaluation** measure progress. A **supervisor**, **tutor**, or **mentor** guides learners. The **principal**, **teacher**, or **educator** supports **students**, **pupils**, **learners**, **peers**, and **classmates**. **Group discussion**, **distance learning**, **online learning**, **blended learning**, and **self-study** are common. **Lifelong learning** needs **critical thinking** and **problem-solving**, not just **memorisation** or **rote learning**. **Motivation**, **concentration**, **participation**, and **attendance** matter. Avoid **plagiarism**; use **citation**, **reference**, and **bibliography**. A good **abstract** and **literature review** follow **methodology**. A **questionnaire**, **survey**, **interview**, or **observation** can support an **experiment** and **data analysis**. **Findings**, **conclusion**, **hypothesis**, and **theory** guide **research**. **Publication** in a **journal** or presentation at a **conference** or **symposium** shares results.

**课程与教学**

在大学里，教学有多种形式。**讲座**是**讲师**或**教授**授课的大课。**辅导课**规模较小，而**研讨课**或**工作坊**则涉及讨论。学生要做**展示**，并完成**作业**、**论文**、**文章**、**项目**或**课程作业**。**学位论文**或**毕业论文**篇幅更长。**阅读**、**复习**和**反馈**很重要。**评估**和**评价**衡量进步。**导师**、**辅导员**或**指导者**引导学习者。**校长**、**教师**或**教育者**支持**学生**、**小学生**、**学习者**、**同伴**和**同学**。**小组讨论**、**远程学习**、**在线学习**、**混合学习**和**自学**都很常见。**终身学习**需要**批判性思维**和**解决问题**能力，而不仅仅是**记忆**或**死记硬背**。**动机**、**专注**、**参与**和**出勤**都很重要。要避免**抄袭**；使用**引用**、**参考文献**和**参考书目**。好的**摘要**和**文献综述**要遵循**方法论**。**问卷**、**调查**、**访谈**或**观察**可以支持**实验**和**数据分析**。**研究结果**、**结论**、**假设**和**理论**指导**研究**。在**期刊**上**发表**，或在**会议**或**研讨会**上展示，可以分享成果。

# 01-03 学习方式与能力

**Learning Styles and Abilities**

Students have different **learning styles**. Some are **visual**, some are **auditory**, and others are **kinaesthetic**. Good learners use **independent learning**, **collaborative learning**, **active learning**, and avoid **passive learning**. They develop **critical thinking**, **problem-solving**, **creativity**, and **curiosity**. **Motivation** and **self-discipline** support **time management**, **note-taking**, and **summarising**. **Memorisation** is useful, but **understanding**, **application**, **analysis**, **synthesis**, and **evaluation** are deeper. **Reflection** and **feedback** improve learning. **Practice**, **repetition**, and **revision** strengthen **concentration**, **attention**, **memory**, and **retention**. **Transfer** helps use knowledge in new situations. Key outcomes include **skill**, **competence**, **literacy**, **numeracy**, and **digital literacy**. Students also need **communication**, **collaboration**, **presentation**, **research skills**, **analytical skills**, and **practical skills**, not only **theoretical knowledge**. **Lifelong learning** turns a student into an **autonomous learner**.

**学习方式与能力**

学生有不同的**学习风格**。有些人是**视觉型**，有些人是**听觉型**，还有些人是**动觉型**。优秀的学习者会运用**独立学习**、**合作学习**、**主动学习**，并避免**被动学习**。他们培养**批判性思维**、**解决问题**、**创造力**和**好奇心**。**动机**和**自律**支持**时间管理**、**记笔记**和**总结**。**记忆**有用，但**理解**、**应用**、**分析**、**综合**和**评价**更深入。**反思**和**反馈**能改善学习。**练习**、**重复**和**复习**能增强**专注**、**注意力**、**记忆**和**保持**。**迁移**有助于在新情境中使用知识。关键成果包括**技能**、**能力**、**读写能力**、**计算能力**和**数字素养**。学生还需要**交流**、**合作**、**展示**、**研究技能**、**分析技能**和**实践技能**，而不仅仅是**理论知识**。**终身学习**把学生变成**自主学习者**。

# 01-04 考试与评估

**Exams and Assessment**

Assessment takes many forms. An **exam**, **test**, or **quiz** checks knowledge. **Assessment** and **evaluation** may produce a **grade**, **mark**, or **score**. Students hope to **pass**, not **fail**. If they fail, they may **resit** or **retake**. A **deadline** requires **preparation** and **revision**. **Performance** is judged by a **criterion**, **standard**, or **rubric**. **Feedback** can be **formative** or **summative**. **Continuous assessment**, a **final exam**, **multiple-choice**, an **essay question**, an **oral exam**, a **practical exam**, **coursework**, and a **portfolio** are common. Results lead to a **certificate** or **qualification**. A **GPA**, **transcript**, and **academic record** show progress. **Cheating** and **plagiarism** are serious. An **invigilator** watches the room, an **examiner** marks the work, and a **candidate** waits for the **result**.

**考试与评估**

评估有多种形式。**考试**、**测试**或**小测验**检查知识。**评估**和**评价**可能产生**分数**、**分数**或**得分**。学生希望**通过**，而不是**不及格**。如果不及格，他们可能**重考**或**重修**。**截止日期**要求**准备**和**复习**。**表现**由**标准**、**标准**或**评分标准**来评判。**反馈**可以是**形成性的**或**总结性的**。**持续评估**、**期末考试**、**多项选择**、**论文题**、**口试**、**实践考试**、**课程作业**和**作品集**都很常见。结果会带来**证书**或**资格**。**平均绩点**、**成绩单**和**学业记录**显示进步。**作弊**和**抄袭**很严重。**监考人**监督考场，**考官**评分，**考生**等待**结果**。

# 01-05 教育政策与问题

**Education Policy and Problems**

Education **policy** and **reform** depend on **funding**, a **budget**, and **resource** allocation. Schools need **facility**, **infrastructure**, and **equipment**, including **technology**, **digital**, **online**, and **virtual** tools. A major problem is **inequality** and the lack of **equal opportunity**. **Access**, **inclusion**, and **diversity** are key goals, but **gender**, **gap**, and **disadvantage** remain. **Rural** and **urban** schools differ, and **poverty**, **migration**, and **globalisation** create new challenges. **International students** and **cultural exchange** bring benefits, while **brain drain** worries some countries. **Curriculum reform** and **standardised tests** are debated. Education should develop **creativity**, **innovation**, and **employability**, including **vocational**, **technical**, and **practical skills**. It should build **knowledge**, **competence**, **skill**, **literacy**, **numeracy**, and **digital literacy**.

**教育政策与问题**

教育**政策**和**改革**取决于**资金**、**预算**和**资源**分配。学校需要**设施**、**基础设施**和**设备**，包括**技术**、**数字**、**在线**和**虚拟**工具。一个主要问题是**不平等**和缺乏**平等机会**。**机会**、**包容**和**多样性**是关键目标，但**性别**、**差距**和**劣势**仍然存在。**农村**和**城市**学校不同，**贫困**、**迁移**和**全球化**带来新挑战。**国际学生**和**文化交流**带来好处，而**人才流失**让一些国家担忧。**课程改革**和**标准化考试**引发争论。教育应培养**创造力**、**创新**和**就业能力**，包括**职业的**、**技术的**和**实践技能**。它应建立**知识**、**能力**、**技能**、**读写能力**、**计算能力**和**数字素养**。

# 01-06 留学与国际教育

**Studying Abroad and International Education**

Many students choose to **study abroad** or go **overseas**. As an **international student**, you may join an **exchange programme** and apply for a **scholarship**. You need a **visa**, an **application**, and **admission**. You must pay **tuition** and **living cost**, and find **accommodation**. You may face **culture shock** and a **language barrier**, but you can **adapt** through **orientation**, a **host family**, and life on **campus**. Check your **qualification**, **credit transfer**, **degree**, **transcript**, **reference letter**, and **personal statement** before the **deadline**. Complete **enrolment** and visit the **international office** or **student union**. A **multicultural** environment gives a **global perspective**, makes you more **independent**, provides valuable **experience**, helps you **broaden horizons**, and improves **career prospects**.

**留学与国际教育**

许多学生选择**留学**或去**海外**。作为**国际学生**，你可能参加**交换项目**并申请**奖学金**。你需要**签证**、**申请**和**录取**。你必须支付**学费**和**生活成本**，并找到**住宿**。你可能会面临**文化冲击**和**语言障碍**，但你可以通过**迎新指导**、**寄宿家庭**和**校园**生活来**适应**。在**截止日期**前检查你的**资格**、**学分转换**、**学位**、**成绩单**、**推荐信**和**个人陈述**。完成**注册**，并访问**国际学生办公室**或**学生会**。**多元文化的**环境带来**全球视野**，让你更**独立**，提供宝贵的**经历**，帮助你**开阔眼界**，并改善**职业前景**。

# 02 学术研究

---

# 02-01 研究方法

**Research Methods**

**Research methods** can be **qualitative** or **quantitative**. A **methodology** explains the overall **approach** and **procedure**. Common methods include an **experiment**, **survey**, **interview**, **questionnaire**, **observation**, and **case study**. Researchers select a **sample** and define **variables**. They may test a **hypothesis** through **data collection** and **analysis**. A **participant** may join a **control group** or **experimental group**. Studies can show **correlation** or **causation**. Good research needs **reliability**, **validity**, and low **bias**. It should be **objective**, not **subjective**. A **literature review** and **theoretical framework** guide the study. **Empirical** work may use a **field study** or **laboratory** setting. A **longitudinal** study follows people over time, while a **cross-sectional** study compares groups at one point.

**研究方法**

**研究方法**可以是**定性的**或**定量的**。**方法论**解释整体的**方法**和**程序**。常见方法包括**实验**、**调查**、**访谈**、**问卷**、**观察**和**案例研究**。研究者选择**样本**并定义**变量**。他们可能通过**数据收集**和**分析**来检验**假设**。**参与者**可能加入**对照组**或**实验组**。研究可以显示**相关性**或**因果关系**。好的研究需要**信度**、**效度**和低**偏差**。它应该是**客观的**，而不是**主观的**。**文献综述**和**理论框架**指导研究。**实证**工作可能使用**实地研究**或**实验室**环境。**纵向**研究长期跟踪人们，而**横断面**研究在一个时间点比较不同群体。

# 02-02 数据与证据

**Data and Evidence**

**Data** and **evidence** support research. **Statistics** use a **figure**, **graph**, **chart**, or **table** to show a **percentage**, **proportion**, or **rate**. Researchers look for a **trend**, **pattern**, or **correlation**. **Significance** depends on **sample size**. Common measures include **mean**, **median**, **mode**, **standard deviation**, and **variance**. Data can be **qualitative** or **quantitative**, **primary** or **secondary**. A **source** must be **reliable**, **valid**, **accurate**, and **precise**. It should not be **biased** and should be **representative** and **random**. A **survey** produces a **result** or **finding**. A **conclusion** may **support** or **contradict** a theory. Data can **indicate**, **suggest**, **demonstrate**, or **prove** an idea.

**数据与证据**

**数据**和**证据**支持研究。**统计**使用**数字**、**图表**、**图表**或**表格**来显示**百分比**、**比例**或**比率**。研究者寻找**趋势**、**模式**或**相关性**。**显著性**取决于**样本量**。常见指标包括**平均数**、**中位数**、**众数**、**标准差**和**方差**。数据可以是**定性的**或**定量的**，**一手**或**二手**的。**来源**必须**可靠**、**有效**、**准确**和**精确**。它不应有**偏差**，并应具有**代表性**和**随机性**。**调查**产生**结果**或**发现**。**结论**可能**支持**或**反驳**理论。数据可以**表明**、**暗示**、**证明**或**证实**一个观点。

# 02-03 论文与发表

**Papers and Publication**

A **paper** or **article** is often based on a **thesis** or **dissertation**. To **publish** in a **journal**, authors **submit** a **manuscript**. The **draft** goes through **peer review**. An **editor** and **reviewer** may request **revision**. After **acceptance** or **rejection**, the paper moves to **proof** and **copyright**. A good paper includes an **abstract**, **keywords**, **introduction**, **methodology**, **results**, **discussion**, **conclusion**, **literature review**, **appendix**, **footnote**, and **endnote**. **Citation**, **reference**, and **bibliography** are essential. Avoid **plagiarism**. Some journals have a high **impact factor** or **citation index**. **Open access** and **preprint** are increasingly common. **Conference proceedings** also publish research.

**论文与发表**

**论文**或**文章**通常基于**学位论文**或**毕业论文**。要在**期刊**上**发表**，作者需**提交**一份**稿件**。**草稿**要经过**同行评审**。**编辑**和**审稿人**可能要求**修改**。在**录用**或**拒稿**后，论文进入**校样**和**版权**阶段。好的论文包括**摘要**、**关键词**、**引言**、**方法论**、**结果**、**讨论**、**结论**、**文献综述**、**附录**、**脚注**和**尾注**。**引用**、**参考文献**和**参考书目**必不可少。避免**抄袭**。一些期刊有很高的**影响因子**或**引文索引**。**开放获取**和**预印本**越来越普遍。**会议论文集**也会发表研究。

# 02-04 学术伦理

**Academic Ethics**

**Academic ethics** concerns **morality**, **integrity**, **honesty**, and **responsibility**. Researchers must avoid **plagiarism**, **fabrication**, and **falsification**. They should give proper **citation** and **acknowledgement**. **Consent**, **confidentiality**, **anonymity**, and **privacy** protect participants. **Informed consent** is required. **Research misconduct** includes **bias** and **conflict of interest**. **Peer review** must be fair. **Authorship** and **credit** should reflect contribution. **Accountability** and **transparency** are important. **Reproducibility** supports trust. **Animal testing** and **human subjects** need **ethics committee** or **IRB** approval.

**学术伦理**

**学术伦理**涉及**道德**、**诚信**、**诚实**和**责任**。研究者必须避免**抄袭**、**捏造**和**篡改**。他们应给予适当的**引用**和**致谢**。**同意**、**保密**、**匿名**和**隐私**保护参与者。需要**知情同意**。**学术不端**包括**偏差**和**利益冲突**。**同行评审**必须公平。**作者身份**和**功劳**应反映贡献。**问责**和**透明**很重要。**可重复性**支持信任。**动物实验**和**人类受试者**需要**伦理委员会**或**IRB**批准。

# 02-05 理论与假设

**Theory and Hypothesis**

A **theory** is a well-supported explanation. A **hypothesis** is a testable **assumption** or **premise**. A **concept** or **framework** helps organise ideas. A **model** or **paradigm** guides research. A **principle**, **law**, **theorem**, **proposition**, or **postulate** states a relationship. Researchers identify a **variable**, such as an **independent variable**, **dependent variable**, or **control variable**. They examine **correlation** and **causation**. A **prediction** is tested to **verify** or **falsify** a hypothesis. Evidence may **support** or **reject** it. **Empirical** work connects **theoretical** and **conceptual** ideas. **Abstract** concepts become **concrete** through study. A **phenomenon** and its **mechanism** are explained.

**理论与假设**

**理论**是有充分支持的解释。**假设**是可检验的**假定**或**前提**。**概念**或**框架**有助于组织观点。**模型**或**范式**指导研究。**原则**、**定律**、**定理**、**命题**或**公设**陈述一种关系。研究者识别**变量**，如**自变量**、**因变量**或**控制变量**。他们考察**相关性**和**因果关系**。**预测**被检验以**验证**或**证伪**假设。证据可能**支持**或**拒绝**它。**实证**工作连接**理论的**和**概念的**观点。**抽象的**概念通过研究变得**具体**。**现象**及其**机制**得到解释。

# 02-06 学术会议与期刊

**Academic Conferences and Journals**

An **academic conference**, **symposium**, **seminar**, or **workshop** brings researchers together. A **presentation**, **paper**, or **poster** shares findings. **Proceedings** collect the papers. A **journal** also **publishes** research. **Peer review** involves an **editor** and **reviewer**. Authors **submit** an **abstract** before the **deadline**. They pay a **registration** **fee** and attend a **session**, **keynote**, or **panel** **discussion**. **Networking** is valuable. A **speaker** presents, and the **host** organises the **venue**. Participants **attend**, **participate**, **present**, and **publish**.

**学术会议与期刊**

**学术会议**、**研讨会**、**研讨课**或**工作坊**把研究者聚在一起。**展示**、**论文**或**海报**分享发现。**会议论文集**收集论文。**期刊**也**发表**研究。**同行评审**涉及**编辑**和**审稿人**。作者在**截止日期**前**提交**一份**摘要**。他们支付**注册****费**，参加**分会**、**主题演讲**或**小组讨论**。**社交**很有价值。**演讲者**展示，**主办方**组织**场地**。参与者**出席**、**参与**、**展示**和**发表**。

# 03-01 气候变化

**Climate Change**

**Climate change** is one of the biggest **environmental** issues. The **greenhouse effect** traps **heat** in the **atmosphere**. **Carbon dioxide**, **methane**, and other **greenhouse gases** come from **fossil fuels**, **industry**, **transport**, and **deforestation**. **Global warming** causes **rising temperatures**, **melting ice**, **glacier** loss, and **sea-level rise**. It also leads to **extreme weather**, such as **drought**, **flood**, **heatwave**, and **storm**. **Climate scientists** use **climate models** to **predict** future **emissions** and **temperature** changes. **Mitigation** means reducing **carbon emissions**; **adaptation** means preparing for **unavoidable** impacts. **International agreements**, like the **Paris Agreement**, aim to limit **global warming** and achieve **carbon neutrality**. **Renewable energy** and **energy efficiency** are key solutions.

**气候变化**

**气候变化**是最大的**环境**问题之一。**温室效应**把**热量**困在**大气**中。**二氧化碳**、**甲烷**和其他**温室气体**来自**化石燃料**、**工业**、**交通**和**森林砍伐**。**全球变暖**导致**气温上升**、**冰川融化**、**冰川**消失和**海平面上升**。它还导致**极端天气**，如**干旱**、**洪水**、**热浪**和**风暴**。**气候科学家**使用**气候模型**来**预测**未来的**排放**和**温度**变化。**减缓**意味着减少**碳排放**；**适应**意味着为**不可避免的**影响做准备。**国际协议**，如**巴黎协定**，旨在限制**全球变暖**并实现**碳中和**。**可再生能源**和**能源效率**是关键解决方案。

# 03-02 环境污染

**Environmental Pollution**

**Pollution** harms **air**, **water**, and **soil**. **Air pollution** comes from **factories**, **vehicles**, and **burning** fuels. It produces **smog**, **particulate matter**, and **toxic** gases. **Water pollution** is caused by **industrial waste**, **sewage**, **chemicals**, **pesticides**, and **plastic**. **Soil contamination** comes from **heavy metals**, **fertilisers**, and **oil spills**. **Noise pollution** and **light pollution** also affect cities. **Waste** management includes **landfill**, **incineration**, **recycling**, and **composting**. **Plastic waste** harms **marine life**. **Pollutants** can **accumulate** in the **food chain**. **Governments** set **emission standards** and **regulations**. **Clean-up** and **remediation** are expensive. **Public awareness** and **individual action** can reduce pollution.

**环境污染**

**污染**危害**空气**、**水**和**土壤**。**空气污染**来自**工厂**、**车辆**和**燃烧**燃料。它产生**烟雾**、**颗粒物**和**有毒**气体。**水污染**由**工业废物**、**污水**、**化学品**、**农药**和**塑料**造成。**土壤污染**来自**重金属**、**化肥**和**石油泄漏**。**噪音污染**和**光污染**也影响城市。**废物**管理包括**垃圾填埋**、**焚烧**、**回收**和**堆肥**。**塑料废物**危害**海洋生物**。**污染物**会在**食物链**中**积累**。**政府**制定**排放标准**和**法规**。**清理**和**修复**代价高昂。**公众意识**和**个人行动**可以减少污染。

# 03-03 资源保护

**Resource Conservation**

**Resource conservation** means protecting **natural resources** for the future. **Non-renewable resources**, such as **coal**, **oil**, **natural gas**, and **minerals**, will eventually **run out**. **Renewable resources**, such as **solar**, **wind**, **hydro**, and **biomass**, can be **sustainable**. **Conservation** includes **reducing** **consumption**, improving **efficiency**, and **reusing** materials. **Recycling** saves **raw materials** and **energy**. **Water conservation** protects **freshwater** and **groundwater**. **Forest** protection prevents **logging** and **desertification**. **Soil** conservation prevents **erosion**. **Biodiversity** depends on **habitat** protection. **Governments** create **protected areas**, **national parks**, and **reserves**. **Sustainable management** balances **economic growth** and **environmental protection**.

**资源保护**

**资源保护**意味着为未来保护**自然资源**。**不可再生资源**，如**煤炭**、**石油**、**天然气**和**矿物**，最终会**耗尽**。**可再生资源**，如**太阳能**、**风能**、**水能**和**生物质能**，可以是**可持续的**。**保护**包括**减少****消耗**、提高**效率**和**再利用**材料。**回收**节省**原材料**和**能源**。**水资源保护**保护**淡水**和**地下水**。**森林**保护防止**伐木**和**沙漠化**。**土壤**保护防止**侵蚀**。**生物多样性**依赖于**栖息地**保护。**政府**建立**保护区**、**国家公园**和**保留地**。**可持续管理**平衡**经济增长**和**环境保护**。

# 03-04 生物多样性

**Biodiversity**

**Biodiversity** means the **variety** of **life** on Earth. It includes **species**, **genetic** diversity, and **ecosystem** diversity. **Ecosystems** such as **forests**, **wetlands**, **coral reefs**, and **grasslands** support **wildlife**. **Habitat loss**, **deforestation**, **pollution**, **climate change**, and **overexploitation** threaten **species**. Many **plants** and **animals** become **endangered**, **vulnerable**, or **extinct**. **Conservation** efforts include **protected areas**, **wildlife reserves**, **breeding programmes**, and **habitat restoration**. **Endangered species** such as **tigers**, **pandas**, and **whales** need protection. **Invasive species** can harm **native** **species**. **Ecosystem services** include **pollination**, **water purification**, and **soil fertility**. Protecting **biodiversity** is essential for **ecological balance** and human **well-being**.

**生物多样性**

**生物多样性**意味着地球上**生命**的**多样性**。它包括**物种**、**遗传**多样性和**生态系统**多样性。**生态系统**如**森林**、**湿地**、**珊瑚礁**和**草原**支持**野生动物**。**栖息地丧失**、**森林砍伐**、**污染**、**气候变化**和**过度开发**威胁**物种**。许多**植物**和**动物**变得**濒危**、**易危**或**灭绝**。**保护**努力包括**保护区**、**野生动物保留地**、**繁殖计划**和**栖息地恢复**。**濒危物种**如**老虎**、**熊猫**和**鲸鱼**需要保护。**入侵物种**会伤害**本地** **物种**。**生态系统服务**包括**授粉**、**水净化**和**土壤肥力**。保护**生物多样性**对**生态平衡**和人类**福祉**至关重要。

# 03-05 可持续发展

**Sustainable Development**

**Sustainable development** meets present needs without harming future **generations**. It balances **economic growth**, **social equity**, and **environmental protection**. The **UN Sustainable Development Goals** (SDGs) set **targets** for **poverty**, **hunger**, **health**, **education**, **clean water**, **clean energy**, and **climate action**. **Sustainability** requires **responsible consumption**, **resource efficiency**, and **green technology**. **Renewable energy**, **public transport**, and **sustainable agriculture** reduce **carbon footprint**. **Governments**, **businesses**, and **individuals** share **responsibility**. **Long-term** planning and **international cooperation** are essential. **Quality of life** and **well-being** matter, not just **profit**. **Circular economy** and **waste reduction** support a **greener** future.

**可持续发展**

**可持续发展**满足当前需求，而不损害未来**世代**。它平衡**经济增长**、**社会公平**和**环境保护**。**联合国可持续发展目标**（SDGs）为**贫困**、**饥饿**、**健康**、**教育**、**清洁水**、**清洁能源**和**气候行动**设定了**目标**。**可持续性**需要**负责任的消费**、**资源效率**和**绿色技术**。**可再生能源**、**公共交通**和**可持续农业**减少**碳足迹**。**政府**、**企业**和**个人**分担**责任**。**长期**规划和**国际合作**至关重要。**生活质量**和**福祉**很重要，而不仅仅是**利润**。**循环经济**和**减少废物**支持更**绿色**的未来。

# 03-06 环保政策

**Environmental Policy**

**Environmental policy** aims to protect the **environment** through **laws**, **regulations**, and **incentives**. **Governments** set **emission standards**, **pollution limits**, and **environmental taxes**. **Regulation** can **ban** harmful **chemicals**, **control** **waste disposal**, and **protect** **natural resources**. **International agreements**, such as the **Paris Agreement** and **Kyoto Protocol**, promote **global cooperation**. **Carbon pricing**, **cap and trade**, and **subsidies** for **renewable energy** are common **policy tools**. **Environmental impact assessment** (EIA) evaluates **projects**. **Compliance** and **enforcement** are important. **Public participation** and **transparency** improve **governance**. **Green policy** supports **sustainable development** and **clean technology**.

**环保政策**

**环保政策**旨在通过**法律**、**法规**和**激励措施**保护**环境**。**政府**制定**排放标准**、**污染限值**和**环境税**。**监管**可以**禁止**有害**化学品**，**控制****废物处理**，并**保护****自然资源**。**国际协议**，如**巴黎协定**和**京都议定书**，促进**全球合作**。**碳定价**、**总量管制与交易**和**可再生能源****补贴**是常见的**政策工具**。**环境影响评估**（EIA）评估**项目**。**合规**和**执法**很重要。**公众参与**和**透明度**改善**治理**。**绿色政策**支持**可持续发展**和**清洁技术**。

# 03-07 个人环保行为

**Individual Environmental Actions**

**Individuals** can take many **actions** to protect the **environment**. **Reduce**, **reuse**, and **recycle** are key **principles**. People can **save energy** by turning off **lights** and using **public transport**, **cycling**, or **walking**. **Water conservation** includes taking shorter **showers** and fixing **leaks**. **Consumers** can choose **sustainable products**, **organic food**, and **reusable bags**. **Waste separation** and **composting** reduce **landfill** waste. **Planting trees** and **gardening** support **biodiversity**. **Volunteering** for **clean-ups** helps **communities**. **Awareness** and **education** encourage **green habits**. Small **changes** can reduce **carbon footprint** and **protect** the **planet**.

**个人环保行为**

**个人**可以采取许多**行动**来保护**环境**。**减少**、**再利用**和**回收**是关键**原则**。人们可以通过关**灯**、使用**公共交通**、**骑自行车**或**步行**来**节约能源**。**水资源保护**包括缩短**淋浴**时间和修理**漏水**。**消费者**可以选择**可持续产品**、**有机食品**和**可重复使用的袋子**。**垃圾分类**和**堆肥**减少**垃圾填埋**废物。**植树**和**园艺**支持**生物多样性**。**志愿服务**参加**清理**活动帮助**社区**。**意识**和**教育**鼓励**绿色习惯**。小小的**改变**可以减少**碳足迹**并**保护****地球**。
