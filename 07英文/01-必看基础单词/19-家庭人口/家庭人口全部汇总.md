# 19-01 家庭结构

## 一、精读文章

**Family Structure**

**Family structure** refers to the **composition** and **organisation** of a **family**. **Types** include **nuclear family**, **extended family**, **single-parent family**, **blended family**, and **childless** **couple**. A **nuclear family** consists of **parent** and **children**. An **extended family** includes **grandparent**, **aunt**, **uncle**, and **cousin**. **Marriage**, **divorce**, **remarriage**, and **separation** change **family** **structure**. **Kinship**, **lineage**, and **household** are key **concepts**. **Role** of **father**, **mother**, and **child** varies across **culture**. **Urbanisation**, **migration**, and **globalisation** affect **family** **pattern**. **Single** **parent**, **orphan**, and **adoption** are common **situations**. **Support**, **care**, and **responsibility** are essential.

## 二、中文翻译

**家庭结构**指**家庭**的**构成**和**组织**。**类型**包括**核心家庭**、**大家庭**、**单亲家庭**、**混合家庭**和**无子女** **夫妇**。**核心家庭**由**父母**和**子女**组成。**大家庭**包括**祖父母**、**姑姨**、**叔舅**和**堂表兄弟姐妹**。**婚姻**、**离婚**、**再婚**和**分居**改变**家庭** **结构**。**亲属关系**、**血统**和**住户**是关键**概念**。**父亲**、**母亲**和**孩子**的**角色**因**文化**而异。**城市化**、**迁移**和**全球化**影响**家庭** **模式**。**单亲**、**孤儿**和**收养**是常见**情况**。**支持**、**照顾**和**责任**至关重要。

# 19-02 婚姻

## 一、精读文章

**Marriage**

**Marriage** is a **legal**, **social**, and **cultural** **institution** between two **people**. It involves **commitment**, **love**, **partnership**, and **responsibility**. **Types** include **monogamy**, **polygamy**, and **arranged marriage**. **Wedding**, **ceremony**, **vow**, **ring**, and **honeymoon** are common **traditions**. **Spouse**, **husband**, and **wife** have **rights** and **obligation**. **Divorce**, **separation**, and **widowhood** end **marriage**. **Age**, **consent**, and **law** regulate **marriage**. **Culture**, **religion**, and **family** influence **choice** of **partner**. **Same-sex marriage** is legal in many **countries**. **Communication**, **trust**, and **respect** sustain a healthy **marriage**.

## 二、中文翻译

**婚姻**是两个人之间的**法律**、**社会**和**文化****制度**。它涉及**承诺**、**爱**、**伴侣关系**和**责任**。**类型**包括**一夫一妻制**、**一夫多妻制**和**包办婚姻**。**婚礼**、**仪式**、**誓言**、**戒指**和**蜜月**是常见**传统**。**配偶**、**丈夫**和**妻子**拥有**权利**和**义务**。**离婚**、**分居**和**丧偶**结束**婚姻**。**年龄**、**同意**和**法律**规范**婚姻**。**文化**、**宗教**和**家庭**影响**伴侣**的**选择**。**同性婚姻**在许多**国家**是合法的。**沟通**、**信任**和**尊重**维持健康的**婚姻**。

# 19-03 育儿

## 一、精读文章

**Parenting**

**Parenting** is the **process** of **raising** and **caring** for a **child**. It includes **nurture**, **discipline**, **education**, **protection**, and **support**. **Parenting** **style** may be **authoritative**, **authoritarian**, **permissive**, or **uninvolved**. **Attachment**, **bond**, and **trust** develop early. **Parent** provides **love**, **care**, **guidance**, and **boundary**. **Child** needs **nutrition**, **healthcare**, **education**, and **play**. **Working** **parent** **balance** **job** and **family**. **Single** **parent** and **grandparent** may take **responsibility**. **Childcare**, **nursery**, and **school** support **family**. **Positive** **parenting** improves **development**, **confidence**, and **well-being**.

## 二、中文翻译

**Generational Relationships**

**Generational relationships** are the **connections** between **different** **generations** within a **family** or **society**. They include **grandparent**, **parent**, **child**, and **grandchild**. **Generation** **gap** may cause **conflict**, **misunderstanding**, and **tension**. **Value**, **attitude**, and **lifestyle** differ between **young** and **old**. **Tradition** and **modernity** may **clash**. **Respect**, **communication**, and **understanding** bridge the **gap**. **Care** for **elderly** and **support** for **young** are **responsibilities**. **Inheritance**, **legacy**, and **memory** connect **generations**. **Intergenerational** **programme** and **dialogue** promote **harmony**.

# 19-05 人口增长

## 一、精读文章

**Population Growth**

**Population growth** is the **increase** in the **number** of **people** in a **region** or **country**. It is affected by **birth rate**, **death rate**, **fertility**, **migration**, and **life expectancy**. **Rapid** **growth** can cause **overpopulation**, **resource** **shortage**, **pollution**, and **unemployment**. **Slow** **growth** or **decline** leads to **ageing** and **labour shortage**. **Demographic** **transition** describes **change** from high to low **birth** and **death** **rate**. **Family** **planning**, **education**, and **healthcare** influence **growth**. **Government** **policy** may **encourage** or **limit** **population** **growth**. **Sustainable** **development** must **balance** **population**, **resource**, and **environment**.

## 二、中文翻译

**人口增长**

**人口增长**是**地区**或**国家**中**人口****数量**的**增加**。它受**出生率**、**死亡率**、**生育率**、**迁移**和**预期寿命**影响。**快速****增长**可能导致**人口过剩**、**资源****短缺**、**污染**和**失业**。**缓慢****增长**或**下降**导致**老龄化**和**劳动力短缺**。**人口****转变**描述从高**出生**和**死亡****率**到低**出生**和**死亡****率**的**变化**。**计划生育**、**教育**和**医疗**影响**增长**。**政府****政策**可能**鼓励**或**限制****人口****增长**。**可持续****发展**必须**平衡****人口**、**资源**和**环境**。

# 19-06 老龄化

## 一、精读文章

**Ageing**

**Ageing** is the **process** of **growing** **old**. It is a **demographic** **trend** in many **countries**. **Life expectancy** rises while **birth rate** and **fertility** fall. The **elderly** and **senior** **population** grows. **Old age** brings **opportunity** and **challenge**. **Retirement** and **pension** systems face **pressure**. **Healthcare**, **care**, and **nursing** services must **expand**. **Dependency** ratio rises, and the **workforce** may face **labour shortage**. **Migration** and **immigration** can help. **Policy**, **welfare**, and **social** support are **essential**. **Isolation**, **loneliness**, **disease**, **disability**, and reduced **mobility** affect many **elderly** people. **Technology** and **assistive** devices can help. **Family** and **generation** ties matter. An **ageing** society can also develop a **silver economy** and create new **opportunities**.

# 19-07 移民

## 一、精读文章

**Migration**

**Migration** is the **movement** of **people** from one **place** to another. It can be **internal**, **international**, **voluntary**, or **forced**. **Push** **factor** includes **poverty**, **conflict**, **persecution**, and **unemployment**. **Pull** **factor** includes **opportunity**, **education**, **freedom**, and **family**. **Migrant**, **immigrant**, **emigrant**, **refugee**, and **asylum** **seeker** are key **terms**. **Migration** affects **population**, **economy**, **culture**, and **society**. **Integration**, **assimilation**, and **multiculturalism** describe **adaptation**. **Remittance** supports **family** and **community**. **Government** **policy**, **border** **control**, and **visa** regulate **migration**. **Migration** can **enrich** **culture** but may cause **tension** and **conflict**.
