# 10-01 新闻

## 一、精读文章

**News**

**News** is information about recent **events**. It is reported by **journalists**, **reporters**, and **correspondents** through **newspaper**, **magazine**, **television**, **radio**, and **online** **media**. A **news** **story** should be **accurate**, **objective**, and **balanced**. **Sources** must be **reliable** and **credible**. **Headlines**, **articles**, **reports**, and **interviews** present **facts** and **opinions**. **Editor** and **editorial** decide what to **publish**. **Press** **freedom** allows **media** to **investigate** and **report** without **censorship**. **Bias**, **sensationalism**, and **propaganda** can distort **news**. **Readers** and **viewers** should **evaluate** **information** critically.

# 10-02 广告

## 一、精读文章

**Advertising**

**Advertising** is a form of **marketing** that **promotes** **products**, **services**, or **brands**. It uses **media** such as **television**, **radio**, **newspaper**, **magazine**, **billboard**, and **online** **platforms**. **Advertisers** create **campaigns** with **slogans**, **images**, and **messages** to **attract** **consumers**. **Target** **audience** and **demographics** guide **advertising**. **Persuasion**, **appeal**, and **emotion** are common **techniques**. **Commercials** and **sponsored** **content** are everywhere. **Advertising** can **inform**, but it can also **mislead**. **Regulation** and **ethics** aim to **protect** **consumers**, especially **children**. **Brand** **loyalty** and **consumer** **behaviour** are key **goals**.

## 二、中文翻译

**广告**

# 10-03 社交媒体

## 一、精读文章

**Social Media**

**Social media** **platforms** such as **Facebook**, **Twitter**, **Instagram**, **TikTok**, and **YouTube** allow **users** to **create**, **share**, and **interact** with **content**. **Users** build **profiles**, **post** **updates**, **photos**, and **videos**, and **comment** or **like**. **Social** **networking** connects **friends**, **family**, and **strangers**. **Influencers** and **bloggers** reach large **audiences**. **Hashtags** and **trends** spread quickly. **Social media** can **inform**, **entertain**, and **mobilise**, but also spread **misinformation**, **fake news**, and **cyberbullying**. **Privacy**, **security**, and **screen time** are major **concerns**. **Digital literacy** helps **users** stay safe and **responsible**.

**Information Spread**

**Information** spreads through **media**, **social networks**, and **word of mouth**. **Communication** **channels** include **television**, **radio**, **newspaper**, **internet**, and **social media**. **Messages** can reach a **global** **audience** quickly. **Viral** **content** spreads fast. **Journalists**, **bloggers**, and **influencers** act as **gatekeepers**. **Filter** **bubbles** and **echo** **chambers** limit **exposure** to different **views**. **Propaganda** and **rumour** can **manipulate** **public** **opinion**. **Accuracy** and **verification** are essential. **Media** **literacy** helps people **evaluate** **sources** and **avoid** **misinformation**.

# 10-05 媒体影响

**Media Influence**

**Media** has a powerful **influence** on **society**, **culture**, and **individual** **behaviour**. It **shapes** **public** **opinion**, **attitudes**, and **values**. **Television**, **film**, **advertising**, and **social media** affect how people **think**, **feel**, and **act**. **Media** can **educate**, **inform**, and **entertain**, but also **manipulate**, **persuade**, and **mislead**. **Agenda-setting**, **framing**, and **cultivation** describe **media** **effects**. **Violence**, **body** **image**, and **consumerism** are common **concerns**. **Children** and **young** people are especially **vulnerable**. **Critical** **thinking** and **media** **literacy** help **audiences** **resist** **harmful** **influence**.

## 一、精读文章

**Misinformation and Fake News**

**Misinformation** is false or **inaccurate** **information**. **Disinformation** is **deliberately** **false** **information** meant to **deceive**. **Fake news** spreads through **social media**, **websites**, and **messaging** **apps**. **Rumours**, **hoaxes**, and **conspiracy** **theories** confuse the **public**. **Bots** and **trolls** amplify **false** **content**. **Fact-checking**, **verification**, and **source** **evaluation** are essential. **Media** **literacy** helps **users** **identify** **misinformation**. **Platforms** and **governments** try to **regulate** and **remove** **harmful** **content**. **Trust** in **media** and **democracy** is at **risk**.

# 10-07 言论自由

## 一、精读文章

**Freedom of Speech**

**Freedom of speech** is the **right** to **express** **opinions** without **censorship** or **fear**. It is a **fundamental** **human right** and a **cornerstone** of **democracy**. It includes **spoken**, **written**, and **artistic** **expression**. However, **limits** exist: **hate speech**, **defamation**, **incitement**, and **obscenity** are often **restricted**. **Censorship**, **propaganda**, and **suppression** threaten **free** **speech**. **Media**, **press**, and **internet** **freedom** are closely related. **Debate**, **dissent**, and **criticism** are essential for **progress**. **Responsibility** comes with **freedom**.

## 二、中文翻译
