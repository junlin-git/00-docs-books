**家庭结构**指**家庭**的**构成**和**组织**。**类型**包括**核心家庭**、**大家庭**、**单亲家庭**、**混合家庭**和**无子女** **夫妇**。**核心家庭**由**父母**和**子女**组成。**大家庭**包括**祖父母**、**姑姨**、**叔舅**和**堂表兄弟姐妹**。**婚姻**、**离婚**、**再婚**和**分居**改变**家庭** **结构**。**亲属关系**、**血统**和**住户**是关键**概念**。**父亲**、**母亲**和**孩子**的**角色**因**文化**而异。**城市化**、**迁移**和**全球化**影响**家庭** **模式**。**单亲**、**孤儿**和**收养**是常见**情况**。**支持**、**照顾**和**责任**至关重要。

# 19-06 老龄化

**Ageing**

**Ageing** is the **process** of **growing** **old**. It is a **demographic** **trend** in many **countries**. **Life expectancy** rises while **birth rate** and **fertility** fall. The **elderly** and **senior** **population** grows. **Old age** brings **opportunity** and **challenge**. **Retirement** and **pension** systems face **pressure**. **Healthcare**, **care**, and **nursing** services must **expand**. **Dependency** ratio rises, and the **workforce** may face **labour shortage**. **Migration** and **immigration** can help. **Policy**, **welfare**, and **social** support are **essential**. **Isolation**, **loneliness**, **disease**, **disability**, and reduced **mobility** affect many **elderly** people. **Technology** and **assistive** devices can help. **Family** and **generation** ties matter. An **ageing** society can also develop a **silver economy** and create new **opportunities**.

**老龄化**

**老龄化**是**变老**的**过程**。在许多**国家**，它是一个**人口****趋势**。**预期寿命**上升，而**出生率**和**生育率**下降。**老年人**和**年长者**人口增长。**老年**带来**机遇**和**挑战**。**退休**和**养老金**体系面临**压力**。**医疗**、**护理**和**看护**服务必须**扩大**。**抚养**比上升，**劳动力**可能面临**劳动力短缺**。**迁移**和**移民**可以提供帮助。**政策**、**福利**和**社会**支持至关重要。**孤立**、**孤独**、**疾病**、**残疾**和**行动能力**下降影响许多**老年人**。**技术**和**辅助**设备可以提供帮助。**家庭**和**代际**纽带很重要。**老龄化**社会也可以发展**银发经济**并创造新**机遇**。

**Data** can be **presented** using **number**, **figure**, **statistic**, **percentage**, **proportion**, **ratio**, **rate**, **average**, **median**, **range**, and **frequency**. **Graph**, **chart**, **table**, **diagram**, and **pie** **chart** **display** **data**. **Increase**, **decrease**, **peak**, **bottom**, **fluctuate**, and **stabilise** describe **change**. **Account for**, **constitute**, **comprise**, and **make up** describe **proportion**. **Compare**, **contrast**, and **rank** organise **data**. **Approximately**, **roughly**, **nearly**, **about**, and **just over** show **estimation**. **Trend**, **pattern**, and **correlation** reveal **relationship**. **Accurate** and **reliable** **data** support **analysis**.

**描述数据**

**数据**可以使用**数字**、**数字**、**统计数字**、**百分比**、**比例**、**比率**、**率**、**平均数**、**中位数**、**范围**和**频率**来**呈现**。**图表**、**图表**、**表格**、**示意图**和**饼状图****展示****数据**。**增加**、**减少**、**达到顶峰**、**触底**、**波动**和**稳定**描述**变化**。**占**、**构成**、**包括**和**组成**描述**比例**。**比较**、**对比**和**排名**组织**数据**。**大约**、**粗略地**、**将近**、**大约**和**略高于**显示**估计**。**趋势**、**模式**和**相关性**揭示**关系**。**准确的**和**可靠的****数据**支持**分析**。

# 20-10 观点态度

**Opinions and Attitudes**

An **opinion** is a **view**, **belief**, or **judgment** about something. An **attitude** is a **feeling** or **position** toward an **issue**. People may **agree**, **disagree**, **support**, **oppose**, **approve**, or **criticise**. **Argument**, **claim**, **assertion**, and **position** express **opinion**. **Evidence**, **reason**, and **example** support **argument**. **Bias**, **prejudice**, and **assumption** may affect **judgment**. **Objective** and **subjective** describe **attitude**. **Neutral**, **positive**, **negative**, **optimistic**, and **pessimistic** describe **outlook**. **Debate**, **discussion**, and **controversy** involve **different** **views**. **Respect** and **open-mindedness** improve **communication**.

**观点态度**

**观点**是对某事的**看法**、**信念**或**判断**。**态度**是对**问题**的**感受**或**立场**。人们可能**同意**、**不同意**、**支持**、**反对**、**赞成**或**批评**。**论点**、**主张**、**断言**和**立场**表达**观点**。**证据**、**理由**和**例子**支持**论点**。**偏见**、**成见**和**假设**可能影响**判断**。**客观的**和**主观的**描述**态度**。**中立的**、**积极的**、**消极的**、**乐观的**和**悲观的**描述**看法**。**辩论**、**讨论**和**争议**涉及**不同**的**观点**。**尊重**和**开放心态**改善**沟通**。
