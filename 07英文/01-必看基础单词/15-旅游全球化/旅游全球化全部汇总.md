# 15-01 旅游业

## 一、精读文章

**Tourism**

**Tourism** is the **industry** of **travel** for **leisure**, **business**, or **pleasure**. It includes **hotel**, **resort**, **restaurant**, **tour**, **guide**, **attraction**, and **transport**. **Tourists** visit **destination** to experience **culture**, **nature**, **history**, and **entertainment**. **Tourism** creates **employment**, **income**, and **infrastructure**. It can **promote** **understanding** and **economic** **growth**. However, **overtourism**, **pollution**, **congestion**, and **damage** to **heritage** are **concerns**. **Sustainable** **tourism** aims to **balance** **profit**, **environment**, and **community**. **Seasonality**, **safety**, and **service** **quality** affect the **industry**.

## 二、中文翻译

**旅游业**

**旅游业**是为**休闲**、**商务**或**娱乐**而**旅行**的**行业**。它包括**酒店**、**度假村**、**餐厅**、**旅行**、**导游**、**景点**和**交通**。**游客**访问**目的地**，体验**文化**、**自然**、**历史**和**娱乐**。**旅游业**创造**就业**、**收入**和**基础设施**。它可以**促进****理解**和**经济****增长**。然而，**过度旅游**、**污染**、**拥堵**和对**遗产**的**破坏**是**担忧**。**可持续****旅游**旨在**平衡****利润**、**环境**和**社区**。**季节性**、**安全**和**服务****质量**影响**行业**。

# 15-02 国际旅行

## 一、精读文章

**International Travel**

**International travel** involves **crossing** **borders** to visit **foreign** **countries**. **Travellers** need **passport**, **visa**, **ticket**, **insurance**, and sometimes **vaccination**. **Airport**, **airline**, **flight**, **customs**, and **immigration** are part of the **journey**. **Language**, **currency**, **time zone**, and **culture** differ. **Tourists** may experience **culture shock** and **jet lag**. **Transport**, **accommodation**, and **itinerary** must be **planned**. **Safety**, **health**, and **security** are **concerns**. **International travel** promotes **tolerance**, **understanding**, and **global** **connection**.

## 二、中文翻译

# 15-03 文化交流

## 一、精读文章

**Cultural Exchange**

**Cultural exchange** is the **sharing** of **ideas**, **customs**, **values**, and **traditions** between **different** **cultures**. It happens through **travel**, **education**, **festival**, **art**, **music**, **food**, and **language**. **Exchange** **programme** and **study** **abroad** allow **students** to experience **foreign** **culture**. **Cultural exchange** promotes **tolerance**, **respect**, **understanding**, and **diversity**. It can **challenge** **stereotype** and **prejudice**. However, it may also cause **culture shock**, **conflict**, or **loss** of **identity**. **Globalisation** increases **interaction** but may lead to **homogenisation**. **Preservation** of **local** **culture** is important.

## 二、中文翻译

**文化交流**

**文化交流**是**不同****文化**之间**思想**、**习俗**、**价值观**和**传统**的**分享**。它通过**旅行**、**教育**、**节日**、**艺术**、**音乐**、**食物**和**语言**发生。**交换****项目**和**留学**让**学生**体验**外国****文化**。**文化交流**促进**宽容**、**尊重**、**理解**和**多样性**。它可以**挑战****刻板印象**和**偏见**。然而，它也可能导致**文化冲击**、**冲突**或**身份**的**丧失**。**全球化**增加**互动**，但可能导致**同质化**。**本地****文化**的**保护**很重要。

# 15-04 全球化影响

## 一、精读文章

**The Impact of Globalisation**

**Globalisation** is the **integration** of **economies**, **societies**, and **cultures** worldwide. It increases **trade**, **investment**, **migration**, and **communication**. **Multinational** **corporation** operates across **border**. **Free trade**, **tariff**, and **trade agreement** shape **commerce**. **Outsourcing** and **offshoring** move **job** and **production**. **Global** **supply chain** connects **producer** and **consumer**. **Competition** and **efficiency** increase, but **inequality** and **exploitation** may worsen. **Cultural exchange** and **homogenisation** occur. **Protectionism** and **nationalism** push back. **Sustainable** and **fair trade** aim to balance **growth** and **equity**.

## 二、中文翻译

**全球化影响**

**全球化**是**经济**、**社会**和**文化**在全球范围内的**一体化**。它增加**贸易**、**投资**、**迁移**和**交流**。**跨国公司**跨越**边境**经营。**自由贸易**、**关税**和**贸易协定**塑造**商业**。**外包**和**离岸外包**转移**工作**和**生产**。**全球****供应链**连接**生产者**和**消费者**。**竞争**和**效率**提高，但**不平等**和**剥削**可能加剧。**文化交流**和**同质化**发生。**保护主义**和**民族主义**反弹。**可持续**和**公平贸易**旨在平衡**增长**和**公平**。

# 15-05 本地文化

## 一、精读文章

**Local Culture**

**Local culture** includes **custom**, **tradition**, **language**, **festival**, **cuisine**, **craft**, **music**, and **ritual** of a **community**. It reflects **identity**, **heritage**, and **value**. **Globalisation**, **tourism**, and **urbanisation** can **threaten** **local culture**. **Preservation**, **protection**, and **revitalisation** are important. **Museum**, **festival**, and **heritage** **site** help **maintain** **tradition**. **Local** **artisan** and **craftsman** keep **skill** alive. **Community** **participation** and **education** pass **culture** to the next **generation**. **Authenticity** and **respect** are essential when **sharing** **culture** with **visitor**.

# 15-06 生态旅游

## 一、精读文章

**Ecotourism**

**Ecotourism** is **responsible** **travel** to **natural** **area** that **conserves** the **environment** and **improves** **local** **well-being**. It **minimises** **impact**, **supports** **conservation**, and **respects** **culture**. **Ecotourists** enjoy **wildlife**, **biodiversity**, **rainforest**, **reef**, and **national park**. **Activities** include **hiking**, **birdwatching**, **diving**, and **safari**. **Guideline** and **certification** ensure **sustainability**. **Community** **involvement** and **benefit** are essential. **Ecotourism** **educates** **visitor** about **nature** and **conservation**. It **contrasts** with **mass tourism**, which can **damage** **ecosystem**.

# 15-07 旅游经济

## 一、精读文章

**Tourism Economy**

**Tourism economy** refers to the **economic** **impact** of **tourism** on a **region** or **country**. It **generates** **income**, **employment**, **foreign** **exchange**, and **investment**. **Tourism** **sector** includes **hotel**, **restaurant**, **transport**, **entertainment**, and **retail**. **Tourist** **spending** supports **local** **business** and **community**. **Government** **policy**, **infrastructure**, and **marketing** **promote** **tourism**. **Seasonality** and **external** **shock** can **affect** **revenue**. **Leakage** occurs when **profit** leaves the **local** **economy**. **Sustainable** **tourism** and **fair** **distribution** of **benefit** are **important** for **long-term** **growth**.
