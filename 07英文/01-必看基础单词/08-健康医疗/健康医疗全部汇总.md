# 08-01 身体健康

## 一、精读文章

**Physical Health**

**Physical health** is the **well-being** of the **body**. It depends on **exercise**, **fitness**, **nutrition**, and **sleep**. Regular **physical activity** includes **workout**, **training**, **walking**, **running**, **cycling**, and **swimming**. A **balanced diet** provides **vitamins**, **minerals**, **protein**, **carbohydrate**, **fat**, and **fibre**. **Hydration** and **water** are essential. **Sleep** and **rest** help **recovery**. Good **hygiene** prevents **infection** and supports **immunity**. **Check-up**, **screening**, and **vaccination** detect or prevent **disease**. **Symptoms** such as **pain**, **fatigue**, and **weakness** should not be ignored. **Weight**, **BMI**, **obesity**, and **sedentary** **lifestyle** affect health. **Stamina**, **strength**, **flexibility**, and **endurance** improve with regular **habit**.

# 08-02 心理健康

## 一、精读文章

**Mental Health**

**Mental health** is as important as **physical health**. It includes **psychological**, **emotional**, and **social** **well-being**. **Stress**, **anxiety**, and **depression** are common **mental health** issues. **Mood**, **emotion**, and **feeling** affect daily life. **Self-esteem**, **confidence**, and **resilience** help people **cope** with problems. **Therapy**, **counselling**, and **support** can **treat** **mental illness**. **Stigma** and **awareness** matter. **Mindfulness**, **meditation**, and **relaxation** reduce **tension**. **Burnout** results from **pressure** and poor **work-life balance**. **Isolation** and **loneliness** harm **well-being**. **Social connection**, **happiness**, and **satisfaction** improve **mental health**. **Disorder**, **diagnosis**, and **treatment** require professional care.

# 08-03 医疗体系

## 一、精读文章

**Healthcare Systems**

A **healthcare system** provides **medical** **services** to a **population**. It includes **hospital**, **clinic**, **doctor**, **physician**, **nurse**, **surgeon**, and **specialist**. **Patients** receive **treatment**, **diagnosis**, **prescription**, **medicine**, and **surgery**. **Emergency** care and **ambulance** services save lives. **Insurance**, **public**, and **private** **healthcare** affect **access**, **affordability**, and **quality**. **Universal** **healthcare** aims to cover everyone. **Wait time**, **referral**, **primary care**, **secondary care**, and **tertiary care** are part of the system. **Pharmacy**, **vaccination**, and **prevention** reduce costs. **Funding**, **resource**, **staff shortage**, and **infrastructure** are key challenges.

## 二、中文翻译

# 08-04 疾病与预防

## 一、精读文章

**Disease and Prevention**

A **disease** is an **illness** or **disorder** of the body or mind. **Infection** is caused by **virus**, **bacteria**, **pathogen**, or **parasite**. Some diseases are **contagious**, **infectious**, or **epidemic**. A **pandemic** spreads worldwide. **Chronic** diseases last long; **acute** diseases are short. **Symptom**, **sign**, **diagnosis**, **treatment**, and **cure** are key terms. **Prevention** includes **vaccination**, **hygiene**, **sanitation**, **clean water**, and **screening**. **Immune** **system** and **immunity** protect the body. **Risk factor** includes **smoking**, **alcohol**, **obesity**, and **sedentary lifestyle**. **Early detection**, **outbreak**, **transmission**, **quarantine**, and **medication** are important. **Antibiotic** **resistance** is a growing **public health** threat.

## 二、中文翻译

# 08-05 营养与运动

## 一、精读文章

**Nutrition and Exercise**

**Nutrition** and **exercise** are essential for **health**. A **balanced diet** provides **nutrient**, **vitamin**, **mineral**, **protein**, **carbohydrate**, **fat**, **fibre**, and **calorie**. Too much **sugar**, **salt**, and **processed food** can cause **obesity** and **malnutrition**. **Physical activity** includes **aerobic** exercise, **strength training**, **flexibility**, and **endurance**. **Workout**, **fitness**, **stamina**, and **metabolism** improve with regular **habit**. **Hydration** and **sleep** support **recovery**. A **sedentary** **lifestyle** increases **risk**. **Weight**, **BMI**, and **diet** should be managed carefully. **Health** is a **balance** of **nutrition** and **movement**.
