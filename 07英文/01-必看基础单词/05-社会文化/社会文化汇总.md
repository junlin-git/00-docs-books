# 05-01 社会变迁

## 一、精读文章

**Social Change**

**Social change** refers to the **transformation** of **society** over time. It includes **evolution**, **transition**, and **shift** in **values**, **norms**, and **lifestyle**. **Modernisation**, **industrialisation**, **urbanisation**, and **globalisation** drive change. **Migration** and **mobility** increase **diversity** and **multicultural** **identity**. **Demographic** change affects each **generation**. **Tradition** and **custom** may be preserved or challenged. **Reform** and **revolution** can bring rapid change. **Technology**, **economy**, **inequality**, **class**, and **gender** shape **family** and **community**. The **individual** and the **collective** must **adapt** or **resist**. **Conflict** and **integration** often occur together. **Progress** and **development** may improve **welfare**, but can also create new problems.

## 二、中文翻译

**社会变迁**指**社会**随时间发生的**转变**。它包括**价值**、**规范**和**生活方式**的**演进**、**过渡**和**转变**。**现代化**、**工业化**、**城市化**和**全球化**推动变化。**迁移**和**流动**增加**多样性**和**多元文化的****身份**。**人口**变化影响每一**代人**。**传统**和**习俗**可能被保留或挑战。**改革**和**革命**可带来快速变化。**技术**、**经济**、**不平等**、**阶级**和**性别**塑造**家庭**和**社区**。**个人**和**集体**必须**适应**或**抵抗**。**冲突**和**融合**常常同时发生。**进步**和**发展**可能改善**福祉**，但也可能带来新问题。

# 05-02 文化差异

## 一、精读文章

**Cultural Differences**

**Cultural differences** exist in **values**, **norms**, **beliefs**, **customs**, and **traditions**. **Culture** includes **language**, **religion**, **ritual**, **food**, **dress**, and **art**. **Ethnicity**, **race**, and **nationality** shape **identity**. People may hold **stereotypes**, **prejudice**, and **discrimination**, but **tolerance**, **respect**, and **understanding** reduce conflict. **Communication** differs: **nonverbal** **gestures**, **eye contact**, **silence**, and **etiquette** vary. Some cultures are **individualist**; others are **collectivist**. **Hierarchy**, **equality**, **time**, **punctuality**, **directness**, and **indirectness** also differ. **Globalisation** increases **multicultural** contact. **Acculturation**, **assimilation**, and **culture shock** are common experiences. **Adaptation** and **open-mindedness** help people bridge **cultural** gaps.

## 二、中文翻译

**文化差异**

**文化差异**存在于**价值**、**规范**、**信仰**、**习俗**和**传统**中。**文化**包括**语言**、**宗教**、**仪式**、**食物**、**服饰**和**艺术**。**族裔**、**种族**和**国籍**塑造**身份**。人们可能持有**刻板印象**、**偏见**和**歧视**，但**宽容**、**尊重**和**理解**可以减少冲突。**交流**方式不同：**非语言的****手势**、**眼神接触**、**沉默**和**礼仪**各不相同。有些文化是**个人主义的**；其他是**集体主义的**。**等级**、**平等**、**时间**、**守时**、**直接**和**间接**也有差异。**全球化**增加**多元文化的**接触。**文化适应**、**同化**和**文化冲击**是常见经历。**适应**和**开放心态**帮助人们弥合**文化**差距。

# 05-03 传统与现代

## 一、精读文章

**Tradition and Modernity**

**Tradition** and **modernity** often coexist. **Traditional** **custom**, **heritage**, and **ritual** connect people to their **ancestors**. **Festivals** and **ceremonies** express **belief** and **value**. **Generations** differ: the **youth** may prefer **change**, while the **elderly** may wish to **preserve** and **conserve** old ways. Some traditions **adapt**; others are **abandoned** or **replaced** by **technology**, **urbanisation**, and **globalisation**. **Consumerism** and **materialism** challenge **community** and **family** life. **Individualism** grows, but many still seek **identity** and **roots**. **Nostalgia** and **continuity** exist alongside **progress**, **reform**, and **innovation**. The key is to find a **balance** between **tradition** and **modern** life.

## 二、中文翻译

**传统与现代**

**传统**与**现代性**常常共存。**传统的****习俗**、**遗产**和**仪式**把人们与**祖先**联系起来。**节日**和**典礼**表达**信仰**和**价值**。**世代**之间存在差异：**年轻人**可能更喜欢**变化**，而**老年人**可能希望**保留**和**保护**旧方式。一些传统会**适应**；其他则被**抛弃**或被**技术**、**城市化**和**全球化****取代**。**消费主义**和**物质主义**挑战**社区**和**家庭**生活。**个人主义**增长，但许多人仍在寻找**身份**和**根源**。**怀旧**和**连续性**与**进步**、**改革**和**创新**并存。关键是在**传统**与**现代**生活之间找到**平衡**。

# 05-04 社区生活

## 一、精读文章

**Community Life**

**Community** life takes place in a **neighbourhood**, **village**, **town**, **suburb**, or **city**. **Residents** may be **local** or newly arrived. **Rural** and **urban** communities differ. The **council** provides **facility**, **amenity**, **service**, and **infrastructure**, such as **transport**, **school**, **hospital**, **library**, and **park**. **Recreation** and **social** **interaction** build **support** **network** and **cohesion**. **Volunteer** work and **charity** help **inclusion** and **belonging**. **Safety**, **crime**, **poverty**, and **inequality** affect **quality of life**. **Participation**, **engagement**, **civic** **responsibility**, and **community** **spirit** are important. A strong community supports **residents** and improves **well-being**.

## 二、中文翻译

**社区**生活发生在**邻里**、**村庄**、**城镇**、**郊区**或**城市**。**居民**可能是**本地**人，也可能是新来者。**农村**和**城市**社区不同。**议会**提供**设施**、**便利设施**、**服务**和**基础设施**，如**交通**、**学校**、**医院**、**图书馆**和**公园**。**娱乐**和**社交****互动**建立**支持****网络**和**凝聚力**。**志愿**工作和**慈善**有助于**包容**和**归属感**。**安全**、**犯罪**、**贫困**和**不平等**影响**生活质量**。**参与**、**投入**、**公民****责任**和**社区****精神**很重要。强大的社区支持**居民**并改善**福祉**。

# 05-05 社会平等

## 一、精读文章

**Social Equality**

**Social equality** means **fairness**, **justice**, and **equal opportunity** for all. **Inequality** appears in **race**, **ethnicity**, **gender**, **age**, **disability**, **class**, **income**, and **wealth**. **Discrimination**, **prejudice**, **bias**, and **stereotype** limit **access** to **education**, **healthcare**, and **employment**. **Human rights**, **civil rights**, and **law** aim to **protect** people. **Policy** and **reform**, such as **affirmative action** or **quota**, try to reduce **disadvantage**. **Diversity**, **inclusion**, and **representation** are key goals. **Privilege** and **marginalised** groups must be understood. **Empowerment** of **vulnerable** people supports **equity** and **social justice**.

## 二、中文翻译

**社会平等**
