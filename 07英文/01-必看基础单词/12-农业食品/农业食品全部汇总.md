# 12-01 农业

## 一、精读文章

**Agriculture**

**Agriculture** is the **practice** of **farming**, **cultivating** **crops**, and **raising** **livestock**. It includes **crop** **production**, **animal** **husbandry**, **fishery**, and **forestry**. **Farmers** use **soil**, **water**, **seed**, **fertiliser**, and **pesticide** to grow **food**, **grain**, **vegetable**, **fruit**, and **fibre**. **Irrigation**, **harvest**, **plough**, and **planting** are key **activities**. **Agriculture** provides **food**, **income**, and **employment** for millions. **Climate**, **weather**, and **soil** **quality** affect **yield**. **Sustainable** **farming** protects **environment** and **biodiversity**. **Technology**, **mechanisation**, and **genetic** **modification** increase **productivity**. **Agriculture** is vital to **economy** and **food security**.

# 12-02 粮食安全

## 一、精读文章

**Food Security**

**Food security** means all people have **access** to **sufficient**, **safe**, and **nutritious** **food**. It includes **availability**, **access**, **utilisation**, and **stability**. **Hunger**, **famine**, **malnutrition**, and **starvation** are serious **problems**. **Causes** include **poverty**, **conflict**, **drought**, **flood**, **climate change**, and **poor** **distribution**. **Population** **growth** increases **demand**. **Food** **production**, **supply chain**, **storage**, and **transport** affect **availability**. **Government** **policy**, **subsidy**, and **aid** help **vulnerable** people. **Sustainable** **agriculture** and **fair** **trade** improve **food security**. **Waste** and **loss** must be reduced.

# 12-03 食品加工

## 一、精读文章

**Food Processing**

**Food processing** transforms **raw** **ingredients** into **food** **products**. It includes **cleaning**, **cutting**, **cooking**, **freezing**, **drying**, **canning**, **packaging**, and **preserving**. **Processing** extends **shelf life**, improves **safety**, and adds **convenience**. **Additive**, **preservative**, **flavour**, **colour**, and **sweetener** are common. **Nutrition** may be reduced by **processing**. **Ultra-processed** **food** is linked to **obesity** and **health** problems. **Quality**, **hygiene**, and **regulation** are essential. **Label** and **ingredient** **list** help **consumers** make **choices**.

# 12-05 有机食品

## 一、精读文章

**Organic Food**

**Organic food** is produced without **synthetic** **fertiliser**, **pesticide**, or **chemical** **additive**. **Organic** **farming** uses **natural** **method**, **compost**, **crop** **rotation**, and **biological** **control**. **Organic** **food** is often considered **healthier**, **safer**, and more **environmentally** **friendly**. It may be more **expensive** and less **available**. **Certification**, **label**, and **standard** ensure **quality**. **Consumers** choose **organic** for **health**, **taste**, **environment**, and **animal** **welfare**. **Demand** for **organic** **product** is growing. **Sustainable** **agriculture** and **local** **food** support **organic** **movement**.
