# 03-01 气候变化

## 一、精读文章

**Climate Change**

**Climate change** is one of the biggest **environmental** issues. The **greenhouse effect** traps **heat** in the **atmosphere**. **Carbon dioxide**, **methane**, and other **greenhouse gases** come from **fossil fuels**, **industry**, **transport**, and **deforestation**. **Global warming** causes **rising temperatures**, **melting ice**, **glacier** loss, and **sea-level rise**. It also leads to **extreme weather**, such as **drought**, **flood**, **heatwave**, and **storm**. **Climate scientists** use **climate models** to **predict** future **emissions** and **temperature** changes. **Mitigation** means reducing **carbon emissions**; **adaptation** means preparing for **unavoidable** impacts. **International agreements**, like the **Paris Agreement**, aim to limit **global warming** and achieve **carbon neutrality**. **Renewable energy** and **energy efficiency** are key solutions.

## 二、中文翻译

**气候变化**

**气候变化**是最大的**环境**问题之一。**温室效应**把**热量**困在**大气**中。**二氧化碳**、**甲烷**和其他**温室气体**来自**化石燃料**、**工业**、**交通**和**森林砍伐**。**全球变暖**导致**气温上升**、**冰川融化**、**冰川**消失和**海平面上升**。它还导致**极端天气**，如**干旱**、**洪水**、**热浪**和**风暴**。**气候科学家**使用**气候模型**来**预测**未来的**排放**和**温度**变化。**减缓**意味着减少**碳排放**；**适应**意味着为**不可避免的**影响做准备。**国际协议**，如**巴黎协定**，旨在限制**全球变暖**并实现**碳中和**。**可再生能源**和**能源效率**是关键解决方案。

# 03-02 环境污染

## 一、精读文章

**Environmental Pollution**

**Pollution** harms **air**, **water**, and **soil**. **Air pollution** comes from **factories**, **vehicles**, and **burning** fuels. It produces **smog**, **particulate matter**, and **toxic** gases. **Water pollution** is caused by **industrial waste**, **sewage**, **chemicals**, **pesticides**, and **plastic**. **Soil contamination** comes from **heavy metals**, **fertilisers**, and **oil spills**. **Noise pollution** and **light pollution** also affect cities. **Waste** management includes **landfill**, **incineration**, **recycling**, and **composting**. **Plastic waste** harms **marine life**. **Pollutants** can **accumulate** in the **food chain**. **Governments** set **emission standards** and **regulations**. **Clean-up** and **remediation** are expensive. **Public awareness** and **individual action** can reduce pollution.

## 二、中文翻译

**环境污染**

**污染**危害**空气**、**水**和**土壤**。**空气污染**来自**工厂**、**车辆**和**燃烧**燃料。它产生**烟雾**、**颗粒物**和**有毒**气体。**水污染**由**工业废物**、**污水**、**化学品**、**农药**和**塑料**造成。**土壤污染**来自**重金属**、**化肥**和**石油泄漏**。**噪音污染**和**光污染**也影响城市。**废物**管理包括**垃圾填埋**、**焚烧**、**回收**和**堆肥**。**塑料废物**危害**海洋生物**。**污染物**会在**食物链**中**积累**。**政府**制定**排放标准**和**法规**。**清理**和**修复**代价高昂。**公众意识**和**个人行动**可以减少污染。

# 03-03 资源保护

## 一、精读文章

**Resource Conservation**

**Resource conservation** means protecting **natural resources** for the future. **Non-renewable resources**, such as **coal**, **oil**, **natural gas**, and **minerals**, will eventually **run out**. **Renewable resources**, such as **solar**, **wind**, **hydro**, and **biomass**, can be **sustainable**. **Conservation** includes **reducing** **consumption**, improving **efficiency**, and **reusing** materials. **Recycling** saves **raw materials** and **energy**. **Water conservation** protects **freshwater** and **groundwater**. **Forest** protection prevents **logging** and **desertification**. **Soil** conservation prevents **erosion**. **Biodiversity** depends on **habitat** protection. **Governments** create **protected areas**, **national parks**, and **reserves**. **Sustainable management** balances **economic growth** and **environmental protection**.

## 二、中文翻译

**资源保护**

**资源保护**意味着为未来保护**自然资源**。**不可再生资源**，如**煤炭**、**石油**、**天然气**和**矿物**，最终会**耗尽**。**可再生资源**，如**太阳能**、**风能**、**水能**和**生物质能**，可以是**可持续的**。**保护**包括**减少****消耗**、提高**效率**和**再利用**材料。**回收**节省**原材料**和**能源**。**水资源保护**保护**淡水**和**地下水**。**森林**保护防止**伐木**和**沙漠化**。**土壤**保护防止**侵蚀**。**生物多样性**依赖于**栖息地**保护。**政府**建立**保护区**、**国家公园**和**保留地**。**可持续管理**平衡**经济增长**和**环境保护**。

# 03-04 生物多样性

## 一、精读文章

**Biodiversity**

**Biodiversity** means the **variety** of **life** on Earth. It includes **species**, **genetic** diversity, and **ecosystem** diversity. **Ecosystems** such as **forests**, **wetlands**, **coral reefs**, and **grasslands** support **wildlife**. **Habitat loss**, **deforestation**, **pollution**, **climate change**, and **overexploitation** threaten **species**. Many **plants** and **animals** become **endangered**, **vulnerable**, or **extinct**. **Conservation** efforts include **protected areas**, **wildlife reserves**, **breeding programmes**, and **habitat restoration**. **Endangered species** such as **tigers**, **pandas**, and **whales** need protection. **Invasive species** can harm **native** **species**. **Ecosystem services** include **pollination**, **water purification**, and **soil fertility**. Protecting **biodiversity** is essential for **ecological balance** and human **well-being**.

## 二、中文翻译

**生物多样性**

**生物多样性**意味着地球上**生命**的**多样性**。它包括**物种**、**遗传**多样性和**生态系统**多样性。**生态系统**如**森林**、**湿地**、**珊瑚礁**和**草原**支持**野生动物**。**栖息地丧失**、**森林砍伐**、**污染**、**气候变化**和**过度开发**威胁**物种**。许多**植物**和**动物**变得**濒危**、**易危**或**灭绝**。**保护**努力包括**保护区**、**野生动物保留地**、**繁殖计划**和**栖息地恢复**。**濒危物种**如**老虎**、**熊猫**和**鲸鱼**需要保护。**入侵物种**会伤害**本地** **物种**。**生态系统服务**包括**授粉**、**水净化**和**土壤肥力**。保护**生物多样性**对**生态平衡**和人类**福祉**至关重要。

# 03-05 可持续发展

## 一、精读文章

**Sustainable Development**

**Sustainable development** meets present needs without harming future **generations**. It balances **economic growth**, **social equity**, and **environmental protection**. The **UN Sustainable Development Goals** (SDGs) set **targets** for **poverty**, **hunger**, **health**, **education**, **clean water**, **clean energy**, and **climate action**. **Sustainability** requires **responsible consumption**, **resource efficiency**, and **green technology**. **Renewable energy**, **public transport**, and **sustainable agriculture** reduce **carbon footprint**. **Governments**, **businesses**, and **individuals** share **responsibility**. **Long-term** planning and **international cooperation** are essential. **Quality of life** and **well-being** matter, not just **profit**. **Circular economy** and **waste reduction** support a **greener** future.
