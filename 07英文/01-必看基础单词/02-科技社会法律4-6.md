# 04-01 信息技术

**Information Technology**

**Information technology** (IT) includes **computers**, **hardware**, **software**, **networks**, and **data**. A **system** processes **information** and **digital** **data**. **Users** interact with **devices** such as **laptops**, **smartphones**, and **tablets**. **Operating systems**, **applications**, and **programs** run on these devices. **Databases** store **information**, while **servers** provide **services**. **Cloud computing** allows **storage** and **processing** online. **Cybersecurity** protects **networks** from **hackers**, **viruses**, and **malware**. **IT support** helps **users** solve **technical** problems. **Digital transformation** changes how **businesses** and **governments** operate.

**信息技术**

**信息技术**（IT）包括**计算机**、**硬件**、**软件**、**网络**和**数据**。**系统**处理**信息**和**数字** **数据**。**用户**与**设备**交互，如**笔记本电脑**、**智能手机**和**平板电脑**。**操作系统**、**应用程序**和**程序**在这些设备上运行。**数据库**存储**信息**，而**服务器**提供**服务**。**云计算**允许在线**存储**和**处理**。**网络安全**保护**网络**免受**黑客**、**病毒**和**恶意软件**侵害。**IT 支持**帮助**用户**解决**技术**问题。**数字化转型**改变**企业**和**政府**的运作方式。

# 04-02 人工智能

**Artificial Intelligence**

**Artificial intelligence** (AI) enables machines to **learn**, **reason**, and **solve problems**. **Machine learning** uses **algorithms** and **data** to **train** **models**. **Deep learning** uses **neural networks**. AI can **recognise** **images**, **speech**, and **patterns**. **Natural language processing** helps computers **understand** and **generate** **language**. **Robots** and **self-driving cars** use AI. **Automation** increases **efficiency** but may replace **jobs**. **Ethical** concerns include **bias**, **privacy**, and **accountability**. **AI ethics** guides **responsible** **development**. **Regulation** and **transparency** are important. AI can **assist** **doctors**, **teachers**, and **researchers**. **Future** **innovation** depends on **human** **control** and **trust**.

**人工智能**

**人工智能**（AI）使机器能够**学习**、**推理**和**解决问题**。**机器学习**使用**算法**和**数据**来**训练****模型**。**深度学习**使用**神经网络**。AI 可以**识别****图像**、**语音**和**模式**。**自然语言处理**帮助计算机**理解**和**生成****语言**。**机器人**和**自动驾驶汽车**使用 AI。**自动化**提高**效率**，但可能取代**工作**。**伦理**问题包括**偏差**、**隐私**和**问责**。**AI 伦理**指导**负责任的****开发**。**监管**和**透明度**很重要。AI 可以**协助****医生**、**教师**和**研究者**。**未来****创新**取决于**人类****控制**和**信任**。

**Automation and Robots**

**Automation** uses **technology** to perform **tasks** without **human** **labour**. **Robots** work in **factories**, **warehouses**, **hospitals**, and **homes**. **Industrial robots** **assemble** **products**, **weld**, **paint**, and **package** **goods**. **Artificial intelligence** makes robots more **intelligent** and **flexible**. **Self-driving cars**, **drones**, and **delivery robots** are examples. **Automation** increases **productivity**, **precision**, and **safety**, but may cause **job losses**. **Workers** need **retraining** and new **skills**. **Ethical** issues include **responsibility** for **accidents** and **human-robot interaction**. **Future** **developments** may bring **smart homes**, **smart factories**, and **fully automated** **systems**.

**自动化与机器人**

**自动化**使用**技术**在没有**人类****劳动**的情况下执行**任务**。**机器人**在**工厂**、**仓库**、**医院**和**家庭**中工作。**工业机器人****组装****产品**、**焊接**、**喷漆**和**包装****货物**。**人工智能**使机器人更**智能**、更**灵活**。**自动驾驶汽车**、**无人机**和**送货机器人**是例子。**自动化**提高**生产力**、**精度**和**安全性**，但可能导致**失业**。**工人**需要**再培训**和新**技能**。**伦理**问题包括**事故****责任**和**人机交互**。**未来****发展**可能带来**智能家居**、**智能工厂**和**全自动****系统**。

# 04-05 数据隐私

**Data Privacy**

**Data privacy** protects **personal information** from **misuse**. **Personal data** includes **names**, **addresses**, **phone numbers**, **emails**, **passwords**, and **financial** details. **Companies** collect **data** through **cookies**, **tracking**, and **surveys**. **Users** often give **consent** without reading **terms** and **conditions**. **Data breaches** and **leaks** expose **private** information. **Hackers** steal **data** for **fraud** and **identity theft**. **Governments** pass **laws** such as **GDPR** to **protect** **privacy**. **Encryption**, **anonymity**, and **security** measures reduce **risk**. **Transparency** and **accountability** are important. **Individuals** should **protect** their **data** and **stay alert** online.

**数据隐私**

**数据隐私**保护**个人信息**免遭**滥用**。**个人数据**包括**姓名**、**地址**、**电话号码**、**电子邮件**、**密码**和**财务**细节。**公司**通过 **Cookie**、**追踪**和**调查**收集**数据**。**用户**常常在未阅读**条款**和**条件**的情况下给出**同意**。**数据泄露**和**泄漏**暴露**私人**信息。**黑客**窃取**数据**用于**欺诈**和**身份盗窃**。**政府**通过**法律**，如 **GDPR**，来**保护****隐私**。**加密**、**匿名**和**安全**措施降低**风险**。**透明度**和**问责**很重要。**个人**应**保护**自己的**数据**并在网上**保持警惕**。

# 05-01 社会变迁

**Social Change**

**Social change** refers to the **transformation** of **society** over time. It includes **evolution**, **transition**, and **shift** in **values**, **norms**, and **lifestyle**. **Modernisation**, **industrialisation**, **urbanisation**, and **globalisation** drive change. **Migration** and **mobility** increase **diversity** and **multicultural** **identity**. **Demographic** change affects each **generation**. **Tradition** and **custom** may be preserved or challenged. **Reform** and **revolution** can bring rapid change. **Technology**, **economy**, **inequality**, **class**, and **gender** shape **family** and **community**. The **individual** and the **collective** must **adapt** or **resist**. **Conflict** and **integration** often occur together. **Progress** and **development** may improve **welfare**, but can also create new problems.

**社会变迁**

**社会变迁**指**社会**随时间发生的**转变**。它包括**价值**、**规范**和**生活方式**的**演进**、**过渡**和**转变**。**现代化**、**工业化**、**城市化**和**全球化**推动变化。**迁移**和**流动**增加**多样性**和**多元文化的****身份**。**人口**变化影响每一**代人**。**传统**和**习俗**可能被保留或挑战。**改革**和**革命**可带来快速变化。**技术**、**经济**、**不平等**、**阶级**和**性别**塑造**家庭**和**社区**。**个人**和**集体**必须**适应**或**抵抗**。**冲突**和**融合**常常同时发生。**进步**和**发展**可能改善**福祉**，但也可能带来新问题。

# 05-02 文化差异

**Cultural Differences**

**Cultural differences** exist in **values**, **norms**, **beliefs**, **customs**, and **traditions**. **Culture** includes **language**, **religion**, **ritual**, **food**, **dress**, and **art**. **Ethnicity**, **race**, and **nationality** shape **identity**. People may hold **stereotypes**, **prejudice**, and **discrimination**, but **tolerance**, **respect**, and **understanding** reduce conflict. **Communication** differs: **nonverbal** **gestures**, **eye contact**, **silence**, and **etiquette** vary. Some cultures are **individualist**; others are **collectivist**. **Hierarchy**, **equality**, **time**, **punctuality**, **directness**, and **indirectness** also differ. **Globalisation** increases **multicultural** contact. **Acculturation**, **assimilation**, and **culture shock** are common experiences. **Adaptation** and **open-mindedness** help people bridge **cultural** gaps.

**文化差异**

**文化差异**存在于**价值**、**规范**、**信仰**、**习俗**和**传统**中。**文化**包括**语言**、**宗教**、**仪式**、**食物**、**服饰**和**艺术**。**族裔**、**种族**和**国籍**塑造**身份**。人们可能持有**刻板印象**、**偏见**和**歧视**，但**宽容**、**尊重**和**理解**可以减少冲突。**交流**方式不同：**非语言的****手势**、**眼神接触**、**沉默**和**礼仪**各不相同。有些文化是**个人主义的**；其他是**集体主义的**。**等级**、**平等**、**时间**、**守时**、**直接**和**间接**也有差异。**全球化**增加**多元文化的**接触。**文化适应**、**同化**和**文化冲击**是常见经历。**适应**和**开放心态**帮助人们弥合**文化**差距。

**Tradition and Modernity**

**Tradition** and **modernity** often coexist. **Traditional** **custom**, **heritage**, and **ritual** connect people to their **ancestors**. **Festivals** and **ceremonies** express **belief** and **value**. **Generations** differ: the **youth** may prefer **change**, while the **elderly** may wish to **preserve** and **conserve** old ways. Some traditions **adapt**; others are **abandoned** or **replaced** by **technology**, **urbanisation**, and **globalisation**. **Consumerism** and **materialism** challenge **community** and **family** life. **Individualism** grows, but many still seek **identity** and **roots**. **Nostalgia** and **continuity** exist alongside **progress**, **reform**, and **innovation**. The key is to find a **balance** between **tradition** and **modern** life.

**传统与现代**

**传统**与**现代性**常常共存。**传统的****习俗**、**遗产**和**仪式**把人们与**祖先**联系起来。**节日**和**典礼**表达**信仰**和**价值**。**世代**之间存在差异：**年轻人**可能更喜欢**变化**，而**老年人**可能希望**保留**和**保护**旧方式。一些传统会**适应**；其他则被**抛弃**或被**技术**、**城市化**和**全球化****取代**。**消费主义**和**物质主义**挑战**社区**和**家庭**生活。**个人主义**增长，但许多人仍在寻找**身份**和**根源**。**怀旧**和**连续性**与**进步**、**改革**和**创新**并存。关键是在**传统**与**现代**生活之间找到**平衡**。

# 05-05 社会平等

# 05-06 人口老龄化

**Population Ageing**

**Population ageing** is a global **demographic** trend. **Life expectancy** rises, while **birth rate** and **fertility** fall. The **elderly** and **senior** population grows. **Old age** brings both **opportunity** and **challenge**. **Retirement** and **pension** systems face pressure. **Healthcare**, **care**, and **nursing** services must expand. **Dependency** ratios rise, and the **workforce** may face **labour shortage**. **Migration** and **immigration** can help. **Policy**, **welfare**, and **social** support are essential. **Isolation**, **loneliness**, **disease**, **disability**, and reduced **mobility** affect many **elderly** people. **Technology** and **assistive** devices can help. **Family** and **generation** ties matter. An **ageing** society can also develop a **silver economy** and create new **opportunities**.

**人口老龄化**

**人口老龄化**是一个全球**人口**趋势。**预期寿命**上升，而**出生率**和**生育率**下降。**老年人**和**年长者**人口增长。**老年**带来**机遇**和**挑战**。**退休**和**养老金**体系面临压力。**医疗**、**护理**和**看护**服务必须扩大。**抚养**比上升，**劳动力**可能面临**劳动力短缺**。**迁移**和**移民**可以提供帮助。**政策**、**福利**和**社会**支持至关重要。**孤立**、**孤独**、**疾病**、**残疾**和**行动能力**下降影响许多**老年人**。**技术**和**辅助**设备可以提供帮助。**家庭**和**代际**纽带很重要。**老龄化**社会也可以发展**银发经济**并创造新**机遇**。

# 06-01 政府职能

**Government Functions**

Government functions include making laws, providing public services, and maintaining order. The **government** has **authority** over the **state**. It creates **policy** and **law**, and carries out **administration** through **officials** and the **bureaucracy**. A **parliament** or **congress** makes **legislation**. The **president** or **prime minister** leads the **executive**. **Local**, **central**, **federal**, and **municipal** governments share power. **Elections** allow citizens to **vote**. **Democracy**, **republic**, and **monarchy** are different systems. A **constitution** defines **sovereignty** and **legitimacy**. **Accountability** and **transparency** are important. Governments **regulate** the economy, collect **taxation**, and manage the **budget**. They provide **welfare**, **infrastructure**, and **defense**. The **judiciary**, **executive**, and **legislative** branches balance each other.

**政府职能**

政府职能包括制定法律、提供公共服务和维护秩序。**政府**对**国家**拥有**权威**。它制定**政策**和**法律**，并通过**官员**和**官僚机构**进行**行政**管理。**议会**或**国会**制定**立法**。**总统**或**总理**领导**行政**部门。**地方**、**中央**、**联邦**和**市政**政府分享权力。**选举**让公民**投票**。**民主**、**共和**和**君主制**是不同的制度。**宪法**界定**主权**和**合法性**。**问责**和**透明**很重要。政府**监管**经济，征收**税收**，管理**预算**。它们提供**福利**、**基础设施**和**国防**。**司法**、**行政**和**立法**部门相互制衡。

---

# 06-02 公共政策

**Public Policy**

**Public policy** is a **decision** or **plan** by **government**. It includes **regulation**, **legislation**, and **reform**. The **process** has **agenda** setting, **formulation**, **implementation**, and **evaluation**. **Objectives** and **targets** guide **policy**. **Tools** include **subsidy**, **incentive**, **tax**, and **funding**. **Welfare**, **healthcare**, **education**, **housing**, **transport**, and **environment** are key **areas**. A **budget** determines **allocation**. **Stakeholders** and **consultation** shape **debate**. **Opposition** and **support** affect **outcome**. **Impact**, **effectiveness**, **efficiency**, **equity**, and **feasibility** are **criteria**.

**公共政策**

**公共政策**是**政府**的**决定**或**计划**。它包括**监管**、**立法**和**改革**。**过程**包括**议程**设定、**制定**、**实施**和**评估**。**目标**和**指标**指导**政策**。**工具**包括**补贴**、**激励**、**税收**和**资金**。**福利**、**医疗**、**教育**、**住房**、**交通**和**环境**是关键**领域**。**预算**决定**分配**。**利益相关者**和**咨询**塑造**辩论**。**反对**和**支持**影响**结果**。**影响**、**有效性**、**效率**、**公平**和**可行性**是**标准**。

---

# 06-03 法律体系

**Legal System**

A **legal system** includes **courts**, **judges**, **lawyers**, and **laws**. A **case** may go to **trial**. The **prosecutor** and **defendant** present **evidence** and **witnesses**. **Testimony** and **verdict** lead to a **sentence**. **Appeals** are possible. **Constitutions**, **statutes**, and **regulations** form the **law**. **Civil** law deals with **contracts**, **property**, and **torts**. **Criminal** law deals with **offences**. **Rights**, **obligations**, and **liability** matter. **Justice**, **judiciary**, **enforcement**, and **compliance** are essential.

**法律体系**

**法律体系**包括**法院**、**法官**、**律师**和**法律**。**案件**可能进入**审判**。**检察官**和**被告**出示**证据**和**证人**。**证词**和**裁决**导致**判决**。**上诉**是可能的。**宪法**、**法规**和**规章**构成**法律**。**民事**法律处理**合同**、**财产**和**侵权**。**刑事**法律处理**犯罪**。**权利**、**义务**和**责任**很重要。**正义**、**司法**、**执法**和**合规**至关重要。

---

# 06-04 犯罪与惩罚

**Crime and Punishment**

**Crime** includes **offences** such as **theft**, **robbery**, **burglary**, **fraud**, **murder**, and **assault**. **Drug trafficking**, **corruption**, and **bribery** are serious. **Punishment** may be a **fine**, **probation**, **community service**, **prison**, or **capital punishment**. **Rehabilitation**, **deterrence**, and **retribution** are goals. **Victims** and **offenders** are central. **Law enforcement** and **police** investigate. **Recidivism** is a concern. **Justice** requires fair **trials** and **sentences**.

**犯罪与惩罚**

**犯罪**包括**罪行**，如**盗窃**、**抢劫**、**入室盗窃**、**欺诈**、**谋杀**和**袭击**。**贩毒**、**腐败**和**贿赂**很严重。**惩罚**可以是**罚款**、**缓刑**、**社区服务**、**监狱**或**死刑**。**康复**、**威慑**和**报应**是目标。**受害者**和**罪犯**是核心。**执法**和**警察**进行调查。**累犯**是一个问题。**正义**需要公正的**审判**和**判决**。

---

# 06-05 人权

**Human Rights**

**Human rights** are basic **freedoms** and **entitlements** for all people. They include **civil**, **political**, **economic**, **social**, and **cultural** rights. **Freedom** of **speech**, **religion**, **assembly**, and **association** are fundamental. **Equality** and **non-discrimination** are key. **Dignity**, **liberty**, and **justice** must be protected. **Torture**, **slavery**, and **genocide** are violations. **Refugees** and **asylum seekers** flee **persecution**. **International law**, the **UN**, and **treaties** promote **human rights**. **Protection**, **promotion**, and **obligation** are important.

**人权**

**人权**是所有人的基本**自由**和**权利**。它们包括**公民**、**政治**、**经济**、**社会**和**文化**权利。**言论**、**宗教**、**集会**和**结社**自由是基本的。**平等**和**不歧视**是关键。**尊严**、**自由**和**正义**必须受到保护。**酷刑**、**奴隶制**和**种族灭绝**是侵犯行为。**难民**和**寻求庇护者**逃离**迫害**。**国际法**、**联合国**和**条约**促进**人权**。**保护**、**促进**和**义务**很重要。

# 06-06 国际关系

**International Relations**

**International relations** involve **foreign policy**, **diplomacy**, and **cooperation** between **states**. **Embassies** and **ambassadors** represent countries. **Treaties**, **agreements**, and **alliances** shape **relations**. **Conflict**, **war**, and **peace** are central. **Negotiation**, **sanctions**, **trade**, and **aid** are tools. **International organisations** such as the **United Nations**, **NATO**, and the **European Union** play roles. **Sovereignty**, **intervention**, **humanitarian** action, and **global** **security** are debated. **Bilateral** and **multilateral** **summits** aim to solve problems.

**国际关系**

**国际关系**涉及**国家**之间的**外交政策**、**外交**和**合作**。**大使馆**和**大使**代表国家。**条约**、**协议**和**联盟**塑造**关系**。**冲突**、**战争**和**和平**是核心。**谈判**、**制裁**、**贸易**和**援助**是工具。**国际组织**如**联合国**、**北约**和**欧盟**发挥作用。**主权**、**干预**、**人道主义**行动和**全球****安全**引发争论。**双边**和**多边****峰会**旨在解决问题。

# 06-07 公共安全

**Public Safety**

**Public safety** protects people from **crime**, **disaster**, and **emergency**. **Police**, **fire** services, and **emergency** teams respond. **Accidents**, **fires**, **floods**, and **earthquakes** require **rescue**, **evacuation**, and **warning** systems. **Terrorism**, **crime**, and **cyber threats** are risks. **Prevention**, **response**, and **protection** are key. **Surveillance**, **border** control, **customs**, and **immigration** support **security**. **Health** **pandemics** need **quarantine** and **safety measures**. **Regulation** and **enforcement** ensure **public safety**.

**公共安全**

**公共安全**保护人们免受**犯罪**、**灾难**和**紧急情况**的伤害。**警察**、**消防**服务和**紧急**团队响应。**事故**、**火灾**、**洪水**和**地震**需要**救援**、**疏散**和**警报**系统。**恐怖主义**、**犯罪**和**网络威胁**是风险。**预防**、**响应**和**保护**是关键。**监控**、**边境**控制、**海关**和**移民**支持**安全**。**健康****流行病**需要**隔离**和**安全措施**。**监管**和**执法**确保**公共安全**。
