# 11-01 城市规划

## 一、精读文章

**Urban Planning**

**Urban planning** is the **design** and **management** of **cities** and **towns**. It involves **land use**, **zoning**, **housing**, **transport**, and **infrastructure**. **Planners** aim to create **sustainable**, **liveable**, and **functional** **communities**. They consider **population** **growth**, **migration**, and **urbanisation**. **Public** **space**, **green** **space**, **park**, and **recreation** **area** improve **quality of life**. **Traffic**, **congestion**, and **pollution** must be managed. **Residential**, **commercial**, and **industrial** **zones** are separated. **Building**, **height**, **density**, and **design** are regulated. **Citizen** **participation** and **consultation** shape **plans**. **Sustainability** and **equity** are key **goals**.

## 二、中文翻译

**城市规划**

**城市规划**是**城市**和**城镇**的**设计**和**管理**。它涉及**土地利用**、**分区**、**住房**、**交通**和**基础设施**。**规划者**旨在创造**可持续**、**宜居**和**功能性**的**社区**。他们考虑**人口****增长**、**迁移**和**城市化**。**公共****空间**、**绿色****空间**、**公园**和**娱乐****区**改善**生活质量**。**交通**、**拥堵**和**污染**必须得到管理。**住宅**、**商业**和**工业****区**被分开。**建筑**、**高度**、**密度**和**设计**受到监管。**公民****参与**和**咨询**塑造**规划**。**可持续性**和**公平**是关键**目标**。

# 11-02 公共交通

## 一、精读文章

**Public Transport**

**Public transport** includes **bus**, **train**, **subway**, **metro**, **tram**, **light rail**, and **ferry**. It provides **affordable**, **efficient**, and **accessible** **mobility** for **commuters** and **passengers**. **Bus stop**, **station**, **platform**, and **terminal** are key **facilities**. **Timetable**, **route**, **fare**, and **ticket** help **users** plan **journeys**. **Transit** reduces **traffic**, **congestion**, and **pollution**. **Government** **subsidy** and **investment** support **public transport**. **Integration** with **cycling** and **walking** improves **connectivity**. **Reliability**, **comfort**, and **safety** attract more **riders**.

## 二、中文翻译

**公共交通**

**公共交通**包括**公交车**、**火车**、**地铁**、**轻轨**、**有轨电车**和**渡轮**。它为**通勤者**和**乘客**提供**负担得起**、**高效**和**无障碍**的**出行**。**公交站**、**车站**、**站台**和**终点站**是关键**设施**。**时刻表**、**路线**、**票价**和**车票**帮助**用户**规划**行程**。**公共交通**减少**交通**、**拥堵**和**污染**。**政府****补贴**和**投资**支持**公共交通**。与**骑行**和**步行**的**整合**改善**连通性**。**可靠性**、**舒适性**和**安全性**吸引更多**乘客**。

# 11-03 私家车

## 一、精读文章

**Private Cars**

**Private cars** provide **freedom**, **convenience**, and **flexibility** for **drivers** and **owners**. **Car** **ownership** has grown with **income** and **urbanisation**. **Vehicle**, **automobile**, and **motor** **vehicle** are common terms. **Fuel**, **petrol**, **diesel**, **electric**, and **hybrid** are **types** of **car**. **Parking**, **garage**, **license**, **insurance**, and **maintenance** are **costs**. **Traffic**, **congestion**, **pollution**, and **emission** are **problems**. **Car** **use** contributes to **carbon** **footprint** and **climate** **change**. **Governments** **regulate** **speed**, **safety**, and **emission**. **Alternatives** include **public transport**, **cycling**, **walking**, and **car** **sharing**.

# 11-04 交通拥堵

## 一、精读文章

**Traffic Congestion**

**Traffic congestion** occurs when **vehicles** exceed **road** **capacity**. It causes **delay**, **queuing**, **frustration**, and **wasted** **time**. **Peak** **hour**, **rush** **hour**, and **bottleneck** are common **terms**. **Causes** include **population** **growth**, **car** **ownership**, **poor** **planning**, and **accident**. **Effects** include **pollution**, **emission**, **stress**, and **economic** **loss**. **Solutions** include **public transport**, **cycling**, **walking**, **car** **sharing**, **congestion** **charge**, **toll**, and **parking** **restriction**. **Traffic** **management**, **signal**, and **smart** **system** improve **flow**. **Road** **expansion** may not solve the **problem**.

## 二、中文翻译

**交通拥堵**

**交通拥堵**发生在**车辆**超过**道路****容量**时。它导致**延误**、**排队**、**挫败感**和**浪费****时间**。**高峰****时段**、**高峰****时间**和**瓶颈**是常用**术语**。**原因**包括**人口****增长**、**汽车****拥有量**、**糟糕****规划**和**事故**。**影响**包括**污染**、**排放**、**压力**和**经济****损失**。**解决方案**包括**公共交通**、**骑行**、**步行**、**汽车****共享**、**拥堵****费**、**通行费**和**停车****限制**。**交通****管理**、**信号**和**智能****系统**改善**流量**。**道路****扩张**可能无法解决**问题**。

# 11-05 住房

## 一、精读文章

**Housing**

**Housing** is a basic **need** and a key **urban** **issue**. **Types** include **house**, **apartment**, **flat**, **condo**, **villa**, and **shelter**. **Rent**, **mortgage**, **loan**, and **deposit** affect **affordability**. **Housing** **shortage**, **homelessness**, and **slum** are serious **problems**. **Overcrowding**, **poor** **condition**, and **lack** of **facility** harm **health**. **Government** **policy**, **subsidy**, **public** **housing**, and **social** **housing** help **low-income** **families**. **Urban** **planning**, **land**, **supply**, and **demand** shape the **market**. **Location**, **neighbourhood**, **transport**, and **amenity** affect **quality of life**. **Sustainable** **housing** and **energy** **efficiency** are growing **goals**.
