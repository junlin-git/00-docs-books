# 10-01 新闻

**News**

**News** is information about recent **events**. It is reported by **journalists**, **reporters**, and **correspondents** through **newspaper**, **magazine**, **television**, **radio**, and **online** **media**. A **news** **story** should be **accurate**, **objective**, and **balanced**. **Sources** must be **reliable** and **credible**. **Headlines**, **articles**, **reports**, and **interviews** present **facts** and **opinions**. **Editor** and **editorial** decide what to **publish**. **Press** **freedom** allows **media** to **investigate** and **report** without **censorship**. **Bias**, **sensationalism**, and **propaganda** can distort **news**. **Readers** and **viewers** should **evaluate** **information** critically.

**新闻**

**新闻**是关于近期**事件**的信息。它由**记者**、**通讯员**通过**报纸**、**杂志**、**电视**、**广播**和**在线****媒体**报道。**新闻****故事**应当**准确**、**客观**和**平衡**。**来源**必须**可靠**和**可信**。**标题**、**文章**、**报道**和**采访**呈现**事实**和**观点**。**编辑**和**社论**决定**出版**什么。**新闻****自由**允许**媒体**在没有**审查**的情况下**调查**和**报道**。**偏见**、**耸人听闻**和**宣传**可能扭曲**新闻**。**读者**和**观众**应批判性地**评估****信息**。

It includes **availability**, **access**, **utilisation**, and **stability**. **Hunger**, **famine**, **malnutrition**, and **starvation** are serious **problems**. **Causes** include **poverty**, **conflict**, **drought**, **flood**, **climate change**, and **poor** **distribution**. **Population** **growth** increases **demand**. **Food** **production**, **supply chain**, **storage**, and **transport** affect **availability**. **Government** **policy**, **subsidy**, and **aid** help **vulnerable** people. **Sustainable** **agriculture** and **fair** **trade** improve **food security**. **Waste** and **loss** must be reduced.

**粮食安全**

**粮食安全**意味着所有人都能**获得****充足**、**安全**和**有营养**的**食物**。它包括**供应**、**获取**、**利用**和**稳定**。**饥饿**、**饥荒**、**营养不良**和**饿死**是严重**问题**。**原因**包括**贫困**、**冲突**、**干旱**、**洪水**、**气候变化**和**糟糕****分配**。**人口****增长**增加**需求**。**粮食****生产**、**供应链**、**储存**和**运输**影响**供应**。**政府****政策**、**补贴**和**援助**帮助**弱势**人群。**可持续****农业**和**公平****贸易**改善**粮食安全**。**浪费**和**损失**必须减少。

# 12-03 食品加工

**Food Processing**

**Food processing** transforms **raw** **ingredients** into **food** **products**. It includes **cleaning**, **cutting**, **cooking**, **freezing**, **drying**, **canning**, **packaging**, and **preserving**. **Processing** extends **shelf life**, improves **safety**, and adds **convenience**. **Additive**, **preservative**, **flavour**, **colour**, and **sweetener** are common. **Nutrition** may be reduced by **processing**. **Ultra-processed** **food** is linked to **obesity** and **health** problems. **Quality**, **hygiene**, and **regulation** are essential. **Label** and **ingredient** **list** help **consumers** make **choices**.

**食品加工**

**食品加工**将**原料****成分**转化为**食品****产品**。它包括**清洗**、**切割**、**烹饪**、**冷冻**、**干燥**、**罐装**、**包装**和**保存**。**加工**延长**保质期**，提高**安全性**，增加**便利性**。**添加剂**、**防腐剂**、**香料**、**色素**和**甜味剂**很常见。**营养**可能因**加工**而减少。**超加工****食品**与**肥胖**和**健康**问题有关。**质量**、**卫生**和**监管**至关重要。**标签**和**成分****表**帮助**消费者**做出**选择**。

# 12-04 饮食习惯

**Eating Habits**

**Eating habits** vary across **cultures** and **individuals**. A **balanced diet** includes **vegetable**, **fruit**, **grain**, **protein**, **dairy**, and **healthy fat**. **Meal** **pattern**, **snack**, and **portion** affect **health**. **Fast food**, **junk food**, **processed food**, and **sugar** **drink** are linked to **obesity** and **disease**. **Vegetarian**, **vegan**, and **organic** **diets** are growing. **Cooking** at home, **family** **meal**, and **traditional** **cuisine** promote **well-being**. **Food** **culture**, **taboo**, and **custom** shape what people eat. **Nutrition** **education** and **awareness** encourage **healthy** **choices**.

**饮食习惯**

**饮食习惯**因**文化**和**个人**而异。**均衡饮食**包括**蔬菜**、**水果**、**谷物**、**蛋白质**、**乳制品**和**健康脂肪**。**用餐****模式**、**零食**和**份量**影响**健康**。**快餐**、**垃圾食品**、**加工食品**和**含糖****饮料**与**肥胖**和**疾病**有关。**素食**、**纯素**和**有机****饮食**正在增长。在家**烹饪**、**家庭****餐**和**传统****美食**促进**福祉**。**饮食****文化**、**禁忌**和**习俗**塑造人们的饮食。**营养****教育**和**意识**鼓励**健康****选择**。

# 12-05 有机食品

**Organic Food**

**Organic food** is produced without **synthetic** **fertiliser**, **pesticide**, or **chemical** **additive**. **Organic** **farming** uses **natural** **method**, **compost**, **crop** **rotation**, and **biological** **control**. **Organic** **food** is often considered **healthier**, **safer**, and more **environmentally** **friendly**. It may be more **expensive** and less **available**. **Certification**, **label**, and **standard** ensure **quality**. **Consumers** choose **organic** for **health**, **taste**, **environment**, and **animal** **welfare**. **Demand** for **organic** **product** is growing. **Sustainable** **agriculture** and **local** **food** support **organic** **movement**.

**有机食品**在生产过程中不使用**合成****化肥**、**农药**或**化学****添加剂**。**有机****农业**使用**自然****方法**、**堆肥**、**作物****轮作**和**生物****防治**。**有机****食品**通常被认为更**健康**、更**安全**、更**环保**。它可能更**昂贵**，**供应**更少。**认证**、**标签**和**标准**确保**质量**。**消费者**选择**有机**是为了**健康**、**口味**、**环境**和**动物****福利**。对**有机****产品**的**需求**正在增长。**可持续****农业**和**本地****食品**支持**有机****运动**。

# 12-06 转基因

**转基因食品**

**转基因**（GM）**食品**来自**基因**被**基因****工程****改变**的**生物体**。**科学家****插入**或**修改****DNA**以产生**所需的****性状**，如**抗虫性**、**耐旱性**或更高的**产量**。**支持者**认为转基因**作物**可以减少**饥饿**、**农药****使用**和**成本**。**批评者**担心**安全性**、**健康****风险**、**生物多样性**和**伦理****问题**。**监管**、**标签**和**测试**很重要。**公众****舆论**仍有分歧。关于**益处**和**风险**的**争论**仍在继续。
