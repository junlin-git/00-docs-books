# 01-02 Courses and Teaching

At university, teaching takes many forms. A **lecture** is a large class where a **lecturer** or **professor** speaks. A **tutorial** is smaller, and a **seminar** or **workshop** involves discussion. Students give a **presentation** and complete an **assignment**, **essay**, **paper**, **project**, or **coursework**. 
A **thesis** or **dissertation** is longer. **Reading**, **revision**, and **feedback** are important. 

**Assessment** and **evaluation** measure progress. A **supervisor**, **tutor**, or **mentor** guides learners. The **principal**, **teacher**, or **educator** supports **students**, **pupils**, **learners**, **peers**, and **classmates**. **Group discussion**, **distance learning**, **online learning**, **blended learning**, and **self-study** are common. **Lifelong learning** needs **critical thinking** and **problem-solving**, not just **memorisation** or **rote learning**. **Motivation**, **concentration**, **participation**, and **attendance** matter. Avoid **plagiarism**; use **citation**, **reference**, and **bibliography**. A good **abstract** and **literature review** follow **methodology**. A **questionnaire**, **survey**, **interview**, or **observation** can support an **experiment** and **data analysis**. **Findings**, **conclusion**, **hypothesis**, and **theory** guide **research**. 

**Publication** in a **journal** or presentation at a **conference** or **symposium** shares results.

## 二、中文翻译

**课程与教学**

在大学里，教学有多种形式。**讲座**是**讲师**或**教授**授课的大课。**辅导课**规模较小，而**研讨课**或**工作坊**则涉及讨论。学生要做**展示**，并完成**作业**、**论文**、**文章**、**项目**或**课程作业**。**学位论文**或**毕业论文**篇幅更长。**阅读**、**复习**和**反馈**很重要

。**评估**和**评价**衡量进步。**导师**、**辅导员**或**指导者**引导学习者。**校长**、**教师**或**教育者**支持**学生**、**小学生**、**学习者**、**同伴**和**同学**。**小组讨论**、**远程学习**、**在线学习**、**混合学习**和**自学**都很常见。**终身学习**需要**批判性思维**和**解决问题**能力，而不仅仅是**记忆**或**死记硬背**。**动机**、**专注**、**参与**和**出勤**都很重要。要避免**抄袭**；使用**引用**、**参考文献**和**参考书目**。好的**摘要**和**文献综述**要遵循**方法论**。**问卷**、**调查**、**访谈**或**观察**可以支持**实验**和**数据分析**。**研究结果**、**结论**、**假设**和**理论**指导**研究**。

在**期刊**上**发表**，或在**会议**或**研讨会**上展示，可以分享成果。
