# 18-01 犯罪类型

## 一、精读文章

**Types of Crime**

**Crime** takes many forms. **Violent** **crime** includes **murder**, **assault**, **robbery**, and **kidnapping**. **Property** **crime** includes **theft**, **burglary**, **shoplifting**, and **vandalism**. **White-collar** **crime** includes **fraud**, **embezzlement**, **bribery**, and **corruption**. **Organised** **crime** involves **gang**, **drug** **trafficking**, **smuggling**, and **money** **laundering**. **Cybercrime** includes **hacking**, **identity** **theft**, and **online** **fraud**. **Traffic** **offence**, **domestic** **violence**, and **hate** **crime** are also common. **Offender**, **victim**, **suspect**, and **witness** are key **terms**. **Motive**, **evidence**, and **alibi** matter in **investigation**.

## 二、中文翻译

# 18-02 犯罪原因

## 一、精读文章

**Causes of Crime**

**Crime** has many **causes**. **Poverty**, **inequality**, and **unemployment** can lead to **crime**. **Education**, **family**, **peer** **pressure**, and **environment** shape **behaviour**. **Drug**, **alcohol**, and **mental** **health** **issue** contribute to **offending**. **Greed**, **revenge**, and **jealousy** are **motives**. **Social** **exclusion**, **discrimination**, and **lack** of **opportunity** increase **risk**. **Media** **violence** and **video** **game** are debated **factors**. **Genetics** and **personality** may play a role. **Prevention** requires **addressing** **root** **cause** such as **poverty**, **education**, and **support**.

# 18-03 法律惩罚

## 一、精读文章

**Legal Punishment**

**Legal punishment** is the **penalty** imposed by **law** for a **crime**. **Types** include **fine**, **probation**, **community** **service**, **imprisonment**, **life** **sentence**, and **capital** **punishment**. **Goals** include **retribution**, **deterrence**, **rehabilitation**, and **incapacitation**. **Sentence** is decided by **judge** and **jury**. **Mitigating** and **aggravating** **factors** affect **severity**. **Rights** of **defendant** and **victim** must be **protected**. **Appeal** is possible. **Fairness**, **proportionality**, and **justice** are **principles**. **Alternative** **punishment** and **restorative** **justice** are increasingly used.

## 二、中文翻译

**法律惩罚**

**法律惩罚**是**法律**对**犯罪**施加的**处罚**。**类型**包括**罚款**、**缓刑**、**社区****服务**、**监禁**、**无期徒刑**和**死刑**。**目标**包括**报应**、**威慑**、**康复**和**剥夺能力**。**判决**由**法官**和**陪审团**决定。**减轻**和**加重****因素**影响**严重程度**。**被告**和**受害者**的**权利**必须受到**保护**。**上诉**是可能的。**公平**、**相称性**和**正义**是**原则**。**替代****惩罚**和**恢复性****司法**越来越多地被使用。

# 18-04 监狱

## 一、精读文章

**Prison**

**Prison** is a **facility** where **offenders** are **imprisoned** as **punishment**. It aims to **punish**, **deter**, **rehabilitate**, and **protect** **society**. **Conditions** include **overcrowding**, **violence**, **isolation**, and **poor** **sanitation**. **Inmate** and **prisoner** face **mental** **health** **issue**, **stigma**, and **difficulty** in **reintegration**. **Education**, **training**, **counselling**, and **work** **programme** support **rehabilitation**. **Recidivism** remains a **challenge**. **Alternative** such as **probation**, **community** **service**, and **electronic** **tagging** reduce **prison** **population**. **Human** **rights** and **dignity** must be **respected**.

## 二、中文翻译

**监狱**是**罪犯**作为**惩罚**被**监禁**的**设施**。它旨在**惩罚**、**威慑**、**康复**和**保护****社会**。**条件**包括**过度拥挤**、**暴力**、**孤立**和**糟糕的****卫生**。**囚犯**面临**心理****健康****问题**、**污名**和**重新融入**的**困难**。**教育**、**培训**、**咨询**和**工作****项目**支持**康复**。**累犯**仍然是一个**挑战**。**缓刑**、**社区****服务**和**电子****监控**等**替代方案**减少**监狱****人口**。**人权**和**尊严**必须受到**尊重**。

# 18-05 青少年犯罪

## 一、精读文章

**Juvenile Crime**

**Juvenile crime** or **youth** **offending** is **criminal** **behaviour** by **young** **people**, usually under **18**. **Causes** include **family** **breakdown**, **poverty**, **peer** **pressure**, **drug**, **alcohol**, **school** **failure**, and **mental** **health** **issue**. **Risk** **factor** and **protective** **factor** shape **behaviour**. **Young** **offender** may face **arrest**, **caution**, **community** **sentence**, or **detention**. **Juvenile** **justice** **system** focuses on **rehabilitation** rather than **punishment**. **Education**, **counselling**, **mentoring**, and **family** **support** are key. **Prevention** and **early** **intervention** reduce **recidivism** and **help** **young** **people** **reintegrate**.
