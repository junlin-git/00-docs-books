# 13-01 化石能源

## 一、精读文章

**Fossil Fuels**

**Fossil fuels** include **coal**, **oil**, **petroleum**, and **natural gas**. They are **non-renewable** and formed over millions of years from **organic** matter. **Burning** fossil fuels releases **carbon dioxide**, **greenhouse gases**, and other **pollutants**. They power **electricity** **generation**, **transport**, **industry**, and **heating**. **Coal** is the **dirtiest** fossil fuel. **Oil** is used for **petrol**, **diesel**, and **plastic**. **Natural gas** is cleaner but still a **fossil fuel**. **Reserves** are **limited** and **unevenly** distributed. **Extraction**, **mining**, **drilling**, and **refining** have **environmental** **costs**. **Transition** to **renewable** **energy** is essential.

## 二、中文翻译

**化石能源**

**化石燃料**包括**煤炭**、**石油**、**原油**和**天然气**。它们是**不可再生的**，由**有机物**经过数百万年形成。**燃烧**化石燃料释放**二氧化碳**、**温室气体**和其他**污染物**。它们为**发电**、**交通**、**工业**和**供暖**提供动力。**煤炭**是最**脏的**化石燃料。**石油**用于**汽油**、**柴油**和**塑料**。**天然气**更清洁，但仍然是**化石燃料**。**储量**是**有限的**，且**分布不均**。**开采**、**采矿**、**钻探**和**精炼**有**环境** **成本**。向**可再生** **能源** **转型**至关重要。

# 13-02 可再生能源

## 一、精读文章

**Renewable Energy**

**Renewable energy** comes from **natural** **sources** that are **replenished** naturally. It includes **solar**, **wind**, **hydro**, **biomass**, **geothermal**, and **tidal** **power**. **Solar** **panel** converts **sunlight** into **electricity**. **Wind** **turbine** uses **wind** **power**. **Hydropower** uses **water** **flow**. **Biomass** comes from **organic** **material**. **Geothermal** uses **heat** from the Earth. **Renewable** **energy** reduces **carbon** **emission**, **pollution**, and **dependence** on **fossil** **fuels**. It is **sustainable**, **clean**, and **increasingly** **affordable**. **Storage**, **grid**, and **intermittency** are **challenges**. **Investment**, **subsidy**, and **policy** support **growth**.

## 二、中文翻译

# 13-03 能源消耗

## 一、精读文章

**Energy Consumption**

**Energy consumption** is the **amount** of **energy** used by **individuals**, **households**, **industry**, and **transport**. It is measured in **kilowatt-hour**, **joule**, or **tonne** of **oil** **equivalent**. **Demand** grows with **population**, **income**, and **urbanisation**. **Developed** countries consume more **energy** per **capita** than **developing** countries. **Efficiency** and **conservation** reduce **waste**. **Peak** **demand** strains **supply**. **Fossil** **fuels** still dominate, but **renewable** **energy** is growing. **Energy** **intensity** measures **efficiency**. **Behaviour** change and **technology** can lower **consumption**.

# 13-04 资源短缺

## 一、精读文章

**Resource Scarcity**

**Resource scarcity** occurs when **demand** exceeds **supply** of **natural** **resources**. It affects **water**, **food**, **energy**, and **minerals**. **Causes** include **population** **growth**, **overconsumption**, **climate** **change**, **pollution**, and **poor** **management**. **Shortage** can lead to **conflict**, **price** **rise**, and **inequality**. **Water** **scarcity** is a growing **crisis**. **Food** **insecurity** and **energy** **poverty** affect **vulnerable** **populations**. **Sustainable** **management**, **efficiency**, **recycling**, and **conservation** are **solutions**. **International** **cooperation** and **fair** **distribution** are essential.

## 二、中文翻译

# 13-05 回收利用

## 一、精读文章

**Recycling**

**Recycling** is the **process** of **collecting**, **sorting**, and **processing** **waste** into new **products**. It reduces **landfill**, **pollution**, and **resource** **consumption**. Common **materials** include **paper**, **plastic**, **glass**, **metal**, **aluminium**, and **electronic** **waste**. **Composting** recycles **organic** **waste**. **Reuse** and **reduce** are even better than **recycling**. **Collection**, **bin**, **sorting**, and **reprocessing** are key **steps**. **Recycling** saves **energy** and **raw** **materials**. **Government** **policy**, **public** **awareness**, and **facility** support **recycling**. **Circular** **economy** aims to **eliminate** **waste**.

# 13-07 核能

## 一、精读文章

**Nuclear Energy**

**Nuclear energy** is produced by **nuclear** **fission** or **fusion**. **Fission** splits **uranium** **atom** to release **heat**, which **generates** **electricity**. **Nuclear** **power** **plant** produces **low-carbon** **electricity**. It is **efficient** and **reliable**, but **radioactive** **waste**, **safety**, and **cost** are **concerns**. **Accident**, **radiation**, and **meltdown** are **risks**. **Nuclear** **energy** reduces **greenhouse** **gas** **emission** compared with **fossil** **fuels**. **Public** **opinion** is **divided**. **Regulation**, **security**, and **disposal** are essential. **Fusion** may be a future **source** of **clean** **energy**.
