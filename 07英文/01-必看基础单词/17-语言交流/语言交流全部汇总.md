# 17-01 语言学习

## 一、精读文章

**Language Learning**

**Language learning** is the **process** of **acquiring** a **first language**, **second language**, or **foreign language**. It involves **listening**, **speaking**, **reading**, and **writing**. **Learners** develop **vocabulary**, **grammar**, **pronunciation**, **fluency**, and **comprehension**. **Method** includes **immersion**, **practice**, **repetition**, and **exposure**. **Motivation**, **attitude**, and **aptitude** affect **progress**. **Teacher**, **course**, **textbook**, and **app** support **learning**. **Mistake** is part of the **process**. **Bilingual** and **multilingual** learners have an **advantage**. **Language** **acquisition** improves **memory**, **creativity**, and **cultural** **understanding**.

## 二、中文翻译

**语言学习**

**语言学习**是**习得****第一语言**、**第二语言**或**外语**的**过程**。它涉及**听**、**说**、**读**和**写**。**学习者**发展**词汇**、**语法**、**发音**、**流利度**和**理解力**。**方法**包括**沉浸**、**练习**、**重复**和**接触**。**动机**、**态度**和**天赋**影响**进步**。**教师**、**课程**、**教科书**和**应用**支持**学习**。**错误**是**过程**的一部分。**双语**和**多语**学习者有**优势**。**语言****习得**改善**记忆**、**创造力**和**文化****理解**。

# 17-02 双语

## 一、精读文章

**Bilingualism**

**Bilingualism** is the **ability** to use two **languages** fluently. A **bilingual** person may have a **first language** and a **second language**. **Bilingualism** can be **simultaneous** or **sequential**. It **benefits** **cognitive** **development**, **memory**, **attention**, and **problem-solving**. **Bilingual** **children** often have **better** **metalinguistic** **awareness**. **Code-switching** and **translation** are common. **Bilingual** **education** and **immersion** **programme** support **learning**. **Identity**, **culture**, and **heritage** are linked to **language**. **Multilingualism** is an **advantage** in a **globalised** world.

## 二、中文翻译

**双语**

**双语**是流利使用两种**语言**的**能力**。**双语**者可能有**第一语言**和**第二语言**。**双语**可以是**同时的**或**顺序的**。它**有益于****认知****发展**、**记忆**、**注意力**和**解决问题**。**双语****儿童**通常有**更好的****元语言****意识**。**语码转换**和**翻译**很常见。**双语****教育**和**沉浸****项目**支持**学习**。**身份**、**文化**和**遗产**与**语言**相关。**多语制**在**全球化**世界中是一种**优势**。

# 17-03 语言与文化

## 一、精读文章

**Language and Culture**

**Language** and **culture** are closely **linked**. **Language** **reflects** and **shapes** **culture**, **identity**, and **worldview**. **Vocabulary**, **idiom**, **proverb**, and **metaphor** carry **cultural** **meaning**. **Politeness**, **taboo**, **humour**, and **gesture** differ across **cultures**. **Language** **socialisation** transmits **value**, **norm**, and **tradition**. **Bilingual** and **multilingual** people often **switch** between **cultural** **frameworks**. **Language** **loss** can mean **cultural** **loss**. **Translation** and **interpretation** bridge **cultural** **gap**. **Respect**, **awareness**, and **sensitivity** are essential in **cross-cultural** **communication**.

**语言与文化**

# 17-04 沟通技巧

## 一、精读文章

**Communication Skills**

**Communication skills** are the **ability** to **convey** and **receive** **information** effectively. They include **listening**, **speaking**, **writing**, **reading**, and **nonverbal** **cues**. **Clear** **message**, **feedback**, **question**, and **clarification** improve **understanding**. **Empathy**, **respect**, and **openness** build **trust**. **Assertiveness** and **confidence** help **express** **opinion**. **Barrier** such as **noise**, **language**, **culture**, and **emotion** can **distort** **communication**. **Active** **listening**, **paraphrasing**, and **summarising** are key **technique**. **Effective** **communication** improves **relationship**, **teamwork**, and **productivity**.

# 17-05 非语言交流

## 一、精读文章

**Nonverbal Communication**

**Nonverbal communication** includes **gesture**, **posture**, **facial expression**, **eye contact**, **touch**, **silence**, **proxemics**, and **paralanguage**. It often **conveys** **emotion**, **attitude**, and **intention** more than **words**. **Body language**, **tone**, **volume**, and **pace** affect **meaning**. **Smile**, **nod**, and **handshake** are common **signals**. **Cultural** **difference** affects **interpretation** of **nonverbal** **cues**. **Misunderstanding** can occur when **verbal** and **nonverbal** **messages** **conflict**. **Awareness** and **sensitivity** improve **cross-cultural** **communication**.

## 一、精读文章

**Language Loss**

**Language loss** or **language death** occurs when a **language** loses its **speakers** and **transmission** to the next **generation**. **Causes** include **globalisation**, **urbanisation**, **migration**, **assimilation**, and **dominant** **language** **policy**. **Endangered**, **minority**, and **indigenous** **languages** are at **risk**. **Loss** means **loss** of **culture**, **knowledge**, and **identity**. **Revitalisation**, **documentation**, **education**, and **community** **effort** can **preserve** **language**. **Bilingual** **education** and **mother** **tongue** **programme** support **maintenance**. **Awareness** and **respect** for **linguistic** **diversity** are essential.

# 17-07 翻译

**Translation**

**Translation** is the **process** of **converting** **text** from one **language** to another. **Interpretation** is **oral** **translation**. **Translator** and **interpreter** need **fluency**, **accuracy**, **cultural** **knowledge**, and **subject** **expertise**. **Literal** and **free** **translation** are two **approaches**. **Equivalence**, **meaning**, **style**, and **tone** must be **preserved**. **Untranslatable** **word**, **idiom**, and **metaphor** pose **challenge**. **Machine** **translation** and **software** are increasingly **common**, but **human** **judgment** remains **essential**. **Translation** **bridges** **culture**, **communication**, and **understanding** in a **globalised** world.
