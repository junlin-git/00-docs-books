# 13-01 化石能源

**Fossil Fuels**

**Fossil fuels** include **coal**, **oil**, **petroleum**, and **natural gas**. They are **non-renewable** and formed over millions of years from **organic** matter. **Burning** fossil fuels releases **carbon dioxide**, **greenhouse gases**, and other **pollutants**. They power **electricity** **generation**, **transport**, **industry**, and **heating**. **Coal** is the **dirtiest** fossil fuel. **Oil** is used for **petrol**, **diesel**, and **plastic**. **Natural gas** is cleaner but still a **fossil fuel**. **Reserves** are **limited** and **unevenly** distributed. **Extraction**, **mining**, **drilling**, and **refining** have **environmental** **costs**. **Transition** to **renewable** **energy** is essential.

**化石能源**

**化石燃料**包括**煤炭**、**石油**、**原油**和**天然气**。它们是**不可再生的**，由**有机物**经过数百万年形成。**燃烧**化石燃料释放**二氧化碳**、**温室气体**和其他**污染物**。它们为**发电**、**交通**、**工业**和**供暖**提供动力。**煤炭**是最**脏的**化石燃料。**石油**用于**汽油**、**柴油**和**塑料**。**天然气**更清洁，但仍然是**化石燃料**。**储量**是**有限的**，且**分布不均**。**开采**、**采矿**、**钻探**和**精炼**有**环境** **成本**。向**可再生** **能源** **转型**至关重要。

# 13-02 可再生能源

**Renewable Energy**

**Renewable energy** comes from **natural** **sources** that are **replenished** naturally. It includes **solar**, **wind**, **hydro**, **biomass**, **geothermal**, and **tidal** **power**. **Solar** **panel** converts **sunlight** into **electricity**. **Wind** **turbine** uses **wind** **power**. **Hydropower** uses **water** **flow**. **Biomass** comes from **organic** **material**. **Geothermal** uses **heat** from the Earth. **Renewable** **energy** reduces **carbon** **emission**, **pollution**, and **dependence** on **fossil** **fuels**. It is **sustainable**, **clean**, and **increasingly** **affordable**. **Storage**, **grid**, and **intermittency** are **challenges**. **Investment**, **subsidy**, and **policy** support **growth**.

**可再生能源**

**可再生能源**来自自然**补充**的**自然****来源**。它包括**太阳能**、**风能**、**水能**、**生物质能**、**地热能**和**潮汐能**。**太阳能****电池板**将**阳光**转化为**电力**。**风力****涡轮机**利用**风能**。**水电**利用**水流**。**生物质能**来自**有机****材料**。**地热能**利用地球的**热量**。**可再生能源**减少**碳排放**、**污染**和对**化石****燃料**的**依赖**。它是**可持续的**、**清洁的**，并且**越来越****负担得起**。**储能**、**电网**和**间歇性**是**挑战**。**投资**、**补贴**和**政策**支持**增长**。

# 13-03 能源消耗

**Energy Consumption**

**Energy consumption** is the **amount** of **energy** used by **individuals**, **households**, **industry**, and **transport**. It is measured in **kilowatt-hour**, **joule**, or **tonne** of **oil** **equivalent**. **Demand** grows with **population**, **income**, and **urbanisation**. **Developed** countries consume more **energy** per **capita** than **developing** countries. **Efficiency** and **conservation** reduce **waste**. **Peak** **demand** strains **supply**. **Fossil** **fuels** still dominate, but **renewable** **energy** is growing. **Energy** **intensity** measures **efficiency**. **Behaviour** change and **technology** can lower **consumption**.

**能源消耗**

**能源消耗**是**个人**、**家庭**、**工业**和**交通**使用的**能源****量**。它以**千瓦时**、**焦耳**或**吨** **石油** **当量**来衡量。**需求**随着**人口**、**收入**和**城市化**增长。**发达**国家的人均**能源**消耗高于**发展中**国家。**效率**和**节约**减少**浪费**。**峰值****需求**使**供应**紧张。**化石****燃料**仍占主导，但**可再生****能源**正在增长。**能源****强度**衡量**效率**。**行为**改变和**技术**可以降低**消耗**。

# 13-04 资源短缺

**Resource Scarcity**

**Resource scarcity** occurs when **demand** exceeds **supply** of **natural** **resources**. It affects **water**, **food**, **energy**, and **minerals**. **Causes** include **population** **growth**, **overconsumption**, **climate** **change**, **pollution**, and **poor** **management**. **Shortage** can lead to **conflict**, **price** **rise**, and **inequality**. **Water** **scarcity** is a growing **crisis**. **Food** **insecurity** and **energy** **poverty** affect **vulnerable** **populations**. **Sustainable** **management**, **efficiency**, **recycling**, and **conservation** are **solutions**. **International** **cooperation** and **fair** **distribution** are essential.

**资源短缺**

**资源短缺**发生在**自然****资源**的**需求**超过**供应**时。它影响**水**、**食物**、**能源**和**矿物**。**原因**包括**人口****增长**、**过度消费**、**气候****变化**、**污染**和**糟糕****管理**。**短缺**可能导致**冲突**、**价格****上涨**和**不平等**。**水****短缺**是一个日益严重的**危机**。**粮食****不安全**和**能源****贫困**影响**弱势****人群**。**可持续****管理**、**效率**、**回收**和**节约**是**解决方案**。**国际****合作**和**公平****分配**至关重要。

# 13-05 回收利用

**Recycling**

**Recycling** is the **process** of **collecting**, **sorting**, and **processing** **waste** into new **products**. It reduces **landfill**, **pollution**, and **resource** **consumption**. Common **materials** include **paper**, **plastic**, **glass**, **metal**, **aluminium**, and **electronic** **waste**. **Composting** recycles **organic** **waste**. **Reuse** and **reduce** are even better than **recycling**. **Collection**, **bin**, **sorting**, and **reprocessing** are key **steps**. **Recycling** saves **energy** and **raw** **materials**. **Government** **policy**, **public** **awareness**, and **facility** support **recycling**. **Circular** **economy** aims to **eliminate** **waste**.

**回收利用**

**回收利用**是将**废物****收集**、**分类**和**加工**成新**产品**的**过程**。它减少**垃圾填埋**、**污染**和**资源****消耗**。常见**材料**包括**纸**、**塑料**、**玻璃**、**金属**、**铝**和**电子****废物**。**堆肥**回收**有机****废物**。**再利用**和**减少**甚至比**回收**更好。**收集**、**垃圾桶**、**分类**和**再加工**是关键**步骤**。**回收**节省**能源**和**原材料**。**政府****政策**、**公众****意识**和**设施**支持**回收**。**循环****经济**旨在**消除****废物**。

# 13-06 节能

**Energy Saving**

**Energy saving** means using less **energy** to achieve the same **result**. It includes **efficiency**, **conservation**, and **behaviour** change. **Insulation**, **LED** **light**, **efficient** **appliance**, and **smart** **thermostat** reduce **consumption**. **Public** **transport**, **cycling**, **walking**, and **carpooling** save **fuel**. **Turning** off **light**, **unplugging** **device**, and **lowering** **heating** help. **Renewable** **energy** and **storage** improve **sustainability**. **Government** **incentive**, **subsidy**, and **regulation** promote **energy** **efficiency**. **Saving** **energy** reduces **cost**, **emission**, and **pollution**.

**节能**

**节能**意味着用更少的**能源**达到相同的**结果**。它包括**效率**、**节约**和**行为**改变。**隔热**、**LED** **灯**、**高效** **电器**和**智能** **恒温器**减少**消耗**。**公共** **交通**、**骑行**、**步行**和**拼车**节省**燃料**。**关** **灯**、**拔掉** **设备**和**降低** **供暖**有帮助。**可再生** **能源**和**储能**改善**可持续性**。**政府** **激励**、**补贴**和**法规**促进**能源** **效率**。**节约** **能源**减少**成本**、**排放**和**污染**。

# 13-07 核能

**Nuclear Energy**

**Nuclear energy** is produced by **nuclear** **fission** or **fusion**. **Fission** splits **uranium** **atom** to release **heat**, which **generates** **electricity**. **Nuclear** **power** **plant** produces **low-carbon** **electricity**. It is **efficient** and **reliable**, but **radioactive** **waste**, **safety**, and **cost** are **concerns**. **Accident**, **radiation**, and **meltdown** are **risks**. **Nuclear** **energy** reduces **greenhouse** **gas** **emission** compared with **fossil** **fuels**. **Public** **opinion** is **divided**. **Regulation**, **security**, and **disposal** are essential. **Fusion** may be a future **source** of **clean** **energy**.

**核能**

**核能**通过**核** **裂变**或**聚变**产生。**裂变**分裂**铀** **原子**释放**热量**，进而**发电**。**核** **电站**产生**低碳** **电力**。它**高效**且**可靠**，但**放射性** **废物**、**安全**和**成本**是**担忧**。**事故**、**辐射**和**熔毁**是**风险**。与**化石** **燃料**相比，**核能**减少**温室** **气体** **排放**。**公众** **舆论**存在**分歧**。**监管**、**安全**和**处置**至关重要。**聚变**可能是未来**清洁** **能源**的**来源**。

# 15-03 文化交流

**Cultural Exchange**

**Cultural exchange** is the **sharing** of **ideas**, **customs**, **values**, and **traditions** between **different** **cultures**. It happens through **travel**, **education**, **festival**, **art**, **music**, **food**, and **language**. **Exchange** **programme** and **study** **abroad** allow **students** to experience **foreign** **culture**. **Cultural exchange** promotes **tolerance**, **respect**, **understanding**, and **diversity**. It can **challenge** **stereotype** and **prejudice**. However, it may also cause **culture shock**, **conflict**, or **loss** of **identity**. **Globalisation** increases **interaction** but may lead to **homogenisation**. **Preservation** of **local** **culture** is important.

**文化交流**

**文化交流**是**不同****文化**之间**思想**、**习俗**、**价值观**和**传统**的**分享**。它通过**旅行**、**教育**、**节日**、**艺术**、**音乐**、**食物**和**语言**发生。**交换****项目**和**留学**让**学生**体验**外国****文化**。**文化交流**促进**宽容**、**尊重**、**理解**和**多样性**。它可以**挑战****刻板印象**和**偏见**。然而，它也可能导致**文化冲击**、**冲突**或**身份**的**丧失**。**全球化**增加**互动**，但可能导致**同质化**。**本地****文化**的**保护**很重要。

# 15-04 全球化影响

**The Impact of Globalisation**

**Globalisation** is the **integration** of **economies**, **societies**, and **cultures** worldwide. It increases **trade**, **investment**, **migration**, and **communication**. **Multinational** **corporation** operates across **border**. **Free trade**, **tariff**, and **trade agreement** shape **commerce**. **Outsourcing** and **offshoring** move **job** and **production**. **Global** **supply chain** connects **producer** and **consumer**. **Competition** and **efficiency** increase, but **inequality** and **exploitation** may worsen. **Cultural exchange** and **homogenisation** occur. **Protectionism** and **nationalism** push back. **Sustainable** and **fair trade** aim to balance **growth** and **equity**.

**全球化影响**

**全球化**是**经济**、**社会**和**文化**在全球范围内的**一体化**。它增加**贸易**、**投资**、**迁移**和**交流**。**跨国公司**跨越**边境**经营。**自由贸易**、**关税**和**贸易协定**塑造**商业**。**外包**和**离岸外包**转移**工作**和**生产**。**全球****供应链**连接**生产者**和**消费者**。**竞争**和**效率**提高，但**不平等**和**剥削**可能加剧。**文化交流**和**同质化**发生。**保护主义**和**民族主义**反弹。**可持续**和**公平贸易**旨在平衡**增长**和**公平**。

# 15-05 本地文化

**Local Culture**

**Local culture** includes **custom**, **tradition**, **language**, **festival**, **cuisine**, **craft**, **music**, and **ritual** of a **community**. It reflects **identity**, **heritage**, and **value**. **Globalisation**, **tourism**, and **urbanisation** can **threaten** **local culture**. **Preservation**, **protection**, and **revitalisation** are important. **Museum**, **festival**, and **heritage** **site** help **maintain** **tradition**. **Local** **artisan** and **craftsman** keep **skill** alive. **Community** **participation** and **education** pass **culture** to the next **generation**. **Authenticity** and **respect** are essential when **sharing** **culture** with **visitor**.

**本地文化**

**本地文化**包括**社区**的**习俗**、**传统**、**语言**、**节日**、**美食**、**手工艺**、**音乐**和**仪式**。它反映**身份**、**遗产**和**价值观**。**全球化**、**旅游业**和**城市化**可能**威胁****本地文化**。**保护**、**维护**和**复兴**很重要。**博物馆**、**节日**和**遗产****地**帮助**维持****传统**。**本地****工匠**和**手艺人**使**技能**得以延续。**社区****参与**和**教育**将**文化**传给下一**代**。与**游客****分享****文化**时，**真实性**和**尊重**至关重要。

# 15-06 生态旅游

**Ecotourism**

**Ecotourism** is **responsible** **travel** to **natural** **area** that **conserves** the **environment** and **improves** **local** **well-being**. It **minimises** **impact**, **supports** **conservation**, and **respects** **culture**. **Ecotourists** enjoy **wildlife**, **biodiversity**, **rainforest**, **reef**, and **national park**. **Activities** include **hiking**, **birdwatching**, **diving**, and **safari**. **Guideline** and **certification** ensure **sustainability**. **Community** **involvement** and **benefit** are essential. **Ecotourism** **educates** **visitor** about **nature** and **conservation**. It **contrasts** with **mass tourism**, which can **damage** **ecosystem**.

**生态旅游**

**生态旅游**是到**自然****区域**的**负责任的****旅行**，它**保护****环境**并**改善****本地****福祉**。它**最小化****影响**、**支持****保护**并**尊重****文化**。**生态游客**享受**野生动物**、**生物多样性**、**雨林**、**珊瑚礁**和**国家公园**。**活动**包括**徒步**、**观鸟**、**潜水**和**游猎**。**指南**和**认证**确保**可持续性**。**社区****参与**和**受益**至关重要。**生态旅游****教育****游客**了解**自然**和**保护**。它与**大众旅游**形成**对比**，后者可能**破坏****生态系统**。

# 15-07 旅游经济

**Tourism Economy**

**Tourism economy** refers to the **economic** **impact** of **tourism** on a **region** or **country**. It **generates** **income**, **employment**, **foreign** **exchange**, and **investment**. **Tourism** **sector** includes **hotel**, **restaurant**, **transport**, **entertainment**, and **retail**. **Tourist** **spending** supports **local** **business** and **community**. **Government** **policy**, **infrastructure**, and **marketing** **promote** **tourism**. **Seasonality** and **external** **shock** can **affect** **revenue**. **Leakage** occurs when **profit** leaves the **local** **economy**. **Sustainable** **tourism** and **fair** **distribution** of **benefit** are **important** for **long-term** **growth**.

**旅游经济**

**旅游经济**指**旅游业**对**地区**或**国家**的**经济****影响**。它**产生****收入**、**就业**、**外汇**和**投资**。**旅游业****部门**包括**酒店**、**餐厅**、**交通**、**娱乐**和**零售**。**游客****支出**支持**本地****企业**和**社区**。**政府****政策**、**基础设施**和**营销****促进****旅游业**。**季节性**和**外部****冲击**可能**影响****收入**。**漏损**发生在**利润**离开**本地****经济**时。**可持续****旅游**和**利益**的**公平****分配**对**长期****增长**很**重要**。
