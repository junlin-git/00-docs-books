## 第五篇｜心智、韧性与超越（新增 · 高阶学术抽象词）

### English Version

A wise leader must **temper** (1. moderate/soften) his criticism with kindness, but if his **temper** (2. anger) flares, trust erodes. To succeed, we must **weather** (1. endure) every setback, though the **weather** (2. climate) may remain harsh. Before hiring, companies **screen** (1. examine) candidates thoroughly, yet they also use a **screen** (2. display panel) to present data. The project manager will **chart** (1. map out) a clear roadmap, and he often refers to a statistical **chart** (2. graph) for reference. We need to **divorce** (1. separate) emotion from logic, just as a legal **divorce** (2. legal separation) divides assets. The mirror **reflects** (1. casts back) our image, but we should also **reflect** (2. ponder deeply) on our past decisions. A business plan may **project** (1. forecast) huge profits, yet we should not **project** (2. cast/transmit) our own fears onto others. He is **smart** (1. intelligent), but wearing a **smart** (2. neat) suit also helps him appear professional. Rising oil prices will **fuel** (1. power) inflation, which in turn will **fuel** (2. intensify) public discontent. The museum can **house** (1. accommodate) thousands of artifacts, but the director's office is in a separate **house** (2. building). The professor will **chair** (1. preside over) the academic committee, and he always sits in the **chair** (2. seat) at the center.
