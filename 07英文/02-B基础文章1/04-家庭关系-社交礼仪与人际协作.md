# 📄 文章：家庭关系、社交礼仪与人际协作（完整·科学记忆版）

---

## 🔖 英文原文

**Family** constitutes the most **intimate** **social** **unit**, offering **emotional** **sustenance** and **lifelong** **companionship**. A **harmonious** **family** **atmosphere** **nurtures** **moral** **virtues**, **instills** a **sense** of **responsibility**, and **shapes** **core** **values**. **Mutual** **respect**, **effective** **communication**, and **judicious** **tolerance** are **indispensable** for **preserving** **stable** **family** **bonds** and **resolving** **internal** **discord**.

**Social** **interaction** is an **inescapable** **facet** of **adult** **life**. Across **various** **social** **settings**, individuals must **master** **fundamental** **etiquette**, **cooperate** **actively** with **peers**, make **appropriate** **compromises**, and **coordinate** **group** **efforts** **efficiently**. **Genuine** **friendship** **rests** on **sincerity**, **mutual** **trust**, and **steadfast** **encouragement**—not on **superficial** **entertainment** or **material** **gain**. **Maintaining** **proper** **social** **boundaries**, **practicing** **courteous** **expression**, and **exercising** **sound** **judgment** help individuals **build** **robust** **networks** and **avert** **unnecessary** **interpersonal** **friction**.
