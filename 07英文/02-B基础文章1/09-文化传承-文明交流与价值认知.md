## 🔖 英文原文（加粗为核心词汇）
Culture **embodies** the **spiritual** **essence** and **historical** **legacy** of a nation. **Traditional** **customs**, **classical** **literature**, **ancient** **architecture**, and **folk** **arts** carry **profound** cultural **connotations** and **national** **spirit**.Cultural **inheritance** **fortifies** **collective** **identity**, **refines** **aesthetic** **sensibilities**, and **enriches** the **spiritual** life of the people.

In an **era** of **globalization**, **cross-cultural** **engagement** has become **increasingly** **frequent**. **Sensible** **assimilation** of **foreign** **excellence** and **prudent** **preservation** of **indigenous** **heritage** **foster** cultural **innovation** and **diversity**. We should **discard** **outdated** **stereotypes**, **overcome** cultural **biases**, **adopt** an **inclusive** **mindset**, and **promote** **mutual** **understanding** among **civilizations**. Cultural **confidence** and an **open-minded** **perspective** are **essential** **attributes** for the youth of today.
