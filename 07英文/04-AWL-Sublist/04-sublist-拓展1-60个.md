# Article 1: Education, Access and Communication

Education gives people **access** to better **jobs**. Schools need **adequate** funding, not just an **annual** budget increase. It is **apparent** that students with a positive **attitude** **communicate** better and **concentrate** longer. Teachers may **attribute** success to effort rather than talent. The **approximate** cost of reform is high, but the **goal** is clear. Researchers **investigate** how learners **debate** ideas and test each **hypothesis**. A **grant** can support such work. In short, access, attitude, and communication are key.

## 中文翻译

教育让人们有机会获得更好的工作。学校需要足够的资金，而不仅仅是每年的预算增长。很明显，态度积极的学生沟通更好，也能更长时间集中注意力。教师可能把成功归因于努力而不是天赋。改革的 approximate 成本很高，但目标是明确的。研究人员调查学习者如何辩论观点并检验每一个假设。拨款可以支持这类工作。简而言之，机会、态度和沟通是关键。

**Covered AWL families:** access, adequate, annual, apparent, approximate, attitude, attribute, communicate, concentrate, debate, goal, grant, hypothesis, investigate, job.

# Article 2: Civil Society, Code and Commitment

A **civil** society needs a shared **code** of conduct. Citizens **commit** to rules and **confer** with each other. There is a **contrast** between theory and practice. Every policy has a **cycle**: policies **emerge**, face **error**, and then change. Social **dimension** and **domestic** issues are linked. **Ethnic** diversity enriches a nation; **hence**, tolerance matters. Governments **implement** laws, but they must not **implicate** innocent people or **impose** unfair burdens.

## 中文翻译

公民社会需要一套共同的行为准则。公民承诺遵守规则，并相互商议。理论与实践之间存在对比。每一项政策都有一个循环：政策出现，遭遇错误，然后改变。社会层面与国内问题相互关联。民族多样性丰富一个国家；因此，宽容很重要。政府实施法律，但不得牵连无辜者，也不得强加不公平的负担。

**Covered AWL families:** civil, code, commit, confer, contrast, cycle, dimension, domestic, emerge, error, ethnic, hence, implement, implicate, impose.

# Article 3: Integration, Work and Mechanism

Modern systems **integrate** data from **internal** and external sources. A clear **label** helps users understand each **mechanism**. It is **obvious** that machines **occupy** more space, and every **option** affects **output**. The **overall** design runs **parallel** to human decision-making. Engineers set each **parameter** and **phase** in the process. They **predict** results, while the **principal** aim remains safety. **Prior** experience helps.

## 中文翻译

现代系统整合来自内部和外部来源的数据。清晰的标签帮助用户理解每一种机制。很明显，机器占据更多空间，而每一个选项都会影响产出。总体设计与人决策并行。工程师在流程中设定每一个参数和阶段。他们预测结果，而首要目标仍然是安全。先前的经验有帮助。

**Covered AWL families:** integrate, internal, label, mechanism, obvious, occupy, option, output, overall, parallel, parameter, phase, predict, principal, prior.

# Article 4: Professional Projects, Promotion and Policy

**Professional** teams **project** future needs and **promote** new ideas. Under a strict **regime**, they **resolve** conflicts and **retain** talent. A **series** of projects shows one **statistic** after another: social **status**, **stress**, and **subsequent** outcomes. To **sum** up, a **summary** must be clear. **Despite** pressure, they **undertake** the work.

## 中文翻译

专业团队预测未来需求并推广新想法。在严格的制度下，他们解决冲突并留住人才。一系列项目展示出一个又一个统计数字：社会地位、压力以及随后的结果。总之，摘要必须清晰。尽管有压力，他们仍然承担这项工作。

**Covered AWL families:** professional, project, promote, regime, resolve, retain, series, statistic, status, stress, subsequent, sum, summary, undertake, despite.

## access
**UK. /ˈækses/. US. /ˈækses/. n/v**
>**底层概念**：接近、进入或使用某事物的机会与权利。
>**核心定义**：通道；使用权；进入；获取。
- **词源逻辑**
  - **词根词缀**：ad-（朝向）+ cedere（走），走向某处
  - **核心隐喻**：打开门走进去
  - **避坑**：access to（对……的接触/使用），to是介词
- **语境例句**
  > *Students need access to reliable information.*
  > 学生需要获取可靠信息。
- **高频搭配**
  - **access to** . 对……的使用权/接触机会
    > *Everyone should have access to clean water.*
    > 每个人都应能获得清洁水。
  - **gain access to** . 获得进入/使用
    > *Hackers gained access to the database.*
    > 黑客获取了数据库的访问权。
- **近义词极简区分**
  - **entry** .n. **底层概念**：进入。**核心定义**：入口；进入权。例：*entry to the building* 译：进入大楼。
  - **admission** .n. **底层概念**：准许进入。**核心定义**：入场；录取。例：*admission to university* 译：大学录取。
- **派生词**
  - **accessible** .adj. **底层概念**：可接近的。**核心定义**：可进入的；可获得的。例：*accessible to the public* 译：公众可进入的。
  - **accessibility** .n. **底层概念**：可接近性。**核心定义**：无障碍；可及性。例：*website accessibility* 译：网站可访问性。
- **派生词汇总表格**

| 派生词 | 词性 | 中文释义 | 高频搭配 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| access | n/v | 通道；使用权；获取 | access to 对……的使用权 |
| accessible | adj | 可进入的；可获得的 | accessible to 对……可及 |
| accessibility | n | 可及性；无障碍 | website accessibility 网站可访问性 |

## adequate

- **语境例句**

  - **adequate supply** . 充足供应

- **近义词极简区分**

  - **enough** .adj. **底层概念**：足够。**核心定义**：足够的。例：*enough time* 译：足够时间。
- **派生词**

  - **adequacy** .n. **底层概念**：足够性。**核心定义**：充分。例：*the adequacy of the supply* 译：供应的充足性。
