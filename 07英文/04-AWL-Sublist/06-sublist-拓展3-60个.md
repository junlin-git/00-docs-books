# Article 1: Academic Writing and Research

Academic writing begins with an **abstract** that must be **accurate**. Researchers **acknowledge** their sources and **cite** them correctly. An **expert** may **edit** the draft and give **explicit** guidance. Every **index** and **input** should be checked carefully. Teachers **instruct** students to **utilise** library tools. A **lecture** on **intelligence** can widen the **scope** of study. Scholars **trace** ideas back to their origins and compare different theories.

## 中文翻译

学术写作从摘要开始，而摘要必须准确。研究者承认其资料来源并正确引用。专家可以编辑草稿并给出明确指导。每个索引和输入都应仔细检查。教师指导学生利用图书馆工具。一场关于智力的讲座可以拓宽研究范围。学者追溯思想的起源并比较不同理论。

**Covered AWL families:** abstract, accurate, acknowledge, cite, edit, expert, explicit, index, input, instruct, intelligence, lecture, scope, trace, utilise.

# Article 2: Society, Law and Government

The **author** of a report may discuss social **bond**s. A **capable** government should **cooperate** with communities and not **discriminate** on the basis of **gender**. A **diverse** society has many **domain**s, from housing **estate**s to public services. Costs may **exceed** the budget. **Federal** and **ministry** officials must remain **neutral**. A public debate should **precede** any new **subsidy**.

## 中文翻译

报告的作者可能会讨论社会纽带。一个有能力的政府应与社区合作，不应基于性别进行歧视。多元社会有许多领域，从住宅区到公共服务。成本可能超出预算。联邦和部委官员必须保持中立。任何新补贴都应先经过公开辩论。

**Covered AWL families:** author, bond, capable, cooperate, discriminate, diverse, domain, estate, exceed, federal, gender, ministry, neutral, precede, subsidy.

# Article 3: Business, Work and Economy

Firms **aggregate** data and **allocate** resources. Managers **assign** tasks and **attach** importance to clear goals. A **brief** meeting can be **flexible**; **furthermore**, a bonus is an **incentive**. The **incidence** of turnover may **inhibit** growth. Companies **incorporate** feedback and **initiate** change at regular **interval**s. Workers may **migrate** for a **minimum** wage, but **motive** matters.

## 中文翻译

公司汇总数据并分配资源。管理者分配任务并重视清晰目标。简短会议可以很灵活；此外，奖金是一种激励。人员流动的发生率可能抑制增长。公司吸收反馈，并每隔一段时间发起变革。工人可能为了最低工资而迁移，但动机也很重要。

**Covered AWL families:** aggregate, allocate, assign, attach, brief, flexible, furthermore, incentive, incidence, incorporate, initiate, interval, migrate, minimum, motive.

# Article 4: Health, Science and Change

Hospitals **display** data to **enhance** care. **Ignorance** can **inhibit** recovery. A **rational** approach helps patients **recover**. Studies **reveal** risks, and **tape** recordings may show them. New drugs can **transform** treatment and **transport** medicine **overseas**. Researchers **presume** that stress may **underlie** illness. A **fee** may apply; **nevertheless**, care must continue.

## 中文翻译

医院展示数据以提升护理。无知可能抑制康复。理性的方法帮助病人恢复。研究揭示风险，录音可能显示这些风险。新药可以改变治疗，并将药物运往海外。研究者推测压力可能是疾病的根源。可能需要收费；尽管如此，护理必须继续。

**Covered AWL families:** display, enhance, ignorance, inhibit, rational, recover, reveal, tape, transform, transport, overseas, presume, underlie, fee, nevertheless.

## abstract
**UK. /ˈæbstrækt/. US. /ˈæbstrækt/. adj/n/v**
>**底层概念**：从具体事物中抽离出本质；抽象的；摘要。
>**核心定义**：抽象的；摘要；提取。
- **词源逻辑**
  - **词根词缀**：abs-（离开）+ trahere（拉），拉离具体事物
  - **核心隐喻**：把本质从具体中抽出来
  - **避坑**：abstract 作形容词指抽象；作名词指论文摘要
- **语境例句**
  > *The concept is too abstract for beginners.*
  > 这个概念对初学者太抽象。
- **高频搭配**
  - **abstract concept** . 抽象概念
    > *Freedom is an abstract concept.*
    > 自由是一个抽象概念。
  - **in the abstract** . 抽象地
    > *It is easier to agree in the abstract.*
    > 抽象地达成一致更容易。
- **近义词极简区分**
  - **theoretical** .adj. **底层概念**：理论的。**核心定义**：理论上的。例：*theoretical framework* 译：理论框架。
  - **summary** .n. **底层概念**：摘要。**核心定义**：总结。例：*paper summary* 译：论文摘要。
- **派生词**
  - **abstraction** .n. **底层概念**：抽象化。**核心定义**：抽象概念。例：*a high level of abstraction* 译：高度抽象。
  - **abstractly** .adv. **底层概念**：抽象地。**核心定义**：抽象地。例：*abstractly defined* 译：抽象定义的。
- **派生词汇总表格**

| 派生词 | 词性 | 中文释义 | 高频搭配 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| abstract | adj/n/v | 抽象的；摘要；提取 | abstract concept 抽象概念 |
