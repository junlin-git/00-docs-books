# Article 1: Educational Research: From Data to Evidence

Educational research is a broad **area** of study. Researchers often **analyse** large sets of **data** to understand how students learn. Before they begin, they must **define** the **concept** they are investigating. They also **assess** existing studies and **assume** that previous findings are reliable only after checking their **context**. A good **approach** **consists** of clear questions, suitable methods, and careful interpretation. **Available** evidence may **derive** from surveys, interviews, or tests. Researchers **estimate** effects and look for **evident** patterns.

If the **research** is well designed, it can show which teaching methods work and which do not. For example, a study may **analyse** exam results across a wide **area** and then **assess** whether class size matters. It should not **assume** that correlation is causation. Instead, it must place every finding in **context** and **define** each key term clearly. A strong **approach** also **consists** of ethical rules, because **data** about students must be used responsibly. When **available** resources are limited, researchers may **derive** fewer conclusions, but they can still **estimate** likely trends. Such work becomes **evident** only after repeated testing. In short, good **research** turns raw **data** into reliable knowledge, and it does so by combining careful **analysis** with clear **concepts**.

## 中文翻译

教育研究是一个广阔的研究领域。研究人员经常分析大量数据，以理解学生如何学习。在开始之前，他们必须界定自己所调查的概念。他们也会评估现有研究，并且只有在检查其背景之后，才假定先前的研究发现是可靠的。一个好的方法包括清晰的问题、合适的方法和谨慎的阐释。可获得的证据可能来自问卷调查、访谈或测试。研究人员估计效应，并寻找明显的模式。

如果研究设计良好，它可以显示哪些教学方法有效，哪些无效。例如，一项研究可以分析广泛区域的考试成绩，然后评估班级规模是否重要。它不应假定相关就是因果。相反，它必须把每一项发现置于背景中，并清晰界定每个关键术语。一个有力的方法还包括伦理规则，因为关于学生的数据必须被负责任地使用。当可用资源有限时，研究人员可能得出较少的结论，但他们仍然可以估计可能的趋势。这样的工作只有在反复检验之后才会变得明显。简而言之，好的研究把原始数据转化为可靠知识，而它做到这一点，靠的是把仔细的分析与清晰的概念结合起来。

**Covered AWL families:** analyse, approach, area, assess, assume, available, concept, consist, context, data, define, derive, estimate, evident, research.



# Article 2: Economic Policy, Contracts and Legal Rules

A modern **economy** depends on stable rules. Public **authority** must **create** conditions for fair trade and investment. Governments often **legislate** to protect workers and consumers, while **legal** rules make sure that a **contract** is respected. Good **policy** should follow the **principle** of transparency and accountability. The private **sector** plays a **significant** **role** in growth, but it cannot succeed without trust.

**Finance** provides capital for new businesses, and **income** supports consumer demand. Countries **export** goods and services to earn foreign currency. Each policy choice has costs and **benefits**. For instance, lower taxes may raise **income** in the short term, but they may also reduce public **finance** for hospitals and schools. A **contract** between a company and the government must be clear, or disputes will follow. **Legal** experts may need to **interpret** the rules, but in this article the focus is on how **authority** and **policy** shape the **economy**. A **significant** challenge is to balance market freedom with social protection. If the **legal** system is weak, the private **sector** may avoid risk, and the whole **economy** may suffer. Therefore, a responsible **authority** should **create** fair rules, enforce **contracts**, and use **policy** to support long-term **benefit** for all.

## 中文翻译

现代经济依赖稳定的规则。公共权威必须为公平贸易和投资创造条件。政府常常立法以保护工人和消费者，而法律规则确保合同得到尊重。好的政策应遵循透明和问责的原则。私营部门在增长中发挥重要作用，但如果没有信任，它就无法成功。

金融为新企业提供资本，收入支撑消费者需求。国家出口商品和服务以赚取外汇。每一项政策选择都有成本和益处。例如，降低税收可能在短期内提高收入，但也可能减少用于医院和学校的公共财政。公司或政府之间的合同必须清晰，否则就会产生纠纷。法律专家可能需要解释规则，但本文的重点是权威和政策如何塑造经济。一个重大挑战是平衡市场自由与社会保护。如果法律体系薄弱，私营部门可能规避风险，整个经济都可能受损。因此，负责任的权威应创造公平规则，执行合同，并利用政策支持所有人的长期利益。

**Covered AWL families:** authority, benefit, contract, create, economy, export, finance, income, legal, legislate, policy, principle, sector, role, significant.


# Article 3: Environment, Society and Work

Protecting the **environment** is a **major** **issue** in every country. Governments **establish** laws, but every **individual** must also **involve** themselves in solving the problem. A key **factor** is how **labour** is organised. The **function** of a good **method** is to **identify** problems early and prevent damage. Changes **occur** over a long **period**, and only a small **percent** of firms may **process** information quickly enough to adapt.

In many societies, the **process** of reform begins with public pressure. Citizens **identify** pollution as a **major** threat, and then governments **establish** new standards. This **method** can work, but it takes time. During that **period**, some industries may resist because they fear higher costs. A **factor** that often decides success is whether ordinary **individuals** feel included. If they **involve** themselves in local projects, the **process** becomes more effective. The **function** of **labour** also changes: green industries need new skills, and workers must adapt. A small **percent** of companies may lead, while others follow later. When problems **occur**, authorities must respond quickly. The **environment** is not separate from the **economy** or society. It is a shared **issue** that affects every **individual**, every **labour** market, and every **major** decision. Only a long-term **method** can **process** these challenges fairly.

## 中文翻译

在许多社会，改革过程始于公众压力。公民把污染识别为重大威胁，然后政府建立新标准。这种方法可能有效，但需要时间。在此期间，一些行业可能抵制，因为它们担心更高成本。一个常常决定成功的因素是普通个人是否感到被包容。如果他们参与地方项目，过程就会变得更有效。劳动的功能也在变化：绿色产业需要新技能，工人必须适应。很小比例的公司可能领先，其他公司随后跟进。当问题发生时，当局必须迅速回应。环境并不与经济或社会分离。它是一个共同问题，影响每个个人、每个劳动力市场和每项重大决策。只有长期的方法才能公平地处理这些挑战。

**Covered AWL families:** environment, establish, factor, function, identify, individual, involve, issue, labour, major, method, occur, percent, period, process.

# Article 4: Structure, Theory and Change

A **theory** should **indicate** how different parts **constitute** a whole. Researchers **interpret** findings and **distribute** results to the public. There is no simple **formula** for success; conclusions **vary** by **source**, **specific** context, and **structure**. If evidence is **similar**, scholars may **proceed** with greater confidence. Studies **require** authors to **respond** to criticism and to revise their claims.

Every **section** of a report should be clear. A **specific** example can make an abstract idea easier to understand. When two studies use a **similar** method, their results may still **vary** because the **source** of data is different. A **theory** is not a fact, but it helps explain patterns. Researchers **interpret** data, **indicate** limits, and **distribute** findings through journals. If a study **requires** more evidence, authors should **proceed** carefully and **respond** to questions. The **structure** of an argument matters as much as the evidence itself. In a **specific** field, one **formula** may work well, but in another it may fail. Good research therefore **indicates** uncertainty, **interprets** results fairly, and **responds** to new evidence. Over time, theories **vary**, but the best ones **constitute** a reliable guide for future work.

## 中文翻译

理论应指出不同部分如何构成一个整体。研究人员解释研究发现，并向公众传播结果。成功没有简单的公式；结论因来源、具体背景和结构而异。如果证据相似，学者们可能会更有信心地继续。研究要求作者回应批评并修改自己的主张。

报告的每个部分都应清晰。一个具体的例子可以让抽象概念更容易理解。当两项研究使用相似的方法时，它们的结果仍可能不同，因为数据来源不同。理论不是事实，但它有助于解释模式。研究人员解释数据，指出局限，并通过期刊传播研究发现。如果一项研究需要更多证据，作者应谨慎继续并回应问题。论证的结构与证据本身同样重要。在一个具体领域，某个公式可能很有效，但在另一个领域可能失败。因此，好的研究指出不确定性，公平地解释结果，并回应新证据。随着时间推移，理论会变化，但最好的理论构成未来工作的可靠指南。

**Covered AWL families:** constitute, distribute, formula, indicate, interpret, proceed, require, respond, section, similar, source, specific, structure, theory, vary.


## analyse
**UK. /ˈænəlaɪz/. US. /ˈænəlaɪz/. v**
>**底层概念**：把一个整体拆分成多个组成部分，逐一检视研究，探寻内在结构、原因与规律。
>**核心定义**：分析；剖析；研究。
- **词源逻辑**
  - **ana-**（分开）+ **lyein**（松开），字面“松开、拆解开来”
  - **核心隐喻**：将复杂事物拆解碎片化，由表及里审视细节
  - **避坑**：≠ examine（简单检查）；analyse侧重拆解研究内在逻辑，不是单纯查看外观
- **语境例句**
  > *Researchers analyse data to identify social trends.*
  > 研究人员分析数据以找出社会趋势。
- **高频搭配**
  - **analyse data** . 分析数据
    > *We need to analyse the survey data before drawing conclusions.*
    > 我们需要先分析调研数据再得出结论。
  - **analyse causes** . 剖析原因
    > *The report analyses the causes of youth unemployment.*
    > 这份报告剖析青年失业的成因。
  - **analyse results** . 分析结果
    > *Scientists will analyse the experimental results next week.*
    > 科学家将于下周分析实验结果。
- **近义词极简区分**
  - **examine** .v. **底层概念**：仔细查看、检验，可用于实物或抽象事物。**核心定义**：检查，审查。例：*The doctor examined the patient.* 译：医生检查了病人。
  - **evaluate** .v. **底层概念**：依据标准评判价值优劣。**核心定义**：评估，评价。例：*Teachers evaluate students' performance.* 译：老师评估学生表现。
- **派生词**
  - **analysis** .n. **底层概念**：analyse对应的名词，指分析这一行为或分析报告。**核心定义**：分析；分析报告。例：*The analysis shows rising pollution levels.* 译：分析显示污染水平在上升。
  - **analytical** .adj. **底层概念**：具备拆解分析的思维特质。**核心定义**：善于分析的；分析性的。例：*an analytical mind* 译：善于分析的头脑。
  - **analyst** .n. **底层概念**：从事分析工作的人。**核心定义**：分析师；分析员。例：*a market analyst* 译：市场分析师。
  - **analytically** .adv. **底层概念**：以分析的方式思考处理。**核心定义**：分析性地。例：*Think analytically when solving problems.* 译：解决问题时要用分析性思维。
