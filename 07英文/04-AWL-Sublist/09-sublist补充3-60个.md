# Article 1: Research, Evidence and Communication

Researchers should not **abandon** ethical standards when they **accompany** data collection with careful checks. Over time, they **accumulate** evidence, even when early results are **ambiguous**. They **append** raw data to reports and **appreciate** peer review. An **arbitrary** decision can introduce **bias**, so teams **automate** routine analysis. A clear **chart** helps **clarify** patterns that **coincide** with earlier findings. Knowledge is a **commodity**, and good methods **complement** each other. All work must **conform** to ethical rules.

## 中文翻译

研究者在用仔细检查伴随数据收集时，不应放弃伦理标准。随着时间推移，他们积累证据，即使早期结果模棱两可。他们在报告后附加原始数据，并感激同行评审。武断的决定会引入偏见，因此团队将常规分析自动化。清晰的图表有助于澄清与早期发现一致的模式。知识是一种商品，好的方法相互补充。所有工作都必须遵守伦理规则。

**Covered AWL families:** abandon, accompany, accumulate, ambiguous, append, appreciate, arbitrary, automate, bias, chart, clarify, coincide, commodity, complement, conform.

**Contemporary** debates often **contradict** older assumptions. Trust is **crucial** when **currency** values **denote** economic health. Auditors **detect** errors and **deviate** from routine only when necessary. War can **displace** communities, creating human **drama** and **eventual** recovery. Museums **exhibit** art that may **exploit** social themes. Prices **fluctuate**, so a clear **guideline** can **highlight** risk and protect the public.

当代辩论常常与旧有假设相矛盾。当货币价值表示经济健康时，信任至关重要。审计人员检测错误，只在必要时偏离常规。战争可能使社区流离失所，造成人间戏剧和最终恢复。博物馆展出可能利用社会主题的艺术。价格波动，因此清晰的指导方针可以突出风险并保护公众。

There is an **implicit** risk that new technology may **induce** rapid change. It is **inevitable** that **infrastructure** must be updated. Engineers **inspect** plants under **intense** pressure and **manipulate** systems to **minimise** waste. **Nuclear** power can **offset** carbon emissions. Each **paragraph** of a report should explain a **plus** for society. A **practitioner** offers practical insight, while a **predominant** view shapes the **prospect** for clean energy.

新技术可能引发快速变化，这存在隐含风险。基础设施必须更新是不可避免的。工程师在巨大压力下检查工厂，并操纵系统以最小化浪费。核能可以抵消碳排放。报告的每一段都应解释对社会的优势。从业者提供实用见解，而主流观点塑造清洁能源的前景。

## abandon
**UK. /əˈbændən/. US. /əˈbændən/. v/n**
>**底层概念**：完全放弃、抛弃某人或某物，不再管。
>**核心定义**：放弃；抛弃；遗弃；放任。
- **词源逻辑**
  - **词根词缀**：a-（朝向）+ bandon（控制），交由他人控制
  - **核心隐喻**：把东西丢下不管
  - **避坑**：abandon 指彻底放弃；give up 更口语
- **语境例句**
  > *They abandoned the project due to lack of funds.*
  > 由于资金不足，他们放弃了该项目。
- **高频搭配**
  - **abandon a plan** . 放弃计划
    > *The government abandoned the plan.*
    > 政府放弃了该计划。
  - **abandon hope** . 放弃希望
    > *Do not abandon hope.*
    > 不要放弃希望。
- **近义词极简区分**
  - **desert** .v. **底层概念**：遗弃。**核心定义**：抛弃。例：*desert a family* 译：抛弃家庭。
  - **quit** .v. **底层概念**：停止。**核心定义**：退出。例：*quit a job* 译：辞职。
- **派生词**
  - **abandonment** .n. **底层概念**：放弃。**核心定义**：遗弃。例：*child abandonment* 译：遗弃儿童。
  - **abandoned** .adj. **底层概念**：被抛弃的。**核心定义**：废弃的。例：*an abandoned building* 译：废弃建筑。
