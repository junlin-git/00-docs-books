# Article 1: Education, Management and Shared Responsibility

Education systems face **constant** pressure. Schools must offer an **alternative** to traditional testing under some **circumstances**. Teachers **comment** on student progress, and schools **compensate** for learning gaps. A key **component** of success is parental **consent**. **Considerable** resources are needed, but budgets **constrain** reform. Parents and teachers **contribute** time and effort. Social **convention** can **coordinate** expectations across a school. The **core** of education is not **corporate** branding but student growth. Exam results **correspond** to effort and support.

## 中文翻译

教育系统面临持续的压力。在某些情况下，学校必须提供传统测试之外的替代方案。教师评论学生的进步，学校弥补学习差距。成功的一个关键组成部分是家长同意。需要相当多的资源，但预算限制改革。家长和教师贡献时间和精力。社会惯例可以协调整个学校的期望。教育的核心不是企业品牌，而是学生成长。考试成绩与努力和支持相对应。

**Covered AWL families:** alternative, circumstance, comment, compensate, component, consent, considerable, constant, constrain, contribute, convention, coordinate, core, corporate, correspond.

# Article 2: Research, Evidence and Justification

Researchers **deduce** patterns from evidence. Experiments **demonstrate** cause and effect. They **document** methods and results. A few theories **dominate** the field. The **emphasis** is on reliability. Checks **ensure** accuracy and **exclude** bias. A clear **framework** guides the study. Governments **fund** projects that **illustrate** findings. Results **imply** possible trends. **Initial** results are promising. For **instance**, small trials **interact** with larger ones. Researchers **justify** conclusions with data.

## 中文翻译

研究人员从证据中推断模式。实验证明因果关系。他们记录方法和结果。少数理论主导该领域。重点在于可靠性。检查确保准确性并排除偏差。清晰的框架指导研究。政府资助那些说明研究结果的项目。结果暗示可能的趋势。初步结果很有希望。例如，小型试验与大型试验相互作用。研究人员用数据证明结论的合理性。

**Covered AWL families:** deduce, demonstrate, document, dominate, emphasis, ensure, exclude, framework, fund, illustrate, imply, initial, instance, interact, justify.

Society has many **layers**. Researchers **link** culture to behaviour. They **locate** patterns in data. Schools **maximise** opportunities and reduce **minor** risks. **Negative** experiences affect the **outcome**. A **partner** can share a **philosophy** of life. **Physical** health matters. A large **proportion** of people **publish** their views online. People **react** to change differently. They **register** for courses and **rely** on community support.

社会有许多层次。研究人员把文化与行为联系起来。他们在数据中定位模式。学校最大化机会并减少次要风险。负面经历影响结果。伙伴可以分享人生哲学。身体健康很重要。很大一部分人在网上发表观点。人们对变化的反应不同。他们注册课程并依赖社区支持。

## alternative
**UK. /ɔːlˈtɜːnətɪv/. US. /ɔːlˈtɜːrnətɪv/. n/adj**
>**底层概念**：可供替代的另一个选择；非传统的。
>**核心定义**：替代方案；可供选择的；另类的。
- **词源逻辑**
  - **词根词缀**：alternare（交替）→ alternus（交替的）+ -ative
  - **核心隐喻**：两条路交替，可选另一条
  - **避坑**：alternative to（……的替代品），to是介词
- **语境例句**
  > *We need an alternative to fossil fuels.*
  > 我们需要化石燃料的替代品。
- **高频搭配**
  - **alternative to** . ……的替代方案
    > *Solar power is an alternative to coal.*
    > 太阳能是煤炭的替代方案。
  - **alternative energy** . 替代能源
    > *Alternative energy is growing fast.*
    > 替代能源发展迅速。
- **近义词极简区分**
  - **option** .n. **底层概念**：可选择的项目。**核心定义**：选项。例：*a viable option* 译：可行选项。
  - **choice** .n. **底层概念**：选择行为。**核心定义**：选择。例：*make a choice* 译：做出选择。
- **派生词**
  - **alternatively** .adv. **底层概念**：作为另一种选择。**核心定义**：或者。例：*Alternatively, we could walk.* 译：或者我们可以走路。
  - **alternate** .v/adj. **底层概念**：交替。**核心定义**：轮流；交替的。例：*alternate days* 译：隔天。
- **派生词汇总表格**

| 派生词 | 词性 | 中文释义 | 高频搭配 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| alternative | n/adj | 替代方案；另类的 | alternative to 替代 |
| alternatively | adv | 或者 | alternatively speaking 换种说法 |
