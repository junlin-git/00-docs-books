# Article 1: Academic Research and Education

In **academic** research, students must **adjust** to new methods and become **aware** of ethical rules. Their **capacity** to learn is tested by every **challenge**. A first **draft** helps **enable** progress. **Fundamental** questions **generate** new ideas. **Logic** and **mental** effort are essential. The **notion** of **objective** truth guides their work.

在学术研究中，学生必须适应新方法，并意识到伦理规则。他们的学习能力受到每一个挑战的检验。他们咨询导师，并与同伴保持联系。初稿有助于促成进步。根本性问题产生新想法。逻辑和脑力努力至关重要。客观真理的概念指导他们的工作。

Governments often **amend** a **clause** in a contract to resolve a **conflict**. A **licence** protects **margin** and **revenue**. A **stable** **welfare** system and **liberal** policies help business.

## academic
**UK. /ˌækəˈdemɪk/. US. /ˌækəˈdemɪk/. adj/n**
>**底层概念**：与学校、学术研究相关的。

- **词源逻辑**

  - **核心隐喻**：属于知识殿堂的
  - **避坑**：academic 可指学术或纯理论的
- **语境例句**
  > *She has a strong academic background.*
  > 她有很强的学术背景。
- **高频搭配**
  - **academic performance** . 学业表现
    > *Academic performance improved this year.*
    > 今年学业表现提高了。
  - **academic research** . 学术研究
    > *Academic research takes time.*
    > 学术研究需要时间。
- **近义词极简区分**

  - **educational** .adj. **底层概念**：教育的。**核心定义**：教育性的。例：*educational system* 译：教育体系。
