# Article 1: Education, Research and Community

Education helps students **achieve** their goals. A school must **administrate** its programs fairly and provide **appropriate** support. Teachers **assist** learners by giving feedback. Researchers often **conduct** studies to understand how learning works. Language **acquisition** is a **complex** process. It does not fall into one simple **category**. Each **chapter** of a textbook may **feature** different methods. Schools **evaluate** progress and **compute** scores. They may **conclude** that early help matters. The local **community** should **focus** on students’ needs. A good teacher can **assist** weaker learners and **conduct** small experiments in class. School leaders **administrate** daily affairs, but they also need to **evaluate** whether their methods are **appropriate**. When a school **focus**es on learning rather than test scores, students can **achieve** more. Language **acquisition** is not a single **category**; it is a **complex** journey. Every **chapter** of a learner’s life can **feature** new challenges. Therefore, the whole **community** should support education and help students **achieve** their potential.

## 中文翻译

# Article 2: Economy, Investment and Regulation

A **region** may **regulate** trade to **secure** its economy. Companies **invest** money and **consume** natural **resources**. Consumers **purchase** everyday **items** with **credit**. A government **commission** studies the **impact** of new rules. A national **strategy** may **restrict** some imports. A **survey** can show how people spend and save. Banks **transfer** funds between accounts. If a country wants to **secure** growth, it must **invest** in education and infrastructure. However, it should not **consume** resources faster than they can be replaced. A **commission** may recommend that firms **regulate** their own behaviour. A **strategy** based on a **survey** can **restrict** harmful practices. Consumers who **purchase** **items** on **credit** may later face debt. Therefore, **regulation** and **investment** must work together. In one **region**, a **strategy** may focus on **credit** control; in another, it may focus on **resource** protection. The **impact** of policy can **transfer** wealth from one group to another. A responsible government will **regulate** markets, **secure** fair competition, and help people **invest** wisely.

## 中文翻译

一个地区可能监管贸易以保障其经济安全。公司投资资金并消耗自然资源。消费者用信用购买日常物品。一个政府委员会研究新规则的影响。国家战略可能限制某些进口。一项调查可以显示人们如何消费和储蓄。银行在账户之间转移资金。如果一个国家想要保障增长，就必须投资教育和基础设施。然而，它消耗资源的速度不应快于资源被替代的速度。委员会可能建议企业自我监管其行为。基于调查的战略可以限制有害做法。用信用购买物品的消费者以后可能面临债务。因此，监管和投资必须共同发挥作用。在一个地区，战略可能侧重于信用控制；在另一个地区，则可能侧重于资源保护。政策的影响可能把财富从一个群体转移给另一个群体。负责任的政府会监管市场，保障公平竞争，并帮助人们明智投资。

**Covered AWL families:** commission, consume, credit, impact, invest, item, purchase, region, regulate, resource, restrict, secure, strategy, survey, transfer.

**Culture** and **tradition** shape the way people live. **Design**, music, and food are **distinct** **elements** of daily life. A **previous** study in a **journal** used a **text** from an ancient **site**. People **reside** in towns and villages, and their **normal** routines reflect local customs. The **primary** aim of the study was to understand what is **relevant** to modern life. A **positive** attitude toward tradition can help a community keep its identity. At the same time, every culture has the **potential** to change. What was once **normal** may later seem old-fashioned. A **distinct** style of **design** can be **relevant** to tourism and local business. A **journal** article may explain how people **reside** and work. The **primary** **element** of culture is not money but shared meaning. **Previous** generations passed on **tradition**; today, young people may give it a new **design**. A **positive** view of change and a **distinct** local identity can coexist. The **potential** for cultural exchange is great. **Normal** life is never static; it changes as people **reside**, work, and create. What is **relevant** today may become **tradition** tomorrow.

## achieve
**UK. /əˈtʃiːv/. US. /əˈtʃiːv/. v**
>**底层概念**：通过努力达到目标。
>**核心定义**：实现；达到；取得。
- **词源逻辑**
  - **词根词缀**：a-（朝向）+ chief（头、顶），达到顶点
  - **核心隐喻**：抵达目标终点
  - **避坑**：后接目标/成就，不接人
- **语境例句**
  > *She achieved her goal of becoming a doctor.*
  > 她实现了成为医生的目标。
- **高频搭配**
  - **achieve a goal** . 实现目标
    > *Students can achieve their goals with hard work.*
    > 学生通过努力可以实现目标。
  - **achieve success** . 取得成功
    > *The company achieved great success.*
    > 公司取得了巨大成功。
- **近义词极简区分**
  - **accomplish** .v. **底层概念**：完成任务。**核心定义**：完成。例：*accomplish a task* 译：完成任务。
  - **attain** .v. **底层概念**：达到。**核心定义**：获得。例：*attain a standard* 译：达到标准。
- **派生词**
  - **achievement** .n. **底层概念**：成就。**核心定义**：成就。例：*a great achievement* 译：伟大成就。
  - **achievable** .adj. **底层概念**：可实现的。**核心定义**：可达到的。例：*an achievable target* 译：可实现的目标。
- **派生词汇总表格**
