# 📄 文章 06：城市可持续发展与绿色基础设施

Sustainable urban development **reconciles** economic growth, social equity, and environmental protection.

Green **infrastructure**—green roofs, **permeable** **pavements**, urban forests—provides ecosystem services like **stormwater** management, heat **mitigation**, and biodiversity support. However, **implementation** faces barriers: **upfront** costs, maintenance responsibilities, and lack of **political will**. Nevertheless, **co-benefits**, such as improved mental health and reduced energy consumption, often **outweig** investments. Successful cases, like Singapore's Gardens by the Bay or Copenhagen's **cloudburst** management, **demonstrate** **feasibility**. **Scaling** up requires **integrated** planning across **sectors**, public-private partnerships, and community engagement. 

Ultimately, green cities are not a **luxury** but a necessity for climate **resilience**.

##  中文翻译  
可持续城市发展调和经济增长、社会公平和环境保护。

绿色基础设施——绿色屋顶、透水铺装、城市森林——提供生态系统服务，如雨水管理、热缓解和生物多样性支持。然而，实施面临障碍：前期成本、维护责任和缺乏政治意愿。尽管如此，协同效益，如改善心理健康和降低能耗，往往超过投资。成功案例，如新加坡滨海湾花园或哥本哈根暴雨管理，证明了可行性。扩大规模需要跨部门综合规划、公私伙伴关系和社区参与。

最终，绿色城市不是奢侈品，而是气候韧性的必需品。

    > 该计划调和了利益相关者之间的分歧。
  - **reconcile growth with protection** . 协调增长与保护
    > *Cities must reconcile growth with environmental protection.*
