# 📄 文章 02：教育公平与数字鸿沟

The COVID-19 pandemic **abruptly** shifted **instruction** online, **exposing** a **chasm** between students with reliable **broadband** and those without.

This digital divide, long acknowledged but **inadequately** addressed, has **exacerbated** educational **inequities**. Affluent school **districts** **swiftly** deployed remote learning platforms, **whereas** **under-resourced** communities **grappled** with malfunctioning devices and **spotty** connectivity.

Moreover, the **pivot** to virtual classrooms has **amplified** disparities in parental support: children from educated households received **substantial** academic **scaffolding**, while those in less privileged environments often lacked adult supervision.

Nevertheless, the crisis also **catalysed** innovations—open educational resources, community Wi-Fi hotspots, and television-based instruction—that may **narrow** the gap if **sustained**.

However, technological fixes alone prove **insufficient**; teacher training, **curricular** adaptation, and psychosocial support are equally **imperative**. To **forge** a more equitable system, **policymakers** must treat internet access as a public **utility**, not a luxury **commodity**.

---

## 🌐 中文翻译

新冠疫情突然将教学转向线上，暴露了拥有可靠宽带的学生与没有宽带的学生之间的鸿沟。

这种长期被承认但未得到充分解决的数字鸿沟，加剧了教育不平等。富裕学区迅速部署了远程学习平台，而资源不足的社区则在故障设备和信号不稳定中挣扎。

此外，向虚拟教室的转变放大了家长支持的差异：来自受教育家庭的孩子获得了大量学业支持，而条件较差环境中的孩子往往缺乏成人监督。

然而，这场危机也催生了创新——开放教育资源、社区Wi-Fi热点和电视教学——如果持续下去，可能会缩小差距。

然而，仅靠技术解决方案是不够的；教师培训、课程调整和心理社会支持同样至关重要。为了建立一个更公平的体系，政策制定者必须将互联网接入视为一种公共事业，而非奢侈品。
