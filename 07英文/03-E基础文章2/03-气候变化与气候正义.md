## 📄 文章 03：气候变化与气候正义

 
Climate change is no longer a distant **spectre**; its **manifestations**—**intensified** **hurricanes**, **protracted** **droughts**, and record-breaking heatwaves—are **palpable** across continents.

While **mitigation** efforts focus on reducing greenhouse gas emissions, the principle of climate **justice** demands equal attention. This framework **posits** that those who bear the least historical responsibility for carbon emissions—developing nations and **impoverished** communities—often suffer the **gravest** **consequences**.

**Furthermore**, adaptation measures, such as building sea walls or developing **drought-resistant** **crops**, are **prohibitively** expensive for **vulnerable** populations, creating a vicious cycle of **vulnerability**.

Industrialised countries have **pledged** financial assistance under the Paris Agreement, yet **disbursement** has been **sluggish** and conditional. A just transition necessitates not only technological transfers but also the **inclusion** of **marginalised** voices in international **negotiations**.

The ethical **imperative** is **unequivocal**: climate action must be **predicated** on **solidarity**, not charity.


气候变化不再是一个遥远的幽灵；其表现形式——增强的飓风、持久的干旱和破纪录的热浪——在全球各地都触手可及。

虽然减缓努力的重点是减少温室气体排放，但气候正义原则也需要同等重视。这一框架认为，那些对碳排放负有最少历史责任的国家——发展中国家和贫困社区——往往会遭受最严重的后果。

此外，适应措施，如修建海堤或开发抗旱作物，对脆弱人群来说昂贵得令人望而却步，形成脆弱的恶性循环。

工业化国家在《巴黎协定》下承诺了财政援助，但支付一直缓慢且有条件。公正的转型不仅需要技术转移，还需要在國際谈判中纳入边缘化的声音。

道德必要性是明确的：气候行动必须以团结而非慈善为基础。
