# 📄 文章 07：企业社会责任与利润最大化

Corporate social responsibility (CSR) has **evolved** from **philanthropic** add-on to strategic **imperative**. 

**Firms** increasingly recognise that environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance correlates with long-term profitability, risk reduction, and brand **reputation**. However, critics argue that CSR is a **greenwashing** **tactic**, **distracting** from profit-seeking core. **Empirical** studies **yield** mixed results: some find positive ESG–financial linkage, others no significant effect. The **stakeholder** theory posits that firms must balance **interests** of **shareholders**, employees, communities, and **planet**. Conversely, shareholder **primacy** **insists** on **maximising** returns. Regulation, such as **mandatory** ESG **disclosure**, can level the playing field. 

Ultimately, responsible **capitalism** may reconcile ethics with efficiency, but requires genuine **commitment**, not mere **rhetoric**.

# 中文翻译  
企业社会责任已从慈善附加品演变为战略必需。

企业日益认识到环境、社会和治理表现与长期盈利能力、风险降低和品牌声誉相关。然而，批评者认为CSR是绿色洗白策略，分散了对利润追求核心的关注。实证研究结果不一：有些发现ESG与财务正相关，有些则无显著效应。利益相关者理论认为企业必须平衡股东、员工、社区和地球的利益。相反，股东至上原则坚持回报最大化。监管，如强制性ESG披露，可以创造公平竞争环境。最终，负责任的资本主义可以调和伦理与效率，但需要真正的承诺，而非空谈。
