# 📄 文章 10：教育技术与个性化学习（完整·科学记忆·场景动作版）

🔖 英文原文  
Educational technology (EdTech) promises to personalise learning, adapting content and pace to individual students' needs through AI-driven platforms. Adaptive learning systems analyse performance data to recommend remedial exercises or advanced challenges, potentially reducing achievement gaps. However, concerns abound: data privacy, algorithmic bias, and over-reliance on screens may harm social development. Furthermore, teachers' roles shift from knowledge transmitters to learning facilitators, requiring new professional development. Evidence from randomised controlled trials shows moderate effects, but contextual factors—infrastructure, teacher training, and parental support—significantly moderate outcomes. A balanced approach integrates EdTech with human interaction, leveraging technology for formative assessment while preserving pedagogical relationality.

🌐 中文翻译  
教育技术承诺个性化学习，通过AI驱动平台根据学生个体需求调整内容和节奏。自适应学习系统分析表现数据，推荐补救练习或高级挑战，有可能缩小成绩差距。然而，担忧众多：数据隐私、算法偏见和过度依赖屏幕可能损害社会发展。此外，教师角色从知识传递者转变为学习促进者，需要新的专业发展。随机对照试验的证据显示中等效应，但情境因素——基础设施、教师培训和家长支持——显著调节结果。平衡的方法将教育技术与人际互动结合，利用技术进行形成性评价，同时保留教学的关系性。

📖 内容解说  
本文属教育技术与心理学。结构：承诺（personalise）→ 机制（adaptive systems）→ 潜在效益（reduce gaps）→ 担忧（privacy, bias, screen overuse）→ 教师角色转变 → 证据（RCTs）及调节因素 → 平衡方案（tech + human）。注意利弊权衡和调节变量。

🎬 逻辑链条：教育技术 6 步记忆法  
EdTech promises personalised learning（AI 调整 content/pace）→ adaptive systems analyze data，推荐练习，可能 reduce gaps → 但 concerns：privacy, bias, screen overuse harm social dev → 教师角色从 transmitter 到 facilitator，需新培训 → RCTs 显示 moderate effects，但 context（infrastructure, training, support）moderates → 平衡：integrate EdTech with human interaction，用于 formative assessment，保留 relationality。
