## Article 09 — Population Ageing & Economic Impact


Population **ageing**, driven by declining fertility and rising life **expectancy**, is **reconstituting** the **demographic** profiles of advanced economies.
While this transition reflects improved **healthcare** and living standards, it **imposes** **substantial** **fiscal** **burdens**: pension obligations **escalate**, healthcare **expenditures** swell, and the **shrinking** **workforce** threatens productivity.

Traditional **pay-as-you-go** pension systems face **insolvency** without **parametric** reforms—raising retirement ages, adjusting contribution rates, or **incentivising** private savings.
Moreover, older populations tend to be more **risk-averse**, potentially **dampening** **entrepreneurial** dynamism and innovation. However, this perspective risks **ageism**, overlooking the valuable human capital that experienced workers bring—**mentorship**, **institutional** memory, and interpersonal skills.
Policy responses must be multifaceted: promoting lifelong learning, redesigning jobs to accommodate physical limitations, and fostering intergenerational **solidarity** .

Ultimately, societal ageing is not a **crisis** to be resolved but a reality to be managed with **foresight** and **creativity**, recognising that **longevity** is an achievement, not a **liability**.


### Chinese
人口老龄化，由生育率下降和预期寿命延长驱动，正在重塑发达经济体的人口概况。虽然这种转变反映了医疗保健和生活水平的提高，但它带来了巨大的财政负担：养老金支出攀升，医疗支出膨胀，萎缩的劳动力威胁生产力。

传统的现收现付养老金制度若没有参数改革——提高退休年龄、调整缴费率或激励私人储蓄——将面临破产。此外，老年人口往往更规避风险，可能抑制创业活力和创新。然而，这种观点有可能涉及年龄歧视，忽视了有经验的工人带来的宝贵人力资本——指导、制度记忆和人际交往技能。政策回应必须是多方面的：促进终身学习、重新设计工作以适应身体限制，以及促进代际团结。

最终，社会老龄化不是需要解决的危机，而是需要用远见和创造力来管理的现实，认识到长寿是一种成就，而非负担。
