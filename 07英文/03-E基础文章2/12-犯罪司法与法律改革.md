## 📄 文章：犯罪、司法与法律改革（Crime, Justice & Legal Reform）

An effective legal framework must **deter** criminal behavior while offering avenues for **rehabilitation**. Harsh **penalties** alone often fail to reduce **recidivism**; instead, they may **victimize** marginalized groups further. **Prosecutors** and **magistrates** must balance **retributive** justice with **restorative** principles, ensuring that **offenders** are not merely **incarcerated** but are prepared for eventual **parole**.

**Adjudicating** complex cases requires **impartiality** and a deep understanding of sociological **mitigation** factors. While the **prosecution** seeks accountability, the system should prioritize **orderly** social reintegration over **offensive** punitive measures. Ultimately, the **correlation** between education and reduced crime rates suggests that investing in human capital is a more **sustainable** form of **deterrence** than mere imprisonment.

## 🌐 中文翻译

一个有效的法律框架必须**威慑**犯罪行为，同时为**康复**提供途径。仅靠严厉的**惩罚**往往无法降低**累犯率**；相反，它们可能进一步**使……成为受害者**边缘化群体。**检察官**和**地方法官**必须平衡**报复性**司法与**恢复性**原则，确保**罪犯**不仅被**监禁**，而且为最终的**假释**做好准备。

**裁决**复杂案件需要**公正**以及对社会学**减轻**因素的深刻理解。虽然**检方**寻求问责，但制度应优先考虑**有序的**社会重返社会，而非**攻击性的**惩罚性措施。最终，教育与犯罪率降低之间的**相关性**表明，投资于人力资本是一种比单纯监禁更**可持续**的**威慑**形式。

## deter . /dɪˈtɜː(r)/（英） /dɪˈtɜːr/（美） · v.
