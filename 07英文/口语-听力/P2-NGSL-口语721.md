# NGSL-Spoken 30篇对话覆盖计划：第1–10篇

> 格式：每篇先完整英文对话，再完整中文翻译。  
> 主题：问候介绍、家庭朋友、日常作息、工作职业、学校学习、购物价格、餐厅食物、咖啡店闲聊、交通问路、旅行酒店。

## 第 1 篇：Greetings and Introductions 问候与介绍

**英文**

A: Hi, I’m Li. Nice to meet you.  
B: Nice to meet you too, Li. I’m Anna.  
A: Where are you from, Anna?  
B: I’m from Canada. And you?  
A: I’m from China. I live in Beijing now.  
B: Oh, nice. What do you do?  
A: I’m a student. I study English.  
B: That’s great. How long have you been here?  
A: About two months. I’m still learning.  
B: Your English is good. Don’t worry.  
A: Thanks. I’m a little nervous when I speak.  
B: That’s normal. Just take your time.  
A: Thank you. Can I ask your number?  
B: Sure. Let’s keep in touch.  
A: Great. See you later, Anna.  
B: See you, Li. Have a nice day.

**中文**

A：嗨，我是李。很高兴认识你。  
B：我也很高兴认识你，李。我是安娜。  
A：安娜，你来自哪里？  
B：我来自加拿大。你呢？  
A：我来自中国。我现在住在北京。  
B：哦，不错。你做什么工作？  
A：我是学生。我学英语。  
B：太好了。你在这里多久了？  
A：大约两个月。我还在学习。  
B：你的英语很好。别担心。  
A：谢谢。我说的时候有点紧张。  
B：这很正常。慢慢来。  
A：谢谢。我能问一下你的电话号码吗？  
B：当然。我们保持联系吧。  
A：太好了。回头见，安娜。  
B：再见，李。祝你今天愉快。

## 第 2 篇：Family and Friends 家庭与朋友

**英文**

A: Do you have a big family, Tom?  
B: Not really. I have one brother and one sister.  
A: Are they older or younger?  
B: My brother is older, and my sister is younger.  
A: What about your parents?  
B: They live in the countryside. They’re retired.  
A: Do you see them often?  
B: I visit them once a month. I call them every week.  
A: That’s nice. Do you have many friends here?  
B: A few. My best friend is from work.  
A: What do you usually do together?  
B: We sometimes eat out, watch movies, or go for a walk.  
A: That sounds fun. I need to meet more people.  
B: You can join us this weekend if you like.  
A: Really? I’d love to. Thanks!  
B: No problem. You’re welcome to come.

**中文**

A：汤姆，你家人多吗？  
B：不算多。我有一个哥哥和一个姐姐。  
A：他们比你大还是比你小？  
B：我哥哥比我大，我姐姐比我小。  
A：你父母呢？  
B：他们住在乡下。他们退休了。  
A：你经常见他们吗？  
B：我一个月去看他们一次。我每周给他们打电话。  
A：那很好。你在这里朋友多吗？  
B：有几个。我最好的朋友是同事。  
A：你们通常一起做什么？  
B：我们有时出去吃饭、看电影，或者散步。  
A：听起来很有趣。我需要多认识一些人。  
B：如果你愿意，这周末可以加入我们。  
A：真的吗？我很愿意。谢谢！  
B：没问题。欢迎你来。

## 第 3 篇：Daily Routine 日常作息

**英文**

A: What time do you usually get up?  
B: I get up at seven. Sometimes at seven thirty.  
A: That’s early. Do you have breakfast?  
B: Yes, I always have breakfast. I need coffee.  
A: What do you do after breakfast?  
B: I go to work. I start at nine.  
A: How do you go to work?  
B: I take the bus. Sometimes I walk.  
A: Are you busy at work?  
B: Yes, very busy. I have a lot to do.  
A: What time do you finish?  
B: I finish at six. Then I go home and cook.  
A: Do you watch TV at night?  
B: Not much. I usually read or listen to music.  
A: What time do you go to bed?  
B: Around eleven. I need enough sleep.  
A: Me too. Good night!  
B: Good night. Sleep well.

**中文**

A：你通常几点起床？  
B：我七点起床。有时七点半。  
A：那挺早的。你吃早饭吗？  
B：吃，我总是吃早饭。我需要咖啡。  
A：早饭后你做什么？  
B：我去上班。我九点开始。  
A：你怎么去上班？  
B：我坐公交车。有时我走路。  
A：你工作忙吗？  
B：忙，非常忙。我有很多事要做。  
A：你几点下班？  
B：我六点下班。然后我回家做饭。  
A：你晚上看电视吗？  
B：不太看。我通常看书或者听音乐。  
A：你几点睡觉？  
B：大约十一点。我需要足够的睡眠。  
A：我也是。晚安！  
B：晚安。好好睡。

## 第 4 篇：Work and Jobs 工作与职业

**英文**

A: What do you do for work, Sarah?  
B: I work in a bank. I’m an assistant.  
A: How long have you worked there?  
B: For about three years.  
A: Do you like your job?  
B: Yes, mostly. The people are friendly.  
A: What are your main duties?  
B: I answer calls, help customers, and prepare reports.  
A: Is it stressful?  
B: Sometimes. We have deadlines and meetings.  
A: How do you deal with stress?  
B: I take short breaks and talk to colleagues.  
A: What about your boss?  
B: She’s strict but fair. I learn a lot from her.  
A: Do you want to change jobs?  
B: Maybe one day. But right now, I need the money.  
A: That’s understandable. Good luck!  
B: Thanks. You too.

A：莎拉，你做什么工作？

A：你在那里工作多久了？  
B：大约三年了。  
A：你喜欢你的工作吗？  
B：喜欢，大部分时候。人们很友好。  
A：你的主要职责是什么？  
B：我接电话、帮助客户、准备报告。  
A：压力大吗？  
B：有时。我们有截止日期和会议。  
A：你怎么应对压力？  
B：我短暂休息，和同事聊聊天。  
A：你的老板呢？  
B：她很严格但很公平。我从她那里学到很多。  
A：你想换工作吗？  
B：也许有一天。但现在，我需要钱。  
A：可以理解。祝你好运！  
B：谢谢。你也是。

B: Thank you. I’ll do my best.

## 第 6 篇：Shopping and Prices 购物与价格

**英文**

A: Excuse me, can you help me?

B: What size do you wear?

B: Of course. The fitting room is over there.  
A: Thanks. … It fits well. I’ll take it.  
B: Great. How would you like to pay?  
A: By card, please.  
B: No problem. Here’s your receipt.  
A: Thank you. Do you have bags?  
B: Yes, here you go. Have a nice day.  
A: You too. Goodbye.

A：打扰一下，你能帮我吗？

A：我想是中号。

A：蓝色或白色。这件多少钱？  
B：二十美元。  
A：还不错。我可以试穿吗？  
B：当然。试衣间在那边。  
A：谢谢。……很合身。我买了。  
B：好的。你想怎么付款？  
A：刷卡，谢谢。  
B：没问题。这是你的收据。

## 第 9 篇：Transport and Directions 交通与问路

**英文**

A: Excuse me, how do I get to the station?  
B: You can take the bus or the subway.  
A: Which is faster?  
B: The subway is faster, but the bus is cheaper.  
A: Where is the subway station?

A: Is it far?

A: Okay. How much is the ticket?  
B: It’s two dollars. You can buy it at the machine.  
A: Do I need to change lines?  
B: Yes, change at Central Station.  
A: Which line should I take?  
B: Take Line 2, then transfer to Line 5.  
A: Thank you so much.  
B: No problem. Have a safe trip.  
A: You too. Bye!

A：打扰一下，去车站怎么走？

A：哪个更快？

A：地铁站在哪里？  
B：直走，然后在红绿灯处左转。  
A：远吗？  
B：不太远。走路大约十分钟。  
A：好的。车票多少钱？  
B：两美元。你可以在机器上买。  
A：需要换乘吗？  
B：需要，在中央站换乘。  
A：我应该坐哪条线？  
B：坐2号线，然后换乘5号线。

B：没问题。旅途平安。

## 第 11 篇：Airport and Flights 机场与航班

**英文**

A: Good morning. May I see your passport and ticket, please?  
B: Here you are. I’m flying to London.  
A: How many bags are you checking in?  
B: Just one. And I have a carry-on.  
A: Please place your luggage on the scale.  
B: Okay. Is it too heavy?  
A: No, it’s fine. Here’s your boarding pass.  
B: Thank you. Which gate is it?  
A: Gate 22. Boarding starts at 10:30.  
B: Is the flight on time?  
A: Yes, it’s on schedule.  
B: Great. Where is security?  
A: Go through the doors on your right.  
B: Thanks. Do I need to take out my laptop?  
A: Yes, and any liquids as well.  
B: Okay. Have a good flight.  
A: You too. Enjoy your trip.

**中文**

A：早上好。请让我看一下您的护照和机票。  
B：给你。我飞往伦敦。  
A：您要托运几件行李？  
B：就一件。还有一件随身行李。  
A：请把行李放在秤上。  
B：好的。超重了吗？  
A：没有，没问题。这是您的登机牌。  
B：谢谢。在几号登机口？  
A：22号登机口。10:30开始登机。  
B：航班准点吗？  
A：准点。  
B：太好了。安检在哪里？  
A：从您右边的门过去。  
B：谢谢。需要把笔记本电脑拿出来吗？  
A：是的，液体也要拿出来。  
B：好的。祝您飞行愉快。  
A：您也是。旅途愉快。

B: A little. It might be windy in the afternoon.  
A: I prefer warm weather.  
B: Me too. I can’t wait for summer.

A: That’s perfect for me.  
B: But it can be humid.

B: Well, you’re lucky. I prefer spring.  
A: Spring is nice too. Not too hot, not too cold.  
B: Exactly. Let’s hope for a sunny weekend.

## 第 13 篇：Health and Doctor 健康与看病

**英文**

A: Good morning. What’s the problem?  
B: I have a headache and a fever.  
A: How long have you felt like this?  
B: Since yesterday evening.  
A: Do you have a cough?  
B: Yes, a little. And my throat hurts.

B: Do I need medicine?  
A: I’ll give you a prescription. Take it twice a day.  
B: Where can I get it?  
A: At the pharmacy next door.  
B: Should I stay home from work?  
A: Yes, rest for two days.  
B: Okay. Thank you, doctor.  
A: Drink plenty of water and sleep well.  
B: I will. Goodbye.  
A: Goodbye. Get well soon.

**中文**

A：早上好。哪里不舒服？

A：这样多久了？  
B：从昨天晚上开始。  
A：咳嗽吗？  
B：有一点。而且喉咙痛。  
A：我量一下体温。……38.5度。  
B：严重吗？  
A：是低烧。你需要休息和补充水分。  
B：需要吃药吗？  
A：我给你开个处方。一天吃两次。  
B：在哪里拿药？

A：真好笑。我也想要宠物。  
B：你应该养一只。它们带来很多快乐。  
A：也许养猫。猫更容易照顾。

## 第 27 篇：Clothes and Fashion 衣服与时尚

**英文**

A: I like your jacket. It looks new.  
B: Thanks. I bought it last week.  
A: Where did you get it?  
B: At the mall. It was on sale.  
A: Nice. I need some new clothes too.  
B: What are you looking for?  
A: A dress for a wedding.  
B: What colour?  
A: Maybe blue or green.  
B: Those colours suit you.  
A: Thanks. Do you like shopping?  
B: Sometimes. I don’t like crowded stores.  
A: Me neither. I prefer shopping online.  
B: Online is easier. But you can’t try things on.  
A: That’s true. You have to guess the size.  
B: I always check the size chart.  
A: Good idea. I’ll do that next time.  
B: Let me know if you find something nice.

**中文**

A：在哪里买的？  
B：在商场。打折。

B：你在找什么？  
A：参加婚礼的裙子。

B：我总是看尺码表。

## 第 29 篇：Expressing Opinions 表达观点

**英文**

B: That’s true. It’s not for everyone.  
A: Do you prefer working from home or the office?  
B: I prefer a mix. Two days at home, three in the office.  
A: That sounds balanced. What about you?  
A: I like the office. I focus better there.  
B: I understand. Everyone is different.  
A: What do you think about social media?  
B: I think it has good and bad sides.  
A: Like what?  
B: It connects people, but it can also waste time.  
A: I agree. It’s a tool, not a solution.  
B: Well said. We need to use it wisely.  
A: Do you think technology makes life better?  
B: Mostly yes. But we shouldn’t depend on it too much.  
A: That’s a fair point.  
B: Thanks. It’s good to share views.

**中文**

## 第 30 篇：Future Plans and Dreams 未来计划与梦想

A: What are your plans for the future?  
B: I want to travel around the world.  
A: That’s a big dream. Where would you go first?  
B: I’d start in Japan. Then Europe.  
A: That sounds amazing. Do you have other goals?  
B: I want to learn another language.  
A: Which one?  
B: Spanish. I think it’s useful.  
A: Good choice. What about your career?  
B: I hope to start my own business one day.  
A: What kind of business?  
B: Something related to food. I love cooking.  
A: You should do it. You’re a great cook.  
B: Thanks. But I need more experience first.  
A: That’s wise. Take your time.  
B: What about you? What are your dreams?

B: Really? What kind?  
A: A novel. I’ve had the idea for years.  
B: You should start writing. Don’t wait.  
A: You’re right. Let’s both chase our dreams.
