# 日常场景对话与常用口语表达

口语是语言的"出口"，靠的是模式记忆（Pattern Recognition）而非翻译思维。本章提供 20 个真实场景对话，帮助建立口语直觉。

---

## 第一章：打招呼与寒暄（Greetings & Small Talk）

### 场景1：初次见面

**对话**：
> **A**: Hi, I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you!  
> **B**: Hey Sarah! I'm Tom. Great to meet you too.  
> **A**: So what do you do?  
> **B**: I'm a software engineer. How about you?  
> **A**: I'm a teacher. Where are you from originally?  
> **B**: I'm from Shanghai, but I've been living in London for three years now.  
> **A**: Oh wow, do you like it here?  
> **B**: Yeah, I love it! The city has so much to offer.  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 | 语境 |
|------|------|------|
| Nice/Great/Good to meet you. | 很高兴认识你。 | 初次见面 |
| What do you do? | 你做什么工作？ | 职业询问 |
| How about you? / And you? | 你呢？ | 转移问题 |
| Where are you from originally? | 你老家是哪里？ | 问来源 |
| I've been living here for... | 我住在这里...年了 | 持续时态 |
| The city has so much to offer. | 这个城市有很多好东西。 | 赞美地方 |

---

### 场景2：日常问候

**对话**：
> **A**: Hey! How are you doing?  
> **B**: Pretty good, thanks! Been a bit busy lately. How about yourself?  
> **A**: Not bad, just the usual. Did you have a good weekend?  
> **B**: It was awesome! I went hiking with some friends. What about you?  
> **A**: I stayed home mostly, caught up on some reading.  
> **B**: Nice! Well, I'd better get going. Let's catch up soon!  
> **A**: Definitely! Take care!  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|
| How are you doing? | 你最近怎么样？ | 比 How are you? 更口语 |
| Pretty good / Not bad | 还不错 | 口语常用回答 |
| Been a bit busy lately. | 最近有点忙。 | 省略了 I've |
| Did you have a good weekend? | 周末过得好吗？ | 周一常用 |
| Caught up on... | 补上/追上... | catch up on 补做拖欠的事 |
| I'd better get going. | 我得走了。 | 礼貌告别 |
| Let's catch up soon! | 我们找时间叙叙旧吧！ | 老朋友间 |

## 第二章：餐厅与点餐（At a Restaurant）

### 场景3：预订和点餐

**对话**：
> **Waiter**: Good evening! Do you have a reservation?  
> **Customer**: Yes, I booked a table for two under the name "Chen".  
> **Waiter**: Perfect, right this way, please.  
> —— (seated) ——  
> **Waiter**: Can I get you something to drink while you look at the menu?  
> **Customer**: Yes, I'd like a sparkling water, please.  
> **Waiter**: Are you ready to order, or do you need a few more minutes?  
> **Customer**: I think we're ready. I'll have the grilled salmon, and she'll have the pasta.  
> **Waiter**: Great choice! How would you like your salmon cooked?  
> **Customer**: Medium, please.  
> **Waiter**: Coming right up!  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Do you have a reservation? | 您有预订吗？ |
| I booked a table for two. | 我订了一张两人桌。 |
| Can I get you something to drink? | 能给您来点喝的吗？ |
| I'll have the... | 我要... （点菜固定句式） |
| How would you like it cooked? | 您要几分熟？ |
| Coming right up! | 马上就来！ |

---

### 场景4：结账

**对话**：
> **Customer**: Excuse me, could we get the bill, please?  
> **Waiter**: Of course! Will you be paying together or separately?  
> **Customer**: Together, please.  
> **Waiter**: That'll be £45.50.  
> **Customer**: Here you go. Keep the change.  
> **Waiter**: Thank you so much! Have a lovely evening!  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Could we get the bill/check? | 能把账单给我们吗？ |
| Together or separately? | 合在一起还是分开付？ |
| Here you go. | 给你。（递东西时说） |
| Keep the change. | 不用找零了。 |

## 第三章：购物（Shopping）

### 场景5：实体店购物

**对话**：
> **Shop assistant**: Hi there, can I help you with anything?  
> **Customer**: Yes, I'm looking for a jacket — something warm for winter.  
> **Assistant**: Sure! What size are you?  
> **Customer**: I'm usually a medium.  
> **Assistant**: We have some great options over here. What's your budget?  
> **Customer**: Around £80 to £100.  
> **Assistant**: This one is on sale for £90. It's very popular.  
> **Customer**: Can I try it on?  
> **Assistant**: Of course! The fitting rooms are just over there.  
> —— (after trying on) ——  
> **Customer**: I'll take it! Do you have it in black?  
> **Assistant**: Let me check... Yes, we do!  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Can I help you with anything? | 有什么需要帮忙的吗？ |
| I'm looking for... | 我在找... |
| What's your budget? | 您的预算是多少？ |
| It's on sale. | 在打折。 |
| Can I try it on? | 我能试穿吗？ |
| The fitting rooms | 试衣间 |
| I'll take it. | 我要这个了。 |
| Do you have it in...? | 有...颜色/尺码吗？ |

---

### 场景6：退换货

**对话**：
> **Customer**: Hi, I'd like to return this shirt please. I bought it last week.  
> **Staff**: Of course. Do you have your receipt?  
> **Customer**: Yes, here it is. The issue is it shrank after washing.  
> **Staff**: I'm sorry about that. Would you like a refund or an exchange?  
> **Customer**: I'd prefer an exchange for a larger size, if you have one.  
> **Staff**: Let me check our stock...  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| I'd like to return/exchange this. | 我想退/换这个。 |
| Do you have your receipt? | 您有收据吗？ |
| It shrank / It's damaged / It doesn't fit. | 缩水了/坏了/不合适。 |
| Would you like a refund or an exchange? | 您要退款还是换货？ |
| I'd prefer... | 我更倾向于... |

## 第四章：交通与问路（Transport & Directions）

### 场景7：问路

**对话**：
> **A**: Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the train station?  
> **B**: Sure! Go straight down this road for about two blocks, then turn left at the traffic lights. The station will be on your right, you can't miss it.  
> **A**: Is it far from here?  
> **B**: It's about a 10-minute walk.  
> **A**: Great, thank you so much!  
> **B**: No problem, have a good trip!  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Could you tell me how to get to...? | 您能告诉我怎么去...吗？ |
| Go straight (down/along)... | 一直走... |
| Turn left/right at... | 在...处向左/右转 |
| It's on your left/right. | 在你的左/右边。 |
| You can't miss it. | 你不会错过的（很好找）。 |
| It's about a X-minute walk. | 走路大概X分钟。 |

---

### 场景8：乘坐公共交通

**对话**：
> **A**: Excuse me, which bus goes to Oxford Street?  
> **B**: You want the number 23. It stops just across the road.  
> **A**: How often does it come?  
> **B**: Every 8 minutes or so.  
> **A**: Do I need to buy a ticket before boarding?  
> **B**: No, you can tap your card on the bus.  
> **A**: Perfect, thank you!

## 第五章：工作场景（Workplace）

### 场景9：会议中

**实用表达汇总**：

| 功能 | 英文表达 | 中文 |
|------|---------|------|
| 开始会议 | Shall we get started? / Let's kick off. | 我们开始吧。 |
| 提出观点 | I'd like to point out that... | 我想指出... |
| 表达同意 | That's a great point. / I couldn't agree more. | 说得很好。/完全同意。 |
| 表达不同意 | I see your point, but... / With respect, I disagree. | 我明白你的意思，但... |
| 请求澄清 | Could you elaborate on that? / What do you mean by...? | 能展开说吗？ |
| 打断（礼貌） | Sorry to interrupt, but... / Can I just jump in here? | 对不起打断一下... |
| 总结 | To sum up... / To wrap up... | 总结一下... |
| 结束会议 | That's all for today. / Let's call it a wrap. | 今天就到这里。 |

## 第六章：医疗就诊（At the Doctor's）

### 场景11：看诊

**对话**：
> **Doctor**: What seems to be the problem today?  
> **Patient**: I've been having a terrible headache for the past three days, and I feel a bit nauseous.  
> **Doctor**: On a scale of 1 to 10, how would you rate the pain?  
> **Patient**: About a 7. It's mostly on the right side.  
> **Doctor**: Any fever or vomiting?  
> **Patient**: No fever, but I did vomit once yesterday.  
> **Doctor**: I see. I'd like to run some tests. In the meantime, I'll prescribe some painkillers.  

**关键表达**：
| 表达 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| What seems to be the problem? | 怎么不舒服？ |
| I've been having a... | 我一直有... |
| On a scale of 1 to 10... | 1到10分的话... |
| I'd like to run some tests. | 我想做一些检查。 |
| Prescribe | 开（药方） |
| I feel nauseous/dizzy/feverish | 我感到恶心/头晕/发烧 |
